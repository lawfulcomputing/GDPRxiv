Advice on the draft amendment of the Fireworks Decision regarding pyro-pass  
Dear Ms. Heijnen,  

In your letter dated April 26, 2023, the Data Protection Authority (AP) was consulted pursuant to Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation regarding the draft Decision of […] to amend the Fireworks Decision in connection with the implementation of the Benelux decision concerning the introduction of a pyro-pass and several other amendments (hereinafter: the draft).  

The AP has a comment on the draft and advises that this be taken into account.  

Scope of the draft  

Pursuant to Directive 2013/29/EU1, certain pyrotechnic articles2 may only be offered on the market to individuals with specialized knowledge. The Benelux decision regarding the introduction of the pyro-pass3 (hereinafter: the Benelux decision) aims to introduce a uniform control document (pyro-pass) so that a person wishing to purchase pyrotechnic articles within the Benelux can prove that they possess the required specialized knowledge.

The Netherlands must maintain a register of issued pyro-passes4 under the Benelux decision. This pyro-pass register must be accessible to a market participant prior to the provision of a pyrotechnic article.5  

The Collection Act IenW 2021 contains the legal basis for data processing in the pyro-pass register. The draft contains a further elaboration of the pyro-pass and the pyro-pass register, including the issuance of the pyro-pass, the verification of the pyro-pass, and the accessibility of the pyro-pass register.  

Advice  

Simple inquiry of the pyro-pass register  
According to the explanation accompanying the Collection Act IenW 2021, consultation of the pyro-pass register by a market participant is limited to an inquiry based on the serial number of the pyro-pass to verify whether the relevant pyro-pass is valid. It does not allow the market participant to freely search the pyro-pass register for data of other persons than the buyer presenting themselves, according to the explanation.6  

The AP advises that this inquiry of the pyro-pass register by means of the serial number of the pyro-pass be included in the draft, so that it is legally ensured that not all personal data in the register are visible to a market participant and that a market participant cannot easily access personal data by searching other than by the serial number.  

Workload of the AP  

The AP notes that the draft is not expected to require additional effort from the AP.  

Disclosure of the advice  

The AP intends to make this advice public no earlier than four weeks after the date on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. Unless otherwise notified, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
