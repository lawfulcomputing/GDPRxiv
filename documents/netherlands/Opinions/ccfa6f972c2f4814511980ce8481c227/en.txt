Subject 
Advice on the draft for the Implementation Act regarding the regulation on information to be added to money transfers and transfers of crypto assets 

Dear Ms. Kaag,

In your letter dated July 14, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation regarding the draft amendment of the Act on the Prevention of Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorism and certain other laws in connection with the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 concerning information to be added to money transfers and transfers of certain crypto assets and amending Directive (EU) 2015/849 (Implementation Act regarding the regulation on information to be added to money transfers and transfers of crypto assets) (hereinafter: the draft).

The assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments.

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
