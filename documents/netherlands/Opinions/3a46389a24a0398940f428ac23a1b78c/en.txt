Advice on a draft for a Decree on the exceptional use of frequency space 
Dear Mr. Blok, 

In your letter dated October 29, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the GDPR regarding a draft for a Decree on the exceptional use of frequency space (hereinafter: the draft). 

The AP has no comments on the draft. 

The AP intends to make this advice public on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl as soon as the text of the (amended) draft is made public. The AP would appreciate being informed of the expected date of publication as soon as it is known.
