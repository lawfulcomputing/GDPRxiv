Subject  
Advice on the draft amendment of the Regulation on the recognition and inspection authority for APK  

Dear Ms. Heijnen,  

In your letter dated August 14, 2023, the Data Protection Authority (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft Regulation of the Secretary of State for Infrastructure and Water Management to amend the Regulation on the recognition and inspection authority for APK in connection with the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2021/392 of the Commission of March 4, 2021, concerning the monitoring and reporting of data related to CO2 emissions from passenger cars and light commercial vehicles in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2019/631 of the European Parliament and the Council and repealing Implementing Regulations (EU) No. 1014/2010, (EU) No. 293/2012, (EU) 2017/1152, and (EU) 2017/1153 of the Commission (OJ EU 2021, L 77).  

The assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments.  

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
