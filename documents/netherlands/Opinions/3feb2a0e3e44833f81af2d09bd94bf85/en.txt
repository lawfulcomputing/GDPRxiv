Subject 
Advice on the draft for the Act to improve the connection between vocational education and the labor market 
Dear Mr. Dijkgraaf, 

In your letter dated August 30, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft for the Amendment of the Education and Vocational Education Act and several other laws in connection with improving the connection of vocational education to the labor market (Act to improve the connection between vocational education and the labor market). 

The assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments. 

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
