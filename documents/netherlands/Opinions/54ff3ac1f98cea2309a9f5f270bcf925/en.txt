Dear Ms. Visser,  
In your letter dated October 7, 2021, the Dutch Data Protection Authority (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft for a Regulation from the Minister of Infrastructure and Water Management, no. IENW/BSK-2021/219814, to amend the Regulation on the Competence of Drivers 2012 in connection with the (re)introduction of the practical trainer certificate for further training and some technical amendments (hereinafter: the draft).  
The AP has no comments on the draft.  
The AP intends to make this advice public on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl as soon as the text of the (amended) draft is made public. The AP would appreciate being informed of the expected date of publication as soon as this is known.
