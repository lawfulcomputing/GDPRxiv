Subject 
Advice on the amendment of the Passport Implementation Regulation Netherlands 2001 regarding identity cards for detainees 
Dear Ms. Van Huffelen, 

In your letter dated April 26, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft for the Regulation amending the Passport Implementation Regulation Netherlands 2001 in connection with the designation of municipalities with a penitentiary institution for the issuance of identity cards to detainees (hereinafter: the draft). 

The AP has no comments on the draft. 

The AP intends to make this advice public no earlier than four weeks from now on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. Unless otherwise notified, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
