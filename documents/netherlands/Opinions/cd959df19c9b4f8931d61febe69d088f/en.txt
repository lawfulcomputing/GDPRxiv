Advice on the draft Bill for balanced internationalization  

Dear Mr. Dijkgraaf, 

In your letter dated July 19, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft Proposal for a law to amend, among other things, the Higher Education and Research Act in connection with the establishment of regulations concerning the management of effective foreign language education offerings (hereinafter: the draft). 

The assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments. 

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
