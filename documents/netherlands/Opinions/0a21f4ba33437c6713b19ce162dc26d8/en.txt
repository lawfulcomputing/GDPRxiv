Advice on amendment of the SUWI Decree and amendment of the Decree on rules for main residence in the same household AOW 
Dear Ms. Van Gennip, 

In a letter dated June 9, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), regarding the draft decision to amend the SUWI Decree in connection with the provision of certain data to insurers and some adjustments in Annex I, and to amend the Decree on rules for main residence in the same household AOW in connection with several technical changes (hereinafter: the draft). 

The AP has no comments on the draft. 

The AP intends to make this advice public no earlier than four weeks from now on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. Unless stated otherwise, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
