Subject 
Advice on the draft for the Decision on the security assessment of investments, mergers, and acquisitions 
Dear Ms. Adriaansens, 

In a letter dated July 13, 2022, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft for a Decision containing regulations for the implementation of the Act on the security assessment of investments, mergers, and acquisitions concerning the content of the notification, the information provision by other authorities, the provision of the proceeds to the acquirer, and the setting of retention periods (Decision on the security assessment of investments, mergers, and acquisitions) (hereinafter: the draft). 

The AP has no comments on the draft. 

The AP intends to make this advice public on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl as soon as the text of the draft is made public. The AP would appreciate being informed of the expected date of publication as soon as it is known.
