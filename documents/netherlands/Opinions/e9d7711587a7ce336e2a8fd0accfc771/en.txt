Subject 
Advice on the draft amendment to the Student Finance Act 2000 
Dear Mr. Dijkgraaf, 

In your letter dated August 31, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation regarding the draft of the Decision of [...] to amend, among other things, the Student Finance Act 2000 in connection with a limited expansion of the target group for student finance and the statutory tuition fees, as well as the determination of data to be provided for surveys among students (hereinafter: the draft). 

Assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments. 

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
