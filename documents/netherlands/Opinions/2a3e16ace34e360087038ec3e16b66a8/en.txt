Advice on the draft for the Implementation Act for the Digital Services Regulation  
Dear Ms. Adriaansens,  

In your letter dated August 24, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation regarding the draft for the Act implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 of the European Parliament and the Council of October 19, 2022, concerning a single market for digital services and amending Directive 2000/31/EC (Implementation Act for the Digital Services Regulation) (hereinafter: the draft).  

The assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments.  

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
