Advice on Regulation of Animal Products  
Dear Mr. Adema,  

In a letter dated November 9, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft Regulation of the Minister of Agriculture, Nature and Food Quality amending the Regulation on Animal Products in connection with the inclusion of rules regarding the registration of qualified persons (hereinafter: the draft). In this letter, advice was also requested regarding the intended amendment of the Decree on Animal Products. The AP has provided separate advice on this matter in a letter dated December 19, 2023 (z2023-299154).  

The assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments.  

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
