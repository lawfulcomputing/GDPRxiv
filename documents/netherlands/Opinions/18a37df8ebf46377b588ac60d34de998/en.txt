Subject 
Advice on the draft Implementation Decision on the Directive on Professional Qualifications in Inland Navigation 
Dear Mr. Harbers, 

In your letter dated November 14, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft amendment to the Inland Navigation Decision for the implementation of Directive 2017/2397 of the European Parliament and the Council on the recognition of professional qualifications in inland navigation and repealing Council Directives 91/672/EEC and 96/50/EC (Implementation Decision on the Directive on Professional Qualifications in Inland Navigation, hereinafter: the draft). 

The assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments. 

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
