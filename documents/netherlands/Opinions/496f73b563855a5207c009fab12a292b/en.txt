Subject  
Advice on the draft regulation regarding the provision of the energy label via MijnOverheid  
Dear Mr. De Jonge,

In a letter dated June 8, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft for the Regulation of the Minister for Housing and Spatial Planning to amend the Regulation on Energy Performance of Buildings and the Environmental Regulation in connection with a basis for providing the energy label via MijnOverheid (hereinafter: the draft).

The AP has no comments on the draft.

The AP intends to make this advice public no earlier than four weeks from now on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. Unless stated otherwise, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
