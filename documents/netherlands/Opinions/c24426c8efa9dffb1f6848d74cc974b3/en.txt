Dear Ms. Heijnen, 

In your letter dated October 10, 2023, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation regarding the draft for the Regulation of the Secretary of State for Infrastructure and Water Management, dated […], no. IENW/BSK-2023/290314, establishing rules concerning the pyro-pass and amending the Regulation on other pyrotechnic articles in connection with the implementation of the Benelux decision aimed at combating the improper use of pyrotechnic articles (Pyro-pass Regulation) (hereinafter: the draft). 

The assessment of the personal data aspects of the draft does not give the AP any reason to make comments. 

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
