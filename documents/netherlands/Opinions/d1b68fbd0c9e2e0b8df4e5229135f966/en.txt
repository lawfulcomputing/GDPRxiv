Subject 
Advice on the draft Implementation Act of the Sixth Directive on Motor Vehicle Insurance 
Dear Ms. Yesilgöz-Zegerius,

In a letter dated October 10, 2022, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft Implementation Act of the Sixth Directive on Motor Vehicle Insurance (Amendment of the Liability Insurance for Motor Vehicles Act and the Financial Supervision Act in connection with the implementation of Directive (EU) 2021/2118 amending Directive 2009/103/EC concerning insurance against the statutory liability that may arise from participation in traffic by motor vehicles and the control of insurance against this liability; hereinafter referred to as the draft).

The AP has no comments on the draft.

The AP intends to make this advice public no earlier than four weeks from now on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. Unless stated otherwise, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
