Advice on the draft amendment to the Decree on the working conditions of posted workers in the EU  
regarding the implementation of Directive 2020/1057/EU  
Dear Ms. Van Gennip,  
In your letter dated August 30, 2022, the Dutch Data Protection Authority (AP) was consulted based on the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft amendment to the Decree on the working conditions of posted workers in the EU in connection with the implementation of Directive 2020/1057/EU (hereinafter: the draft).  
The AP has no comments on the draft.  
The AP intends to make this advice public on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl as soon as the text of the (amended) draft is made public. The AP would appreciate being informed of the expected date of publication as soon as it is known.
