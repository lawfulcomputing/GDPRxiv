Subject 
Legislative advice on the Manning of Seagoing Vessels Act 
Dear Mr. Harbers,

In a letter dated October 25, 2022, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft legislative proposal containing rules on the manning of seagoing vessels (Manning of Seagoing Vessels Act) (hereinafter: the draft).

The AP has no comments on the draft.

The AP intends to make this advice public no earlier than four weeks from now on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. Unless stated otherwise, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
