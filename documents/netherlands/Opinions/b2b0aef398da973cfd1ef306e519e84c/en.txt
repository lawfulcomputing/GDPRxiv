Amendment of the Water Authority Act  

Dear Mr. Harbers,

In your letter dated August 4, 2022, the Data Protection Authority (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft bill for the Amendment of the Water Authority Act, the Water Act, and the General Administrative Law Act in connection with strengthening the application of the benefit principle in water system charges, accommodating new developments, and resolving several bottlenecks (hereinafter: the draft).

The AP has no comments on the draft.

The AP intends to make this advice public on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl after four weeks. Unless otherwise notified, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
