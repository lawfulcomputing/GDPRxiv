In your letter dated July 6, 2023, the Data Protection Authority (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft amendment of the Implementation Decision of the Pension Act and the Act on Mandatory Occupational Pension Schemes in relation to data provision for choice guidance in pensions.

This advice will be published on the AP's website within two weeks.
