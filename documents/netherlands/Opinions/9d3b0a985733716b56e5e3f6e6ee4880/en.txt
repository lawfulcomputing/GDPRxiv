Subject 
Supplementary advice on the amended draft bill for an amendment to the Act on the Recovery Operation for Benefits 

Dear Ms. De Vries,

In a letter dated December 22, 2022, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, paragraph 4, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the supplementary draft bill for the Act on the Recovery Operation for Benefits (hereinafter: the draft).

The AP has no comments on the draft.

The AP intends to make this advice public no earlier than four weeks from now on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. Unless otherwise notified, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
