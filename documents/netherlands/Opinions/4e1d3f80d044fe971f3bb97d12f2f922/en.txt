Subject  
Draft Bill Strengthening the Position of Parents and Students in Appropriate Education  
Dear Mr. Wiersma,

In a letter dated October 6, 2022, the Authority for Personal Data (AP) was consulted pursuant to the provisions of Article 36, fourth paragraph, of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding the draft bill amending the Primary Education Act, the Primary Education Act BES, the Secondary Education Act 2020, the Special Education Centers Act, and the Schools Participation Act in connection with strengthening the position of students who require additional support and their parents (hereinafter: the draft). On November 4, 2022, the AP received a new version of the draft bill.

The advice is limited to Article I, part G, Article II, parts F and H, and Article IV, parts C and E.

The AP has no comments on the draft.

The AP intends to make this advice public no earlier than four weeks from now on the website www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl. Unless otherwise notified, it assumes that there are no objections to this.
