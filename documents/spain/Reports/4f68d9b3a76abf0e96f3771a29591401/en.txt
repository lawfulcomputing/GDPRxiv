Legal Department  
N/REF: 0097/2022  

After examining your request for a report, submitted to this Legal Department, regarding the modification of the Code of Conduct for data processing in advertising activities presented by the Association for the Self-Regulation of Commercial Communication (“AUTOCONTROL”), I am pleased to inform you of the following:  

By Resolution of the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection dated October 9, 2020, the Code of Conduct for data processing in advertising activities promoted by AUTOCONTROL was approved, and the Jury of Advertising was accredited as an internal supervisory body.  
The draft code of conduct had been favorably reported by this Legal Department in our Report 59/2020 dated September 16, 2020.  

This report aims to address the draft modification and extension of the approved code of conduct, as provided for in section 19 thereof, by the Board of Directors of Autocontrol on September 29, 2022, and submitted by the General Director of Autocontrol on October 14, 2022.  

The request for a report is accompanied by the one issued by the General Subdirectorate for Promotion and Authorizations (SGPA) of this Spanish Agency for Data Protection (AEPD).  
The admissibility requirements are met as indicated in section IV of the SGPA Report, which were analyzed in our Report 59/2020.  

Regarding the content of the modifications, they are systematized in section V of the aforementioned SGPA Report, highlighting the changes introduced in the conflict resolution system and the extension of the code through the inclusion of a sanctioning regime.  

Concerning the modification of the conflict resolution system, the modification of the mediation procedure stands out, differentiating those cases in which it is initiated at the request of the interested party from those that arise from the referral of a complaint by the AEPD.  

In the first case, the procedure is simplified by eliminating the preliminary phase of offering the company the possibility of having the complaint managed by its Data Protection Officer (DPO), if one has been appointed, and establishing the voluntary nature of the intervention of the Jury of Advertising in a second phase, as an extrajudicial dispute resolution body, for both the interested party and the adhering entity. This modification allows for a more expedited procedure while respecting the functions assigned to the DPO by the GDPR, as they will be able to actively participate in the mediation process by analyzing the complaint and resolving it during the mediation substantiation period.  

With the same aim of expediting the procedure, a specific section has been introduced regarding the processing to be followed when it concerns a complaint referred by the AEPD. The possibility for the AEPD to refer complaints to the supervisory bodies of the codes of conduct is expressly included in Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and the guarantee of digital rights, and the deadlines established in the code for its resolution are aligned with the administrative deadlines applicable to the AEPD for the exercise of its competencies.  

Regarding the inclusion of a specific sanctioning regime, it is independent of that provided for in the statutes of AUTOCONTROL, being applied by the supervisory body and affecting any entity adhering to the Code, which reinforces the function of the Jury of Advertising as a supervisory body and encourages better compliance with the code.  

For all these reasons, it is considered that the proposed modification and extension of the code of conduct, which complies with the provisions of current regulations on personal data protection, represents a benefit for the interested parties by expediting the processing and resolution of the complaints they present, while also potentially incentivizing the adherence of entities to the code and increasing the mechanisms aimed at ensuring its proper compliance, which enhances its added value, and therefore, a favorable opinion is issued.
