This Agency expressed its opinion in report 97/2020 regarding a previous version of the project, highlighting the need to conduct an impact assessment of the regulation contained therein on the fundamental right to the protection of personal data, as well as making various observations that should be taken into account in its wording.  
As a consequence of this, a new section has been included in the Regulatory Impact Analysis Report related to the "Impact assessment of risks regarding the protection of personal data," and the appropriate modifications have been made in the preamble and in the wording, therefore a favorable report is issued.
