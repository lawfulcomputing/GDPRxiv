I.  
AUDIENCE MEASUREMENT TOOLS AND COOKIES  
The management of a website or mobile application by a publisher generally requires the use of traffic or performance statistics, which are often essential for service provision. One of the technical solutions to gather the necessary information to produce such statistics is the use of data storage and retrieval devices: cookies or similar technologies (throughout this document, all of them will be encompassed under the generic term "cookies").  
The information processed through the use of cookies for this purpose can be managed directly by the publisher or by a provider that can offer a comparative audience measurement service. In that case, the provider would act as the data processor for one or more publishers.  

II.  
CONDITIONS FOR EXEMPTING CONSENT  
Cookies used for the purpose of obtaining traffic or performance statistics could be exempt from consent under certain conditions. To be limited to what is strictly necessary for the provision of the service and, therefore, be exempt from consent in accordance with Article 22.2 of Law 34/2002, of July 11, on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce, these cookies, or similar technologies, must have a purpose strictly limited to the exclusive measurement of the audience of the site or application. This processing must be carried out exclusively on behalf of the publisher and used solely to produce anonymous statistical data.  
Conversely, to be exempt from consent, these cookies or similar technologies must not result in the data being cross-referenced with other processing operations or in the data being transmitted to third parties. Furthermore, they must not allow the aggregated tracking of the navigation of the person using different applications or browsing different websites. Therefore, any solution that uses the same identifier across multiple sites (for example, through cookies placed on a third-party domain loaded by several sites) to cross-reference, duplicate, or measure a unified reach rate of content is excluded.  
Some analytics and audience measurement services, implemented by the publisher itself or by providers, do not fall within the scope of the exemption. This occurs, in particular, when they declare that they reuse the data for other purposes, as is the case with several audience measurement offerings available in the market.  
Therefore, and in general, the AEPD considers that for the proper management of a website, only the following measurements are strictly necessary:  
•  
Audience measurement, page by page;  
•  
The list of pages from which a link has been followed to request the current page (sometimes called "referrer"), whether internal or external to the site, by page and aggregated daily;  
•  
Determination of the type of device, browser, and screen size of visitors, by page and aggregated daily;  
•  
Statistics on page load time, by page and aggregated hourly;  
•  
Statistics on the time spent on each page, bounce rate, scroll depth, by page and aggregated daily;  
•  
Statistics on user actions (clicks, selections), by page and aggregated daily;

•  
Statistics on the geographical area of origin of the requests, by page and aggregated daily.  
Therefore, any other processing of the data beyond these listings by both the publisher and the provider must have the consent of the interested party to be considered lawful.  
III. GUARANTEES THAT THE PUBLISHER MUST IMPLEMENT REGARDING EXEMPT COOKIES  
A.  
MINIMUM GUARANTEES  
When using cookies or similar technologies exempt from the obligation to obtain consent for audience measurement, and in order to respect the rights of individuals, the following minimum guarantees must be implemented:  
1) Users will be informed of the use of these cookies or similar technologies considered exempt for the purpose of audience measurement, for example, through the site's privacy policy or the mobile application;  
2) The lifespan of these cookies or similar technologies will be limited to a period that allows for a meaningful comparison of audiences over time, such as a duration of thirteen months, and will not be automatically extended on new visits;  
3) The information collected through these cookies or similar technologies will be retained for a maximum period of twenty-five months;  
4) The aforementioned lifespan and retention period will be subject to periodic review to limit it to what is strictly necessary.  
B.  
RECOURSE TO A PROVIDER OF AUDIENCE MEASUREMENT SERVICES THAT SERVES MULTIPLE PUBLISHERS  
A provider that offers a comparative audience measurement service to multiple publishers must provide objective guarantees to the publisher that:  
1) The data is collected, processed, and stored independently for each publisher; and  
2) The cookies or similar technologies used are completely independent of each other and of any other cookie or similar technology.  
C.  
ADDITIONAL GUARANTEES WHEN THE PUBLISHER RELIES ON AN AUDIENCE MEASUREMENT SERVICE PROVIDER  
When the publisher relies on an audience measurement service provider, they must comply with the following additional guarantees:  
1) Have a contractual commitment with the provider that meets the requirements of Article 28 of the GDPR, which explicitly states:  
a) The obligation not to reuse the data collected under any circumstances, within the framework of the contract.  
b) Restrict the processing of the data to the purposes established above as strictly necessary.  
c) Comply with the guarantees established in the case of providing service to multiple publishers.  
d) That any transfer of data outside the European Union complies with the compliance conditions established in the GDPR.  
2) Carry out and document an assessment, either by itself or by an independent third party, of whether it is possible to configure, and whether the tools provided by the provider are configured to ensure compliance with the requirements listed in the previous section.
