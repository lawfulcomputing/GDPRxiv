INFORMATION ON  
CONSENT TO  
PROCESS PERSONAL DATA  
OF MINORS
Consent is one of the 
grounds that can legitimize the 
processing of personal data. 
Art. 6.1.a) GDPR. 
Consent; any 
manifestation of free, 
specific, informed, and unequivocal 
will by which the data subject 
accepts, either by means of a 
statement or a clear affirmative 
action, the processing of personal 
data concerning them. Art. 4.11 GDPR.
The data controller, before 
obtaining consent, must 
provide basic information 
at least about their identity, the purposes 
of the processing, the recipients 
of the data, and the exercise of 
the rights. Art. 13.1 GDPR.
The request for consent 
must be presented in such a way that it 
is clearly distinguishable from other 
matters, in an intelligible and 
easily accessible manner, using 
clear and simple language. Art. 7.2 
GDPR.
It is the responsibility of the data 
controller to prove its 
existence. Art. 7.1 GDPR.
Educational institutions and any others that engage in 
activities involving minors 
will ensure the protection of the best interests of the minor 
and their fundamental rights, especially the right 
to the protection of personal data, in the publication or 
dissemination of their personal data through information 
society services. Art. 92 LOPDGDD.
Consent for the 
use of their personal data 
will be granted by their parents or 
legal guardians. Art. 7.1 LOPDGDD.
MINORS UNDER 14 YEARS OLD
The data controller 
will make reasonable efforts to 
verify that consent was 
given or authorized by the holder 
of parental authority or guardianship 
over the child, taking into account 
the available technology. Art. 8.2 
GDPR.
MINORS BETWEEN  
14 AND 18 YEARS OLD
They may grant 
consent for the 
use of their personal data 
themselves, unless 
a specific regulation requires 
the presence of parents or 
guardians. Art. 7.1 LOPDGDD.
The data subject will have the right 
to withdraw their consent at 
any time. The withdrawal 
of consent will not affect 
the lawfulness of the processing 
based on consent 
prior to its withdrawal (without retroactive effects). Before giving their 
consent, the data subject 
will be informed of this. It will be as 
easy to withdraw consent 
as it is to give it. Art. 7.3 GDPR.
