RECOMMENDATIONS FOR USERS  
WHEN USING CHATBOTS  
WITH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE  
General advice regarding the systems.  
Check that the following is provided:  
a privacy policy and legal notice that includes:  
clear and precise identification of the data controller,  
along with their location, tax, and contact information;  
clear information on data protection with reference to  
the GDPR;  
information on how to exercise data protection rights;  
information on whether the chatbot continues to learn  
from conversations held with users and what  
operations are performed with that data once improved.  
Advice for users.  
Do not accept that:  
unnecessary registration data is requested;  
consent is requested without defining the purpose for which  
the data will be processed and without allowing it to be  
withdrawn at any time, or transfers are made to countries  
that do not offer sufficient guarantees.  
The Agency also recommends limiting the personal data  
that is exposed, not providing personal data of third  
parties if there are doubts that the processing will  
transcend the domestic sphere, and considering that  
there are no guarantees that the information provided  
by the chatbot is correct. Depending on the system,  
it may cause emotional harm, misinformation, or  
induce deception.  
Minors  
A chatbot is not a toy.  
Do not allow minors in your care  
to use it without your supervision.
