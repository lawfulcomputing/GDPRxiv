PROTECTION OF MINORS ON THE INTERNET  
Recommendations for Parents and Guardians  

EDUCATE MINORS about the risks to their privacy  
and safety when using mobile technologies. Encourage the  
responsible use of technology.  

LIMIT the time minors spend online.  

LET THEM KNOW that it is necessary to take measures for their own  
safety.  

USE PARENTAL CONTROL. Utilize operating systems,  
internet providers, and third parties that offer parental control options to monitor the use of mobile devices.  
Configure the options, as parental control provides different  
functionalities such as content filtering, time limitations,  
application blocking, social media usage details,  
GPS location, etc.  

CHOOSE THE TOOL that best fits your needs  
and offers guarantees to avoid introducing new risks.  

INFORM YOURSELF about data retention periods and ensure  
that data is not used for purposes other than those  
you require.  

USE BROWSERS, LAUNCHERS, AND APPLICATIONS  
IN THEIR CHILDREN'S VERSION, which are alternatives that may be  
less intrusive.  

DO NOT FORGET that other connected devices such as Smart TVs or  
video game consoles are exposed to similar risks.  

EXCESSIVE BLOCKING CAN BE  
COUNTERPRODUCTIVE. Keep in mind that some tools  
over-block. Keep the possibility of unblocking  
content open at the minor's request and agree with them on  
the filters and restrictions.  

STAY ALERT AND IN COMMUNICATION WITH YOUR  
CHILDREN. Remember that parental control tools are not  
foolproof, so configure privacy and security options appropriately in  
applications and social networks. Talk to your  
children and explain why it is also necessary to take  
precautions on the internet.
