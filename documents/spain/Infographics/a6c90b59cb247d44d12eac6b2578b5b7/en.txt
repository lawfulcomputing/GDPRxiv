**How can I report the dissemination of sensitive images?**  
The claimant must describe the circumstances under which the non-consensual dissemination of the images has occurred, indicating in particular whether the affected person is a victim of gender-based violence, abuse, sexual assault, or harassment, or if they belong to any other particularly vulnerable group such as minors, persons with disabilities, or those with serious illness or at risk of social exclusion, as well as specifying the web address or addresses where the images have been published.

The Spanish Agency for Data Protection has a priority channel for reporting the unlawful dissemination of sensitive content, a system aimed at providing a rapid response in exceptionally delicate situations, such as those involving the dissemination of sexual or violent content.

**What if it involves images of sensitive content?**  
In exceptionally delicate situations, when the images include sexual content or depict acts of violence, putting the rights and freedoms of the affected individuals at high risk, especially victims of gender-based violence or minors, the channels offered by online service providers may not be sufficiently effective and quick to prevent the continued dissemination of the images.  
The objective of the priority channel is to address these situations by establishing a pathway in which the received claims will be analyzed as a priority, allowing the Agency, as an independent Authority, to adopt urgent measures if necessary to limit the continued processing of personal data.

**What decision can the Agency make?**  
After analyzing the submitted claim, the Agency will determine the possible urgent adoption of precautionary measures to prevent the continued processing of personal data. On the other hand, the Agency will assess whether it is appropriate to initiate a sanctioning procedure against those who have disseminated that material.  
When the request for the removal of the images has been unsuccessful, the affected individuals can file a complaint at the electronic headquarters of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection, attaching documentation proving that they have first requested the removal from the online service provider.
