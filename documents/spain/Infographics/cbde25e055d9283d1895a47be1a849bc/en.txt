Enjoy your vacation and protect your personal data  
The information you publish can compromise you. Always think about who can see what you share.  

If you publish information about other people, such as their photos, make sure it is not set to open to everyone and remember that you would like to be asked in advance.  
Avoid giving information about your location. This data can be used to know when you are not at home.  
Ticket codes and boarding passes contain personal and travel data. Do not share photos of these documents.
