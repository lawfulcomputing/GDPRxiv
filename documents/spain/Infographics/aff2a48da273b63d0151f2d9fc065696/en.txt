DECALOGUE OF PRINCIPLES
1. 
The protection system for minors against 
inappropriate content must ensure that 
identification, tracking, or locating minors 
through the Internet is not possible.
2. 
Age verification must be aimed at allowing 
those of the appropriate age to prove their 
status as “authorized persons to access,” and 
not allow the accreditation of the status of 
“minor.”
3. 
Accreditation for access to inappropriate 
content must be anonymous for Internet service 
providers and third entities.
4. 
The obligation to prove the status of 
“authorized person to access” will be limited 
only to inappropriate content. 
5. 
Age verification must be carried out 
accurately and the age categorized as 
“authorized person to access.”
6. 
The system must ensure that individuals 
cannot be profiled based on their browsing.
7. 
The system must ensure that the activity of 
a person is not linked across different services.
8. 
The system must guarantee the exercise of 
parental authority by the parents. 
9. 
Every protection system for minors against 
inappropriate content must guarantee the 
fundamental rights of all individuals in their 
access to the Internet. 
10. 
Every protection system for minors against 
inappropriate content must have a defined 
governance framework.
