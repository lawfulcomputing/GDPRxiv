YOUR DATA PROTECTION RIGHTS

Data protection regulations grant you a series of rights. To exercise them, you must contact the entity that is processing your data (“data controller”).

Right to Information  
The data controller must always identify themselves, inform you about the purpose of your data usage, and additionally tell you:  
• The reason why your data is necessary  
• How long they will retain it  
• How you can exercise your data protection rights  
• What the legal basis for the processing is  
• Whether they will disclose it to third parties or transfer it to other countries  
• If they will use it to create profiles, you have the right to object if automated decisions that affect you legally or similarly are made  

Right of Access  
Use this right to find out if an entity is processing your data. You can obtain information about:  
• The categories of data processed, its purpose, and to whom it is sent  
• Whether there are international transfers of your data  
• From whom it has been obtained and the retention periods  
• Whether profiles are created and automated decisions are made  
• The rights that assist you  

Right of Rectification  
This allows you to correct your data or complete it if it is inaccurate or incomplete.  

Right of Opposition  
You can object to an entity processing your data:  
• For personal reasons unless the data controller demonstrates a legitimate interest  
• When the processing is aimed at direct marketing  

Right of Deletion (“Right to be Forgotten”)  
You can request the deletion of your personal data when:  
• They are no longer necessary for the purposes for which they were collected  
• You withdraw the consent you provided, provided there is no other legitimate basis for the processing  
• Your data has been processed unlawfully  
• You have opposed the processing and the legitimate interest does not prevail, or if the processing is aimed at direct marketing  
• They must be deleted to comply with a legal obligation  
• They were obtained while you were a minor in relation to information society services  

Right to Restriction of Processing  
This allows you to request the suspension of the processing of your data when:  
• You contest its accuracy, during the period in which it is verified  
• You oppose the processing, while it is verified whether the legitimate interest of the data controller prevails  
• The processing is unlawful, but you oppose its deletion and instead request that it be limited  
• When you need them for the formulation, exercise, or defense of claims  

Right to Data Portability  
When the processing is based on your consent or the execution of a contract, and is carried out by automated means, you can receive your data in a format that allows you to transmit it to another data controller.
