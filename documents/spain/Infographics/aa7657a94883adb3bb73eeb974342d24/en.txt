Advice for Parents, Teachers, and Educational Institutions  

Parents and teachers must be especially careful  
with the publication of photographs, videos, or audio of  
minors before publishing this content.  
Educational institutions and teachers must pay attention to the new technologies they use in the development of  
their educational tasks and in their organization.  
Some apps and cloud services may not provide the necessary security or may process minors' data to create learning profiles, preferences, or behavior.  
Teachers should only use applications that offer sufficient security and are supervised by the institution and inform parents or guardians about it. In case of doubt, they can consult the data protection officer of the institution.  
Parents and teachers should preferably communicate through the platforms and means provided by the educational institution.  
When institutions intend to use students' personal data for purposes other than educational functions, such as using images for advertising or promotion on social media, they must inform parents or guardians, or the students themselves if they are over 14 years old, and request their consent.  
Family members who take images at an event of the educational institution (parties, sports events, etc.) may only use them in their personal and domestic sphere, and they should not be shared or published openly on social media.  
The education and awareness of younger children for safe use of the Internet and its services and applications is everyone's responsibility. Therefore, it is important that we set a good example for them.
