**More information:**  
**How do screens affect health?**  
The misuse of the digital world affects physical, mental, sexual, and social health.  
In childhood and adolescence, it also affects neurodevelopment and learning.  

**Sleep**  
Greater risk of sleeping fewer hours  
and having less restorative sleep.  

**Visual Fatigue**  
with onset or worsening of myopia  
and presence of red eye, itching, and tearing.  

**Acceptance of Risky Behaviors**  
due to access to age-inappropriate information:  
risky sexual behaviors,  
sexual dysfunction.  

**Impulsivity**  
with decreased ability to wait.  

**Anxiety**  

**Obesity**  
due to higher caloric intake  
and decreased physical activity.  

**Pain**  
in the back and joints due to poor  
postures when using devices.  

**Alteration**  
in neurodevelopment and learning.  

**Social Isolation**  
in-person and high social comparison.  

**Decrease**  
in attention  
and behavioral alterations due to feeling  
the need to be connected and having  
difficulties accepting adults' limits.
