Facilita-Emprende is 
a tool from 
the AEPD designed to 
address compliance 
with the obligations of 
data protection regulations.
Facilita-Emprende is aimed at startups and 
entrepreneurs whose data processing, due to 
the use of new technologies and the innovative 
nature of the products and services they 
develop, may present additional complexity 
when interpreting risks.
The AEPD has developed Facilita-Emprende to 
assist in complying with the obligations of the 
GDPR for those small businesses for which, 
due to the characteristics of their data processing, 
Facilita-RGPD is not applicable.
Facilita-Emprende can be accessed through 
the AEPD website:
https://www.aepd.es/es/guias-y-herramientas/
herramientas/facilita-emprende
It is recommended that Facilita-Emprende be used by 
newly established companies with innovative 
business models that rely on any of the following 
technologies: collaborative platforms, marketplaces 
or e-commerce, development of SaaS solutions, 
games, web or mobile applications, or massive data 
analysis.
Facilita-Emprende offers, through a 
more detailed description of the data processing, 
a series of additional recommendations 
tailored to these specific compliance needs.
