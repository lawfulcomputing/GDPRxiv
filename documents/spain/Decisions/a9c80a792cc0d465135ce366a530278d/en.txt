BACKGROUND
FIRST: A.A.A. (hereinafter, the claimant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for the Protection of Data on February 13, 2023. The complaint is directed against VODAFONE ESPAÑA, S.A.U. with NIF A80907397 (hereinafter, the respondent, Vodafone, or Lowi). The reasons on which the complaint is based are as follows:

The Constitutional Court has repeatedly declared that the principles of criminal law, among which is the principle of culpability, are applicable, with certain nuances, to administrative sanctioning law, as both are manifestations of the punitive order of the State (STC 18/1987, 150/1991), and that objective or non-fault liability cannot exist in the administrative sanctioning sphere, whereby the possibility of imposing sanctions for mere results, without establishing a minimum level of culpability even in terms of mere negligence, is excluded (SSTC 76/1990 and 164/2005).  
The principle of culpability, guaranteed by Article 25 of the Constitution, limits the exercise of the State's "ius puniendi" and requires, as stated by the Constitutional Court in ruling 129/2003, of June 20, that the imposition of sanctions be based on the requirement of the subjective element of fault, to guarantee the principle of responsibility and the right to a sanctioning procedure with all guarantees (STS of March 1, 2012, Rec 1298/2009).  
Certainly, the principle of culpability, provided for in Article 130.1 of Law 30/1992, of November 26, on the Legal Regime of Public Administrations and the Common Administrative Procedure, states that only those responsible for acts constituting administrative violations can be sanctioned, even in terms of simple non-compliance. This "simple non-compliance" cannot therefore be understood as the admission of objective liability in administrative sanctioning law, as the majority jurisprudence of our Supreme Court (starting from its rulings of January 24 and 25 and May 9, 1983) and the doctrine of the Constitutional Court (after its STC 76/1990) emphasize that the principle of culpability, even without explicit recognition in the Constitution, is inferred from the principles of legality and prohibition of excess (Article 25.1 CE), or from the inherent requirements of a Rule of Law, whereby the existence of intent or fault is required (in this sense STS of January 21, 2011, Rec 598/2008).

It is appropriate to grade the sanction to be imposed on the respondent and set it at the amount of €200,000 for the alleged infringement of Article 6.1) classified in Article 83.5.a) of the aforementioned GDPR.  
Therefore, in accordance with the applicable legislation and having assessed the criteria for grading the sanctions whose existence has been established, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:  
FIRST: TO IMPOSE on VODAFONE ESPAÑA, S.A.U. with NIF A80907397, for an infringement of Article 6.1 of the GDPR, classified in Article 83.5 of the GDPR, a fine in the amount of €200,000 (two hundred thousand euros).
