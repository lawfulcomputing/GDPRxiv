From the actions carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

FACTS  
FIRST: On April 13, 2022, a complaint was filed with registration number REGAGE22e00013123651 with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against SERVIGUIT SERVICIOS 1970, S.L. with NIF B01740661 (hereinafter, the complained party). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The complainant states that they received two charges in their bank account from the company Telefónica, listed under the name of the company SERVIGUIT SERVICIOS 1970. They indicate that they have verified that the owner of the aforementioned company is a person from whom they purchased a property in September 2021. They attach to their complaint bank receipts and the deed of sale of the mentioned apartment.  
Along with the notification, the following documentation is provided:  
- Three bank receipts from TELEFÓNICA DE ESPAÑA, S.A.U. in the name of the complainant domiciled at CAIXABANK.  
- Deed of sale of the previously mentioned property.  

SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the aforementioned complaint was forwarded to the complained party for analysis and to inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in the data protection regulations.  
This act is recorded as notified in both cases, but no response has been received.  

THIRD: On July 13, 2022, in accordance with Article 65 of the LOPDGDD, the complaint filed was admitted for processing.  

FOURTH: The General Sub-Directorate of Data Inspection proceeded to carry out preliminary investigative actions to clarify the facts in question, by virtue of the functions assigned to control authorities in Article 57.1 and the powers granted in Article 58.1 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and in accordance with the provisions of Title VII, Chapter I, Section two, of the LOPDGDD, being aware of the following points:

II  
Principles Relating to Processing  
Letter a) of Article 5.1 of the GDPR advocates:  
"1. Personal data shall be:  
a) processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently in relation to the data subject (‘lawfulness, fairness, and transparency’);"  

III  
Lawfulness of Processing  
Article 6.1 of the GDPR establishes the following:  
"The processing shall be lawful only if at least one of the following applies:  
a) the data subject has given consent to the processing of his or her personal data for one or more specific purposes;  
b) the processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract;  
c) the processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject;  
d) the processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of another natural person;  
e) the processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller;  
f) the processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party, except where such interests are overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject which require protection of personal data, in particular when the data subject is a child.  
The provision in letter f) of the first paragraph shall not apply to processing carried out by public authorities in the performance of their tasks."  

IV  
Conclusion  
The complainant claims to have received two charges in their bank account from the company TELEFÓNICA, listed under the name of the company SERVIGUT SERVICIOS 1970. They indicate that they have verified that the owner of the aforementioned company is a person to whom they sold a property in September 2021. They attach bank receipts and the deed of sale of the mentioned apartment to their complaint.  
Upon reviewing the mentioned receipts, it is confirmed that the complainant received the payments in their bank account. An indication that the direct debit may have been made by the complainant is the prior celebration between both parties of a legal transaction related to the sale of a property. However, this contract does not, by itself, prove that the party being complained about was the one who subsequently set up the direct debit for the receipts.  
During the preliminary investigative actions, information regarding this matter has been repeatedly requested from the telecommunications operator TELEFÓNICA DE ESPAÑA S.A., which has not provided all the required information regarding whether the party being complained about was the one who at that time would have provided the complainant's account number. Without prejudice to the responsibility that may correspond to this company, this has resulted in a lack of information about who and through what means the direct debit was made. Additionally, TELEFÓNICA DE ESPAÑA informs that the complainant had been a previous customer.  
For its part, the party being complained about also repeatedly denies having made the direct debit. This, combined with the lack of sufficient information regarding the conditions under which the direct debit would have occurred, raises reasonable doubt about the commission of an infringement by the party being complained about.  
Therefore, based on the indications in the previous paragraphs, no evidence has been found that proves the existence of an infringement within the jurisdiction of the Spanish Agency for the Protection of Data.  
Thus, by virtue of the principle of presumption of innocence, as it has not been possible to attribute the commission of the infringement to the party being complained about, in accordance with the aforementioned, the Director of the Spanish Agency for the Protection of Data, IT IS AGREED:  
FIRST: TO ARCHIVE the present proceedings.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to SERVIGUIT SERVICIOS 1970, S.L. and to the complainant.  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 50 of the LOPDGDD, this Resolution will be made public once it has been notified to the interested parties.
