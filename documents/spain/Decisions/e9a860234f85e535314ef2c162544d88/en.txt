RESOLUTION OF FILE OF ACTIONS  
From the actions carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

FACTS  
FIRST: On March 31, 2022, a complaint was filed with registration number REGAGE22e00010663358 with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against XFERA MÓVILES, S.A. with NIF A82528548 (hereinafter, XFERA). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The complainant submits a complaint for receiving a commercial call on their mobile line ***TELEFONO.1 on March 31, 2022, from the line ***TELEFONO.2, despite being registered on the Robinson List since July 16, 2018.  
Along with the notification, the following is provided:  
- Proof of registration on the Robinson List.  
- Telephone bill and mobile phone contract.  
- Screenshot of incoming calls from March 31, 2022, showing the call from line ***TELEFONO.2 at 16:23 hours.  

SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the complaint was forwarded to XFERA for analysis and to inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in data protection regulations.  
On April 21, 2022, a response was received by this Agency indicating that they have verified in their system that the complainant's number has not been commercially impacted by any action authorized by XFERA and states that their collaborators must provide them with a monthly updated list of outgoing numbers they will use, with the use of uncommunicated numbers not being permitted, and they have verified that the calling line is not associated with any of their collaborators.  
Additionally, they conducted an internal investigation and found that the complainant's number had been used by two distributors in a face-to-face modality, but the number used does not correspond with the calling line ***TELEFONO.2. Although the number does not correspond with that presented in this complaint, they have been penalized by terminating the contractual relationship with them.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 114.1.b) of Law 11/2022, of June 28, General Telecommunications Law (hereinafter LGTel) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative proceedings.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, as long as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
Finally, the Fourth Additional Provision "Procedure in relation to the competencies attributed to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection by other laws" establishes that: "The provisions in Title VIII and its development regulations shall apply to the procedures that the Spanish Agency for Data Protection must process in exercising the competencies attributed to it by other laws."  

II  
Right to the protection of personal data and privacy in relation to unsolicited communications, traffic and location data, and subscriber directories  
Article 48 of Law 9/2014, of May 9, General Telecommunications Law (in force at the time the infringement was committed), under the heading "Right to the protection of personal data and privacy in relation to unsolicited communications, traffic and location data, and subscriber directories," provides in its subsection 1.b) the following:  
"1. Regarding the protection of personal data and privacy in relation to unsolicited communications, end users of electronic communications services shall have the following rights:  
(...)  
b) To oppose receiving unwanted calls for commercial communication purposes made through systems other than those established in the previous letter and to be informed of this right."

III  
Conclusion  
Through the actions carried out by this Agency, evidence has been obtained of the phone call made from ***TELEFONO.2 to the claimant's number. It has been verified that the ownership of this line corresponds to an individual with a prepaid contract, so the operator of this line lacks data related to the contract, invoices, copies of identification documents of residence or bank account.  
The responding party acknowledges that the number of the claimant ***TELEFONO.1 has been used by two of its distributors in a face-to-face modality, but the number used does not correspond to the number ***TELEFONO.2 reported by the claimant in this file. It has been verified that the responding party penalized the two distributors who used the claimant's data, terminating the contractual relationship with them on April 27, 2022.  
Therefore, based on the information provided in the previous paragraphs, it has not been possible to identify the responsible party for the commercial call since the phone listed as the caller belongs to a prepaid mobile line and no information is available about it.  
Thus, in accordance with the statement made by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection,  
IT IS AGREED:  
FIRST: TO PROCEED TO FILE the present actions.
