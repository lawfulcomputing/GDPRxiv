RESOLUTION OF SANCTIONING PROCEDURE  
From the procedure instructed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

BACKGROUND  
On November 7, 2024, a proposal for resolution was made, proposing the following:  
FIRST: The DEPARTMENT OF UNIVERSAL HEALTH AND PUBLIC HEALTH OF THE VALENCIAN GOVERNMENT (hereinafter, the complainant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection on May 16, 2023. The complaint is directed against MARINA SALUD, S.A. with NIF A97563563 (hereinafter, the defendant). The reasons on which the complaint is based are as follows:  
The complainant is the contracting authority within the framework of a contract related to the provision of healthcare services in the Health Department of Denia (in force since February 1, 2009). It states that the defendant (concessionaire entity), as the data processor, carries out processing of specially protected personal data under the responsibility of the Department.  
It then presents a series of facts:  
“- During an inspection carried out on January 19, 2023, the complainant highlighted the use of specific software. In this context, the defendant was required to present the license/support and/or service provision contracts signed with the companies owning the healthcare information systems used in the Denia Hospital, and they refused to present them.  
- On January 31, 2023, the defendant was notified of the intention not to extend the contract and its consequent termination on January 31, 2024, as well as the rules for the recovery of direct management of the public service.  
- On January 27, 2023, instructions were issued for the processing, access, and use of data. In the aforementioned instructions, it is indicated that the concessionaire entity will not resort to another processor without the authorization of the Department.”  
In short, the complainant, suspecting that the current data processor's application is being dismantled, informs the AEPD that the defendant may be violating data protection regulations regarding the obligations of the data processor, putting at risk, in their opinion, the return of the data and the recovery of the service, just a few months before the contract's expiration, as well as the security and fundamental rights of the individuals to whom the data refers. No copy of the contract of assignment between the Department and the concessionaire entity is provided.

Considering the above, it is necessary to refer to the applicable framework regarding the relationship between our client and the Conselleria at the time the contracts were signed, as well as the regime applicable to them. Regarding the relationship that linked our client with the Conselleria, in its capacity as the data controller of the data processed by MARINA SALUD, it should be noted that, at least until the adoption of the Instructions, it was governed by the Agreement on the processing of personal data in compliance with Article 12 of the LOPD, in which, as indicated, the Conselleria (as the data controller) granted our client (as the data processor) a general power of attorney for the subcontracting of those processing activities that were necessary for the proper management of the services subject to the concession.  
As indicated, the Agreement expressly provided that the Conselleria authorized and, consequently, empowered Marina Salud "to subcontract, on behalf of and for the account of the controller, the processing of data necessary for the provision of the services subject to the concession."  
2.  
The Conselleria was aware at the time of the approval of the Instructions of the subprocessors contracted by MARINA SALUD, also stating their suitability for the purposes provided for in the RGPD and the LOPDGDD. The consequence of all this is that what is indicated in the Initiation Agreement is not aligned with reality, given that:  
• The Conselleria, as the data controller, was aware at the time of filing its complaint of the subprocessors contracted by our client and the scope of the services provided by them.  
• The Conselleria considered the aforementioned data processors to be suitable (i) requiring our client not to replace them with others without its authorization; and (ii) directly contracting them as data processors once the concession was reversed.  
3.  
The fact that our client did not provide the Conselleria with the contracts entered into with the subprocessors does not constitute a breach of personal data protection regulations.

II  
Preliminary Issues  
In the present case, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 4.1 and 4.2 of the GDPR, it is noted that personal data processing is carried out in which the reported party is the data controller, which it performs in the exercise of its competencies and attributions.  
The figure of the "data controller" is defined in Article 4.7 of the GDPR as "the natural or legal person, public authority, service, or other body that, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing; if the law of the Union or of the Member States determines the purposes and means of the processing, the data controller or the specific criteria for its appointment may be established by the law of the Union or of the Member States."  
For the provision of the services contemplated in the concession contract, referred to the provision of healthcare services in the Department of Health of Denia, it is required that the reported party carry out personal data processing in which it acts as the data processor. Among these processes are the collection, adaptation or modification, registration, extraction, enabling access, comparison or interconnection, consultation, structuring, use, conservation, communication by transmission, and destruction of personal data, which include categories of special data, health data (diagnoses, treatments, vaccines, diagnostic tests), data related to sexual life, or genetic data.  
Article 4.8 of the GDPR understands "data processor" as "the natural or legal person, public authority, service, or other body that processes personal data on behalf of the data controller."  
The reported party acts as the data processor, as inferred from the Resolution of the Conseller of Health dated 07/02/2005, in which the reported party was awarded the public service management contract by concession (***REFERENCE.1), the object of which was the provision of comprehensive healthcare in Area 12, Department of Health of Denia.
