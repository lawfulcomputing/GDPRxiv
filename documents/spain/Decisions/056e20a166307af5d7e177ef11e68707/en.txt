RESOLUTION OF RECOURSE FOR RECONSIDERATION  
Examined the recourse for reconsideration filed by ALL IN DIGITAL MARKETING, S.L. (hereinafter, the appellant) against the resolution issued by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection dated September 10, 2024, and based on the following  

FACTS  
FIRST: On September 10, 2024, a resolution was issued by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection in file EXP202309925, by virtue of which a fine of THREE THOUSAND EUROS (€3,000) was imposed on ALL IN DIGITAL MARKETING, S.L. for the commission of an infringement of Article 21 of the LSSI, classified under Article 38.4.d) of the LSSI.  
This resolution, which was notified to the appellant on October 3, 2024, was issued following the processing of the corresponding sanctioning procedure, in accordance with the provisions of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD), and subsidiarily under Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), regarding the processing of sanctioning procedures.  

SECOND: As proven facts of the aforementioned sanctioning procedure, PS/00407/2023, the following was recorded:  
“FIRST: As acknowledged by both the claimant and the defendant, the claimant A.A.A. registered on the website allzone.es on 01/02/2023, to place his order reference ***REFERENCIA.1, making an online purchase of a laptop on 22/02/2023, through the defendant's website, where he accepted all the general purchase conditions and privacy policy.  
SECOND: As acknowledged by both the claimant and the defendant, the claimant first requested to unsubscribe from the newsletter and associated offers on 20/03/2023. However, on 23/03/2023, he received another electronic communication regarding participation in a raffle sent by ALL IN ZONE.  
THIRD: As acknowledged by both the claimant and the defendant, on the same day 23/03/2023, the claimant again requested to unsubscribe by clicking the “cancel subscription” option that appeared in the offer email received on that day 23/03/23, as a free option made available to the customer to cancel his subscription to the newsletter and associated offers whenever he wishes.  
FOURTH: The claimant provided the following emails, the sending of which is acknowledged by the defendant, all of which were sent after the request to unsubscribe from the newsletter and offers made on March 23, 2023: March 23, March 28, April 13, April 17, April 20, April 25, May 2, May 11, May 16, 2023, as well as on June 8, 15, 16, and 22, 2023. All of them were sent from the address allzoners@allzone.es directed to the email address ***EMAIL.1 belonging to the claimant, inviting him to participate in various product or service raffles, containing at the end of each email a link that would allow, in principle, to cancel the subscription to these emails.”  

THIRD: The appellant submitted on October 24, 2024, to this Spanish Agency for Data Protection, a recourse for reconsideration, which contains an analysis of the evidence provided in the sanctioning procedure divided into 8 sections, almost identical to that made in the written allegations to the Proposal for Resolution and the previous written submission made on 24-1-24 during the evidentiary phase, the only difference with the last written allegations to the Proposal for Resolution of 15-5-24 being the precisions or clarifications made with an asterisk in sections 5 and 7 of their recourse document, and the submission of a new document (Document 6), along with the previously submitted documents (Documents 1 to 5).  
Specifically, along with the recourse, the following documents are submitted:  
- Report on the purchase procedure carried out by the claimant, detailing the steps to follow to acquire the product DOC1.  
- Privacy policy, DOC 2.  
- Screenshot of customer registration on the website, DOC 3.

- Screenshot of the customer area where, according to the appellant: it can specifically be seen the place from which the customer can activate and deactivate their consent to receive or not receive commercial communications from the claimed website. DOC 6.

III  
Request for suspension of execution  
On the other hand, the request in the appeal for reconsideration submitted makes a request for the suspension of the execution of the contested resolution, stating the following:  
“Based on this, I REQUEST that, accepting our allegations, you restore the resolution being contested, granting the appeal, issuing another that nullifies the imposed sanction, and in any case SUSPEND THE EXECUTION OF THE SANCTION UNTIL ALL ADMINISTRATIVE OR CONTENTIOUS-ADMINISTRATIVE AVENUES HAVE BEEN EXHAUSTED.”  
The appealing party is requesting that the execution be suspended until all administrative or contentious-administrative avenues have been exhausted, but the circumstances required by law to agree to this suspension do not exist for the following reasons:  
a) Regarding the suspension until exhausting the administrative avenue.  
It is not appropriate to agree to the precautionary suspension of the contested resolution in the administrative avenue since sanctioning resolutions are not executable until the administrative avenue is exhausted.  
Thus, although it is true that the general rule in administrative law is that according to Article 117.1 of the LPACAP: “The filing of any appeal, except in cases where a provision establishes otherwise, shall not suspend the execution of the contested act,” this immediate executability does not apply when the resolutions are sanctioning, regarding which Article

90.3º of the LPACAP on “Specialties of the resolution in sanctioning procedures” establishes the following:  
“3. The resolution that concludes the procedure shall be executive when no ordinary appeal can be filed against it in the administrative pathway, and it may include the necessary precautionary measures to ensure its effectiveness as long as it is not executive, which may consist of maintaining the provisional measures that may have been adopted.

b) Regarding the precautionary suspension until exhausting the contentious-administrative pathway.  
A different issue is whether the precautionary suspension of the firm resolution in the administrative pathway can be agreed upon, which would occur once the periods indicated have elapsed from the notification of the resolution of this appeal, in which case the provisions of Article 90.3.a) of the LPACAP apply, which states the following:  
This possibility therefore requires that the appealing party expressly communicate their intention to file a contentious-administrative appeal - which has not been done in the appeal document - and, in turn, that they prove that they have filed it within the two-month period, in the terms set forth in the dispositive part of the contested Resolution:

In the present case, the appeal for reconsideration submitted does not provide proof of having filed a contentious-administrative appeal. Therefore, it is not appropriate to agree to the precautionary suspension of the firm resolution in the administrative pathway at this time, without prejudice to agreeing to it later when the filing of the aforementioned contentious-administrative appeal is proven, in accordance with what is stated in the dispositive part of this resolution.
