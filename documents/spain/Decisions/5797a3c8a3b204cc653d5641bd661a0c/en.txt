FACTS  
FIRST: On May 15, 2023, a complaint was filed with entry registration number ***REFERENCE.1 before the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against  
AYUNTAMIENTO DE NOÁIN (VALLE DE ELORZ)/NOAIN (ELORTZIBAR) with NIF P3108700J (hereinafter, AYUNTAMIENTO DE NOÁIN). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The complainant states that on 29/05/23, at 20:30, they suffered an attempted home invasion, which resulted in a pursuit of the criminals, the recording of some videos, and their submission to the Municipal Police of the complained Ayuntamiento via the official phone number ***PHONE.1, through WhatsApp. The following day, the complainant notices that such videos, shared only with the Municipal Police, are circulating without their consent on social media (Facebook, Instagram) and groups of the instant messaging application WhatsApp. The images identify the complainant and their spouse, the entrance to their home, and also the alleged criminals, indicating the personal consequences that this disclosure is having for them.  
Furthermore, they mention that on the same day they filed a complaint with the Civil Guard regarding these events. They do not provide documentation evidencing this claim.  
They allege that on 02/05/23 the publication of the videos extended to media outlets, which is why they filed a complaint in the Aoiz Court and, on 03/05/23, a complaint with the Civil Guard against Diario de Noticias (EDITA ZEROA MULTIMEDIA S.A.- GRUPO NOTICIAS). They do not provide documentation evidencing these claims.  
Along with the complaint, the following documentation is submitted:  
─ Copy of the videos mentioned in the complaint. In one of the videos, taken by the exterior security camera of the residence, only the individuals who allegedly intend to break into the home appear. The other video is recorded from a personal camera of the complainant and includes, in addition to the suspects and the vehicle used to leave the scene, images of two other individuals (presumably the complainant and their spouse).  
─ Screenshots from various social media and instant messaging applications where both the video captured by the external security camera of the building and the video recorded by the complainant appear, along with comments related to these, without the publication dates being determinable.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
In accordance with the provisions of Articles 49 and 50 of LO 7/2021 and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of LOPDGDD, the Presidency of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative actions.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of LOPDGDD states: “The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures.”  
Finally, Article 52 “Applicable regime to procedures processed before data protection authorities” of LO 7/2021 establishes that:  
“1. In the event that the interested parties consider that the processing of personal data has infringed the provisions of this Organic Law or that their request to exercise the rights recognized in Articles 21, 22, and 23 has not been addressed, they shall have the right to file a complaint with the data protection authority.  
2. Such complaints shall be processed by the competent data protection authority in accordance with the procedure established in Title VIII of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, and, where applicable, by the legislation of the Autonomous Communities that is applicable. The general rules on administrative procedures and the legal regime of the public sector shall have a subsidiary character.”  
II  
Article 37 of LO 7/2021, under the heading “Security of processing,” establishes that:  
“1. The data controller and the processor, taking into account the state of the technology and the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of the processing, as well as the levels of risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, shall apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure an adequate level of security, especially regarding the processing of the categories of personal data referred to in Article 13. In particular, they shall apply to the processing of personal data the measures included in the National Security Scheme.  
2. Regarding automated processing, the data controller or processor, following a risk assessment, shall implement control measures for the following purposes:  
a) In access control to equipment, deny access to unauthorized persons to the equipment used for processing.  
b) In control of data media, prevent them from being read, copied, modified, or deleted by unauthorized persons.  
c) In storage control, prevent unauthorized personal data from being introduced, or that such data can be inspected, modified, or deleted without authorization.  
d) In user control, prevent automated processing systems from being used by unauthorized persons through data transmission facilities.  
e) In access control to data, ensure that authorized persons to use an automated processing system can only access the personal data for which they have been authorized.  
f) In transmission control, ensure that it is possible to verify and establish to which bodies personal data have been transmitted or may be transmitted, or to whose disposal personal data may be placed through data communication equipment.  
g) In input control, ensure that it can be verified and confirmed, retrospectively, which personal data have been entered into automated processing systems, at what time, and who entered them.  
h) In transport control, prevent personal data from being read, copied, modified, or deleted without authorization during the transfer of personal data or during the transport of data media.  
i) In recovery control, ensure that installed systems can be restored in the event of interruption.  
j) In reliability and integrity control, ensure that the system functions do not present defects, that operational errors are indicated, and that the stored personal data are not degraded due to system malfunctions.”
