From the proceedings carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

FACTS  
FIRST: On May 22, 2023, a complaint was filed with entry registration number REGAGE(...) before the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against ASNEF-EQUIFAX, SERVICIOS DE INFORMACIÓN SOBRE SOLVENCIA Y CRÉDITO, S.L. with NIF B82064833 (hereinafter, ASNEF) and HOLALUZ-CLIDOM, S.A. with NIF A65445033 (hereinafter, HOLALUZ). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
During the period in which he was a client of HOLALUZ (from March 25, 2021, to April 23, 2022) and due to the consequence of invoices based on high estimated readings, he has submitted dozens of complaints and claims to their Customer Service demanding invoices based on actual readings.  
HOLALUZ demands payment of false invoices without any right. Initially, they demanded payment for invoices corresponding to periods after the cancellation date; after many complaints explaining that during that period he was no longer their client, he received the following message: "We inform you that your incident has been resolved! We have proceeded to correct your billing and issue your cancellation correctly as of 04/22/22, refunding everything that does not correspond to you. So don't worry about anything!".  
Now they are requiring him to pay an invoice from four months prior to the cancellation, after a year since he changed companies, while he continues to await responses to his claims regarding all the inflated invoices he has paid.  
Finally, HOLALUZ provided his personal data to third parties without his consent (I S G F INFORMES COMERCIALES SL and Grupo KRUK), and subsequently included his personal data in the ASNEF delinquent file for non-payment of false invoices.  
The complaining party wants HOLALUZ to refrain from continuing to demand payment and to proceed to delete his data from the ASNEF delinquent file.  
Along with the complaint, the following is submitted:  
- Copy of various emails exchanged between the complaining party and the complained party (between November 2021 and June 2022) regarding various complaints made by the complaining party about the billing carried out by the complained party.  
- Copy of the email dated 12/21/2022 received by the complaining party from HOLALUZ in which it is indicated that "your incident has been resolved! We have proceeded to correct your billing and issue your cancellation correctly as of 04/22/22, refunding everything that does not correspond to you."  
- Communication from ASNEF dated 04/25/2023 informing the complaining party that on 04/24/2023 the complained party requested the registration of their data in the ASNEF file.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
In accordance with the functions conferred to each supervisory authority by Article 57.1 a), f) and h) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Presidency of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative actions.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued in its development and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."

Legality of processing

The first paragraph of Article 6 of the GDPR establishes the following:  
"1. Processing shall be lawful only if at least one of the following conditions is met:  
a) the data subject has given consent to the processing of their personal data for one or more specific purposes;  
b) processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract;  
c) processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject;  
d) processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of another natural person;  
e) processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller;  
f) processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party, except where such interests are overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject which require protection of personal data, in particular when the data subject is a child.  
The provision in point f) of the first paragraph shall not apply to processing carried out by public authorities in the performance of their tasks."  
In the present case, the processing of personal data of the claimant carried out by HOLALUZ falls under the provision of Article 6.1.b) of the GDPR, as the issuance of the unpaid invoice for December 2022 by the claimant corresponds to the period from January 15, 2022, to April 22, 2022, upon detecting incorrect billing and proceeding to correct it in a single invoice.  
Data processing in credit information systems that comply with the provisions of Article 20 of the LOPDGDD can be legitimized based on the provision set out in Article 6.1.f) of the GDPR. By means of a certification dated 12/04/2023, issued by LLEIDA NETWORKS SERVEIS TELEMÁTICS S.A., it is recorded that HOLALUZ informed the claimant of the possibility of their inclusion in the ASNEF delinquent register. This possibility is included in the contract signed between the claimant and HOLALUZ, in which Clause 11.4 of the General Conditions states as a reason for registration in the solvency and credit files the non-payment of invoices by the customer:  
"Holaluz will have 15 business days from the signing of this Contract to verify the solvency of the Customer by consulting the solvency and credit files deemed appropriate and under strict compliance with applicable regulations. The result of this consultation could, if applicable, condition the entry into force of the Contract. In case of non-payment, Holaluz may communicate the Customer's data to such files, always complying with the guarantees provided by current legislation."

Likewise, HOLALUZ, as previously stated, provided the corrective invoice for the month of December 2022 to justify the existence of the debt. On the other hand, the claiming party submitted, along with their claim, a copy of the communication that ASNEF made regarding their inclusion on 24/04/2023 in its file at the request of HOLALUZ, without any record of the claiming party having filed any complaint with ASNEF, having been established that they are no longer registered with ASNEF for any debt with HOLALUZ. In light of the above, it is not evident that either HOLALUZ or ASNEF have processed the claiming party's data without at least one of the conditions established in Article 6 of the GDPR being met.  
III  
Conclusion  
Therefore, based on the information provided in the previous paragraphs, no evidence has been found to substantiate the existence of an infringement within the jurisdiction of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection. All of this is without prejudice to any subsequent actions that this Agency may carry out, applying the investigative and corrective powers it holds. Thus, since responsibility for the processing has not been established as indicated, the Presidency of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection AGREES:  
FIRST: TO ARCHIVE the present proceedings.
