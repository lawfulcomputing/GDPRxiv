Causes that have motivated the incident that originated the complaint.  
The main cause of this incident is the access that healthcare personnel have to the Electronic Clinical History of the patient in public hospitals of the Valencian Community.  
This universal access to the Electronic Clinical History is granted to all healthcare professionals hired in the health system of the Valencian Community in the exercise of their functions, based on the criterion of prioritizing the general interest of preserving individual health, through the ubiquitous treatment of any clinical episode of a citizen of the Valencian Community in the healthcare centers managed by the Ministry of Universal Health and Public Health, and by any formally accredited professional.  
Measures adopted to prevent similar incidents  

The technical and organizational measures that guarantee security in access to the Electronic Clinical History of the Valencian Community, as well as the functional rules of the applications that manage said history, are defined by the Ministry of Universal Health and Public Health, in accordance with its assigned competencies.  
Access to the Electronic Clinical History is restricted to healthcare professionals based on their role in the system, through the application of technical and organizational measures in accordance with RD 3/2010 which regulates the National Security Scheme. Any other person outside the healthcare provision does not have access to the information systems that make up the Electronic Clinical History.  

The Health Department is particularly aware of its responsibility for the custody of its patients' data, and has been applying these measures for some time.
