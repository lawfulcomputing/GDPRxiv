RESOLUTION OF FILE OF PROCEEDINGS  
From the proceedings carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

FACTS  
FIRST: On March 8, 2019, a complaint was filed with entry registration number 012129/2019 before the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against FACUA - CONSUMER AND USER ASSOCIATION IN ACTION (hereinafter, the complained party), in which the complainant stated that the complained party registered individuals as members of their organization without their authorization, and that this had been done with the complainant and with Ms. A.A.A. Furthermore, it was indicated that on January 24, 2019, B.B.B. informed her via email that, taking advantage of the facility provided by FACUA, they had registered the complainant as a full member of FACUA on December 31, 2018, without her having authorized FACUA to do so. It was also claimed that the registration in the name of the complainant was communicated by the complained party to B.B.B. but not to her.  
The complainant continues her writing by stating that she proceeded to investigate whether this was a common practice of FACUA or if it had been an isolated error. To do this, she made calls to three different FACUA delegations (Almería, Jaén, and Cádiz). In these calls, she was informed about how to become a member, and when she asked about the procedure to register the family unit, they indicated that by simply providing the details of third parties via email, they would register them. They explicitly confirmed that the authorization of the third party was not necessary to register them in their files.  
Along with the complaint, the following documents were submitted:  
- Copy of the email chain between FACUA and B.B.B. regarding the registration as a full member of the complainant.  
- DVD of the recordings of the calls made by the complainant to three FACUA delegations (Almería, Jaén, and Cádiz) in which she was informed about how to register as a member of that organization and the procedure to register the "family" unit.  
- Copy of the email chain between FACUA and B.B.B. regarding the registration as a member of Ms. A.A.A.

I  
Competence  
In accordance with the functions conferred on each supervisory authority by Article 57.1 a), f), and h) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative actions.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states that: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection will be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, as long as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
Legality of Processing  
Article 6 of the GDPR, which regulates the legality of processing, establishes the following:  
"1. Processing shall be lawful only if at least one of the following conditions is met:  
a) the data subject has given consent to the processing of their personal data for one or more specific purposes;  
b) processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party or for the implementation at the request of the data subject of pre-contractual measures;  
c) processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject;  
d) processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of another natural person;  
e) processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller;  
f) processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party, except where such interests are overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject which require protection of personal data, in particular when the data subject is a child.  
The provisions of point f) of the first paragraph shall not apply to processing carried out by public authorities in the performance of their tasks.

Furthermore, Article 48.2 of the LOPDGDD establishes that, “In cases of absence, vacancy, or illness of the person holding the Presidency or when any of the grounds for abstention or recusal provided for in Article 23 of Law 40/2015, of October 1, on the Legal Regime of the Public Sector, apply to them, the exercise of the competencies related to the procedures regulated by Title VIII of this organic law shall be assumed by the person holding the management body that performs inspection functions. Therefore, considering that in the present case the reason for abstention provided for in Article 11.2.a) of Law 3/2015, of March 30, regulating the exercise of senior positions in the General State Administration, relating to personal interests, applies, the director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection has proceeded to abstain in the present procedure as of July 20, 2023.  
For the above reasons and in application of the provisions of Article 13 of Law 40/2015, of October 1, on the Legal Regime of the Public Sector (LRJSP) and following the provisions of Article 48.2 of the LOPDGDD, in this case, it will be the person holding the management body that performs inspection functions who will exercise the competencies related to the present procedure.  
IV  
According to the conclusions of the preliminary investigation report, there is evidence of a possible infringement of Article 6.1 of the GDPR as there is no indication that any of the circumstances set out in that article exist to consider that lawful processing has occurred.  
However, the statute of limitations for infringements is established in Articles 72, 73, and 74 of the LOPDGDD as 3, 2, or 1 year depending on whether they are classified, for the purposes of the statute of limitations, as very serious, serious, or minor, and this period has been exceeded since the date of the possible infringement, December 31, 2018, the date on which the complainant was registered as a member of FACUA.  
For informational purposes, it should be noted that the statute of limitations for infringements is not interrupted either by the performance of preliminary investigation actions or by the filing of a contentious-administrative appeal.
