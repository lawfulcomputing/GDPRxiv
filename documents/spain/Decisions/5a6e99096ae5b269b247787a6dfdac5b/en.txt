LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of 5/12, on Personal Data Protection and the guarantee of digital rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued in its development and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  

II  
Expiration is one of the modes of termination of administrative procedures that occurs due to the passage of time, with the aim of fulfilling the requirements derived from legal certainty. In accordance with this nature and with the general obligation of the Administration to issue an express resolution in all procedures, according to Article 21 of the LPACAP, “obligation to resolve,” certain deadlines are established, the passage of which, without an express resolution having been issued, determines the expiration of the procedure, with the wording:  
“1 The Administration is obliged to issue an express resolution and to notify it in all procedures, regardless of their form of initiation.  
In cases of prescription, waiver of the right, expiration of the procedure, or withdrawal of the application, as well as the subsequent disappearance of the object of the procedure, the resolution shall consist of the declaration of the circumstance that occurs in each case, indicating the facts produced and the applicable norms.

The obligation referred to in the first paragraph does not apply to cases of termination of the procedure by agreement or convention, as well as to procedures relating to the exercise of rights subject solely to the duty of responsible declaration or communication to the Administration.  
2. The maximum period within which the express resolution must be notified shall be that established by the regulatory norm of the corresponding procedure.  
This period may not exceed six months unless a law establishes a longer one or it is provided for in European Union law.”  
Article 64 of the LOPDGDD, second paragraph, final, on the form of initiation and duration of the procedure, in force at the date of signing the initiation agreement, prescribes:  
“The procedure shall have a maximum duration of nine months from the date of the initiation agreement or, where applicable, from the draft initiation agreement. Upon expiration of this period, it shall expire and, consequently, the proceedings shall be archived.”  
This circumstance is noted in the initiation agreement notified to the respondent.  
Considering that the request of the complainant for an extension of the deadline is not appropriate, as it occurs on the last day of the same, it is noted, however, that the initiation agreement was issued on 23/09/2022, and given that the last day available to the respondent to make allegations to the proposal was 23/06/2023, the date by which the resolution of the procedure should have been issued and notified, the maximum period provided for by the specific applicable norm has elapsed, therefore it is appropriate to declare such expiration.  

III  
Regarding the effects, Article 95 of the LPACAP states:  
“3. Expiration shall not by itself produce the prescription of actions by the individual or the Administration, but expired procedures shall not interrupt the prescription period.  
In cases where it is possible to initiate a new procedure due to the absence of prescription, the acts and procedures whose content would have remained the same had expiration not occurred may be incorporated into it. In any case, the new procedure must complete the procedures for allegations, proposal of evidence, and hearing of the interested party.”  
In this specific case, it was a few days after the registration period for the race closed, on 19/08/2021, when the respondent sent an email to the participants, indicating: “after analyzing the current situation arising from the COVID-19 pandemic, the organization has decided to request from the participants, as recommended by various public bodies, the following:  
-Certificate of complete vaccination or, failing that, a certificate of having the relevant dose due to having already contracted COVID-19.

- In the absence thereof, with a limit of 48 hours prior to the celebration of the event to be held, a negative PCR or antigen test result." It is added that the aforementioned documentation must be uploaded on the website gomeraparadise.com, responsibility of the respondent, where a "private participant area" is enabled, https://inscripciones.gomesporteventos.com/inscripcion/ix-gomera-paradise/ zona privada/logeo/, enter your registration ID (on the registration receipt and the email with which you registered). This option will be available from Monday, 23/08 until Sunday, 5/09/2021, for the vaccination certificate option. According to the respondent, they requested the data of the vaccination certificates, PCR tests, or antigen tests, due to the epidemiological situation arising from the COVID-19 pandemic in order to ensure the safety of the participants, considering it appropriate, given the situation. They state that "they obtained authorization from Health for the celebration of the event," without providing any document to support this claim. In the email dated 19/08/2021, it was indicated that "the organization has decided to request participants, as recommended by various public bodies, the following," referring to the vaccination certificate, proof of having previously contracted the disease, or a PCR or antigen test 48 hours before the event. Therefore, in accordance with the applicable legislation, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:
