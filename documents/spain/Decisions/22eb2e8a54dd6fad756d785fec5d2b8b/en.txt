In this sense, Recital 75 of the GDPR establishes that: “The risks to the rights and freedoms of individuals, of varying severity and probability, may arise from the processing of data that could cause physical, material, or immaterial harm, particularly in cases where the processing could lead to issues of discrimination, identity theft or fraud, financial losses, damage to reputation, loss of confidentiality of data subject to professional secrecy, unauthorized reversal of pseudonymization, or any other significant economic or social harm; in cases where data subjects are deprived of their rights and freedoms or prevented from exercising control over their personal data; …”

In the case examined, the present sanctioning procedure has its cause, as stated in the facts, from the violation of the regulations on personal data protection, both of the principle of confidentiality of the data and the technical and organizational measures implemented.  
In accordance with the evidence available, such conduct constitutes, on the part of the accused party, the infringement of the provisions of Articles 5.1.f) and 32.1 of the GDPR.
