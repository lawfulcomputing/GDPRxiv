SANCTIONING PROCEDURE RESOLUTION  
From the actions carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following,  

BACKGROUND  
FIRST: On 10/08/22, A.A.A. (hereinafter, the complainant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection.  
The complaint was directed against GRUPO MASSIMO DUTTI, S.A. with NIE: A78115201, responsible for the website ***URL.1 (hereinafter, the defendant), for the alleged violation of data protection regulations: Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council, of 27/04/16, regarding the Protection of Natural Persons in relation to the Processing of Personal Data and the Free Movement of such Data (GDPR), Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD), and Law 34/2002, of July 11, on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI).  
The facts, according to the statements of the complainant, are related to the use of cookies and the obtaining of user consent. During the visit that the complainant claims to have made to the website on 28/09/21, it presented a banner on the first layer of consent in which there was no possibility to "reject all cookies" that were not technical or necessary; moreover, the design of the links was misleading as the button leading to the "manage cookies" option in the control panel uses a "link" design—highlighted or underlined text—while the "Accept all cookies" button uses a typical "button" design—a square box with text. Additionally, the colors and contrast of the buttons are also misleading as different colors were used for the various options presented. The complaint also indicates that it is not as easy to withdraw consent as it is to give it, and that once consent is given by clicking on the "accept all cookies" or "accept some groups of cookies" option through the control panel, it is not possible to withdraw it if one wishes to do so later.  

SECOND: On 04/10/22, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection issued a decision of inadmissibility as, after analyzing the documents provided and the concurrent circumstances, the representation of the complainant was not considered sufficiently accredited.  

THIRD: On 01/11/22, the complainant submitted a written appeal for reconsideration, which was accepted by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection on 15/11/22, taking into account the provisions of Article 5.6 of the LPACAP and after analyzing the document provided by the complainant in which they grant their representation to the organization NOYB (European Center for Digital Rights).

SIXTH: On 22/03/23, the Spanish Data Protection Agency's Directorate initiated sanctioning proceedings against the entity being claimed, upon finding reasonable indications of a violation of the provisions established in Article 22.2 of the LSSI, due to the irregularities detected on its website regarding the "Cookie Policy," namely, for the lack of sufficient information in the first layer about the purposes of the installation of cookies, with an initial fine of 5,000 euros and for the impossibility of managing the cookies used once consent has been given, with an initial fine of 5,000 euros.

First.- Regarding the first of the alleged violations of Article 22.2 of the LSSI File No.: EXP202210347 (PS/00051/2023) Allegations to the Initiation Agreement attributed to my client "for the lack of sufficient information in the first layer about the purposes of the installation of cookies," it should be noted that in the Initiation Agreement this alleged non-compliance is described in detail as follows:

Second.- Regarding the second of the alleged violations of Article 22.2 of the LSSI attributed to my client “due to the impossibility of managing the cookies used once the initial consent has been given,” it should be noted that such a violation is categorically denied, as the user has always had and continues to have the permanent option to manage cookies after giving initial consent through a link to the “Cookie Settings” where they can reject cookies with a simple click.  
Specifically, once the user accepts cookies on the homepage of the website and selects the market (geographical) and catalog (women/men) they wish to access, they always have available in the horizontal menu located in the footer of the website the link to “Cookie Settings” (screenshot 1 below) with which they can access the console where they can reject cookies simply by clicking the “Reject all” button (screenshot 2 below) or re-accept them if they have previously rejected them (screenshot 3), through the corresponding buttons.

It should be emphasized that this multiplication, reiteration, or redundancy does not have any cause in avoiding or remedying a non-compliance. What is intended is the reinforcement of compliance, through a facilitation of access to the “Cookie Settings,” which is nothing more than a facilitation or improvement based on the means of compliance that already existed.  
Specifically, in addition to the link to “Cookie Settings,” which has always been in the horizontal menu of the footer of the website:

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I.-  
Competence:  

The Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure, in accordance with the provisions of Article 43.1, second paragraph, of Law 34/2002, of July 11, on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI).  

II.-  
On the cookie information banner present on the first layer (main page):  
In its guidelines on transparency, the GT29 recommends the use of layered privacy statements or notices, that is, containing information in layers, so that users can access those aspects of the statement or notice that are of greater interest to them, thus avoiding information fatigue, and without prejudice to the fact that all information is available in one place or in a complete document that can be easily accessed if the interested party wishes to consult it in its entirety.  
This system may consist of displaying essential information in a first layer when accessing the page or application, and completing it in a second layer through a page that offers more detailed and specific information about cookies.  
The cookie banner on the first layer must include a generic identification of the purposes of the cookies that will be used, without the need to identify them.

III-1  
On the withdrawal of consent for the use of cookies once granted.  
Users must be able to withdraw the consent previously granted at any time. To this end, the publisher must ensure that it provides information to users in its cookie policy on how they can withdraw consent and delete cookies.  
The user must be able to revoke consent easily. The system offered to withdraw consent must be as easy as that used when consent was granted. It will be considered that this ease exists, for example, when the user has easy and permanent access to the cookie management or configuration system.

III-2  
Classification and Qualification of the Administrative Infraction  
The deficiencies detected in the cookie policy, from the verification carried out on 19/04/23 to the last one on 06/06/23 on the website in question, regarding the impossibility of rejecting non-technical or non-essential third-party cookies once initial consent has been granted, constitutes a violation of the provisions established in Article 22.2 of the LSSI, as it states that:  
“Service providers may use storage and data retrieval devices on the terminal equipment of the recipients, provided that they have given their consent after being provided with clear and complete information about their use, in particular, regarding the purposes of data processing, in accordance with the provisions of Organic Law 15/1999, on the protection of personal data.  
When technically possible and effective, the recipient's consent to accept data processing may be provided through the appropriate settings of the browser or other applications.
