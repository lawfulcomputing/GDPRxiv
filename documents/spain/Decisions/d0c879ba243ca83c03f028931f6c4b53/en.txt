RESOLUTION OF SANCTIONING PROCEDURE  
From the procedure initiated by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

BACKGROUND  
FIRST: As a result of a complaint filed with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection, indications of a possible breach of the regulations within the scope of the competencies of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection were observed, leading to the initiation of actions under file number EXP202308425.  
SECOND: The General Subdirectorate for Data Inspection proceeded to carry out preliminary investigative actions to clarify the facts in question, by virtue of the investigative powers granted to control authorities under Article 58.1 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and in accordance with the provisions established in Title VII, Chapter I, Section Two, of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD hereinafter).  
In the framework of the investigative actions, two requests for information were sent to OVH HISPANO, S.L.U., with NIF B83834747, regarding the complaint indicated in the first section, requesting that, within ten working days, it submit to this Agency the information and documentation specified therein. These requests were recorded as sent on July 24, 2023, and August 29, 2023.  
THIRD: The requests for information were notified in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP) through electronic means. The first of them was received by OVH HISPANO, S.L.U. on July 25, 2023, while the second, a reiteration of the first, was received on August 29, 2023, as evidenced by the acknowledgments of receipt in the file.  
FOURTH: Regarding the requested information, OVH HISPANO, S.L.U. did not submit any response to this Spanish Agency for Data Protection within the deadlines granted for this purpose.  
FIFTH: According to the report obtained from the AXESOR tool, the entity OVH HISPANO, S.L.U. is an SME established in 2003 with a business volume of 30,077,990 euros in 2021.

In a subsidiary manner to the above, OVH HISPANO, S.L.U., in relation to articles 70 and 72.1 of the LOPDGDD and 58.1 of the GDPR, states that "it is understood that OVH cannot be considered as a passive subject as it does not fit into any of the figures subject to the sanctioning regime referred to in the framework of the actions taken by the General Sub-Directorate of Inspection initiated against an unidentified third party, who is actually the data controller in the context of file number EXP202308425.  
It is evident that, in this case, the duty of collaboration was not related to the processing of data for which my client is responsible or designated, but rather, with a generic duty of collaboration with public authorities that any other individual may have, by virtue of Article 18 of Law 39/2015, and which, as previously indicated, was fulfilled in good faith through the CNIL."  
For all the above reasons, OVH HISPANO, S.L.U. requests that "the FILE OF THE PROCEDURE be declared concerning OVH HISPANO, S.L.U. for having responded to the company's information requests in accordance with data protection regulations and, subsidiarily, for OVH not being a subject susceptible to being sanctioned in accordance with data protection regulations."

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
According to the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of the GDPR and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of the LOPDGDD, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states that: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued in its development and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
Filing  
Article 89 of the LPACAP provides as follows:  
"1. The instructing body shall resolve the termination of the procedure, with the filing of the actions, without the need for the formulation of the resolution proposal, when it is evident in the instruction of the procedure that any of the following circumstances exist:  
a) The non-existence of the facts that could constitute the infringement.

b) When the facts are not proven.

d) When there is no person or persons responsible identified, or when they appear to be exempt from liability.

2. The proposal for resolution must indicate the disclosure of the procedure and the deadline for submitting allegations and presenting the documents and information deemed pertinent.  

In the present case, in light of the facts presented, it is considered that OVH HISPANO, S.L.U., although out of time, has responded to the requests made by this Agency before the initiation agreement of the present sanctioning procedure was issued, which leads to the conclusion of the non-existence of the fact that constituted the infraction. Consequently, it is appropriate to agree to archive the procedure.  

Therefore, in accordance with the above, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:  
FIRST: TO ARCHIVE the sanctioning procedure of file number EXP202403439.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to OVH HISPANO, S.L.U., with NIF B83834747.  

In accordance with the provisions of Article 50 of the LOPDGDD, this Resolution will be made public once it has been notified to the interested parties.  

Against this resolution, which concludes the administrative route in accordance with Article 48.6 of the LOPDGDD, and in accordance with the provisions of Article 123 of the LPACAP, the interested parties may optionally file a motion for reconsideration before the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection within one month from the day following the notification of this resolution or directly file an administrative contentious appeal before the Contentious-Administrative Chamber of the National Court, in accordance with the provisions of Article 25 and in section 5 of the fourth additional provision of Law 29/1998, of July 13, regulating the Contentious-Administrative Jurisdiction, within two months from the day following the notification of this act, as provided in Article 46.1 of the aforementioned Law.
