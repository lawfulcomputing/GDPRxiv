BACKGROUND  
FIRST: Terms and scope of the complaint  
A.A.A. (hereinafter, the complainant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection on 21/11/2022. The complaint is directed against the BALEARIC YOUTH INSTITUTE (DEPARTMENT OF FAMILIES AND SOCIAL AFFAIRS) with NIF Q0700516H (hereinafter, the respondent). The reasons on which the complaint is based are as follows:  
The complainant states:  
- On 17/11/2022, they received a letter sent by the respondent demanding their participation in the “first regional survey on the youth of the Balearic Islands.” The questionnaire included questions regarding: sexual orientation, religious affiliation, political positioning of themselves and their parents, voting intention in the upcoming regional elections, sexually transmitted diseases contracted in recent months, whether they had been diagnosed with depression and if they had received treatment for it, to name a few examples.  
- They requested a copy of the survey from the respondent, which has not been provided.  
- There is no information regarding the purpose of the data and its processing, considering that they are of special protection.  
Along with the complaint, they provide:  
- A copy of the letter signed on 8/11/2022, which contains or indicates:  
  - Information about the survey and where collaboration is requested, listing their name and surname as the person who must respond, and if they do so online, a username and password are provided along with the address www.enquestajovesilles.com “put this link in the address bar, not in the search engine.”  
  - It is part of the “official statistics of the Government of the Balearic Islands” in collaboration with the Councils of the other islands.  
  - It indicates that responses can also be given by phone—“an interviewer will call you at your convenience”—for which a phone number is also provided to call or send a WhatsApp “to tell us when you prefer us to call you.” Additionally, another phone number is offered for any clarification or at the email address consulta@enquestajovesilles.com.  
  - “At the beginning of the survey, you will see that we ask for a contact phone number in case we need to clarify any issues.”  
  - “Remember that the information we will ask for is mandatory communication and is protected by statistical confidentiality, meaning it is strictly confidential and cannot be used for any purpose other than the preparation of the official statistics.”  
  - The survey aims to understand the situation, demands, and needs of young people aged 15 to 34 in the Balearic Islands, using the information to facilitate the formulation of youth policies.  
- A copy of screenshots of a WhatsApp conversation held with the respondent regarding the survey, in which the response indicates that “as you can read in the letter” “it is an official statistic and therefore mandatory by law” and an acknowledgment of receipt that certifies the completion of the survey.  
The complainant adds that there is no information indicating the purpose of the data and its processing, “considering that they are of special protection.”

In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of the GDPR, and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of the LOPDGDD, the Presidency of the Spanish Data Protection Agency is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.

“This Regulation applies to the total or partial automated processing of personal data, as well as to the non-automated processing of personal data contained or intended to be included in a filing system.”
