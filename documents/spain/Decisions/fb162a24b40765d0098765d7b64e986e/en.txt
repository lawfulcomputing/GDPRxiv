Among others, the obligation to "contribute the olive harvest production until the effective date of the resignation." The infringement of the principle of confidentiality requires a result (an unauthorized or unlawful processing), including access by third parties, which in this case would be the partners, disclosure or communication of data, as well as the loss, destruction, or accidental damage of personal data.  
The principle entails that the data controller cannot reveal or disclose its content, having the duty to safeguard it, and that the data cannot be known by any person or entity outside of it, as this is precisely what confidentiality consists of. Confidentiality aims to safeguard or protect individuals' right to maintain the privacy of their personal data and ultimately the power of control or disposition over their data. Therefore, the breach of the duty of confidentiality is classified as an infringement, and those obliged to uphold this duty may incur an infringement.
