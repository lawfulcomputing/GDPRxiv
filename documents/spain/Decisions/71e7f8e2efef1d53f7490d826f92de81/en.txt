As a result of the actions carried out, the following points have been noted:  
Due to the nature of the case, it was deemed necessary to conduct an on-site inspection to reliably ascertain the existence of recording cameras, capturing images of clients, and other related aspects. Additionally, to verify on-site the existing protocols and operations regarding this matter, in the environment where the alleged events occurred.  
Two on-site inspections were conducted, the first inspection took place on November 22, 2023, while the second was carried out on December 19, 2023. This was due to the fact that the first inspection requested authorization solely for the investigation actions at SCHOOL FITNESS HOLIDAY & FRANCHISING, S.L., and the second for the other investigated entity, HOLIDAY FIT TRES CANTOS S.L., which manages the physical gym establishment.  
Taking as a reference the action AT/03558/2023, in which the transfer of the complaint to SCHOOL FITNESS HOLIDAY & FRANCHISING, S.L. was ordered, it was this same entity that responded to the information request and did not indicate at any time any different organizational circumstances.  
However, during the course of the first inspection, based on the statements provided by the representatives of the entity and the review of the documentation submitted in the complaint, the business framework in which the investigated entities operate and the organization of the commercial group was clarified, as will be detailed later.  
The results obtained in both inspections were similar, with the main representative of the entities, in the capacity of attorney, being the same person in both cases.  
The inspection consisted of two parts, as reflected in the inspection report. In the first part, the inspection team of this Agency met with the representatives of the complained party, explained the reason for their visit, and detailed the alleged facts, while addressing the statements of the complained party. The second part consisted of the collection of evidence along with the performance of various tests on the information systems used by the gym.  
1. Information obtained.  
- Business organization  
The representatives of HOLIDAY FIT indicated that (i) HOLIDAY FIT TRES CANTOS S.L. is part of the group of companies operating under the commercial brand HOLIDAY GYM, with the parent company of the group being SCHOOL FITNESS HOLIDAY & FRANCHISING S.L., and that (ii) each company in the group has its own legal personality and management autonomy. However, the parent company acts as a coordinator and is responsible for common group management tasks such as brand promotion. In fact, it manages the various accounts that the group has on social media for these promotions and the publication of content. The gym located in Tres Cantos, therefore, is under the jurisdiction of HOLIDAY FIT TRES CANTOS S.L., with the staff working there being employees of this entity.  
The roles played by both entities are those of joint controllers of the processing, as will be discussed below.  
It should be noted that this model has been adapted over time, which is why some of the documentation provided referred to a direct relationship between (…) claimant and SCHOOL FITNESS HOLIDAY & FRANCHISING, S.L.

Processing of Personal Data  
Article 4.1) of the GDPR defines "personal data" as: “any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (‘the data subject’); an identifiable natural person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier, such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier, or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity of that natural person.”  
Article 4.2) of the GDPR defines "processing" as: “any operation or set of operations which is performed on personal data or on sets of personal data, whether or not by automated means, such as collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation or alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure or destruction.”  
In this case, the recording and publication of images and videos captured during classes held in gyms constitutes processing of personal data since the image is a personal data. According to the Guidelines 07/2020 of the EDPB, joint processing occurs when there are several participants in the processing who hold the status of data controllers and jointly determine the purposes and means of a processing operation. Just as with the concepts of "data controller" and "data processor," the "joint controller" is a functional concept, and it is necessary to establish it based on their specific activities in the analyzed case and not based on the formal designation that may appear in the agreement or contract: "52. The assessment of joint responsibility must be based on a factual analysis, and not on a formal analysis, of the actual influence over the purposes and means of the processing. All existing or planned agreements must be subject to verification in relation to the factual circumstances in which the relationship between the parties develops. For this, it is not enough to be guided by a mere formal criterion, at least for two reasons: in some cases, the formal appointment of a data controller may be lacking— for example, by virtue of law or contract—; in others, it may occur that the formal appointment does not reflect the reality of the agreements, as the function of data controller has been formally entrusted to an entity that, in practice, is not in a position to "determine" the purposes and means of the processing."  
In this case, this Agency considers that, although there is an agreement between HOLIDAY FIT TRES CANTOS and SCHOOL FITNESS by which they would act as joint controllers of the processing, in practice, it is SCHOOL FITNESS that determines the purposes and means of the processing consisting of the recording and publication of images and videos on the websites and social media of the group companies in order to promote the commercial brand. It is the one that decides when, how, and what commercial campaigns should be carried out; while HOLIDAY FIT TRES CANTOS is limited to taking images and videos of the activities or other matters that the marketing department of SCHOOL FITNESS indicates to it.  
Therefore, this Agency concludes that SCHOOL FITNESS carries out that processing operation in its capacity as data controller, as it is the one that determines the purposes and means of such activity, pursuant to Article 4.7) of the GDPR. For its part, HOLIDAY FIT TRES CANTOS is considered a data processor, acting on behalf of SCHOOL FITNESS in the recording of the images and videos used for promotional purposes.

The principle of storage limitation set forth in the aforementioned article subordinates the maintenance of personal data to the purpose for which it was collected, allowing for an extension of that period only when it concerns archiving in the public interest, scientific or historical research, or statistical purposes.

II  
Termination of the procedure  
Article 85 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), under the heading "Termination in sanctioning procedures" provides as follows:  
“1. Once a sanctioning procedure has been initiated, if the offender acknowledges their responsibility, the procedure may be resolved with the imposition of the appropriate sanction.  
2. When the sanction is solely of a pecuniary nature or when it is possible to impose a pecuniary sanction and another non-pecuniary sanction but the impropriety of the latter has been justified, voluntary payment by the presumed responsible party, at any time prior to the resolution, will imply the termination of the procedure, except with respect to the restoration of the altered situation or the determination of compensation for the damages caused by the commission of the infringement.  
3. In both cases, when the sanction is solely of a pecuniary nature, the competent authority to resolve the procedure shall apply reductions of at least 20% on the amount of the proposed sanction, which shall be cumulative. The aforementioned reductions must be specified in the notification of initiation of the procedure and their effectiveness will be conditioned on the withdrawal or renunciation of any action or appeal in the administrative route against the sanction. The percentage of reduction provided for in this section may be increased by regulation.”  
III  
Voluntary payment and acknowledgment of responsibility
