Procedure  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: “The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection will be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued in its development, and, as long as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures.”

III  
Preliminary Issues  
In this case, according to the provisions of Articles 4.1 and 4.2 of the GDPR, there is evidence of the processing of personal data, as CP B.B.B. carries out, among other processes, the use of the following personal data of individuals, such as: name and surname and address of the community members, identification number, and economic data in relation to the homeowners' association, among other processes.  
CP B.B.B. carries out this activity in its capacity as data controller, as it is the one who determines the purposes and means of such activity, by virtue of Article 4.7 of the GDPR:  
“‘data controller’ or ‘controller’: the natural or legal person, public authority, service, or other body that, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing; if the law of the Union or of the Member States determines the purposes and means of the processing, the data controller or the specific criteria for its appointment may be established by the law of the Union or of the Member States.”

Obligation Breached. Principles Relating to Processing  
Letter f) of Article 5.1 of the GDPR advocates:

f) treaties in such a way as to ensure adequate security of personal data, including protection against unauthorized or unlawful processing and against loss, destruction, or accidental damage, through the application of appropriate technical or organizational measures ("integrity and confidentiality").  
This principle assigns to CP B.B.B., as the data controller, the obligation to prevent unauthorized or unlawful processing of these data, as well as their loss or destruction.  
Therefore, it should not process personal data if it is not in a position to guarantee the confidentiality and integrity of such data and to prevent a third party from accessing data that does not concern them, as well as to avoid their loss or destruction.  
The data controller is obliged to process personal data in such a way as to ensure their confidentiality through the application of appropriate technical or organizational measures. For a breach of Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR to occur, there must have been a loss of confidentiality or integrity.  
In this case, the principle of confidentiality in the processing of personal data has been violated. Such data, which were under the responsibility of the defendant party, were published on the notice board of the community of property owners of the building, being disseminated.  
In the images provided by the claimant, there is a publication on the notice board, dated December 28, 2022, and handwritten signed by the secretary and the president of the community, where the name, surname, and address are stated, as well as the amount of the fees that were listed as unpaid by the claimant and where payment of the debt is requested. This publication specifies the impossibility of making the proper notification and payment request to the owner, having resulted in unsuccessful attempts made by sending a burofax, and that on December 28, 2022, this announcement was inserted on the visible notice board of the Community of Property Owners.  
However, the claimant provides a copy of the burofax sent on December 28, 2022, to CP B.B.B., which states, "on the past day 23, I received a burofax requesting that within 15 days I proceed to pay an alleged debt I have with that community related to fees and assessments amounting to 422.40 euros, of which 352 euros correspond to fees and 70.40 euros to late interest," and a copy of judgment no. (...) from the Court of First Instance No. X of ***LOCALIDAD.1, which states, "once the alleged debt was claimed on November 23, 2022, the actor here sent a burofax to the Community of Property Owners on December 28, 2022, insisting that said debt does not exist, announcing that, in the event that it is not rectified, legal actions would be taken, thus obliging the acting party to go to court, generating the consequent expenses, which leads to appreciating the bad faith provided for in Article 395.1 of the LEC," therefore the one-month period to withdraw the burofax dated November 25, 2022, which was collected from the postal office on December 23, 2022, was not respected, and there was no legal authorization to publish the communication on the notice board of the community from December 28, 2022, to January 1, 2023.  
The exposure of personal data on the community notice board must meet the requirements specified for such exposure in each case and its functions must be those of notification or citation. According to Article 21.3) of the Horizontal Property Law (LPH):

“3. To initiate the claim through the monitoring procedure, a certificate of the debt settlement agreement issued by the person acting as secretary of the community, with the approval of the president, must be attached to the claim, unless the former is a secretary-administrator with the necessary and legally recognized professional qualification who will not intervene professionally in the judicial claim for the debt, in which case the president's signature will not be required. This certificate must specify the amount owed and its breakdown. In addition to the certificate, the initial request for the monitoring process must include the document evidencing that the debtor has been notified, which may also be done subsidiarily on the notice board or a visible place in the community for a period of at least three days. The initial request for the monitoring procedure may include the approved fees that accrue until the notification of the debt, as well as all expenses and costs incurred in the debt claim, including those arising from the intervention of the secretary-administrator, which will be borne by the debtor.”  
In this case, the disclosure of personal data on the aforementioned notice board does not seem to comply with the provisions set out in the LPH, and it does not appear that there is a necessity or legal authorization to expose the communication subject to the claim.  
In the present case, the CP B.B.B. has violated the provisions of Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR, as there has been a lack of confidentiality in the processing of personal data, resulting from this exposure on the community notice board. The principle of confidentiality aims to prevent unauthorized leaks of personal data by the data subjects.  
For the reasons stated, it is considered that the known facts constitute an infringement, attributable to CP B.B.B., for violating the previously transcribed article.  
V  
Classification of the infringement of Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR and qualification for the purposes of prescription  
Article 83.5 of the GDPR classifies as an administrative infringement the violation of the following article, which will be sanctioned, in accordance with paragraph 2, with administrative fines of up to 20,000,000 EUR or, in the case of a company, an amount equivalent to a maximum of 4% of the total annual global turnover of the previous financial year, opting for the higher amount:  
"a) the basic principles for processing, including the conditions for consent under Articles 5, 6, 7, and 9;"  
For its part, the LOPDGDD in its Article 71, Infringements, states that:  
“Infringements are constituted by the acts and behaviors referred to in paragraphs 4, 5, and 6 of Article 83 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as well as those that are contrary to this organic law.”

For the sole purposes of the prescription period, Article 72.1 of the LOPDGDD establishes the following:  
"Based on what is established in Article 83.5 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the following are considered very serious and will prescribe after three years: the infringements that constitute a substantial violation of the articles mentioned therein and, in particular, the following:

VI  
Warning  
In the event of an infringement of the provisions of the GDPR, among the corrective powers available to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection, as the supervisory authority, Article 58.2 of said Regulation includes the following:  
“2 Each supervisory authority shall have all of the following corrective powers indicated below:  
(…)  
b) issue a warning to any controller or processor when the processing operations have infringed the provisions of this Regulation;.”  
Article 64.3 of the LOPDGDD establishes that the AEPD, after hearing the controller or processor, may issue a warning.  
In the present case, given the nature of the facts and circumstances outlined in the preceding Legal Grounds, it is deemed appropriate to issue a warning to CP B.B.B. for the infringement of the aforementioned article.  
Therefore, in accordance with the applicable legislation, the Presidency of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:  
FIRST: TO ISSUE A WARNING to the COMMUNITY OF PROPERTY OWNERS B.B.B., with NIF ***NIF.1, for an infringement of Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR, classified under Article 83.5.a) of the GDPR.
