LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, and 64.1 of the LOPDGDD, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states that: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued in its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
Preliminary Issues  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 55 of the GDPR, the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to perform the functions assigned to it in Article 57, including the enforcement of the Regulation and promoting awareness among data controllers and processors regarding the
5/17  
obligations that fall upon them, as well as addressing the claims presented by an interested party and investigating, in a timely manner, the reason for such claims.  
Correspondingly, Article 31 of the GDPR establishes the obligation for controllers and processors to cooperate with the supervisory authority that requests it in the performance of their functions. In cases where they have appointed a data protection officer, Article 39 of the GDPR assigns this officer the function of cooperating with said authority.  
In accordance with this regulation, following the procedure established in Article 65.4 of the LOPDGDD, prior to the admission for processing of the claim regarding the exercise of rights regulated in Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, which gives rise to this procedure, the claim was forwarded to the party being claimed against for analysis, to respond to this Agency within a month, and to provide evidence of having given the claimant the appropriate response.  
The result of this forwarding did not allow for the understanding that the claims of the claimant were satisfied. Consequently, on November 23, 2023, for the purposes provided for in Articles 64.1 and 65 of the LOPDGDD, the claim presented was admitted for processing. This admission for processing determines the opening of the present procedure regarding the lack of attention to a request for the exercise of the rights established in Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, regulated in Article 64.1 of the LOPDGDD, according to which:  
“When the procedure refers exclusively to the lack of attention to a request for the exercise of the rights established in Articles 15 to 22 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council, of April 27, 2016, it shall be initiated by an admission agreement, which shall be adopted in accordance with the provisions of Article 65 of this organic law.  
In this case, the deadline for resolving the procedure shall be six months from the date on which the agreement of admission for processing was notified to the claimant. After this period, the interested party may consider their claim to be upheld.”  
Article 58.2 of the GDPR grants the Spanish Data Protection Agency a series of corrective powers for the purpose of rectifying any infringement of the GDPR, including “ordering the controller or processor to address the requests for the exercise of the rights of the interested party under this Regulation.”

Right of Access  
Article 15 of the GDPR, Right of Access of the Data Subject, states that:  
“1. The data subject shall have the right to obtain from the data controller confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning them is being processed and, if that is the case, the right of access to the personal data and the following information:  
a) the purposes of the processing;  
b) the categories of personal data concerned;  
c) the recipients or categories of recipients to whom the personal data have been or will be disclosed, in particular recipients in third countries or international organizations;  
d) where possible, the envisaged period for which the personal data will be stored or, if not possible, the criteria used to determine that period;  
e) the existence of the right to request from the controller rectification or erasure of personal data or restriction of processing of personal data concerning the data subject, or to object to such processing;  
f) the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority;  
g) when the personal data have not been obtained from the data subject, any available information as to their source;  
h) the existence of automated decision-making, including profiling, referred to in Article 22, paragraphs 1 and 4, and at least in such cases, meaningful information about the logic involved, as well as the significance and the envisaged consequences of such processing for the data subject.

VI  
Conclusion  
The rights procedure is initiated as a consequence of the lack of attention to any of the rights regulated in the data protection legislation. Therefore, only the facts related to the exercise of these rights will be analyzed and assessed, excluding the other issues raised by the parties.  
The present case is motivated by the requests to exercise the rights of access and deletion submitted to the entity Colegio Caude, where the daughter of the claiming party was studying early childhood education.  
The responding party acts in the capacity of data controller, being, therefore, the entity to which requests for the exercise of rights regarding personal data protection should be addressed by the interested parties. These circumstances are not altered by the fact that Colegio Caude uses the management tool "Raíces" provided by the Autonomous Community of Madrid to educational centers.  
During the processing of this procedure, from the examination of the documentation provided, it has been established that the claiming party, on 07/05/2023, requested access to the personal data of her minor daughter, specifically to the tests conducted for the preparation of the psychopedagogical evaluation report of the minor, which had been previously delivered to the claiming party.  
Subsequently, on 22/06/2023, the claiming party submitted a new request for access to the personal data of the minor available in the information systems of Colegio Caude or in the "Raíces" system.

The main object of the right of access exercised by the claiming party relates to the psychopedagogical evaluation conducted on their daughter in the educational context. Insofar as those tests include personal data of the interested party, it is understood that they must be provided in accordance with the requested right of access, without this generally implying the obligation to deliver a copy or reproduction of the original documents. It is up to the entity responsible for processing the personal data to choose the manner in which it will provide the information, as long as it guarantees the interested party's access to all their personal data and the complete information associated with it.

In accordance with the above, Colegio Caude must provide the claimant with the evidence collected for the psychopedagogical evaluation of their daughter and the information detailed in Article 15.1 of the GDPR.

The possibility of processing personal data involved in that evaluation is contemplated in Organic Law 2/2006, of May 3, on Education, in which its twenty-third additional provision, section 1, establishes that "educational centers may collect the personal data of their students that are necessary for the exercise of their educational function. Such data may refer to the origin and family and social environment, personal characteristics or conditions, the development and results of their schooling, as well as any other circumstances whose knowledge is necessary for the education and guidance of the students."  
This Organic Law also obliges educational administrations to provide the necessary means for all students to achieve maximum personal, intellectual, social, and emotional development, as well as the objectives established generally in this Law, ensuring the necessary resources so that students who require educational attention different from the ordinary can achieve the maximum possible development of their personal capacities and, in any case, the objectives established generally for all students; as well as to establish the necessary procedures and resources to identify early the specific educational needs of these students (Article 71).  
In the same vein, Law 1/2022, of February 10, Master of the Freedom of Educational Choice of the Community of Madrid, refers in Article 20 to the early identification of the needs of these students, their evaluation and assessment, so that the corresponding educational response can be provided.  
According to Article 21 of this Law 1/2022, this is the main objective of the psycho-pedagogical evaluation, which "includes the collection, analysis, and assessment of all relevant information related to the student, their family, school, and social context, to determine the type of educational need they possess. For the realization of the psycho-pedagogical evaluation, family participation is considered fundamental. Initially, the authorization of the student's parents and legal guardians will be required, and their subsequent collaboration will be essential to gather the necessary aspects to carry out an objective evaluation."  
Article 22 "Psycho-pedagogical Report" of the same Law 1/2022 establishes the following:  
"1. The realization of the psycho-pedagogical evaluation will serve as a basis for the preparation of a psycho-pedagogical report, which will be included in the school file, in which different aspects related to the student will be collected, especially regarding their personal and educational evolution, the educational needs they require, curricular adaptations, the schooling proposal, educational supports, and any other guidance deemed essential, as well as the process for its review or update.  
2. For the realization of the psycho-pedagogical evaluation, the authorization of the student's parents and legal guardians is necessary. A copy of the report resulting from the evaluation must be provided to them, as well as information from the center regarding the measures."

On the other hand, Decree 23/2023, of March 22, from the Governing Council, which regulates educational attention to the individual differences of students in the Community of Madrid, applicable to concerted schools such as Colegio Caude, also refers to the prevention, detection, and identification of barriers to learning and participation, as well as the determination of educational needs as part of the teaching function.  
According to Article 6 of this Decree, the educational needs of students will be determined, in general, after the completion of a psychopedagogical evaluation, which will also serve to substantiate the most appropriate educational response; and which will include the collection, analysis, and assessment of all relevant information related to the student, their family, school, and social context, which is of interest for determining educational needs and proposing appropriate measures.  
In several of the cases outlined in this Decree, the completion of a psychopedagogical evaluation will be mandatory for the initial determination and subsequent monitoring of educational needs.  
"The conclusions of the psychopedagogical evaluation will be recorded in a psychopedagogical report, which will be prepared according to the model included in Annex I and will be signed by the person responsible for conducting the evaluation. It will be attached to the student's academic file and will include aspects related to the identification of barriers to learning and participation (personal and academic evolution of the student, assessment of the level of development in the various areas of current functioning, level of curricular competence, and analysis of the learning context), with the determination of educational needs, the proposal of educational measures, both ordinary and specific, that are required to adequately address those needs, and any other guidance or observation deemed essential, as well as the appropriate time, if applicable, to conduct a new psychopedagogical evaluation. A copy of the psychopedagogical report will be provided to the parents or legal representatives of the student" (Article 6.6 of Decree 23/2023).  
Finally, it should be noted that the second additional provision of Decree 23/2023 establishes that in the obtaining and processing of personal data of students and their families, compliance with the provisions contained in the LOPDGDD, RGPD, and implementing regulations will be ensured.  
In short, the psychopedagogical evaluation is conceived as an essential instrument that educational administrations can employ with the ultimate aim of addressing the individual differences of students and promoting their personal and academic development, which is seen as an obligation of these administrations.
