BACKGROUND  
FIRST: As a result of a complaint filed with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection, indications of a possible breach of the regulations within the scope of the competencies of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection were observed, leading to the initiation of actions under file number EXP202304413.

In the framework of the investigative actions, four requests for information were sent to TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L. with NIF B42981118, related to the complaint indicated in the first section, requiring that within ten working days it present to this Agency the information and documentation specified therein. The second and fourth were not collected by the responsible party within the period for availability, as evidenced by the acknowledgments of receipt in the file, understanding that the notifications were made, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 43.2 and 41.5 of the LPACAP, on August 22 and October 6, 2023.  
FOURTH: Regarding the requested information, TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L. has not submitted any response to this Spanish Agency for Data Protection within the deadlines granted for this purpose.

I  
Competence  
In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of the GDPR, and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of the LOPDGDD, the Director of the Spanish Data Protection Agency is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Data Protection Agency shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, to the extent that they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  

II  
Allegations to the initiation agreement  
In response to the allegations presented by TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L., the following should be noted.  
By virtue of the contract provided and the information contained in the file originating the requests (EXP202304413), it has been determined that TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L. acts as an intermediary in communications with the operator providing the communication service, and is therefore not the data controller or processor of the requested data, and thus is not subject to the sanctioning liability for non-compliance with Article 58.1.a) GDPR, as typified in Article 83.5.e) GDPR.  
However, the following must be addressed regarding the allegations presented concerning the events that occurred.  
Regarding the alleged impossibility by TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L. to have "access to the requested information, unless STG GROUP provides it in a timely manner," it should be highlighted that this was not communicated to this Agency at any time despite the successive requests made, and it was only at the time of presenting allegations to the initiation Agreement of this sanctioning procedure that the appropriate explanations were provided, with TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L. having not sent any response to this Agency until then.  
In relation to the assertion that the provision of the requested information was prior to TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L.'s knowledge of the initiation Agreement of this sanctioning procedure, it is relevant to point out that the response referred to, submitted on December 16, 2023, was after the initiation Agreement of this sanctioning procedure was issued, dated December 12, 2023, and also after the notification of its availability, dated December 13, 2023, as recorded in the acknowledgment of receipt in the file. Furthermore, it is noted that, in accordance with Article 41.6 of the LPACAP, on that date a notice was sent to the email address communicated to this Agency informing it of the availability of said notification in the electronic headquarters and at the single enabled electronic address.

Regarding the assertion that the same requested information was previously requested from LEAST COST ROUTING TELECOM, S.L. and was, it says, resolved on May 11, 2023, by LEAST COST ROUTING TELECOM, S.L., it is emphasized that the unaddressed information requests were issued after the response referred to, and repeatedly, therefore they should not simply be ignored and understood as having been answered by a prior document that, moreover, was not even submitted by TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L.  
From all of this, a lack of diligence on the part of this entity regarding the requests from this Agency made in the file EXP202304413 can be inferred, although no liability in the form of a sanction is determined.  
Finally, regarding the information now provided in response to the requests, this Agency acknowledges receipt of the same, without this declaration implying any pronouncement on its suitability.  

III  
Filing  
Article 89 of the LPACAP provides as follows:  
“1. The instructing body shall resolve the termination of the procedure, with the filing of the actions, without the need for the formulation of a resolution proposal, when it is evident in the instruction of the procedure that any of the following circumstances exist:  
a) The non-existence of the facts that could constitute the infringement.  
b) When the facts are not proven.  
c) When the proven facts do not manifestly constitute an administrative infringement.  
d) When there is no person or persons responsible or when they appear exempt from liability.  
e) When it is concluded, at any time, that the infringement has expired.  
2. In the case of sanctioning procedures, once the instruction of the procedure is concluded, the instructing body shall formulate a resolution proposal that must be notified to the interested parties. The resolution proposal must indicate the disclosure of the procedure and the deadline for formulating allegations and presenting the documents and information deemed pertinent.”  
In the present case, in light of the facts presented, it is considered that TECH TELECOM LEGAL SERVICES, S.L. is not the operator providing the communication service, nor is it therefore the data controller or processor of the requested data obliged by the regulations to provide the information to the supervisory authority, and it is concluded that the proven facts do not manifestly constitute an administrative infringement. Consequently, it is appropriate to agree to the filing of the procedure.
