From the actions taken in this procedure and the documentation in the file, the following has been established:  
PROVEN FACTS  
First: The complaint was based on the fact that the website ***URL.1 did not comply with the current legal regulations regarding cookie policy; therefore, on 20/09/23, it was noted by this Agency that, upon entering the website for the first time, after clearing the terminal equipment of browsing history and cookies, without accepting new cookies or taking any action on the website, cookies that were not technical or necessary were being used without the prior consent of the user.  
Furthermore, the cookie information banner present on the first layer of the website, containing the generic message: “The only cookies we like are the ones you eat. But we have to do this to make your experience the best it can be,” lacked the necessary identification regarding the purposes for which the cookies would be used and whether they were first-party or third-party cookies.  
Finally, it was confirmed that it was impossible to reject cookies that were not technical or necessary since the button present in the cookie information banner on the main page did not respond. There was also no possibility to manage cookies granularly through a control panel, should the user wish to do so, as it did not exist.  
Second: After receiving the allegations regarding the initiation of the file, where essentially the respondent stated that they had corrected the deficiencies detected in the Cookie Policy, on 18/10/23, this Agency accessed the website ***URL.1 again, noting the following characteristics regarding its “Cookie Policy”:  
Regarding the use of cookies before the user gives their consent, it was confirmed that, upon entering the website for the first time, after clearing the terminal equipment of browsing history and cookies, without accepting new cookies or taking any action on the page, the website NO longer used cookies that were not technical or necessary. It was also confirmed that, if the user wished for the website not to use cookies that were not technical or necessary by using the option <<Reject Cookies>>, the website continued not to use such cookies.  
Regarding the cookie information banner on the first layer, it was confirmed that it informed about the purposes for which the cookies were used, as well as how to manage them directly or through the control panel.  
Regarding the information provided in the “Cookie Policy,” it was confirmed that, upon accessing the “Cookie Policy” through the link at the bottom of the main page, the website redirects the user to a new page where information is provided about: what cookies are; what the information collected through cookies is used for; what types of cookies exist and which cookies the website uses and how to manage cookies once consent has been given through the mechanisms available in the browser installed on the terminal equipment.

Regarding the Cookies Policy of the website  

***URL.1  
The respondent states in its allegations regarding the initiation of the file that, currently, the website ***URL.1 displays to the user at the beginning of navigation an informative window that, in a basic manner, alerts about the use of cookies and informs about their purpose and that they are both first-party and third-party cookies.  
It also states that cookies that are not technical or necessary are no longer used without the prior consent of the user and that the installation of these cookies can be customized or they can be kept uninstalled while continuing to navigate the website.  
Therefore, it considers that the necessary and required measures have been adopted to comply with the provisions of the LSSI.  
However, the deficiencies detected in the cookies policy of the website ***URL.1, from the verification conducted by this Agency on 29/09/23 to the latest one dated 18/10/23 on the website in question, regarding the impossibility of rejecting non-technical or necessary third-party cookies and the lack of information present in the cookie banner on the main page, constitutes a violation of the provisions established in Article 22.2 of the LSSI during that time, as it states that:  
“Service providers may use storage and data retrieval devices on the terminal equipment of the recipients, provided that they have given their consent after being provided with clear and complete information about their use, in particular, about the purposes of data processing, in accordance with the provisions of Organic Law 15/1999 on the protection of personal data.  
When technically possible and effective, the consent of the recipient to accept data processing may be provided through the appropriate parameters of the browser or other applications.  
The above shall not prevent the possible storage or technical access solely for the purpose of carrying out the transmission of a communication over an electronic communications network or, to the extent strictly necessary, for the provision of a service of the information society expressly requested by the recipient.”

Termination of the procedure  
Article 85 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter LPACAP), under the heading "Termination in sanctioning procedures" provides as follows:  
“1. Once a sanctioning procedure has been initiated, if the offender acknowledges their responsibility, the procedure may be resolved with the imposition of the appropriate sanction.  
2. When the sanction is solely of a pecuniary nature or when it is possible to impose a pecuniary sanction and another non-pecuniary sanction but the impropriety of the latter has been justified, voluntary payment by the presumed responsible party, at any time prior to the resolution, will imply the termination of the procedure, except regarding the restoration of the altered situation or the determination of compensation for damages caused by the commission of the infringement.  
3. In both cases, when the sanction is solely of a pecuniary nature, the competent authority to resolve the procedure shall apply reductions of at least 20% on the amount of the proposed sanction, which shall be cumulative. The aforementioned reductions must be specified in the notification of initiation of the procedure, and their effectiveness shall be conditioned upon the withdrawal or waiver of any action or appeal in the administrative route against the sanction. The percentage of reduction provided for in this section may be increased by regulation.”  
In accordance with the above,  
the Director of the Spanish Data Protection Agency RESOLVES:
