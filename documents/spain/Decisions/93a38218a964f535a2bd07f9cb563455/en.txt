RESOLUTION OF THE SANCTIONING PROCEDURE  
BACKGROUND  
FIRST: On 17/09/24, A.A.A. (the claimant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection.  
The complaint is directed against the entity, FRESHLY COSMETICS, S.L. with NIF: B55666143, owner of the website, https://www.freshlycosmetics.com/es/ (hereinafter, the respondent), for the alleged violation of Law 34/2002, of July 11, on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI).  
The claimant states in their complaint, among other issues, that this website was fined two years ago for not having cookies properly configured, but as of today, it continues to use cookies without obtaining permission.  
SECOND: On 25/10/24, in accordance with the provisions of Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD), this Agency forwarded the complaint to the respondent for analysis and to report back within one month regarding the issues raised in the complaint.  
THIRD: On 28/11/24, this Agency received a letter from the respondent summarizing their response regarding the "Cookie Policy" of their website:  
- The complaint relates to the implementation of cookies without the proper consent of users. It was identified that when accessing the website www.freshlycosmetics.com without accepting Marketing cookies, it was possible to load a video from Youtube.com, which resulted in the installation of advertising cookies without the user's consent.  
- Regarding the measures adopted: to correct this deficiency and comply with Article 22.2 of the LSSI, the following measures were implemented: Blocking the display of the embedded YouTube video for users who have not granted consent to Marketing cookies. Updating the cookie policy and its dynamic implementation with automated tools, and modifying the landing page to reflect these changes.  
- Regarding the cause of the incident: The internal analysis revealed that this problem did not stem from a deliberate action by Freshly Cosmetics, but from an omission in the controls for implementing third-party cookies. Consequently, audits were strengthened to ensure regulatory compliance, updating their cookie policy and integrating an automatic update system with Cookiebot. The visible information on the website has been modified, and as of December 5, 2024, the blocking of embedded videos for users without consent has been implemented.

First: Regarding the installation of cookies on the terminal equipment prior to consent.  
Let us remember that cookies are small text files that websites store on the user's device (computer, mobile, tablet) through the browser. These cookies are used to store information about the user's interaction with the webpage, for various purposes such as improving the user experience, remembering settings or preferences, or conducting statistical analysis or usage preferences of the site, potentially creating user profiles.  
Article 22.2 of the LSSI establishes that users must be provided with clear and complete information about the use of devices or methods for storing and retrieving data and, in particular, about the purposes of processing that data. This information must be provided in accordance with the provisions of the GDPR.  
Regarding the use of cookies, when it involves processing that allows for the identification of the user, the data controllers must ensure compliance with the requirements established by data protection regulations. However, it is necessary to point out that those cookies necessary for the intercommunication of terminals and the network and those that provide a service expressly requested by the user would be exempt from compliance with the obligations established in Article 22.2 of the LSSI.  
In this regard, the Article 29 Working Party, in its Opinion 4/2012, interpreted that, apart from technical cookies, the exempted cookies would include user input cookies (those used to fill out forms or manage a shopping cart); authentication or user identification cookies (session cookies); user security cookies (those used to detect erroneous and repeated attempts to connect to a website); multimedia player session cookies; session cookies for load balancing; user interface customization cookies; and some plug-in cookies for sharing social content.  
These cookies would be excluded from the scope of application of Article 22.2 of the LSSI, and therefore, it would not be necessary to inform or obtain consent for their use. Conversely, it will be necessary to inform and obtain the prior consent of the user before the use of any other type of cookies, whether first-party or third-party, session or persistent.  
In this case, during the inspection carried out on the website in question, it was detected that upon accessing it for the first time, after clearing the terminal equipment of browsing history and cookies, without accepting new cookies or taking any action on it, the following non-technical cookies were used prior to the user's consent: performance cookies: AB_CHECKOUT and AB_SHIPMENTCOST and targeting cookies: _dyjsession; _dyid; DYID and _dyid_server.

III  
Unfulfilled Obligation  
From the deficiencies detected regarding the cookie policy on the website https://www.freshlycosmetics.com/es/, namely:  
- the use of cookies that are not of a technical or necessary nature without the prior consent of the user,  
- the use of such cookies even after the user has rejected their use, and  
- the impossibility of modifying consent at any time during web browsing when the user wishes to do so,  
The aforementioned defects imply, on the part of the respondent, a violation of Article 22.2 of the LSSI, as it establishes the following:  
“Service providers may use data storage and retrieval devices on the terminal equipment of the recipients, provided that they have given their consent after being provided with clear and complete information about their use, in particular, about the purposes of data processing, in accordance with the provisions of Organic Law 15/1999, of December 13, on the protection of personal data.  
When technically possible and effective, the recipient's consent to accept data processing may be provided through the appropriate parameters of the browser or other applications.

IV.  
Classification of the infringement of Article 22.2 of the LSSI  
This infringement is classified as “minor” in Article 38.4 g) of the aforementioned Law, which considers as such:  
“Using data storage and retrieval devices when the information has not been provided or the consent of the service recipient has not been obtained in the terms required by Article 22.2.”  
For its part, Article 39.1.c), “Sanctions,” establishes that:  
“1. For the commission of the infringements set out in the previous article, the following sanctions will be imposed: c) For the commission of minor infringements, a fine of up to 30,000 euros.”  
V.  
Graduation of the Sanction  
The LSSI, in its Article 40, establishes the “graduation of the amount of sanctions,” taking into account the following criteria: a) The existence of intent. b) The period of time during which the infringement has been committed. c) The recurrence of the commission of infringements of the same nature, when declared by a firm resolution. d) The nature and amount of the damages caused. e) The benefits obtained from the infringement. f) The volume of billing affected by the committed infringement. g) Adherence to a code of conduct or an applicable advertising self-regulation system regarding the committed infringement, which complies with the provisions of Article 18 or in the eighth final provision and has been favorably reported by the competent authority or authorities.  
Based on the evidence obtained, it is considered appropriate to graduate the sanction to be imposed according to the aggravating factor of recurrence for the commission of infringements of the same nature, when declared by a firm resolution (section c), as it is confirmed that, on 03/02/23, a resolution was issued by the Director of the Spanish Data Protection Agency regarding file PS/00315/2022, for the use of third-party cookies that were not of a technical or necessary nature and the impossibility of rejecting third-party cookies, in addition to the lack of information in the “Cookie Policy” of the website.  
According to these criteria, it is deemed appropriate to impose, for the infringement of Article 22.2 of the LSSI, regarding the cookie policy implemented on the website owned by the respondent, a sanction of 10,000 euros (ten thousand euros).

holder of the website, https://www.freshlycosmetics.com/es/ for the violation of  
Article 22.2 of the LSSI, classified as "minor" in Article 38.4 g) of the aforementioned  
regulation, a fine of 10,000 euros (ten thousand euros).  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to FRESHLY COSMETICS, S.L.  
THIRD: To warn the sanctioned party that the imposed fine must be paid  
once this resolution becomes enforceable, in accordance with the provisions of  
Article 98.1.b) of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative  
Procedure of Public Administrations, within the voluntary payment period indicated in  
Article 68 of the General Collection Regulation, approved by Royal Decree  
939/2005, of July 29, in relation to Article 62 of Law 58/2003, of December 17,  
by depositing it into the restricted account No. ES00 0000 0000 0000  
0000 0000, opened in the name of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection at the  
banking entity CAIXABANK, S.A. or, failing that, the collection will proceed in the  
executive period.  
Upon receipt of the notification and once enforceable, if the date of  
enforceability falls between the 1st and the 15th of each month, inclusive, the  
deadline for voluntary payment will be until the 20th of the following month or the  
next business day thereafter, and if it falls between the 16th and the last day of  
each month, inclusive, the payment deadline will be until the 5th of the second  
following month or the next business day thereafter. In accordance with the  
provisions of Article 50 of the LOPDGDD, this Resolution will be made public once  
it has been notified to the interested parties.  
Against this resolution, which concludes the administrative route (Article 48.6 of the  
LOPDGDD), and in accordance with the provisions of Articles 112 and 123 of Law  
39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public  
Administrations, the interested parties may optionally file a motion for  
reconsideration with the Presidency of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection  
within one month from the day following the notification of this resolution or  
directly file a contentious-administrative appeal before the Contentious-Administrative  
Chamber of the National Court, in accordance with the provisions of Article 25 and  
in section 5 of the fourth additional provision of Law 29/1998, of July 13,  
regulating the Contentious-Administrative Jurisdiction, within two months from the  
day following the notification of this act, as provided in Article 46.1 of the  
aforementioned legal text.

suspended if the interested party expresses their intention to file a

formally communicate this fact by means of a written document addressed to the
