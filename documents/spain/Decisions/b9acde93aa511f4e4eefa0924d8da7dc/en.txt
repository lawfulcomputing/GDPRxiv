BACKGROUND  
FIRST: A.A.A. (hereinafter, the claimant) on September 22, 2022, filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection. The complaint is directed against the COMMUNITY OF PROPERTY OWNERS R.R.R., with NIF ***NIF.1 (hereinafter, the respondent). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The claimant requests that the Community of Property Owners to which they belong publish a copy of the minutes of the Ordinary General Meeting of the COMMUNITY OF PROPERTY OWNERS R.R.R., held on April 11, 2022, on a notice board located in a public place, which contains personal data of the owners listed, including that of the claimant, such as: first name, last name, as well as various circumstances related to the situation of the Community following the resolutions of the Provincial Court.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states that: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
The claimed facts materialize in the availability of the minutes of the Ordinary General Meeting of the COMMUNITY OF OWNERS R.R.R., on the notice board of the claimed Community of Owners, located in a public area, where personal data of the owners, including that of the claiming party, such as their name and surname, are displayed in such a way that it can be observed by any person, which could imply a violation of the regulations on the protection of personal data.  
The claimed party states that at no time has it published a copy of the minutes of the ordinary general meeting of the community, held on April 11, 2022, where personal data of the owners were recorded, on a notice board located in a public area.  
It also states that although the notice board of the Community is not in a public area but within the private urbanization itself, the minutes of the Community are not published on the old notice board located within the common areas of this urbanization, but are sent to each owner individually at their home, which does not violate data protection regulations since all recipients of the minutes are owners and members of the Community.  
In response to the allegations presented by the claimed entity, it should be noted that the community of owners is responsible for the processing of personal data of the residents of that community, ensuring at all times that it respects the principles of integrity and confidentiality recognized in the GDPR.  
The dissemination of personal data in spaces that are not restricted to the owners, on the walls of the entrances or on common use notice boards, could violate data protection regulations. However, the responsibility for any potential infringement could only be determined if some means of proof were provided that would allow establishing who disseminated the personal data.  
This is so because access to personal data must comply with a series of principles in order not to violate data protection regulations.  
The cases in which public access to personal data related to matters arising from the management of the Community of Owners is authorized are specified in Law 49/1960, of July 21, on horizontal property (hereinafter, LPH).  
In general, Article 9.h) of the LPH indicates as an obligation of the owner the following:  
"To communicate to whoever performs the functions of secretary of the community, by any means that allows for proof of its receipt, the address in Spain for the purposes of summons and notifications of all kinds related to the community. In the absence of this communication, the address for summons and notifications shall be deemed to be the apartment or premises belonging to the community, with full legal effects for those delivered to the occupant thereof.  
If a summons or notification to the owner is impossible to carry out at the place provided for in the previous paragraph, it shall be understood to have been made by placing the corresponding communication on the notice board of the community, or in a visible place of general use designated for that purpose, with diligence expressing the date and reasons for proceeding with this form of notification, signed by whoever performs the functions of secretary of the community, with the approval of the president. The notification made in this manner shall produce full legal effects within three calendar days."  
In the present case, access to personal data on the aforementioned notice board, such as the name and surname, does not seem to comply with the assumptions set forth in the LPH, as there does not appear to be a need or legal authorization to display the document subject to the claim.  
The Community of Owners, in the exercise of its functions, processes personal data of the individuals who own the homes located in the building. It carries out this activity in its capacity as the data controller, as it is the one who determines the purposes and means of such activity, by virtue of Article 4.7 of the GDPR:  
"data controller" or "controller": the natural or legal person, public authority, service, or other body that, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing; where the law of the Union or of the Member States determines the purposes and means of processing, the controller or the specific criteria for its appointment may be established by the law of the Union or of the Member States.

Article 4, paragraph 12 of the GDPR broadly defines "personal data security breaches" (hereinafter referred to as security breach) as "all breaches of security that lead to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, or alteration of personal data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed, or to unauthorized communication or access to such data."  
In the present case, there is a personal data security breach under the circumstances indicated above, categorized as a confidentiality breach, as the minutes of the Ordinary General Meeting of the COMMUNITY OF PROPERTY OWNERS R.R.R., held on April 11, 2022, were published on the notice board located in a public area, which contained personal data of the owners listed, including that of the claimant, such as: name and surname.  
Within the principles of processing provided in Article 5 of the GDPR, the integrity and confidentiality of personal data is guaranteed in paragraph 1.f) of Article 5 of the GDPR.  
On the other hand, the security of personal data is regulated in Article 32 of the GDPR, which governs the security of processing.  
III  
Despite all that has been indicated, this Agency considers that sufficient evidence has not been provided to rebut the presumption of innocence, as the claimant's assertions can be refuted by those argued by the community in its allegations, since the defendant has repeatedly stated the possibility that other individuals, such as the previous management of the community or other neighbors, may have had access to the key of the display case of the notice board, therefore it is not sufficiently proven who is the owner of the facts that concern us.  
"The Constitutional Court (SSTC 131/2003 and 242/2005, among others) has ruled in this regard by indicating that one of the requirements inherent to the right to the presumption of innocence is that the sanction must be based on acts or incriminating evidence of the conduct attributed and that the burden of proof of the commission of the administrative offense and the participation in it of the accused falls on the acting public administration.  
For its part, Article 28.1 of Law 40/2015, of October 1, on the Legal Regime of the Public Sector establishes as one of the principles of the sanctioning power the principle of "Responsibility" and determines in this regard that:  
"Only individuals and legal entities, as well as, when a law recognizes them the capacity to act, groups of affected individuals, unions, and entities without legal personality and independent or autonomous assets, that are responsible for the same due to intent or negligence, may be sanctioned for acts constituting administrative infractions."

Likewise, it must be taken into account what is established in Article 53.2 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations, which states that:  
"In addition to the rights provided in the previous paragraph, in the case of administrative procedures of a sanctioning nature, the alleged responsible parties shall have the following rights: (…)  
b) To the presumption of non-existence of administrative responsibility until proven otherwise."  
Therefore, after a thorough study of the documents in this file, it seems appropriate to think that there is no certainty regarding the ownership of the facts that concern us, as there are several individuals who could have had access to the key of the display case of the notice board, where the minutes containing personal data were placed.  
Thus, after becoming aware of these facts, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:  
FIRST: TO ARCHIVE the present proceedings.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to the claimant.  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 50 of the LOPDGDD, this Resolution will be made public once it has been notified to the interested parties.  
Against this resolution, which concludes the administrative procedure as provided by Article 114.1.c) of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations, and in accordance with the provisions of Articles 112 and 123 of the aforementioned Law 39/2015, of October 1, the interested parties may optionally file a motion for reconsideration before the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection within one month from the day following the notification of this resolution or directly file a contentious-administrative appeal before the Contentious-Administrative Chamber of the National Court, in accordance with the provisions of Article 25 and paragraph 5 of the fourth additional provision of Law 29/1998, of July 13, regulating Contentious-Administrative Jurisdiction, within two months from the day following the notification of this act, as provided in Article 46.1 of the aforementioned Law.
