FACTS  
FIRST: On September 20, 2022, a complaint was filed with entry registration number REGAGE22e00041247371 before the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against VODAFONE ESPAÑA, S.A.U. with NIF A80907397 (hereinafter, the complained party). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The complainant submits a complaint regarding the receipt of commercial calls on their mobile lines (***PHONE.1, ***PHONE.2, and ***PHONE.3) registered in the Robinson List service and despite the request for the deletion of the complainant's personal data being attended to by the complained entity on March 18, 2022.  
Along with the notification, the following is provided:  
- Copy of the deletion request submitted by the complainant, response addressing the exercised right.  
- Screenshots confirming the receipt of three calls on July 6, September 12, and September 14, 2022, from the calling lines ***PHONE.4, ***PHONE.5, and ***PHONE.6.  
- Copy of the recordings of the calls received from the lines ***PHONE.4 and ***PHONE.5 in which the interlocutor identifies themselves as from the complained entity, attempting to offer their telephony services.  

SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the complaint was forwarded to the complained party, for them to analyze it and inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in data protection regulations.  
The forwarding, which was carried out in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), was recorded on October 24, 2022, as noted in the acknowledgment of receipt in the file.  
On November 28, 2022, a response was received by this Agency indicating:  
“After conducting the appropriate checks, we have verified that the complainant's telephone lines, ***PHONE.1, ***PHONE.2, and ***PHONE.3, are listed in the official Robinson list of ADigital and in Vodafone's internal Robinson list.”  
“We have checked whether the indicated calling numbers in the information request appear in our database with the telephone numbers used by our collaborators to make solicitation calls. To this end, we have verified that there are no numbers associated with our collaborators that make solicitation calls on behalf of Vodafone in our database. Therefore, it does not seem possible to relate the receipt of the calls that Ms. A.A.A. mentions in her complaint with my represented party.”  
“………, currently Vodafone has ceased to work with those entities that have repeatedly violated the contractual obligations established by Vodafone, as well as the legal provisions regarding data protection, and is in the process of selecting those collaborators that guarantee full compliance with current regulations.”  

THIRD: On December 2, 2022, in accordance with Article 65 of the LOPDGDD, the complaint was admitted for processing.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
According to the provisions established in Article 114.1.b) of Law 11/2022, of June 28, General Telecommunications Law (hereinafter LGTel) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative proceedings.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
Finally, the Fourth Additional Provision "Procedure in relation to the competencies attributed to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection by other laws" establishes that: "The provisions in Title VIII and its development regulations shall apply to the procedures that the Spanish Agency for Data Protection must process in exercising the competencies attributed to it by other laws."  

II  
Final Provision Six, Section 2 of LGTel 11/2022  
"The right of end users not to receive unwanted calls for commercial communication purposes as provided in Article 66.1.b) shall come into force within one year from the publication of this law in the 'Official State Gazette.' Until that moment, end users of publicly available interpersonal communication services based on numbering may continue to exercise the right to oppose receiving unwanted calls for commercial communication purposes made through systems other than those established in Article 66.1.a) and to be informed of this right."  

III  
Conclusion
