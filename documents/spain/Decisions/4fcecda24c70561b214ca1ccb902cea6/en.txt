Examined the appeal for reconsideration filed by the entity INFINITY ECOM S.L. with CIF: B42993956, holder of the website, ***URL.1 (hereinafter, "the appealing party"), against the resolution issued by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection in the procedure, PS/00393/2021, for violation of the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of April 27, 2016, regarding the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (GDPR), and in the L.O. 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDPGDD), and based on the following:

FACTS  
FIRST: On January 18, 2023, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection issued a resolution in the sanctioning procedure PS/00393/202, opened against the entity INFINITY ECOM S.L., which was notified on January 23, 2023, under Article 13 of the GDPR, for the lack of necessary information in the "Privacy Policy" on its website, with a penalty of 5,000 euros (five thousand euros), and under Article 22.2 of the LSSI, for the irregularities detected on its website regarding the "Cookies Policy," with a penalty of 5,000 euros (five thousand euros).  
Additionally, the entity INFINITY ECOM S.L. was ordered to implement, within one month, the necessary corrective measures to adapt its actions to the personal data protection regulations, regarding Article 13 of the GDPR, as well as to inform this Agency within the same period about the measures adopted.  

SECOND: As proven facts of the aforementioned sanctioning procedure, PS/00393/2022, the following was recorded:  
On May 9, 2022, this Agency verified the following characteristics regarding its "Privacy Policy" and its "Cookies Policy":  
a) Regarding the "Privacy Policy":  
If accessed through the link located at the bottom of the main page, the "Privacy Policy" leads to a new page ***URL.2, where information is provided about: the personal information collected; how such personal information is used; how that information is shared; the rights that assist the user and how to contact the person responsible for the website.  
Regarding the identification of the person responsible for the processing of personal data obtained through the website, only a contact email address was provided, lacking basic identification data such as the name or corporate name, the CIF or NIF, and the address of the registered office.  

b) Regarding the Cookies Policy:  
1.- Upon entering the website for the first time, after clearing the terminal equipment of browsing history and cookies, without accepting new cookies or taking any action on the website, it was verified that cookies that are not technical or necessary are used (performance cookies: _landing_page; shopify_sa_t; _shopify_y; _shopify_s; _shopify_sa_p; _y; _s; __kla_id; targeting cookies: _pin_unauth; test_cookie; IDEMás; _gcl_au; _fbp).  
2.- There is a cookie information banner on the main page with the following message: "This website uses cookies to ensure you get the best experience on our website." <<More>> <<Agree>>  
If the <<More>> link is clicked, the website displays the "Privacy Policy" page ***URL.3 mentioned above.  
There is no "Cookies Policy." The only information about cookies provided by the website is found on the "Privacy Policy" page: "(...) We collect information from the device using the following technologies: - Cookies are data files that are placed on your device or computer and often include an anonymous unique identifier. For more information about cookies and how to disable them, visit http://www.allaboutcookies.org (...)".  
There is no mechanism that allows rejecting cookies or managing them in a granular manner through a control panel.  

THIRD: On February 20, 2023, a written appeal for reconsideration was received by this Agency, submitted by the entity INFINITY ECOM S.L., in which, among other things, the following is alleged:  
FIRST.- We express total disagreement with the sanction for lack of Cookies policies, as the file developed and prepared by the AEPD mentions the different cookies that the website was using at that time. The website only had a technical redirection problem, but not a lack of content. We reproduce in this document the cookies that the AEPD describes in the file itself.  
Therefore, we can conclude that the website did indeed visibly display the different cookies it used, the domains, as well as the informative descriptions of them.  
If they had not been visible on the website, the AEPD would not have been able to obtain and attach them in this file. As previously mentioned, the only existing problem at the time was a technical redirection failure.  

SECOND.- Regarding the identity and contact details of the responsible party, the company established an email at all times so that any user could contact the company and exercise each and every one of the rights described in the policies themselves.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence.  
The Director of the Spanish Data Protection Agency is competent to resolve this appeal, in accordance with the provisions of Article 123 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter LPACAP) and Article 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD).  
II  
Response to the allegations  
The joint assessment of the documentation in the procedure brings to the attention of the AEPD a view of the reported action that has been reflected in the proven facts stated above. However, in response to the allegations presented by the entity being appealed in the appeal for reconsideration, the following should be indicated:  
a).- Regarding the statement made that it was not until the date of the last requirement that the company became aware that there was a sanction procedure against it, the following should be noted:  
On 10/17/22, the Director of the Spanish Data Protection Agency initiated a sanctioning procedure against the entity being appealed, and according to the records of the AEPD, it was made available to the entity in its electronic headquarters on 10/18/22, noting that it did not have access to it.  
Although the notification was validly carried out by electronic means, being considered completed in accordance with the provisions of Article 41.5 of the LPACAP, for informational purposes, a copy was sent by postal mail to the address: C/ MIGUEL HUE, 1 - 1 POLÍGONO SUR 11408 JEREZ DE LA FRONTERA CÁDIZ, which was returned to sender on 10/28/22, with the message “unknown.”  
On 11/03/22, the notification of the initiation agreement was made through an announcement in the Unique Edict Board of the Official State Gazette, in accordance with Article 44 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (LPACAP), so the notification was validly carried out, being considered completed in accordance with the provisions of Article 41 of the aforementioned Law.  
b).- Regarding the allegations of the entity in which it states that “the identity and contact details of the person responsible, the company established at all times a contact email,” the following should be indicated:  
When accessing the “Privacy Policy” ***URL.2, it was found that information was provided about the information collected by the website; how they used that information or how they shared that information, but it was observed that they did not inform.

Conclusion
