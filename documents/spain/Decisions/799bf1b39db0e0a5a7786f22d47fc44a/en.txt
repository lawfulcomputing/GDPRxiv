RESOLUTION FOR ARCHIVING OF PROCEEDINGS  
From the proceedings carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following:  
FACTS  
FIRST: On January 18, 2022, a complaint was filed with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against FLECA IGLESIAS ARTESANS, S.L. (hereinafter, the complained party).  
The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The complainant states that on December 17, 2021, and January 3, 2022, he received fines for parking a vehicle and also received communication from the Autonomous Body for Economic Management and Collection of the City Council of Salamanca (OAGER) for the collection of the tax on the same vehicle, with license plate ***LICENSE_PLATE.1.  
He indicates that he worked during the summer period (from August 2 to September 7, 2021) as a delivery person at the bakery of the complained party, located in Barcelona, driving several vans, including a Renault Kangoo with license plate ***LICENSE_PLATE.1, whose image is attached. He states that he provided his data (ID, driver's license, and health card) to the owner of the bakery to include him in the vehicle insurance and for the formalization of his employment contract, which ultimately did not occur.  
The complainant, suspecting that said commercial vehicle had been registered in his name, contacted the City Council of Salamanca, where he was informed that said vehicle was registered in his name from August 26 to December 21, 2021, which constitutes processing of his data without his consent.  
On January 7, 2022, he appeared at the facilities of the National Police Station El Coto in Gijón, where he reported the facts exposed regarding identity theft.  
Relevant documentation provided by the complainant:  
He provides a copy of the notifications from the City Council of Barcelona for fines dated November 8 and December 2, 2021.  
He provides a copy of the files of the changes of ownership of the vehicle with license plate ***LICENSE_PLATE.1, the first as the buyer of the vehicle, dated August 26, 2021 (his identifying data and ID are recorded), and the second as the seller dated December 21, 2021 (his identifying data and ID are also recorded).

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
In accordance with the functions conferred to each supervisory authority by Article 57.1 a), f) and h) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and the guarantee of digital rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative proceedings.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
Principles relating to processing  
Letter b) of Article 5.1 of the GDPR states:  
"1. Personal data shall be:  
(...)  
b) collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes, and not further processed in a manner that is incompatible with those purposes; in accordance with Article 89, paragraph 1, further processing of personal data for archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical research purposes, or statistical purposes shall not be considered incompatible with the initial purposes ('purpose limitation');"

III  
Legality of Processing  
For the processing to be carried out lawfully, the provisions of Article 6.1 of the GDPR must be met, which states:  
“1. Processing shall be lawful only if at least one of the following conditions is met:  
a) the data subject has given consent to the processing of their personal data for one or more specific purposes;  
b) processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party or for the implementation of pre-contractual measures at the request of the data subject;  
c) processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject;  
d) processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of another natural person;  
e) processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller;  
f) processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party, except where such interests are overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject which require protection of personal data, in particular when the data subject is a child.  
The provision in point f) of the first paragraph shall not apply to processing carried out by public authorities in the performance of their tasks.”  
IV  
Conclusion  
The complaint indicates that the claimant worked for a short time at the company of the respondent, during which time they provided their personal data (ID card, driver's license, and health card) to the owner of the entity to formalize the employment contract. They believe that their data was used for the purpose of registering a vehicle in their name and transferring it to a third party a few months later; providing supporting documentation for those transfers.  
Following preliminary investigative actions, it has been confirmed that the owner of the vehicle subject to the complaint (HIDRUS CONSULTORIA INTEGRAL DE INGENIERIA SISTEMAS Y SERVICIOS S.L.) commissioned the sale of their vehicle to an employee of a company in the group, named C.C.C., who ended their services on September 1, 2021, due to voluntary resignation. This employee handled all the procedures and management and found a buyer, whom they communicated was Mr. D.D.D., an employee of the bakery FLECA IGLESIAS ARTESANS, S.L. with whom they had previously been in contact.  
The manager who carried out the two changes of ownership of the vehicle (from Hidrus to the claimant and from the claimant to the final buyer) stated that C.C.C. provided them at their office with the necessary documentation for the vehicle transfer procedure. They made the request, as an employee of the selling company, HIDRUS, to transfer the vehicle to the buyer, D.D.D.  
Also, C.C.C. provided them at their office, on December 21, 2022, with the necessary documentation to carry out the vehicle transfer procedure for the second time. This time, the transferor was D.D.D. and the acquirer was B.B.B. They made the request due to the friendship they maintain with the administrator of the company FLECA IGLESIAS ARTESANS, S.L.  
Requests have been made to the involved parties, but either they have not received them or have not responded.  
In the case subject to the complaint, the data processing has been lawful, as the signed authorizations for the purchases and sales of the vehicle in question are on record.  
Regarding the possibility that the respondent may have forged the signatures of the claimant, they were asked about the outcome of the complaint filed regarding these facts, and despite having received the request for information, no response was received.  
Therefore, based on the information provided in the previous paragraphs, no evidence has been found to substantiate the existence of an infringement within the jurisdiction of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection.  
Thus, in accordance with the provisions indicated, by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection,
