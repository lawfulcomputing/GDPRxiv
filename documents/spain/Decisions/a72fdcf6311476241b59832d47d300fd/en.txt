RESOLUTION OF THE APPEAL FOR RECONSIDERATION
Examined the appeal for reconsideration filed by Mr. A.A.A. (hereinafter, the appellant) against the resolution issued by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection, dated August 21, 2023, and based on the following:

FACTS
FIRST: On August 21, 2023, a resolution was issued by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection in file EXP202102521, proceeding to archive the prior investigation proceedings. This resolution was notified to the appellant on August 22, 2023, as evidenced by the acknowledgment of receipt in the file.

SECOND: The appellant submitted on August 24, 2023, to this Spanish Agency for Data Protection, an appeal for reconsideration, fundamentally based on the following: Errors have occurred in the archived resolution regarding the role of the entity subject to the complaint, as it is indicated that it is the data processor, which is not the case, since: 
"This conclusion, which serves as the basis for the resolution subject to this appeal, evidences that an error has been committed by the AEPD in identifying the role played by the reported company, without there being sufficient legal justification in it to support such a conclusion. In this regard, according to what this party pointed out and credibly evidenced in its complaint and in the appeal it filed in September 2022, which was, by the way, upheld by the AEPD, the actions carried out by ARAMARK far exceed what was entrusted to it by the Andalusian Agency for Education, which is specified in the terms that governed the contracting of the catering service in which the conditions of the data processing assignment are outlined. 
Not in vain, the very Second Fact of the resolution subject to this appeal states that the reported company acknowledges that it "acts as a data processor for certain purposes, including the management of absences of the interested parties concerning CEIP ***CEIP.1 of Seville." 
However, that purpose of managing student absences from the educational center is not part of the object of the contract tendered by the Andalusian Agency for Education since it is exclusively limited to the provision of the catering service; that is, to provide food to the students of CEIP ***CEIP.1.

Consequently, it is credibly evidenced, and recognized by the reported company, that ARAMARK is carrying out data processing for purposes that are different and exceed what is permitted in the data processing assignment contract. 
Furthermore, it has been credibly evidenced that the terms governing the contracting, which are included in the present file, do not contemplate the possibility that ARAMARK processes special categories of data. 
Despite this, it has been evidenced throughout the processing of this file that this company is indeed carrying out data processing of health information of minors and that, therefore, it lacks legitimacy.
That is to say, in order to be a beneficiary of a public catering service whose conditions have been set by the Administration in the terms, ARAMARK imposes, unilaterally and outside of what has been expressly authorized by the educational Administration, submission to data processing of which it positions itself as responsible and which aim to achieve purposes quite different from those authorized in the data processing assignment contract.
Having pointed out the above, it is appropriate to remind the AEPD of what is provided in Article 33.2 in fine, which is transcribed below for clarification purposes. "The person who, appearing as a processor, uses the data for their own purposes will also be considered a data controller." 
The provision leaves no room for doubt, in such a way that the organic legislator has clarified that when a data processor uses the data for its own purposes, it will be considered a data controller.
Thus, ARAMARK, with respect to the processing indicated by this party, acts as a data controller and not as a processor under Article 33.2 in fine of the LOPDGDD. 
Moreover, in its capacity as a data controller, it does not have the status of an entity of the Andalusian public sector but rather of a subject governed by private law. 
Therefore, the resolution to refer actions to the Council of Transparency and Data Protection of Andalusia is entirely contrary to law since it cannot be subsumed under the legal arguments contained in the resolution subject to this appeal.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
The Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve this appeal, in accordance with the provisions of Article 123 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter LPACAP) and Article 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD).  

II  
Response to the allegations presented  
In relation to the statements made by the appellant, it should be noted that the main allegation on which the appeal is based was already pointed out by the now appellant in a previous appeal, which is that ARAMARK does not act as a data processor but as a data controller in relation to the provision of the cafeteria service at IES ***IES.1 in Seville.  
During the preliminary investigative proceedings conducted following the acceptance of the first appeal for reconsideration filed by the appellant, the following was established:  
“1. It provides the Register of activities of the processing of personal data of attendance and absences from the cafeteria service dated 21/2/23. Since the respondent is part of an international group, it submits the original documentation extracted from its files in English.  
The aforementioned register states that:  
Aramark uses its website ***URL.1 to market its food services and products to consumers. Aramark operates on a website with three Aramark entities (namely, the respondent and two others). Aramark uses its website as a direct communication channel with parents regarding menus, events, and monthly invoices, therefore, attendance and absence records. This is the main function of the site, which is why Aramark is considered the controller of the main site.  
Additionally, the website has another function that Aramark only perceives as a processor (on behalf of the individual school, respectively the contracting authority (public or private) entity managing the school): indication of allergies and intolerances (shown in a separate map: Aramark as processor).

Given that liability does not arise from the nature of the data controller, but rather from its specific activities in a given context, Aramark acts, for certain specific purposes, as the Data Controller of the website.  
In any case, this position of Data Controller should not be confused with the position of Data Processor that Aramark holds in relation to communications for the purpose of cafeteria assistance.  
Regarding the data related to the cafeteria service, since it is obtained through the Data Controller (Junta Andalucía), Aramark does not have the authority to manage the registrations and cancellations of the service; rather, it will be the Data Controller who carries out such management.

The offered channel meets the basic characteristics of any existing website, app, or platform in the market without exceeding its processing or going beyond the purpose for which it must process the obtained data. Likewise, the data of the interested parties related to the notification of cafeteria attendance is processed solely and exclusively to manage nothing more than the tasks associated with the reference contract.

III  
Conclusion  
Consequently, in the present appeal for reconsideration, the appellant has not provided new facts or legal arguments that would allow for a reconsideration of the validity of the contested resolution.
