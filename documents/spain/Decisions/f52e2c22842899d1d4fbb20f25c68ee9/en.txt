VII. Regarding the facts that motivate the complaint.  
- Operation of Avant through the application and in relation to the aggregation platform.  
As we have stated in point III, Avant is a company dedicated to sustainable mobility, which operates the shared rental service ("sharing") of electric scooters in the Spanish market. Avant offers this service through various platforms, including: the SMOU app, provided by the municipal company BARCELONA DE SERVICIOS MUNICIPALES, SA (B:SM), and the Reby app, which is the one that concerns us due to the allegations raised in this complaint.  
Reby Rides S.L. offers an aggregation service to Avant through its mobile app, a Mobility as a Service (MaaS) platform. In this way, users of the Reby application can geolocate and rent different shared mobility vehicles, including those from Avant.  
For the user to be able to use the service, it is necessary that:  
1. The user must register on the Reby platform, where they give consent for Reby to process their personal data.  
2. If the user wants to use the services of any of the other operators integrated into the app, such as Avant, they will have to register as a user of the operator beforehand.  
To facilitate this registration process for users of Reby's MaaS platform with Avant, the user is asked to give their prior consent for Reby to communicate certain personal data to Avant. On the other hand, in the section on obtaining personal data in Avant's Privacy Policy, as the Agency itself was able to verify, it contemplates the case of receiving data from third-party collaborating companies, such as Reby, always with the authorization of the interested party.  
That is to say, it is acted upon in such a way that the user must always consent in advance to the communication of their data to other mobility operators, contrary to what has been alleged by the City Council of Barcelona. (…)  
- Operation of the payment system for Avant users through the application and in relation to the aggregation platform.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence and applicable regulations  
According to the functions conferred to each supervisory authority by Article 57.1 a), f), and h) of the GDPR and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of the LOPDGDD, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to issue this resolution.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
Possible infringements  
On 06/11/2020, a complaint was received by this Agency directed against the entities TUCYCLE, AVANT, OIZ, and IBERSCOT for the transfer of personal data of users who register in the corresponding mobile "apps" to access the vehicle rental service. This complaint warns that when a person registers in one of the "apps" of these entities and subsequently installs another one, the second recognizes the user and reuses the data provided by the other "collaborating platform" in order to "streamline the process."  
The same complaint pointed out that none of these entities informed the user that their personal data was shared with third companies, which were shared among the mentioned entities.

In turn, Article 37 "Intervention of the data protection officer in case of complaint before the data protection authorities." of the LOPDGDD cited in the previous provision establishes:  
“1. When the data controller or processor has designated a data protection officer, the affected party may, prior to submitting a complaint against them to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection or, where appropriate, to the autonomous data protection authorities, address the data protection officer of the entity against which the complaint is made.

According to the aforementioned, the indicated mechanism prior to the admission of claims filed with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection consists of forwarding them to the Data Protection Officers designated by the data controllers or processors, or to them when they have not been designated, so that they can analyze the claims and respond within a month.  
Thus, when the actions taken in light of the claim address the issues raised by the claimant and the responsible entity adopts the necessary measures to align its actions with the requirements set forth in data protection regulations, this Agency may decide not to continue the procedure following the admission of the claim, as established in Title VIII of the LOPDGDD, which contemplates the possibility of conducting preliminary actions and initiating a sanctioning procedure, if appropriate.  
This occurred in the aforementioned precedent (E/10004/2020), in which the filing of the claim was agreed upon, considering the response given by the entity AVANT.  
As stated in the aforementioned resolution for filing actions, dated 25/02/2021, in its response to the transfer procedure, dated 18/02/2021, AVANT informed that it had made modifications to its privacy policies to inform about the transfer of user data to third-party collaborating companies with which it shares a platform.  
Similarly, it reported that it was in the process of incorporating a mechanism in the user registration process in its mobile application to collect their consent for obtaining their data from other collaborative mobility platforms, with the aim of facilitating the registration process.  
Once the reasons presented were analyzed, it was considered that the initiation of a sanctioning procedure was not warranted, as the processing of the claim in accordance with the provisions of Article 65.4 of the LOPDGDD led to the resolution of the issues raised, without the need to clarify administrative responsibilities within the framework of a sanctioning procedure. This is without prejudice to the Agency, applying the investigative and corrective powers it holds, being able to carry out subsequent actions related to the data processing referred to in the claim.
