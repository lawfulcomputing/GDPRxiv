LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
In accordance with the functions conferred to each supervisory authority by Article 57.1 a), f), and h) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative actions.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: “The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures.”  

II  
Possible infringements  
On November 6, 2020, a complaint was received by this Agency directed against the entities TUCYCLE, AVANT, OIZ, and IBERSCOT for the transfer of personal data of users who register in the corresponding mobile "apps" to access the vehicle rental service. This complaint warns that when a person registers in one of the "apps" of these entities and subsequently installs another one, the second recognizes the user and reuses the data provided by the other "collaborating platform," with the aim of "streamlining the process."  
The same complaint highlighted that none of these entities informed the user that their personal data was shared with third companies, which were shared among the mentioned entities.  

This complaint led to the actions indicated with the number E/10004/2020, in which, by resolution dated February 25, 2021, it was agreed to archive the respective complaint as the initiation of a sanctioning procedure was not warranted since the complaint had been addressed.  
The archiving of actions was agreed upon after following the procedures regulated in Article 65.4 of the LOPDGDD, provided to evaluate whether or not to admit the complaint for processing. This article states the following:

According to the above, the indicated mechanism prior to the admission of claims submitted to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection consists of forwarding them to the Data Protection Officers designated by the data controllers or processors, or to them when they have not designated any, so that they proceed to analyze the claims and respond within one month.  
Thus, when the actions carried out in view of the claim address the issues raised by the claiming party and the responsible entity adopts the necessary measures to align its actions with the requirements set forth in the data protection regulations, the AEPD may decide not to continue the procedure following the admission of the claim, established in Title VIII of the LOPDGDD, which contemplates the possibility of carrying out preliminary actions and initiating a sanctioning procedure, if applicable.
