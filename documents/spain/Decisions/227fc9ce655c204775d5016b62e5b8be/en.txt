RESOLUTION OF RECONSIDERATION APPEAL  
Examined the reconsideration appeal filed by GESTERPOOL, S.L. (hereinafter, the appellant) against the resolution issued by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection dated April 4, 2023, and based on the following  

FACTS  
FIRST: On April 4, 2023, a resolution was issued by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection in file EXP202105473, whereby GESTERPOOL, S.L. (hereinafter GESTERPOOL) was imposed a fine of 10,000 euros (ten thousand euros) for an infringement of Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), classified under Article 83.4.a) of the same Regulation, and qualified as serious for the purposes of prescription under Article 73.l) of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD); and a fine of 5,000 euros (five thousand euros) for an infringement of Article 58.1 of the GDPR, classified under Article 83.5.e) of the aforementioned GDPR, and qualified as very serious under Article 72.1.ñ) and o) of the LOPDGDD.  
This resolution, which was notified to the appellant on April 5, 2023, was issued following the processing of the corresponding sanctioning procedure, in accordance with the provisions of the LOPDGDD, and subsidiarily under Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), regarding the processing of sanctioning procedures.  

SECOND: As proven facts of the aforementioned sanctioning procedure, PS/00668/2022, the following was recorded:  
“FIRST: The entity Naturgy entered into a contract with the entity GESTERPOOL, dated January 1, 2021, under which the latter entity was obliged to provide advisory and commercial and technical support services for customer acquisition, including conducting telemarketing actions, intervening in these actions under the condition of data processor.  
Both entities signed an Addendum, dated July 1, 2021, which modified some clauses of the Contract; and a Data Processing Agreement, also dated July 1, 2021.  
All these documents stipulate that GESTERPOOL may not subcontract the rights and obligations arising from the contract without the express, prior, and written authorization of Naturgy. In the Data Processing Agreement, it is indicated that GESTERPOOL must notify Naturgy, with a minimum notice of one month, the details of the third party with which it intends to subcontract and the specific description of the service that will be the subject of the subcontracting, so that it may oppose.  

SECOND: The complainant has stated that, on November 2, 2021, they received a phone call on behalf of Naturgy in which discounts on electricity and gas supplies were offered.  
THIRD: On November 2, 2021, the complainant digitally signed a “Dual Supply and Services Contract” formalized in their name, which includes the direct debit order. The digital signature of this document is certified by a duly registered third-party entity in the Network Operators Registry, which acts in its capacity as a Trusted Third Party.  
FOURTH: The complainant processed a withdrawal of the contract with Naturgy, which was attended to by that entity on November 4, 2021, without having activated the services.  
FIFTH: Naturgy has stated that the complainant's contracting of its services was carried out with the intervention of Callsisten Energy, S.L., an entity subcontracted by GESTERPOOL without the consent or knowledge of Naturgy.  
SIXTH: Naturgy has declared to this Agency that it has revoked all accesses of GESTERPOOL to the contracting tool and has terminated the contractual relationship with it, for not having followed its instructions and for breaching the obligations imposed to ensure quality in contracting.  
SEVENTH: The entity Telefónica Móviles España, S.A.U., operator of the complainant's line ***PHONE.1, confirmed the receipt on this mobile line of a call made from line ***PHONE.2 on November 2, 2021.  
EIGHTH: The entity Orange Espagne, S.A.U., operator of line ***PHONE.2, reported that the ownership of this line corresponds to the entity Callsisten Energy, S.L., with NIF B01866565. It also provided details of a call made from this line on November 2, 2021, to the complainant's mobile line, with a duration of 1614 seconds.  
NINTH: On August 19, 2022, the Inspection Services of the AEPD sent a request for information to the entity GESTERPOOL, which was delivered on the 25th of the same month. Due to the lack of response, a new request was sent on September 30, 2022, which was also delivered to GESTERPOOL on October 10, 2022, without this entity providing any response.”

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
As a preliminary matter, it should be noted that the new document submitted by the appellant has not been classified as a motion for reconsideration. However, section 2 of article 115 of the LPACAP establishes that the error or absence of classification of the appeal by the appellant shall not be an obstacle to its processing, provided that its true nature is deduced; therefore, the submitted document will be processed as a motion for reconsideration.  
In this case, according to what has been stated, it is clear that the document is submitted by GESTERPOOL as a response to the resolution, expressly indicating that the established deadlines are respected.  

The Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve this appeal, in accordance with the provisions of article 123 of the LPACAP and article 48.1 of the LOPDGDD.

VI  
Sanction  
The violation of the provisions established in Article 28 of the GDPR may be sanctioned with a fine of up to €10,000,000 or, in the case of a company, with a fine of an amount equivalent to a maximum of 2% of the total annual global turnover of the previous financial year, opting for the higher amount, in accordance with Article 83.4 of the GDPR.  
The violation of the provisions established in Article 58.1 of the GDPR may be sanctioned with a fine of up to €20,000,000 or, in the case of a company, with a fine of an amount equivalent to a maximum of 4% of the total annual global turnover of the previous financial year, opting for the higher amount, in accordance with Article 83.5 of the GDPR.  
With respect to the indicated violations, considering the facts presented, it is deemed that the sanction to be imposed is an administrative fine.  
The fine imposed must be, in each individual case, effective, proportionate, and dissuasive, in accordance with the provisions of Article 83.1 of the GDPR. Thus, the condition of microenterprise of the party being claimed and its turnover is considered (as stated in the proceedings (...)).

Adoption of Measures  
Once the infringement has been confirmed, it is necessary to determine whether it is appropriate to impose on GESTERPOOL the adoption of adequate measures to align its actions with the regulations mentioned in this act, in accordance with the provisions of Article 58.2 d) of the GDPR, which states that each supervisory authority may “order the controller or processor to ensure that the processing operations comply with the provisions of this Regulation, where appropriate, in a specific manner and within a specified time frame…”.  
In this case, the entity Naturgy has declared that it has terminated the contractual relationship it had entered into with GESTERPOOL, revoking all access to the contracting tool, due to GESTERPOOL not following its instructions and breaching the imposed obligations.  
Based on this, it is not appropriate to impose on GESTERPOOL the obligation to adopt additional measures beyond the monetary fine.  

III  
Conclusion  
As established in the contested resolution, GESTERPOOL is sanctioned for an infringement of Article 28 of the GDPR, for having used the services of an external entity, as a subcontractor, for the execution of the services that GESTERPOOL was obliged to provide to Naturgy under the contract signed by both entities, which did not contemplate the possibility of such subcontracting. The hiring of a third entity by GESTERPOOL was carried out without the proper authorization from Naturgy.  
On the other hand, a fine is imposed on GESTERPOOL for failing to respond to the information requests made to it by the Inspection Services of this Agency on two occasions. Both requests were duly delivered to GESTERPOOL, which did not provide any response to them.  
None of these issues are referenced by GESTERPOOL in its appeal, in which it does acknowledge the involvement of that third entity in the contracting of services for Naturgy by the claiming party. In this document, that entity merely states that there is a recording of this contracting and that everything was done with the knowledge of Naturgy, but it does not provide any evidence to support this.  
It should be noted, on the other hand, that GESTERPOOL did not submit any written allegations or evidence during the processing of the sanctioning procedure that led to the contested resolution, despite being granted time to do so on two occasions, due to the opening of the procedure and upon being notified of the proposed resolution prepared by the instructor.  
Consequently, in the present appeal for reconsideration, the appellant has not provided new facts or legal arguments that would allow for a reconsideration of the validity of the contested resolution.
