RESOLUTION OF SANCTIONING PROCEDURE  
From the procedure instructed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

BACKGROUND  
FIRST: Ms. A.A.A. (hereinafter, the complainant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection on 08/11/2019. The complaint is directed against Ms. B.B.B. with NIF ***NIF.1 (hereinafter, the respondent) for actions that could violate data protection legislation. The reasons on which the complaint is based are as follows:  
“FIRST. On October 9, 2019, she submitted through the electronic headquarters of the Town Hall of Castropodame a document addressed to the Mayor regarding her working conditions and the payroll for the month of September. Entry 2019-E-RE-198.  
SECOND. On the afternoon of the same day, she became aware that Ms. B.B.B., Secretary of the Town Hall, had posted a photo of the aforementioned document on her personal WhatsApp status.  
THIRD. This fact was reported at the Civil Guard barracks in Bembibre on October 9, 2019, as it could be considered, according to Articles 413 to 418 of the Penal Code, a crime of breach of trust in the custody of documents and violation of secrets, in addition to infringing on her privacy by using her position as a public servant to prevail over her role.”  
Along with the complaint, a copy of the report drawn up by the Main Post of the Civil Guard in Bembibre on 09/10/2019 and a screenshot of the image of the document uploaded to the respondent's WhatsApp are provided.  
SECOND: Prior to this complaint, on 11/10/2019, this Agency had received a letter from the Mayor-President of the Town Hall of Castropodame informing it of the following facts: “Ms. A.A.A. submitted application number 2019-E-RE-198 on October 9, 2019, as a labor employee of the Town Hall of Castropodame, regarding the work she is enduring due to the absence of the other administrative assistants and that she has not been compensated €200 from the payroll for the month of September, which was agreed upon in a negotiation table for performing tasks above her category that have been carried out for years without compensation.  
On the afternoon of October 9, 2019, this Mayor received a call from the worker A.A.A., in which she asked her to verify the WhatsApp status of the Secretary, Ms. B.B.B., because she does not have her phone number and has been informed that Ms. B.B.B. has published a photograph of the document submitted by Ms. A.A.A.  
Upon verifying Ms. B.B.B.'s WhatsApp status, a first status was observed that said “calentito calentito” and a second status in which the document that Ms. A.A.A. submitted that same day through the Electronic Headquarters of the Town Hall of Castropodame was indeed displayed.  
That same afternoon, the Civil Guard barracks in Bembibre requested my presence as a witness to the complaint filed by Ms. A.A.A., where I was questioned and informed that the facts would be investigated.  
Ms. A.A.A. again submitted a request in the municipal entry register number 2019-E-RE-214 on October 10, 2019, informing the Town Hall of Castropodame of the complaint filed against Ms. B.B.B. for using a personal document to publish it openly on her WhatsApp status, requesting that this entity take the necessary measures.  
On October 11, 2019, the worker Ms. A.A.A. was asked to present the complaint made to the Civil Guard, which was registered on the same date with registration number 2019-E-RE-2016.”  
The aforementioned letter was accompanied by the following documents:  
 Letter submitted by the complainant to the Town Hall of Castropodame on 10/10/2019 informing of the facts.  
 Letter submitted by the complainant to the Town Hall of Castropodame on 09/10/2019.

 Copy of the report drawn up at the Main Post of the Civil Guard in Bembibre on 09/10/2019.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and the guarantee of digital rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states that: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, and by the...

II  
In the case examined, the facts revealed consist of the publication on the WhatsApp social network of a written request from the claimant addressed to the City Hall regarding her working conditions, which could violate the regulations on the protection of personal data, the principle of confidentiality of data regulated in Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR.  
It should be noted that upon notification of the initiation agreement, the claimant, by means of a letter dated 28/06/2020, denied the facts and communicated the existence of Preliminary Proceedings, Summary Procedure 0000443/2019 before the Court of First Instance and Instruction No. 8, of Ponferrada (León), requesting the suspension of the procedure due to the existence of criminal prejudiciality regarding the same facts.  
A request was sent to the Court asking for information in order to communicate its opinion regarding the existence of identity of subject, fact, and basis between the alleged administrative infringement and the corresponding criminal infringement, to proceed, if applicable, to the immediate suspension of the initiated procedure.  
To which the Court informed on 08/10/2020 that "there is identity of subject, fact, and basis between the alleged administrative infringement and the corresponding criminal infringement, with the present proceedings currently in process as of this date."  
On 17/12/2021, the same Court communicated that: "on 27/10/2021, an order was issued to continue the proceedings through the procedures of the summary criminal procedure for an alleged crime against personal privacy and revelation of secrets under Articles 197.3 of the Penal Code in relation to Article 198 of the Penal Code, against which an appeal has been filed."  
Subsequently, on 18/07/2022, it indicated that "the present procedure has been sent for trial to the Provincial Court of León where it has been registered as Summary Procedure No. 51/22, to which any inquiries regarding the same should now be directed."  
Section 3 of the Provincial Court of León, in a letter dated 30/01/2023, communicated the holding of an oral trial for 15/03/2023.  
Finally, Section 3 of the Provincial Court of León, on 03/04/2023, Summary Procedure No. ***PROCEDIMIENTO.3 issued Judgment ***SENTENCIA.1, with the following ruling:  
"That we must ACQUIT and WE ACQUIT the claimant of all criminal liability arising from the crime of discovery and revelation of secrets, which motivated the initiation against her of the present criminal case, declaring the procedural costs ex officio."

Against the same, an Appeal could be filed before the same Court, to the Civil and Criminal Chamber of the Superior Court of Justice, within ten days following the last notification of the judgment, without any record of it having been filed.  
The proven facts will bind the AEPD regarding a possible sanctioning procedure, in accordance with the provisions of Article 77.4 of Law 39/2015.  
Article 77.4 of the LPACAP states that: "In sanctioning procedures, the facts declared proven by final criminal judicial resolutions will bind the Public Administrations regarding the sanctioning procedures they process."  
In the Proven Facts of the aforementioned judgment, it is stated that:  
“(...)  
IT IS NOT PROVEN that Ms. B.B.B. generated a status on her WhatsApp social network profile attaching as a document the letter that hours earlier Ms. A.A.A. had submitted to the City Hall, preceded by the expressions "hot, hot," existing between the parties a context of conflict."  
And in the Final Paragraph of Ground IV:  
"For all these reasons, the Chamber, evaluating the evidence presented in the oral trial under Article 741 of the Criminal Procedure Law, reaches the logical, precise, and direct conclusion that what happened is faithfully reflected in the account contained in the proven facts of this resolution, so in the absence of evidence and attending to the principle of presumption of innocence, it is appropriate to agree to the free acquittal of B.B.B. from the crime of discovery and revelation of secrets under Articles 197.1 and 3 of the Penal Code in relation to Article 198 of the same Legal Text for which she was accused by the private prosecution and by the Public Prosecutor's Office, all this as the presumption of innocence has not been destroyed by sufficient evidence to that effect."  
Therefore, in accordance with the applicable legislation and evaluated the criteria for the graduation of the sanctions whose existence has been accredited,  
The Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:  
FIRST: TO FILE against Ms. B.B.B., with NIF ***NIF.1, for infringement of Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR, classified under Article 83.5 of the GDPR.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to Ms. B.B.B.
