FACTS  
FIRST: On September 28, 2023, a complaint was filed with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against VODAFONE ESPAÑA, S.A.U., with NIF A80907397 (hereinafter, the complained party).  
The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
Commercial calls on September 26, 2023, to line ***PHONE.1 registered in the Robinson List, made from line ***PHONE.2 where the interlocutor indicates calling on behalf of the complained party and the complainant indicates being registered in the Robinson List and their opposition to receiving these calls.  
Along with the notification, the following is provided:  
- Certificate of registration in the Robinson List since 09/16/2022,  
- Invoice proving ownership of the line with ***COMPANY.1,  
- Screenshot and recording of the call.  

SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and the guarantee of digital rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the aforementioned complaint was forwarded to the complained party/ALIAS, for them to analyze it and inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in data protection regulations.  
The complaint was forwarded to the Association for the Self-Regulation of Commercial Communication on October 11, 2023, and a response was received on November 23, 2023, informing that the complainant has expressed their disagreement with the response from the complained party, which states:  
“(...) According to the available information... the CNMC, the calling number previously indicated belongs to the following operator; COLT TECHNOLOGY SERVICES, S.A. UNIPERSONAL. Vodafone did not provide your data.  
Furthermore, after conducting the necessary checks, we have verified that the complainant's telephone lines... are listed in the official Robinson list of ADigital... and in Vodafone's internal Robinson list... they have been included as of October 20, 2022, and October 17, 2023.  
From Vodafone, it has been checked whether the indicated calling number in your writing appears in our database with the telephone numbers that our collaborators use to make solicitation calls. To this end, we have verified that it does not appear in our database of numbers associated with our collaborators who make solicitation calls on behalf of Vodafone. Therefore, it does not seem possible to relate the receipt of the calls that you highlight... in your complaint with Vodafone.”

I  
Competence  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 114.1.b) of Law 11/2022, of June 28, General Telecommunications Law (hereinafter LGTel) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative proceedings.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states that: “The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection will be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued in its development and, as long as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures.”  
Finally, the Fourth Additional Provision “Procedure in relation to the competencies attributed to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection by other laws” establishes that: “The provisions in Title VIII and its development regulations shall apply to the procedures that the Spanish Agency for Data Protection must process in exercising the competencies attributed to it by other laws.”  
II  
Final Provision Sixth, Section 2 of LGTel 11/2022  
“The right of end users not to receive unwanted calls for commercial communication purposes as provided in Article 66.1.b) shall come into force one year from the publication of this law in the "Official State Gazette." Until that time, end users of publicly available interpersonal communication services based on numbering may continue to exercise the right to object to receiving unwanted calls for commercial communication purposes made through systems other than those established in Article 66.1.a) and to be informed of this right.”  
The LGTel is applicable to this complaint as the call occurred on September 26, 2023, and the LGTel was published on June 28, 2022, coming into force on June 28, 2023.  
III  
Conclusion  
The investigations carried out by this Agency indicate that the claimed number belongs to MIDAS, a company located in Peru. Information requests have been sent both electronically and by postal mail, and no response has been received to any of the requests sent.  
It should be noted that the LGT does not confer the Spanish Agency for Data Protection with jurisdiction over a company located in Peru.
