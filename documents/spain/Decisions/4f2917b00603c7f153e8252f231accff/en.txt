The reasons on which the complaint is based are as follows:  
The claimant contacted BLU MANAGEMENT, which is a recruitment agency, to apply for a job around May 2021, and had a telephone interview with B.B.B. (hereinafter, the consultant of BLU MANAGEMENT).  
Subsequently, on July 26, 2021, the claimant received a series of messages via WhatsApp (…), where the claimant had worked.  
The person who sent these WhatsApp messages indicated that the one who provided her contact details was the consultant of BLU MANAGEMENT.  
The claimant indicates that she had not given permission to BLU MANAGEMENT to share her data with third parties. For this reason, she contacted BLU MANAGEMENT on July 26, 27, and 30, 2021, having received a response from them, but considers that what they have done is not sufficient to address these facts.

II  
Preliminary Issues  
In the present case, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 4.1 and 4.2 of the GDPR, it is established that there has been a processing of personal data, given that BLU MANAGEMENT collects and retains, among others, the following personal data of individuals: name and phone number, among other processing activities.  
BLU MANAGEMENT carries out this activity in its capacity as data controller, as it determines the purposes and means of such activity, pursuant to Article 4.7 of the GDPR. Furthermore, this is a cross-border processing, as BLU MANAGEMENT is established in Spain, although it provides services to other countries in the European Union.  
Article 4, paragraph 12 of the GDPR broadly defines "personal data security breaches" (hereinafter, security breach) as "all breaches of security that lead to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, or alteration of personal data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed, or to the unauthorized communication or access to such data."  
In this case, there is a personal data security breach under the circumstances mentioned above, categorized as a confidentiality breach, as a friend of BLU MANAGEMENT allegedly used their position to obtain the phone number of the complainant and communicated it to a third party, who then contacted the complainant via WhatsApp.  
Within the principles of processing provided for in Article 5 of the GDPR, the integrity and confidentiality of personal data are guaranteed in paragraph 1.f) of Article 5 of the GDPR.
