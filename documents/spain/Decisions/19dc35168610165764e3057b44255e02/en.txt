BACKGROUND  
FIRST: A.A.A. (*hereinafter, the claimant) on February 24, 2023, filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection. The complaint is directed against AYUNTAMIENTO DE RABANERA DEL PINAR with NIF P0931200J (hereinafter, AYTO.). The reasons on which the complaint is based are as follows:  
The claimant states that the AYTO., through a Decree from the Mayor that has not been approved by the municipal plenary, has installed a video surveillance camera at the outer vertex of the municipal fronton in the access square to the municipality, oriented towards the public road, with its images being broadcast in real-time via YOUTUBE.  
No document is provided along with the notification, but there is an indication that access is made from the YouTube platform by typing "Rabanera del Pinar" in its search bar.  

SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the complaint was forwarded to the AYTO., so that it could analyze it and inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in the data protection regulations.  
The forwarding, which was carried out in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP) by means of electronic notification, was not collected by the responsible party within the availability period, being understood as rejected in accordance with the provisions of Article 43.2 of the LPACAP on March 21, 2023, as stated in the certificate included in the file.  
Although the notification was validly carried out by electronic means, considering the procedure completed in accordance with the provisions of Article 41.5 of the LPACAP, an informational copy was sent by postal mail, which was duly notified on March 29, 2023. In this notification, the obligation to communicate electronically with the Administration was reminded, and the means of access to such notifications were provided, reiterating that, henceforth, notifications would be made exclusively by electronic means.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and the guarantee of digital rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
The image is a personal data  
The physical image of a person, according to Article 4.1 of the GDPR, is personal data and its protection, therefore, is subject to this Regulation. In Article 4.2 of the GDPR, the concept of "processing" of personal data is defined.  
“1) ‘personal data’: any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (‘the data subject’); an identifiable natural person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier, such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier, or one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity of that natural person;  
2) ‘processing’: any operation or set of operations performed on personal data or sets of personal data, whether by automated means or not, such as collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation or alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination, or otherwise making available, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure, or destruction;”  
Therefore, based on the definition transcribed above, even if the recording of images does not occur, the real-time reproduction, as it could be...

IV  
Before addressing the substance of the matter, it is necessary to clarify that one thing is the installation of video surveillance cameras for security reasons and to protect the integrity of individuals against potential uncivil or criminal acts, which are subject to the necessary authorization and compliance with the current data protection regulations; and another thing is the installation of a device(s) for real-time viewing of the town's panorama for meteorological purposes.  
The facts are specified in the presence of a device for real-time broadcasting of a panorama of the town, which could affect the rights of third parties if misaligned.  
Article 22, section 1 of the LOPDGDD (LO 3/2018, December 5) provides the following:  
“Natural or legal persons, public or private, may carry out the processing of images through camera or video camera systems for the purpose of preserving the safety of individuals and property, as well as their facilities.”  
Recently, there has been an increasing use of new technologies by Town Halls for purposes different from the initial nature of video surveillance cameras, which is none other than the protection of safety (for example, monitoring beach capacity, panoramic views of emblematic neighborhoods in cities, traffic control, or providing weather updates, etc.).

Article 1, paragraph 1 of the GDPR states the following:  
"This Regulation applies to the total or partial automated processing of personal data, as well as to the non-automated processing of personal data contained in or intended to be included in a filing system" (*the bold text belongs to this agency).  
The purpose of the so-called meteorological cameras (Webcams) is solely to inform about the weather conditions or occasionally to provide an overview of the state of the main accesses to cities, without processing data associated with identified or identifiable natural persons.  
To comply with current legislation, it will be sufficient that they do not disclose personal data of natural persons in their broadcasts or affect the privacy of nearby homes (for example, affecting the interior or windows of the same).  
Regarding the legitimizing basis, in the case of installations affecting public areas (streets, squares, etc.), they must comply with the provisions of Law 4/1997, of August 4, which regulates the use of video cameras by the Security Forces and Corps in public places, in such a way that authorization from the Government Delegate would be required at the request of the Mayor or the competent Security Councilor, although this is not the case as it is a camera with meteorological purposes.  
The important aspect in this case is to analyze whether the device in question can identify a natural person. If the images are captured with a resolution that allows for the identification of a person, that person would be identifiable and would fall within the scope of the law; otherwise, they would not.  
Analyzing the initial images provided by the respondent, in accordance with what was stated in the complaint, the device is installed at a height of 7 meters, making it impossible to identify any natural person, who would appear as mere dots.  
The majority of the image captured with the device's wide-angle lens is of the sky of the locality, in line with the stated purpose of the system, which is to provide a service of public interest regarding the weather conditions in the locality.  
Additionally, measures have been taken to preserve the privacy of the nearby area during the processing of the procedure, pixelating part of the device's lens to further ensure its main function, which is to provide an overview of the town.  
Finally, it has not been possible to determine that any natural person's rights have been affected, as no objective evidence has been provided with the complaint, nor has it been possible to specify such an aspect during the investigation phase.
