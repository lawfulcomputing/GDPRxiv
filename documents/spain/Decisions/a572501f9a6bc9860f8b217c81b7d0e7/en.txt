1. Without prejudice to what is provided in the fourteenth additional provision and in the fourth transitional provision, the Organic Law 15/1999, of December 13, on the Protection of Personal Data is repealed.  
It is established, in its sixteenth final provision, that: “This organic law will enter into force the day after its publication in the Official State Bulletin.”  
Since the publication of the LOPDGDD was on 06/12/18, its entry into force occurred on 07/12/18, thus repealing the Organic Law 15/1999, of December 13, on the Protection of Personal Data (LOPD) from that date.  
Therefore, in the case at hand, the lack of information in accordance with the current regulations regarding the processing of personal data when personal data was obtained from the users of the website and the impossibility of accessing the “Privacy Policy” during the time the link was non-operational, constitutes a violation of Article 13 of the GDPR.  
III.2  
Administrative infringement  
It is undisputed in this case that the respondent is the owner and responsible party of the website www.cementeriodemarmotas.com and, therefore, the responsible party for the data processing obtained through it.  
In relation to said website, it was found in this case, on one hand, the lack of information in accordance with the current regulations regarding the processing of personal data since the information available through the link in the information banner on the main page referred to the repealed LOPD and, on the other hand, the impossibility of accessing the “Privacy Policy” of the website since its link was inoperative.  
It is considered that these facts violate what is established in Article 13 of the GDPR, thus constituting an infringement classified under Article 83.5 of the GDPR, which states the following:  
“Violations of the following provisions shall be sanctioned, in accordance with paragraph 2, with administrative fines of up to 20,000,000 EUR or, in the case of an enterprise, an amount equivalent to 4% at most of the total annual global turnover of the previous financial year, opting for the higher amount: (…) b) the rights of the data subjects pursuant to Articles 12 to 22; (…)”.

III.-3  
Sanction  
Article 58.2 of the GDPR establishes:  
“Each supervisory authority shall have all of the following corrective powers indicated below: (...) d) to order the controller or processor to bring the processing operations into compliance with the provisions of this Regulation, when appropriate, in a specific manner and within a specified timeframe; (...) i) to impose an administrative fine in accordance with Article 83, in addition to or instead of the measures mentioned in this paragraph, depending on the circumstances of each particular case.”  
According to the provisions of Article 83.2 of the GDPR, the measure provided for in Article 58.2.d) of the aforementioned Regulation is compatible with the sanction consisting of an administrative fine.  
With respect to the infringement of Article 13 of the GDPR, it is considered that the sanction to be imposed is an administrative fine.  
The fine imposed must be, in each individual case, effective, proportionate, and dissuasive, in accordance with what is established in Article 83.1 of the GDPR.  
In order to determine the administrative fine to be imposed, the provisions of Article 83.2 of the GDPR must be observed, which states the following:  
“2. Administrative fines shall be imposed, depending on the circumstances of each individual case, as an additional or substitute measure for the measures contemplated in Article 58, paragraph 2, letters a) to h) and j). In this case, considering the circumstances expressed regarding the defects observed in the operation of the website, from the perspective of data protection regulations, and its subsequent adaptation to the regulations, it is not deemed appropriate to agree on any measures.  
IV.-1  
On the Cookie Policy of the website  
In the verification carried out by this Agency on 24/04/23, it was found that when entering the website www.cementeriodemarmotas.com for the first time, without accepting cookies or taking any action on the page, cookies that were not technical or necessary were being used: three performance cookies: _gid; _ga_1XZJN7SWGN and _ga, and one targeting cookie: _gat_gtag_UA_59353212_5.  
It was also verified that it was not possible to reject cookies that were not technical or necessary or to manage their use granularly through a cookie control panel, as this did not exist.  
It was also noted that the cookie information banner that appeared on the main page did not identify the purposes for which the detected cookies were used.  
Additionally, it was verified that there was no link allowing the user to redirect to the “Cookie Policy” of the website, as the existing link on the main page was inactive.  
Therefore, although it has been confirmed, in the checks conducted by this Agency on 13/09/23 and 20/11/23, that the website controller has modified the cookie policy, this does not eliminate the non-compliance that was established in the verification carried out on 24/04/23, constituting a violation of the provisions of Article 22.2 of the LSSI.  
IV.-2  
Administrative Infringement  
From the deficiencies detected regarding the cookie policy on the website in question: the use of third-party cookies that were not technical or necessary; the impossibility of rejecting cookies and the impossibility of accessing information in the “cookie policy,” during the time the website was not adapted, constitute the commission of the infringement of Article 22.2 of the LSSI, as it establishes that:  
“Service providers may use storage and data retrieval devices on the terminal equipment of the recipients, provided that they have given their consent after being provided with clear and complete information about their use, in particular, about the purposes of the data processing, in accordance with the provisions of Organic Law 15/1999, of December 13, on the protection of personal data.
