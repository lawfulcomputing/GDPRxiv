FACTS  
FIRST: On 21/08/2024, a resolution was issued by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection in file EXP202306308, whereby its first provision stated: "Declare the expiration of the procedure initiated against A.A.A., with NIF ***NIF.1, for an infringement of Article 5.1.c) of the GDPR." This resolution, which was notified to the appellant on ***DATE.1, in accordance with the provisions of Article 44 of the Common Administrative Procedure Law of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), was issued following the processing of the corresponding sanctioning procedure, in accordance with the provisions of Organic Law 3/2018, of 5/12, on Personal Data Protection and the guarantee of digital rights (LOPDGDD), and subsidiarily under the aforementioned LPACAP, regarding the processing of sanctioning procedures.  
SECOND: The appellant submitted on 18/10/2024, to this Spanish Agency for Data Protection, a recourse for reconsideration, fundamentally basing it on the following arguments:  
1) It states that the resolution of the sanctioning file 202306308 was notified to it in the BOE on ***DATE.1, being the resolution for the archiving of the sanctioning procedure issued on 21/08/2024, agreeing to archive the file due to its expiration after the established time had elapsed without a resolution being issued.  
2) It considers that the "alleged infringement-public disclosure-" occurred on ***DATE.2, having passed more than three years without the AEPD resolving within the legal deadline," therefore it considers that according to Article 30 of Law 40/2015, of 1/10, on the Legal Regime of the Public Sector (LRJSP hereinafter) the infringement has prescribed. "The video subject to the infringement was removed from my profile (...) on ***DATE.3." Given the time elapsed, it understands that the possible legal relevance of the case has ceased, as there is no risk of re-victimization towards the affected party, seeking to defend the victim from the actions of the Prosecutor. After this time, the infringement has ceased to have any relevant legal or social effect, "which invalidates the initial arguments of the AEPD."  
3) It considers the proposed resolution issued on 04/06/2024 as arbitrary and disproportionate. Furthermore, it believes that the dismissal of the allegations presented against it is based on reasoning that does not correspond to reality.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
The Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve this appeal, in accordance with the provisions of Article 123 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP) and Article 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD).  
II  
Response to the allegations presented  
Among the general principles of administrative appeals under the LPACAP, Article 112 "general principles," of section 1, states:  
"1. Against resolutions and procedural acts, if the latter directly or indirectly decide the substance of the matter, determine the impossibility of continuing the procedure, produce defenselessness or irreparable harm to legitimate rights and interests, the interested parties may file appeals for reconsideration and optional appeals for review, which may be based on any of the grounds for nullity or annulment provided for in Articles 47 and 48 of this Law.  
Opposition to other procedural acts may be raised by the interested parties for consideration in the resolution that concludes the procedure."  
Article 123 of the LPACAP indicates that "1. Administrative acts that conclude the administrative route may be optionally appealed for reconsideration before the same body that issued them or be directly challenged before the contentious-administrative jurisdiction."  
We must begin by indicating that the resolution against which the appeal for reconsideration is said to be filed declared the expiration of the procedure. Nevertheless, the appellant has not referred to any aspect regarding the content of the resolution that is the subject of this appeal for reconsideration. Instead, they have made general allegations against the proposal or against the initiation agreement and considerations regarding a possible exemption from their future liability, in case they could be held liable, as they consider the facts that allegedly constituted the imputation of the infringement of the procedure whose expiration was declared to be time-barred.  
The appeal is thus limited to expressing disagreements with the content of the initiation agreement or the proposal for resolution, some of which have already been expressed during the processing. On the other hand, it does not express any aspect of the resolution that decrees the expiration of the procedure.  
In light of the above, it is worth recalling that all prior decisions adopted in the phases of processing the administrative procedure, distinct and prior to its final resolution, are considered procedural acts, and in this case, the proposal or the initiation agreement that precedes it are acts prepared during the processing of the sanctioning administrative procedure before the final resolution is issued.  
Given that the contested resolution does not contemplate proven facts, nor does it adopt the proposal, limiting itself to the relationship of the facts, arguments raised in the appeal against content that is not part of the contested resolution cannot be accepted.  
III  
Conclusion  
Consequently, in the present appeal for reconsideration, the appellant has not provided new facts or legal arguments that would allow for a reconsideration of the validity of the contested resolution.  
In view of the cited provisions and others of general application, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:  
FIRST: TO DISMISS the appeal for reconsideration filed by A.A.A. against the resolution of this Spanish Agency for Data Protection issued on 21/08/2024, in file EXP202306308.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to A.A.A.  
THIRD: In accordance with the provisions of Article 50 of the LOPDGDD, this Resolution will be made public once it has been notified to the interested parties.  
Against this resolution, which concludes the administrative route in accordance with Article 48.6 of the LOPDGDD, and in accordance with the provisions of Article 123 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (LPACAP), the interested parties may file a contentious-administrative appeal before the Contentious-Administrative Chamber of the National Court, in accordance with the provisions of Article 25 and paragraph 5 of the fourth additional provision of Law 29/1998, of July 13, regulating the Contentious-Administrative Jurisdiction, within two months from the day following the notification of this act, as provided in Article 46.1 of the aforementioned Law.  
Finally, it is noted that in accordance with the provisions of Article 90.3 a) LPACAP, the firm resolution in the administrative route may be provisionally suspended if the interested party expresses their intention to file a contentious-administrative appeal. If this is the case, the interested party must formally communicate this fact in writing.
