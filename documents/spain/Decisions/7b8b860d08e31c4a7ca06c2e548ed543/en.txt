SANCTIONING PROCEDURE RESOLUTION  
From the procedure initiated by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

BACKGROUND  
FIRST: A.A.A. (hereinafter, the complainant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection on July 29, 2022. The complaint is directed against B.B.B. with NIF ***NIF.1 (hereinafter, the respondent). The grounds for the complaint are as follows:  
The complainant states that the respondent is responsible for a surveillance camera that is directed towards the public road, without prior administrative authorization for doing so and without being properly signposted with the required informative signs of the surveillance area.  
Images of the camera's location are provided.  

SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the complaint was forwarded to the respondent for analysis and to inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in data protection regulations.  
The notification, which was carried out in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), was received on September 14, 2022, as evidenced by the acknowledgment of receipt in the file.  
On October 28, 2022, this Agency received a response indicating:  
- That the respondent is responsible for the surveillance system.  
- That the surveillance system consists of two cameras.  
- That a photograph of the sign warning of the existence of a surveillance area is attached.  
- That the maintenance of the system has been contracted with the company ***EMPRESA.1.  
- That the recording of images is retained for 7 days.  

THIRD: On October 29, 2022, in accordance with Article 65 of the LOPDGDD, the complaint filed by the complainant was admitted for processing.  

FOURTH: On March 16, 2023, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection decided to initiate sanctioning proceedings against the respondent for the alleged infringement of Article 5.1.c) of the GDPR and Article 13 of the GDPR, classified under Article 83.5 of the GDPR.

FIFTH: The aforementioned initiation agreement was notified in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), and the respondent submitted a written statement in which, in summary, stated:  
- That the images captured by the "patio 1" camera are part of the property of the respondent and do not, at any time, affect the promenade, beach, or include buildings.  
In this regard, this Agency notes that, after reviewing the documents in the file, mainly the images corresponding to the area captured by the cameras, it is not observed that they excessively affect public areas or adjacent buildings in a manner contrary to data protection regulations. Therefore, the allegation is accepted, and the infringement attributed under Article 5.1.c of the GDPR is archived.  
- That they do not agree with the attribution of the infringement under Article 13 of the GDPR, as the necessary and regulatory information is provided, being the sign supplied by the company responsible for the maintenance of the surveillance system.  
In this regard, this Agency points out that, although the photo provided with the response to the notification does indeed show a sign warning of the surveillance area, it does not meet the requirements established in Article 22.4 of the LOPDGDD, as it does not include either the identity of the responsible party or the possibility of exercising the rights provided for in Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR.  
- That currently, there are no cameras installed on the property due to the ongoing works being carried out on the building. This fact is evidenced by the photographs attached as annexes.  
In this regard, this Agency notes that, although there are currently no surveillance cameras installed, there were cameras at the time the complaint was filed by the complainant, which constitutes a breach of the aforementioned Article 13 of the GDPR. However, logically, no measures will be imposed regarding the need to complete the information provided on the signs, as no data processing is currently being carried out.  

SIXTH: On April 26, 2023, a resolution proposal was made, proposing:  
That the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection sanction B.B.B., with NIF ***NIF.1, for an infringement of Article 13 of the GDPR, classified under Article 83.5 of the GDPR, with a fine of €300 (Three hundred euros).  

SEVENTH: An attempt was made to notify the resolution proposal by postal mail, but it was not collected at the respondent's address, and the acknowledgment of receipt was returned with the result "absent."  
No allegations have been submitted regarding the resolution proposal.

In view of all the actions taken by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection in the present procedure, the following facts are considered proven:  

PROVEN FACTS  
FIRST: It is established that, at the time the complaint was filed, the respondent had installed surveillance cameras on their property; however, the images from the field of view of these cameras indicate that they do not capture images of the public road, at least not to an extent that would be subject to sanctions under data protection regulations, nor of neighboring buildings.  
SECOND: It is established that, although according to the image provided by the respondent, there are signs warning of the existence of video cameras, the information they provide is insufficient and does not meet the requirements set forth in Article 22 of the LOPDGDD, as it does not include either the identity of the data controller or the possibility of exercising the rights provided for in Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR.  

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and the guarantee of digital rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  

II  
The installation of a video camera entails the unavoidable obligation to inform about its presence through an informative device, in a sufficiently visible location, identifying at least the existence of the processing, the identity of the data controller, and the possibility of exercising the rights provided for in Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR.  
In the present case, although the respondent has provided a photograph of a sign placed next to the cameras, it is observed that it does not meet all the requirements established by Article 22 of the LOPDGDD, as the information provided is incomplete and insufficient.  
Therefore, it is considered that the facts presented violate the provisions of Article 13 of the GDPR, which constitutes an infringement classified under Article 83.5 of the GDPR, which states the following:

III  
In light of the facts presented, it is considered appropriate to impose a sanction on the respondent for the violation of Article 13 of the GDPR classified under Article 83.5 of the GDPR. The sanction to be imposed is an administrative fine of 300 euros (THREE HUNDRED EUROS).  
Therefore, in accordance with the applicable legislation and having assessed the criteria for the graduation of sanctions whose existence has been established,  
the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:
