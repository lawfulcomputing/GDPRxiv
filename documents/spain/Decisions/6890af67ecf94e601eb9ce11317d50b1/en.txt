RESOLUTION OF SANCTIONING PROCEDURE  
From the procedure conducted by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

BACKGROUND  
FIRST: The Spanish Agency for Data Protection became aware through a complaint made by the EXCMO. AYUNTAMIENTO DE LA NUCIA of certain facts that could violate legislation regarding data protection.  
Specifically, this Agency was informed of the discovery of various documents abandoned in an open field, which contain a large number of personal data with varying degrees of sensitivity, some of which are of special category.  
The documents contain personal data of both the citizens of the municipality of La Nucia and individuals affiliated with the Partit Socialista del País Valencià, as well as municipal information obtained by virtue of their status as public officials in the Ayuntamiento and material related to the activities of the party.  
The large amount of documentation found, its diverse nature, and the need to analyze its implications regarding personal data protection and information security necessitate the opening of an investigation to clarify the facts and the responsibility for them.  
Therefore, on March 16, 2022, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection urged the Subdirectorate General for Data Inspection (SGID) to initiate the preliminary investigation proceedings referred to in Article 67 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD) aimed at verifying these facts and their authorship.  

SECOND: The Subdirectorate General for Data Inspection proceeded to carry out preliminary investigation actions to clarify the facts in question, by virtue of the functions assigned to the control authorities in Article 57.1 and the powers granted in Article 58.1 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and in accordance with the provisions of Title VII, Chapter I, Section Two, of the LOPDGDD, being aware of the following points:  
The complaint was received on February 10, 2022, and it details the discovery of documents containing personal data from that Ayuntamiento in an open field located within the municipal term of Benidorm.

The moving contract has a special regulation within the transport contract contained in Chapter VIII of Law 15/2009, of November 11, on the contract for the land transport of goods (LCTTM), Articles 71 and following.  
Article 71 of the LCTTM establishes that “By the moving contract, the carrier is obliged to transport furniture, household goods, items, and their complements from or to homes, business premises, or workplaces, in addition to carrying out the loading, unloading, and transfer operations of the objects to be transported from where they are located to place them in the destination home, premises, or workplace. The rest of the operations, such as preparation, assembly or disassembly, packaging, unpacking, and other complementary tasks, will be at the contractual discretion of the contracting parties.”  
Article 76 of the LCTTM determines the responsibilities of the carrier in case of breach of the transport contract: The carrier's liability for damage or loss of the transported goods may not exceed twenty times the Public Indicator of Multiple Effects/day for each cubic meter of loading space necessary for the fulfillment of the contract.

THIRD: On March 2, 2023, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection decided to initiate sanctioning proceedings against the Socialist Municipal Group of La Nucia, for the alleged infringement of Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR, classified under Article 83.5 of the GDPR, and for the alleged infringements of Articles 32, 34, and 28 of the GDPR, classified under Article 83.4 of the GDPR.  
The initiation agreement was sent, in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), via electronic notification, being received on March 6, 2023, as stated in the certificate included in the file.  

FOURTH: Once the aforementioned initiation agreement was notified in accordance with the rules established in the LPACAP, the Socialist Municipal Group of La Nucia submitted a written statement of allegations in which, in summary, it regrets the error made with the emptying of the Municipal Group's headquarters and the malpractice of the contracted company that deposited documentation containing personal data in an open field, which has led this Data Protection Agency to agree to initiate a sanctioning file.

In accordance with the provisions of Article 28 of the GDPR, the data controller and the data processor must regulate the processing of data in a contract or legal act that binds the processor to the controller; that contract or legal act must establish the object, duration, nature, and purpose of the processing, the type of personal data and categories of data subjects, the obligations and rights of the controller, etc.  
The actions taken have demonstrated that a moving and transport company was contracted to transfer the documentation from the headquarters of the Municipal Socialist Group of La Nucia to the Ecopark and that the contract with this company was made through WhatsApp conversations. That is to say, no type of data processing agreement was signed for the destruction or purging of the documentation, but rather it was trusted that the moving and transport company would transfer the documentation to the Ecopark, although there is no record of subsequent confirmation of the destruction by them. The moving contract is not a data processing agreement but a type of transport contract whose object, definition, nature, and responsibilities are legally regulated.  
Therefore, regarding this contracting, it is not evident that all the requirements set forth in Article 28 of the GDPR are met, which, for data processing by a data processor, implies taking responsibility for the destruction of documents.  
It is also not evidenced that complete technical and organizational measures have been established in accordance with current regulations that enable the guarantees of data security safeguards, specifically in the area of document destruction security.  
Consequently, it is considered that the established facts constitute an infringement attributable to the Municipal Socialist Group of La Nucia for violating Article 28 of the GDPR.
