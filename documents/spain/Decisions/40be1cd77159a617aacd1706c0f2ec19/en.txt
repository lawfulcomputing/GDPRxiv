FACTS  
FIRST: On January 31, 2022, a complaint was filed with entry registration number O00007128e2200004129 before the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against BICLAMEDIA, S.L. with NIF B87022117 (hereinafter, the complained party). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
A complaint has been registered with this Agency for the receipt of unsolicited commercial communications via SMS sent to several telephone lines linked to a Multisim card, where the main number is: ***TELEFONO.1. The numbers linked to the Multisim card are: ***TELEFONO.2, ***TELEFONO.3, and ***TELEFONO.4.  
The received commercial SMS promoting the services of MÁSMÓVIL (on September 3 and October 29, 2021) and VIRGINTELCO (on August 31, 2021, and January 25, 2022).  
The complainant indicates that on December 1, 2021, she began an email exchange with the DPD of the complained party, requesting opposition and clarifying that the number ***TELEFONO.1 is a MoviStar Multisim and that it is this number where the advertising messages are being received in triplicate.  
She receives a response informing her that the number ***TELEFONO.4 has been found and blocked in the database, but not all numbers associated with the Multisim card are blocked.  
The complainant understands that this is not sufficient, as three individuals are affected by the receipt of these commercial communications since they share the phone ***TELEFONO.1, of which she is the holder.  
Along with the notification, screenshots of the commercial SMS, email exchanges with the complained party, and the invoice for the telephone line ***TELEFONO.1 are provided.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
According to the provisions of Article 43.1 of Law 34/2002, of July 11, on information society services and electronic commerce (hereinafter LSSI) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and guarantee of digital rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative proceedings.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD stipulates that: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued for its development and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
Finally, the Fourth Additional Provision "Procedure in relation to the competencies attributed to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection by other laws" establishes that: "The provisions in Title VIII and its development regulations shall apply to the procedures that the Spanish Agency for Data Protection must process in exercising the competencies attributed to it by other laws."  

II  
Prohibition of commercial communications made via email or equivalent electronic communication means  
Article 21 of the LSSI establishes the following:  
"1. The sending of advertising or promotional communications by email or other equivalent electronic communication means that have not been previously requested or expressly authorized by the recipients is prohibited.  
2. The provisions of the previous section shall not apply when there is a prior contractual relationship, provided that the provider has lawfully obtained the contact details of the recipient and uses them to send commercial communications regarding products or services of its own company that are similar to those initially contracted with the client.  
In any case, the provider must offer the recipient the possibility to oppose the processing of their data for promotional purposes through a simple and free procedure, both at the time of data collection and in each of the commercial communications sent to them.  
When the communications have been sent by email, this means must necessarily consist of the inclusion of an email address or another valid electronic address where this right can be exercised, and the sending of communications that do not include such an address is prohibited."  

III  
Conclusion  
Through the actions carried out by this Agency, it has not been possible to verify the receipt of six of the nine SMS messages sent to the claimant (including those submitted to the claim subsequently), allegedly sent to the number ***TELEFONO.1.  
Of the nine commercial SMS messages claimed by the claimant, the receipt of three SMS messages has been confirmed at the internal number of one of the SIM cards associated with the claimant's MultiSIM contract, an internal number that is unknown and is not the subject of the claim, although they were allegedly received at the number ***TELEFONO.1.  
Both the respondent and its manager and the advertising operators have provided evidence of the non-inclusion of the phone numbers provided by the claimant in their databases, except for the number ***TELEFONO.4. The evidence consists of certificates that include screenshots of the unsuccessful search for the numbers in their databases.  
The respondent has provided evidence that the claimant provided the number ***TELEFONO.4 when participating in a raffle and accepted the receipt of commercial communications.  
Therefore, based on the information indicated in the previous paragraphs, no evidence has been found to substantiate the existence of an infringement within the competence of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection.  
Thus, in accordance with the aforementioned, by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection,
