From the procedure instructed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

BACKGROUND  
FIRST: On December 2, 2022, A.A.A. (hereinafter, the claimant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection. The complaint is directed against B.B.B. with NIF ***NIF.1 (hereinafter, the respondent).  
The claimant states that the respondent, (the claimant's brother), has made unauthorized use of the recording of the trial held before the Court of First Instance No. X of Oviedo, as he published the recording of the trial on November 19, 2014, through the YouTube website and from his personal profile, in which the claimant and the respondent were the defendant and plaintiff, respectively, as a result of a legal dispute over their parents' inheritance.  
As the title of such publication, he indicates "The Lies of ***TITLE.1".  
The video lasts 44:33 minutes, has been published on that website for over 8 years, and has more than 110 views.  

SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the complaint was forwarded to the respondent on February 2, 2023, for analysis and to inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in data protection regulations.  
The forwarding, which was carried out in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), was acknowledged on February 13, 2023, as evidenced by the acknowledgment of receipt in the file.  
On March 8, 2023, this Agency received a response indicating that the respondent was provided with the CD containing the recording by the court and was informed that it contained public information.

He also claims that he is unaware if his identity has been impersonated or if someone has accessed his copy of the video or that of the other parties in the procedure and used it, but in any case, he never gave his consent to such actions nor was he aware of them, having learned about them as a result of the present sanctioning procedure.  
As soon as he was informed of the situation, and despite not having any information regarding that YouTube channel, as it is not his, he sought professional help to have it removed, as he certainly has no interest in having his image, data, and family situation exposed to the public, being informed that since it was not created with his phone or email, he cannot order its immediate removal.  
Regarding the image and voice of the participants in the trial that appear in the recording, its viewing allows for the observation that it is of such low quality that it is impossible to recognize anyone.  
The respondent points out that the trial is a public act, so there are hardly any expectations of privacy.  
He also notes that the video in question has had minimal dissemination, as after several years it has only had 110 views.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
In accordance with the provisions of Articles 58.2 and 60 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of April 27, 2016, on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 and 68.2 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: “The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued in its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures.”  
II  
In this case, according to the provisions of Articles 4.1 and 4.2 of the GDPR, there is evidence of the processing of personal data, since the reported party retains, among others, the following personal data of natural persons: name and surname, image and voice, among other treatments.  
The reported party carries out this activity in its capacity as the data controller, as it determines the purposes and means of such activity, by virtue of Article 4.7 of the GDPR.  
For its part, Article 5 of the GDPR regarding “the principles relating to processing” regulates, in section 1.b), the “limitation of purpose.”

III  
In the present case, the publication on the YouTube channel of the recording of the oral hearing of a trial in which the claimant and the defendant were the respondent and the claimant, respectively, is reported. This has been ongoing since February 19, 2014, to the present. The recording consists of the trial held before the Court of First Instance No. X of Oviedo, as a result of the inheritance of the parents of the claimant and the defendant.  
The claimant states that in this recording, the image and voice of all parties are captured, including those of the professionals representing his brother in this procedure, as well as his own and those of the professionals who represented and defended him, along with that of Your Honor and other members of the Court present at the time of the hearing.  
For its part, the defendant states that the CD with the recording was provided to him by the court and was indicated to be public information.  
However, he claims that he has tried to delete the video from YouTube but has been unable to do so.  
This Agency must indicate that this conduct, consisting of the defendant's use of the recording of the trial, would not be in accordance with the law, as Article 236 quinquies of LO 6/1985, of the Judiciary, in its section 3, establishes the following:  
"Personal data that the parties know through the process must be processed by them in accordance with the general data protection regulations. This obligation applies to the professionals who represent and assist the parties, as well as to any other participant in the procedure."  
Therefore, the disclosure of personal data of the individuals appearing in the recording (name and surname, image, and voice) through YouTube could constitute an infringement attributable to the reported party for violating Article 5.1.b) of the GDPR, which requires that personal data collected have a specific, explicit, and legitimate purpose, and that such data cannot be processed further in a manner incompatible with those purposes. Thus, the personal data collected during the oral hearing of a judicial process cannot be subject to public disclosure in a context different from that for which they were obtained, as doing so would violate the principle of purpose limitation regulated in Article 5.1 b) of the GDPR.  
However, the Spanish Agency for Data Protection, upon learning that the defendant cannot remove the video subject to this sanctioning procedure, has decided to request from GOOGLE the identity of the person who uploaded the video on the YouTube channel.  
However, this company has denied the AEPD this information, claiming that GOOGLE is a U.S. company and cannot provide such information in the absence of a legal process in the U.S.
