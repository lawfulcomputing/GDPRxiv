RESOLUTION ON THE ARCHIVING OF PROCEEDINGS  
From the proceedings carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

FACTS  
FIRST: On February 23, 2023, and with an entry registration number (…), a complaint was received by this Agency against CHATWITH.IO WORLDWIDE, S.L. with NIF B88184239 (hereinafter, CHATWITH.IO). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The complainant alleges that after conducting a search on Google, they discovered that their name and surname were published in the directory of legal professionals iurisnow.com, in a profile identifying them as a lawyer specializing in social networks registered with the Illustrious Bar Association of Málaga, which they state is false.  
Along with the notification, a printout of the website www.iurisnow.com is provided, showing their name and a registration number.  

SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), this complaint was forwarded to CHATWITH.IO for analysis and to inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in data protection regulations.  
The transfer, which was carried out in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP) by electronic notification, was not collected by the responsible party within the availability period, being understood as rejected in accordance with the provisions of Article 43.2 of the LPACAP on April 19, 2023, as stated in the certificate in the file.  
Once the notification was made, considering the procedure completed in accordance with the provisions of Article 41.5 of the LPACAP, an additional attempt at paper notification was made for informational purposes, in accordance with the provisions of Article 42 of the LPACAP - by postal mail - which was returned as "Returned to Origin due to excess (Not collected at Office)" on May 8, 2023, after two previous unsuccessful delivery attempts on April 27 and 28, 2023.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
In accordance with the functions conferred to each supervisory authority by Article 57.1 a), f) and h) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative proceedings.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."

V  
Lawfulness of processing  
Article 6.1 of the GDPR provides that:  
“1. Processing shall be lawful only if at least one of the following applies:  
a) the data subject has given consent to the processing of their personal data for one or more specific purposes;  
b) processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract;  
c) processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject;  
d) processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of another natural person;  
e) processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller;  
f) processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party, except where such interests are overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject which require protection of personal data, in particular when the data subject is a child.  
The provision in letter f) of the first paragraph shall not apply to processing carried out by public authorities in the performance of their tasks.”  

VI  
Conclusion  
In this case, after analyzing the reasons presented by the respondent party that are in the file, it is considered that the initiation of sanctioning proceedings is not warranted, since, according to the evidence collected by this Agency, specifically, from the query on the website www.iurisnow.com regarding “specialist lawyers in social networks in Málaga” (pages 5 to 9 of the file), there is no record of the complainant's data being published on that website.  
Additionally, a search was conducted on the website www.iurisnow.com for registered lawyers using the complainant's data (name and surname) (page 10 of the file), with no results found.  
Finally, an internet search was conducted using the complainant's data, with no results obtained relating to the complainant on the website www.iurisnow.com (page 16 of the file).  
Therefore, based on the information indicated in the previous paragraphs, no evidence has been found to substantiate the existence of an infringement within the jurisdiction of the Spanish Data Protection Agency.  
Regarding the document submitted by the complainant dated May 19, 2023, although the privacy policy of the website www.iurisnow.com (page 11 of the file) states that the owner of the website is IURIS MARKETING, S.L. (B88184239), as evidenced in the diligence dated February 2, 2024, in the file, the name IURIS MARKETING, S.L. is the former name of CHATWITH.IO WORLDWIDE, S.L.  
All of this is without prejudice to any subsequent actions that this Agency may carry out, applying the investigative and corrective powers it holds.  
Thus, as it has not been possible to attribute responsibility for the processing as indicated, the Director of the Spanish Data Protection Agency RESOLVES:  
FIRST: TO FILE the present proceedings.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to CHATWITH.IO WORLDWIDE, S.L. and to the complainant.
