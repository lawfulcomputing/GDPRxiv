From the actions carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

FACTS  
FIRST: On 23/06/2022, a document was submitted to this Agency by A.A.A. (hereinafter, the claimant), through which a complaint is filed against UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE DE MADRID with NIF Q2818014I (hereinafter, UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE), for a possible breach of the provisions of personal data protection regulations.  
The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The claimant states that, as a result of a judicial proceeding before the Commercial Court No. 2 of A Coruña, they became aware that the entity that is the counterparty in that process, SIGNE, S.A., has apparently illegitimately accessed a request for access to public information under the Law 19/2013, of December 9, on Transparency, Access to Public Information, and Good Governance. This request would have been made by the claimant on 20/12/2018 to the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE and, according to the claimant, was rejected as evidence by the presiding Judge of the proceeding for not proving that SIGNE, S.A. obtained it in accordance with the procedures established by the applicable administrative regulations.  
In order to obtain more information, the claimant requested access to their personal data from the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE on 27/12/2021, specifically asking about data transmissions to third parties. On 28/12/2021, they received a response from the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE stating that there were no records of their data, and subsequently on 19/01/2022, the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE stated that it had received the request for access to the aforementioned public information, which had been filed without being opened and, according to the respondent, had never been communicated to third parties.  
Attached, among others, are the following documents:  
- Transcript of parts of the video of the preliminary hearing held on 17/12/2021, in which the legal representative of SIGNE, S.A. intervenes to present as evidence the requests for access to public information made by the claimant.  
- Copy of the request for access to public information, dated 20/12/2018, submitted by the claimant to the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE, which is provided as evidence by SIGNE, S.A.  
- Copy of the Resolution issued by the Council of Transparency and Good Governance, dated 16/04/2019.  
- Copy of the emails exchanged between the claimant and the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE after exercising the right of access provided for in the GDPR.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence and applicable regulations  
In accordance with the functions conferred on each control authority by Article 57.1 a), f) and h) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of the LOPDGDD, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative actions.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: “The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued in its development and, as long as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures.”  
II  
Principles relating to processing  
Letter f) of Article 5.1 of the GDPR states:  
“1. Personal data shall be:  
(…)  
f) processed in a manner that ensures appropriate security of the personal data, including protection against unauthorized or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage, by applying appropriate technical or organizational measures (‘integrity and confidentiality’).”

IV  
Conclusion  
Regarding the issue of obtaining by the entity SIGNE, S.A. a copy of the request for access to public information submitted by the complaining party to the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE on 20/12/2018, the documentation in the administrative file records the existence of a service contract signed by both parties on 20/02/2017, under which SIGNE, S.A. undertakes to provide a “Service for the personalization of official university and inter-university degrees, Erasmus Mundus degree and labels, European supplement to the degree, official degrees in electronic format, European supplement to the degree in electronic format, proprietary degrees in electronic format, continuous training, and diploma of advanced studies issued by the Universidad Complutense de Madrid”; until 31/12/2018.  

While it is true that the request was submitted before the contract was terminated, this does not imply that the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE sent the request without anonymizing SIGNE, S.A. during its validity; there being no rational indications to understand that, in fact, there has been a communication of personal data during the contractual relationship.  

Furthermore, the emails exchanged between the employees of the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE and SIGNE, S.A. in October 2019 do not prove the alleged communication of data; since there is no indication in their content of any copy of the request from the complaining party.  

There are also no indications that allow setting the starting date for the calculation of the limitation period at a later date. Therefore, there is no evidence or probative elements that allow determining the moment and manner in which the reported facts occurred.

Given the impossibility of determining the specific day on which the infringement may have occurred, it must be taken into account that the request to which SIGNE, S.A. would have had access was made by the claiming party on 20/12/2018 and that the service contract ended on 31/12/2018, with more than 3 years having passed since those dates, which leads to the declaration of the prescription of the infringement by way of presumption, as there is no evidence to the contrary.  
Regarding the right of access exercised by the claiming party on 27/12/2021, it is noted that on 10/01/2022, the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE responded to the claiming party from the email address ofi.dpd@ucm.es and requested that they specify the data or processing activities they wish to access concerning their personal data, in addition to stating that there is no record of the aforementioned request. It was on 19/01/2022 when the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE, in order to address the personal data access request submitted by the claiming party, provided the claiming party with a copy of the Record of Processing Activities corresponding to the “Electronic Registration” processing activity; however, it did not provide all the information requested by the claiming party, only addressing generic issues regarding personal data. Thus, the actions of the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE could constitute an infringement classified under Article 83.5.b) of the GDPR, which, for the purposes of prescription, Article 74 c) of the LOPDGDD qualifies as minor.  
For the reasons stated, the infringement of Article 15 of the GDPR is prescribed, given that one year has passed since the commission of the aforementioned facts.  

Consequently, as it has not been possible to attribute responsibility for the communication of the claiming party's personal data to the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE and, furthermore, the infringement of Article 15 of the GDPR has prescribed, in accordance with what has been indicated, by the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection, IT IS AGREED:  
FIRST: TO PROCEED TO FILE these proceedings.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to the UNIVERSIDAD COMPLUTENSE DE MADRID and to the claiming party.  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 50 of the LOPDGDD, this Resolution will be made public once it has been notified to the interested parties.  
Against this resolution, which concludes the administrative procedure as provided by Article 114.1.c) of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations, and in accordance with the provisions of Articles 112 and 123 of the aforementioned LPACAP, the interested parties may optionally file a motion for reconsideration before the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection within one month from the day following the notification of this resolution or directly file a contentious-administrative appeal before the Contentious-Administrative Chamber of the National Court, in accordance with the provisions of Article 25 and paragraph 5 of the fourth additional provision of Law 29/1998, of July 13, regulating the Contentious-Administrative Jurisdiction, within two months from the day following the notification of this act, as provided in Article 46.1 of the aforementioned Law.
