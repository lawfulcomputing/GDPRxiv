From the proceedings carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following:  

FACTS  
FIRST: On December 5, 2022, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection urged the General Subdirectorate for Data Inspection to initiate the preliminary investigation proceedings referred to in Article 67 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD) to investigate the GALICIAN HEALTH SERVICE (hereinafter, SERGAS) in relation to the following facts:  
As a consequence of the complaint with entry registration number REGAGE22e00051233319, this Agency was informed of an alleged breach of data protection regulations by the reported entity concerning the modules of special custody, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 16 and 17 of Decree 29/2009, of February 5, which regulates the use and access to electronic medical records.  
These articles establish that "through the IANUS system, mechanisms will be progressively established that allow the patient or user to determine a module or modules of clinical information that may contain those data considered to be of special custody or intimacy in the areas of genetics, sexuality and reproduction, psychiatry, organ transplantation, infectious diseases that may harm their social or work life, as well as data related to domestic violence," and that "the professionals who, in accordance with their functions, need to access the data of modules of special custody will be warned by the IANUS system of this circumstance, so that they indicate the reason for access, exercise extreme caution in its handling, and incorporate within the corresponding modules any new data that may be generated. In the access log, those corresponding to special custody data will be singled out, which will allow for specific audits to be carried out."

1. Definition  
The IANUS clinical history system began its implementation in 2004, and when Decree 29/2009 came into force, the Consellería de Sanidade established that the most effective protection mechanism was the integration of all clinical history information into a module of special custody at the moment the patient so determined.  
Since then, the Consellería de Sanidade has confirmed the need for the integration of all care information into a single container. For example, during a certain time interval, restrictions were implemented for the integration of genetic information from any IANUS patient that had to be lifted due to the care problems generated by the loss of integrity of the information in the communication process between the internal genetic platforms of the Public Health System of Galicia and IANUS.  
This reflection could be extended to any other information mentioned in reference to the modules of special custody.  
There is also another added difficulty, which is evident in processes such as voluntary interruptions of pregnancy or fertility treatments carried out outside the public health system: episodes that are NOT recorded in the IANUS clinical histories are referred to by the patients themselves in subsequent instances.

The authorizations for access are nominal. By default, no one can access the information contained in the medical history/module, and if necessary for the healthcare of the patient, the management of the corresponding area will enable access for the involved professionals.  
In the absence of such authorization for access to these histories/modules, when a user attempts to do so, the system alerts to the existence of protection mechanisms, denies access, and indicates that express authorization is required. This access is always linked to the healthcare activity.  
Regarding the other cases that arise, we can rule out secondary uses such as access for evaluation, accreditation, healthcare planning, teaching, research, scientific publications, or statistical or epidemiological purposes.  
In the case of access for inspection or judicial purposes, we have no record of such occurrences, and they should also be expressly authorized to carry out the access. In the hypothetical case that we receive such a request in the future, duly justified, we believe we would have a legal obligation to address it.  
We can make the above assertions with such certainty because, to date, only 3 individuals have determined their inclusion. One of them passed away 12 years ago, and another is no longer a subject of healthcare provision in the SPSG (Public Health System of Galicia), so in reality, only one patient currently covered has this active protection.  
Not only have no requests for inclusion in the module been received, but since the protocolization of the rights in data protection, only one communication regarding these modules has been received, requesting information about their operation, but without indicating a desire to determine their inclusion.

3. Procedure  
Regarding the description of the procedure followed by the Consellería de Sanidade when a patient/user requests that information from their clinical history be included in a special custody module, we reiterate that 3 requests have been received, and given that volume, no specific procedure has been carried out; instead, the same procedure applicable to any other right concerning data protection would be applied, as indicated at https://www.xunta.gal/exercicio-de-dereitos.  
In the event of exercising this right, the steps we would follow after receiving the request via registration would be:  
1. Response to the request informing them of the possible healthcare risks that may arise from the limitations in accessing the EHR by enabling said module.  
2. In the event that the patient indicates that they understand the risks, the information would be requested, among other information, authorization from the professionals, etc.  
3. The configuration of the user for the special custody module would be carried out by an administrator with user configuration privileges.  

1. Access to Information  
Identification is of dual factor: user and password from the Active Directory plus a signature with a certificate stored on a secure device (cryptographic card).  
Access to the special custody module is through the general access module to the IANUS system; that is, it is not to a specific module but through IANUS, where access to patient sets is enabled based on the privileges assigned to each user.  
Once a user has accessed IANUS, they CANNOT consult the data of a patient in a special custody module unless they have specific permission for that. In fact, when searching for a user included in the special custody module, only authorized professionals will see their data; the rest of the professionals will not receive any response regarding this. Once accessed, they will have access to all the information contained in the clinical history. We reiterate, as in the previous report we sent regarding this file, that experience shows us the necessity for access to EHRs to be complete to ensure adequate healthcare for patients.  
Since the special custody module follows the general access rules of IANUS, the same control measures established by IANUS for access records apply. All accesses made to the EHRs are registered, stored, and evaluated. It is evident that access to a special custody module would draw attention and would be subject to special scrutiny, but given that there is only one patient and only one authorized professional, accesses are anecdotal within the global set of accesses.  

2. Security Measures  
SERGAS states that the highest security measures are applied to all activities carried out in IANUS, both through access mechanisms and through activity logs.  
These measures have not been altered following the definition of special custody modules by Decree 29/2009.  

3. Operation  
The functionality of IANUS that restricts access to the EHRs of patients under special custody is called “VIP Patients.”  
The configuration of these patients is carried out from the administration module of IANUS through a specific permission and can be configured at the professional level or at the patient level.  
1. At the professional level  
From the user administration (professionals) of IANUS, there is a section to assign “VIP Patients” to a specific professional. When a patient is assigned as a VIP patient linked to a professional, only those professionals assigned to them will be able to access their history.  
2. At the patient level  
From the administration of IANUS, the same configuration can also be made, but starting from the patient. That is, the “VIP Patient” is searched for, and subsequently, the professionals to whom access to their history is desired can be managed.  
After selecting the patient, professionals can be added or removed.

Security of processing  
Article 32 of the GDPR stipulates the following:  
"1. Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of the processing, as well as the risks of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons, the controller and the processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, which may include, among others:  
a) the pseudonymization and encryption of personal data;  
b) the ability to ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems and services;

IV  
Conclusion  
In the case at hand, the complaint states: “that SERGAS is not applying in IANUS Decree 29/2009, of February 5, which regulates the use and access to the electronic medical record regarding the Modules of special custody and requests that professionals receive such a warning about access whenever they access modules of special custody.”  
Well, IANUS is the main information system of the Electronic Medical Record (hereinafter, EMR) of the Servizo Galego de Saúde (SERGAS).  
The main functionalities of the program include real-time access to patients' health data, the integration of all professionals in care, continuity of care, or the agility in procedures due to reduced bureaucracy, the incorporation of electronic prescriptions, and the possibility of connection between pharmacies and centers.  
Therefore, IANUS integrates all clinical and administrative information of patients who have had any relationship with SERGAS.  
Part of the information is recorded in IANUS itself, and another part is recorded in the various information systems used by SERGAS, structuring it under a standardized model of EMR common to all patients.  
The roles/users who have access to the EMR of SERGAS are outlined in Decree 29/2009, of February 5, which regulates the use and access to the electronic medical record.  
Access is made through a double factor: the card with a certificate for recognized signature and a combination of username and password with which it verifies the access profile. Access logs are consolidated in a complex data analysis system (SIAC) that serves as a basis for auditing, inspection, and control tasks of the accesses made.  
IANUS began its implementation in 2004, and when Decree 29/2009 came into force (in its articles 16 and 17 refers to the modules of special custody), it was established by the Consellería de Sanidade that the most effective protection mechanism was the integration of all clinical history information into a module of special custody at the moment the patient so determined.  
The Consellería de Sanidade has reached an increasingly reinforced conclusion of the need to unify all information of each patient in IANUS to ensure adequate healthcare for patients, due to the risks that any type of health segmentation may pose. Therefore, it maintains the initial operation of the modules of special custody: what the patient can determine within the modules of special custody is the protection of their entire clinical history.  
It should be noted that access to a module of special custody is through the general access module to the IANUS system, and the authorizations for access to the modules of special custody are nominal. By default, no one can access the information.
