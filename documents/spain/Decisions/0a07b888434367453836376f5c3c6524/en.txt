SANCTIONING PROCEDURE RESOLUTION  
From the procedure instructed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

BACKGROUND  
FIRST: On January 24, 2022, the General Subdirectorate for Data Inspection (SGID) received for evaluation a notification of a personal data security breach sent by the CASTILLA LA MANCHA TALAVERA HEALTH SERVICE with NIF Q4500146H (hereinafter, SESCAM Talavera), received on December 23, 2021, in which it informs the Spanish Agency for Data Protection of the following:  
On the aforementioned date, December 23, 2021, the notification of the first security breach (hereinafter Breach 1) was received from SESCAM Talavera. The security incident is related to an unauthorized access to a patient's medical history by a professional worker of SESCAM Talavera without having any care-related link to the patient. The incident was detected following a complaint filed by the affected patient at the Hospital de Talavera de La Reina. In this complaint, the patient alleges that, after being hospitalized in that hospital for several days in September 2021, his ex-wife, a Nursing Assistant at the same hospital, had accessed his medical history and health data improperly and had disseminated this information to third parties even before the doctor provided the diagnosis.  
They indicate that the date of detection of the breach is December 17, 2021 (estimated), that the events began to take place on September 9, 2021, and that the complaint from the affected patient was filed on October 8, 2021. SESCAM Talavera submitted a written complaint to the Investigating Court of Talavera reporting the unauthorized accesses. On December 23, 2021, the breach was communicated and reported to the affected person.  
On December 28, an extension regarding Breach 1 was received in the electronic registry of this Agency, specifically provided by the Managing Director of the Integrated Care Management of Talavera de la Reina, including the following documentation:  
- Internal form of SESCAM Talavera for the notification of a security breach.  
- The complaint from the affected patient registered on October 8, 2021.  
- Report from the IT Service of SESCAM Talavera listing the accesses to the affected patient's medical history by the Nursing Assistant, dated December 3, 2021.  
- Criminal complaint submitted by SESCAM Talavera to the investigating court of Talavera, signed on December 20, 2021.  
- Submission of internal documents to the Provincial Health Delegation (with a proposal for sanctioning instruction) and to the Inspection Management (for possible relevant investigations), dated November 29 and 25, 2021, respectively.  

SECOND: On December 29, 2021, a second notification of a security breach (hereinafter Breach 2) was received by the same responsible party in the registry of this Agency. It reports unauthorized access to patient medical histories by a doctor from SESCAM Talavera who was under a firm suspension of duties due to disciplinary sanction. This was detected following a notification from another medical professional from the same local office where the events occurred. It involves access to health data of approximately 60 affected individuals. The date of detection is December 27, 2021 (exact date), and the date when the incident began is October 11, 2021. The affected individuals were not notified because, according to their assessment, there is no high risk to the rights and freedoms of the affected individuals as they are all patients of the same medical professional.

In this regard, the work of the former Working Group 29 WP 131, "On the Processing of Personal Data Related to Health in Electronic Medical Records (EMR)," can be taken into account. LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
By virtue of the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and the guarantee of digital rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Data Protection Agency is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Data Protection Agency shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued in its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
Preliminary Issues  
In this case, in accordance with the provisions of Article 4.1 of the GDPR, there is evidence of the processing of personal data, since SESCAM Talavera carries out, among other processes, the collection, registration, organization, storage, consultation, access, use, and deletion of the following personal data of individuals, such as: name, surname, identification number, contact details, health data, etc.  
SESCAM Talavera carries out this activity in its capacity as data controller, as it is the one who determines the purposes and means of such activity, by virtue of Article 4.7 of the GDPR.  
Article 4, paragraph 12 of the GDPR broadly defines "personal data security breaches" (hereinafter security breach) as "all those breaches of security that result in the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, or alteration of personal data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed, or unauthorized communication or access to such data."  
In this case, there is a personal data security breach under the circumstances indicated above, categorized as a confidentiality breach due to unauthorized access to patients' medical records by personnel without any medical justification for such access at the time of the accesses.  
The security of personal data is regulated in Articles 32, 33, and 34 of the GDPR, which govern both the security of processing, the notification of a personal data security breach to the supervisory authority, and the communication to the data subject, respectively.  
III  
Article 5.1 f) of the GDPR  
Article 5.1.f) "Principles relating to processing" of the GDPR states:  
"1. Personal data shall be:  
(...)  
f) processed in a manner that ensures appropriate security of the personal data, including protection against unauthorized or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage, using appropriate technical or organizational measures ('integrity and confidentiality')."  
In this case, it is established that the health data of the affected individuals, contained in the SESCAM Talavera database, were improperly exposed to third parties, due to unauthorized or unlawful access, thereby violating their confidential nature.  
Thus, regarding Breach 1, there were accesses to a patient's medical record by a nursing assistant at the hospital where the patient was admitted without any healthcare justification for such access, thereby accessing the health data of that patient. This is corroborated by the IT report contained in the file, which confirms accesses made on April 1 and on September 9, 10, and 13, 2021.  
Regarding Breach 2, there were two accesses by a doctor to the medical records of his patients (about 60), which occurred on October 11 and November 22, 2021, while he was suspended from duties.  
In this regard, it should not be forgotten, as has been stated in the investigative actions indicated in the Third Background, that the technical and organizational measures were not appropriate to ensure adequate security of personal data, especially for protection against unauthorized or unlawful processing, considering the confidential nature of personal data, particularly health data.

IV  
Classification of the infringement of Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR  
The aforementioned infringement of Article 5.1.f) of the GDPR constitutes the commission of the infringements classified in Article 83.5 of the GDPR, which under the heading "General conditions for the imposition of administrative fines" states:  
"Violations of the following provisions shall be subject to administrative fines of up to 20,000,000 EUR or, in the case of an enterprise, an amount equivalent to a maximum of 4% of the total annual global turnover of the previous financial year, opting for the higher amount:  
a) the basic principles for processing, including the conditions for consent under Articles 5, 6, 7, and 9; (…)”  
In this regard, the LOPDGDD, in its Article 71 "Infringements" establishes that:  
"Acts and behaviors referred to in paragraphs 4, 5, and 6 of Article 83 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as well as those that are contrary to this organic law, constitute infringements."  
For the purposes of the statute of limitations, Article 72 "Very serious infringements" of the LOPDGDD indicates:  
"1. In accordance with what is established in Article 83.5 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the following shall be considered very serious and shall be subject to a three-year statute of limitations: infringements that constitute a substantial violation of the articles mentioned therein and, in particular, the following:  
a) The processing of personal data in violation of the principles and guarantees established in Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. (…)”

1. A single assessment may address a series of similar processing operations that pose similar high risks.

3. The data protection impact assessment referred to in paragraph 1 shall be required in particular in the case of:  
   a) systematic and comprehensive evaluation of personal aspects of natural persons based on automated processing, including profiling, and on which decisions are based that produce legal effects concerning the natural persons or similarly significantly affect them;  
   b) large-scale processing of special categories of data referred to in Article 9, paragraph 1, or of personal data relating to criminal convictions and offenses referred to in Article 10, or

   a) a systematic description of the envisaged processing operations and the purposes of the processing, including, where appropriate, the legitimate interest pursued by the data controller;
