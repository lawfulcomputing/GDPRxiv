II  
Before delving into the substance of the matter, it is worth recalling that a Recurso de Reposición (Appeal for Reconsideration) is a legal instrument to request the acting Public Administration to review, if applicable, an act of the same that is not considered in accordance with the law or erroneous.  
It is not the channel to simultaneously present a Complaint regarding a hypothetical administrative infraction committed by the opposing party (original Claimant).  
Thus, the legal requirements for its submission are outlined in the current applicable regulations, in Law 39/2015, October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations, to which we refer literally.  
It is important to recall the principle of <good faith> in the exercise of actions as stated in Article 7.1 of the Civil Code, as well as an effective appearance of injury to some right within the framework of data protection, after having minimally verified the existence of a breach or hypothetical administrative infraction, avoiding the extrapolation of civil judicial matters <sub iudice> (*still pending adjudication) to this Agency.  
Apart from this, the local Security Forces and Corps (e.g., Local Police) have the authority to go to the scene of the events, verify them, or even make recommendations to the parties, and may, if applicable, issue an Inspection Report for its transfer to this Agency, which will ultimately be responsible for analyzing the facts.  
Having stated the above, it is appropriate to pause on the first allegation presented by the Appellant regarding the alleged expiration of the administrative procedure.  
The Initiation Agreement is dated 07/24/24 and was notified in due time and form to the respondent on 08/07/24, as evidenced in the system of this AEPD.  
Article 42, paragraph 1 of Law 39/2015 (October 1) provides:  
"All notifications made on paper must be made available to the interested party at the electronic headquarters of the acting Administration or Agency so that they can voluntarily access their content" (*the bold type belongs to this agency).  
The Resolution of the Presidency of this agency is dated 01/27/25 and was notified on 02/07/25, after being made available at the electronic headquarters on the same day, at which point access occurred and, therefore, the effective notification of the administrative act took place.  
Therefore, the notification was made once the six-month period established in the LOPDGDD (LO 3/2018, December 5)—Article 64, paragraph 3—had elapsed.

III  
The claiming party in their Appeal document expresses their disagreement with the essential content of the resolution of this Agency regarding the <ownership> of the land on which the image capture device(s) is oriented.  
In the case at hand, the installation of a camera system is an undisputed fact, as is the orientation towards an area in conflict between the parties with opposing interests, which would imply a logical reasoning towards the processing of personal data as a <coercive> measure, so the set of indications could undermine the presumption of innocence (recalling evidentiary criteria STS (2nd Chamber) of December 22, rec. No. 10279/2021).  
The appellant in their Appeal document refers to "the plot being subject to a possessory protection action," so as of the date, it cannot be strictly said to be private property as the matter is sub iudice.  
The expectation of a right in the sense of the claim to be the owner of a piece of land with exclusive character requires a document(s) that credibly certifies this in law, which is not the case with the documentation presented by the appellant.  
The known facts could therefore potentially constitute an infraction, attributable to the respondent under Article 5.1 c) of the GDPR, as they continue, according to the statements of the appellant themselves, in their current location, given that they consider such land to be private.  
According to the TS (e.g., STS September 28, 2022) "in the case of continued infractions, the date to be considered for determining the applicability of the norm is that on which the last infringing act is declared proven."  
Therefore, the above would justify that the device in question could be subject to removal or disconnection from its current location, as it is a presumed infraction that continues to occur at the present time.  
In such a way that this agency can agree to open a new administrative procedure for the appropriate legal effects, where the respondent may again present their arguments or provide the appropriate documentation (including certifying its removal), although this Agency will consider that they have already been extensively warned that the system must comply with current legality (principle of proactive responsibility).  
III  
Conclusion  
Consequently, in the present appeal for reconsideration, the appellant has made the necessary legal arguments that allow for reconsideration of the validity of the contested resolution, in terms of the untimeliness of the resolution period of the administrative procedure.
