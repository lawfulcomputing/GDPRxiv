RESOLUTION ON THE ARCHIVING OF PROCEEDINGS  
From the proceedings carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following:  
FACTS  
FIRST: On January 9, 2023, a complaint was filed with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against FINCEPTIV OÜ (hereinafter, the respondent).  
The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
The complainant claims to have received commercial SMS messages sent from a hidden number and with a generic sender name of a woman, making it difficult to identify or block.  
Along with the complaint, a copy of the SMS messages received between November 29, 2022, and January 9, 2023, is provided.  
The complainant states that the number provided for unsubscribing from the system is an 8-digit number that does not exist.  
A copy of a deletion request dated December 21, 2022, and a copy of two responses from the respondent are also provided.  
In the first response, dated December 21, 2022, they inform the complainant that their request to unsubscribe from the database will be processed within 24 hours.  
In the second response, dated December 22, 2022, they inform the complainant that their unsubscribe request has been processed, but their name and surname appear in the credit request.  
SECOND: In accordance with Article 65.4 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), this complaint was forwarded to the respondent for analysis and to inform this Agency within one month of the actions taken to comply with the requirements set forth in data protection regulations.  
The forwarding, which was carried out in accordance with the rules established in Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations (hereinafter, LPACAP), was acknowledged on March 1, 2023, as noted in the acknowledgment of receipt in the file.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
According to the provisions of Article 43.1 of Law 34/2002, of July 11, on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (hereinafter LSSI) and as provided in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative proceedings.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued for its development and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
Finally, the Fourth Additional Provision "Procedure in relation to the competencies attributed to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection by other laws" establishes that: "The provisions in Title VIII and its development regulations shall apply to the procedures that the Spanish Agency for Data Protection must process in the exercise of the competencies attributed to it by other laws."  
II  
Prohibition of commercial communications made via email or equivalent electronic means  
Article 21 of the LSSI establishes the following:  
"1. The sending of advertising or promotional communications by email or other equivalent electronic means that have not been previously requested or expressly authorized by the recipients is prohibited.  
2. The provisions of the previous section shall not apply when there is a prior contractual relationship, provided that the provider has lawfully obtained..."

III  
Conclusion  
Prior to the initiation of sanctioning proceedings, it is necessary to identify the alleged responsible party for the administrative infringement.  
Article 64 of Law 39/2015, of October 1, on the Common Administrative Procedure of Public Administrations, regarding the initiation agreement in sanctioning procedures, establishes the following:  
“1. The initiation agreement will be communicated to the instructor of the procedure, with the transfer of any actions that exist in this regard, and will be notified to the interested parties, understanding in any case that the accused is included. Likewise, the initiation will be communicated to the complainant when the regulatory norms of the procedure so provide.  
2. The initiation agreement must contain at least:  
a) Identification of the person or persons allegedly responsible.  
…”  
Due to the lack of the NIF (Tax Identification Number) of the complained party, it was requested from the Tax Agency for their identification.  
The Tax Agency has responded to the Spanish Agency for Data Protection that it has been unable to locate the requested NIF, which aims to identify legal persons and entities without legal personality for tax purposes.  
Therefore, although the complaint filed could imply a violation of the LSSI (Law on Information Society Services) and data protection regulations, it is not possible to initiate sanctioning actions due to the lack of tax identification of the alleged responsible party. Without prejudice to the fact that if this data were known by the complainant, they could provide it to this Agency, which would continue the proceedings after verification.
