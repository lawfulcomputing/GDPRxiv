FACTS  
FIRST: On August 23, 2023, a complaint was filed with entry registration number REGAGE23e00056706504 and REGAGE23e00056861736 before the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against VODAFONE ESPAÑA, S.A.U. with NIF A80907397 (hereinafter, the complained party, VODAFONE or LOWI). LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
Competence  
In accordance with the functions conferred upon each supervisory authority by Article 57.1 a), f), and h) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR) and as provided for in Articles 47 and 48.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter LOPDGDD), the Presidency of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to resolve these investigative actions.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, this organic law, the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
Lawfulness of Processing  
Article 6 of the GDPR, which regulates the lawfulness of processing, establishes the following:  
"1. Processing shall be lawful only if at least one of the following conditions is met:  
a) the data subject has given consent to the processing of their personal data for one or more specific purposes;  
b) processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract;  
c) processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject;  
d) processing is necessary in order to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of another natural person;  
e) processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller;  
f) processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party, except where such interests are overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject which require protection of personal data, in particular when the data subject is a child.  
The provision in paragraph f) of the first subsection shall not apply to processing carried out by public authorities in the performance of their tasks.  
2. Member States may maintain or introduce more specific provisions to adapt the application of the rules of this Regulation with regard to processing for compliance with paragraph 1, letters c) and e), establishing more specific conditions."

IV  
Statements of the Respondent  
In this case, the claimant states that their identity has been impersonated to request a duplicate of the SIM card for the telephone line contracted with LOWI.  
In response to the transfer and information actions carried out, this Agency has received a letter from the respondent in which, for the purposes relevant here, it states the following:  
1.- For the duplication of the SIM card, a third party gained control of the claimant's email address.  
2.- The channel through which the duplicate was processed was the Mi Lowi App. After correctly providing the access credentials (email address or mobile phone number and the established password), the third party requested a change of the email to an address that only they had access to.  
3.- In the Mi Lowi application, the fraudster requested a password change and, through the new account, was able to access the password recovery email and enter the claimant's personal area in the Mi Lowi App. From this application, they requested the duplicate SIM card and modified the delivery address.  
4.- The delivery procedure for the Duplicate requires that the person responsible for delivering the SIM card must verify the identity of the recipient, checking that the identifying data of their ID card matched those of the shipment. VODAFONE provides a screenshot of the delivery system in this specific case and attaches a screenshot of the delivery receipt where the messenger included the name and ID document number of the claimant (in this case, the ID document number is entered twice).  
5.- The SIM was immediately blocked.  
6.- As a reinforcement measure, a two-factor authentication has been established in the private area.  

V  
Conclusion  
From the investigative actions carried out, it follows that the respondent has carried out the steps provided by the entity itself for the duplication of the SIM card during the contracting process subject to this complaint. Among these steps are various verification acts in order to identify the requester, which have also been provided during the course of the proceedings. Likewise, the respondent has demonstrated the immediate blocking of the SIM, so that it cannot continue to be used. Consequently, from the facts stated in the complaint and the documentation provided, there are insufficient indications of a violation of data protection regulations by the respondent.  
Thus, as it has not been possible to attribute responsibility for data processing in accordance with the aforementioned, the Presidency of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection RESOLVES:  
FIRST: TO ARCHIVE the present proceedings.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to VODAFONE ESPAÑA, S.A.U. and to the claimant.  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 50 of the LOPDGDD, this Resolution will be made public once it has been notified to the interested parties.
