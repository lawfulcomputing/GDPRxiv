SANCTIONING PROCEDURE RESOLUTION  
From the procedure instructed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following  

BACKGROUND  
FIRST: A.A.A. (*hereinafter, the claimant) filed a complaint with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection on April 12, 2021. The complaint is directed against LODEJU, S.L. with NIF B92047745 (hereinafter, the respondent). The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
“the respondent is responsible for two establishments that have a video surveillance system oriented towards public roads, without administrative authorization for this, and without placing informative signs about the monitored area in the vicinity of its location.”  
Along with the notification, documentary evidence regarding the presence of the cameras oriented disproportionately is provided (Annex I Complaint Document).  

SECOND: On July 27, 2021, in accordance with Article 65 of the LOPDGDD, the complaint filed by the claimant was admitted for processing.  

THIRD: A sanctioning procedure was initiated against the respondent by this agency under the initial reference number ***PROCEDIMIENTO.1, with the actions being subject to electronic notification as it concerns a legal entity, obligated to do so under the terms of Article 14, paragraph 2 of the LPAC (Law 39/2015, October 1).  

FOURTH: On April 4, 2022, a Resolution was issued within the framework of ***PROCEDIMIENTO.1 confirming the improper orientation of the installed cameras, as well as the deficiency in the informative signage, with the entity being sanctioned in the following terms:  
IMPOSE on the company LODEJU, S.L., with NIF B92047745, for an infringement of Article 5.1.c) and 13 of the GDPR, classified under Article 83.5 of the GDPR, a fine of €3000 (three thousand euros).  

FIFTH: On March 17, 2023, a <revoking> Resolution of the Resolution of the Director of this agency was issued, in the terms of Article 109.1 of Law 39/2015 (October 1), considering that the respondent had not been aware of the facts subject to judgment by this Agency, with the aforementioned revocation agreement being notified in due time and form, as evidenced in the information system of this agency.  
REVOKE the resolution of the ***PROCEDIMIENTO.1, dated April 4, 2022, instructed against LODEJU, S.L., with NIF B92047745.

LEGAL GROUNDS  
I  
In accordance with the powers granted to each supervisory authority by Article 58.2 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and as established in Articles 47, 48.1, 64.2, and 68.1 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (hereinafter, LOPDGDD), the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection is competent to initiate and resolve this procedure.  
Furthermore, Article 63.2 of the LOPDGDD states: "The procedures processed by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection shall be governed by the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, by this organic law, by the regulatory provisions issued for its development, and, in so far as they do not contradict them, subsidiarily by the general rules on administrative procedures."  
II  
In this case, the complaint regarding the presence of a "video surveillance camera" affecting the complainant's rights and excessively capturing public space without justified cause is analyzed.  
Since the facts presented have not expired, and after this agency has extensively warned about the need to regularize the video surveillance system subject to the complaint, as well as being informed of the content of the current Article 14, paragraph 2 of Law 39/2015 (October 1) regarding the obligation of notifications by electronic means, the processing of this procedure is initiated, transferring to it the actions carried out within the framework of ***PROCEDURE.1.  
The content of Article 5.1(c) GDPR is initially considered affected, which states: "Personal data shall be:  
c) adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary in relation to the purposes for which they are processed ('data minimization');  
It should be noted that individuals are responsible for ensuring that the installed systems comply with current legislation, demonstrating that they meet all the requirements established by the applicable regulations.  
The installation of such devices must have the mandatory informative sign, indicating the purposes and the responsible party for the processing of personal data, if applicable.

The installed cameras must be oriented towards private property, avoiding intimidation of nearby homes and/or public space with such devices.  
Under no circumstances may the cameras record images of the public thoroughfare or neighboring homes (except for access to the property), as this would be the responsibility of the State Security Forces and Bodies.  
The installed cameras must be appropriate for the intended purpose, that is, the protection of the main access to the residential area, avoiding any impact on the privacy of neighbors or nearby homes that are affected by the recording of images.  
III  
In accordance with the evidence available in this sanctioning procedure, it is considered that the respondent has a video surveillance system that disproportionately affects public space.  
The respondent, as confirmed by the Act (Site Inspection) of the Security Forces (Local Police Marbella) dispatched to the scene of the events—***ADDRESS.1 (Marbella-Málaga)—has image capture devices, which can be viewed on the owner's mobile—B.B.B.—.  
The fact that the holder of the hospitality activity has a terrace license does not justify the installation of a video surveillance system that monitors the width of public space, as this is a task reserved for the Local Security Forces and Bodies, who are responsible for monitoring public space.  
The known facts constitute an infringement attributable to the respondent, classified under Article 5.1(c) GDPR, previously cited, as one of them controls adjacent public space to one of its establishments without justified cause for doing so.  
Article 72, paragraph 1(a) establishes a statute of limitations of three years for "infringements that constitute a substantial violation of the articles mentioned therein and, in particular, the following:  
"a) The processing of personal data in violation of the principles and guarantees established in Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
IV  
Item, the establishment lacks an informative sign indicating that it is a video-monitored area, such that passersby are unaware that it is a video-monitored area, with their data being processed by a conveniently disguised camera.

The facts presented constitute a violation of Article 13 of the GDPR, as there is a lack of a duly approved informational sign indicating the data controller or the purpose(s) of the processing carried out with the device in question.  
Article 22 of Organic Law 3/2018 (December 5) - LOPDGDD - states: “1. Natural or legal persons, public or private, may carry out the processing of images through camera or video camera systems for the purpose of ensuring the safety of persons and property, as well as their facilities (...).”  
4. The duty of information provided for in Article 12 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 shall be deemed fulfilled by placing an informational device in a sufficiently visible location identifying, at a minimum, the existence of the processing, the identity of the controller, and the possibility of exercising the rights provided for in Articles 15 to 22 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. A connection code or internet address to this information may also be included in the informational device.  
In any case, the data controller must keep the information referred to in the aforementioned regulation available to the affected parties.  
The AEPD, in a related report, stipulates that it is not necessary for the signs to be placed directly below the cameras. It is sufficient to place them in a visible location that includes both open and closed spaces where the video camera circuit is operational.  
This sign shall be displayed in a visible location, and at a minimum, at the entrances to the monitored areas, whether indoors or outdoors.  
In the event that the monitored space has several entrances, such a sign indicating the monitored area must be provided at each of them.  
It is reminded that the cameras must be oriented (for example, in the case of the store) at most towards the façade to avoid, as a guideline, the breaking of the display window or towards the main access points, while the interior cameras must be marked with an informational sign indicating their presence to potential customers.  
Article 72 letter h) of the current LO 3/2018 (LOPDGDD) states, “According to what is established in Article 83.5 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the following infringements are considered very serious and shall prescribe after three years for substantial violations of the articles mentioned therein, particularly the following:  
h) The omission of the duty to inform the affected party about the processing of their personal data in accordance with the provisions of Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and 12 of this organic law (...).

In motivating the sanction, the following is taken into account:

- the intentionality or negligence in the infringement; (art. 83.2 b) GDPR), when proceeding to obtain images from a public area, whose jurisdiction is reserved for the State Security Forces and Bodies, being considered a serious negligence for the reasons stated.  
It is noted that against the respondent, ***PROCEDURE.1 has been processed, being aware of it when filing a request for reconsideration against the Director's Resolution dated 04/04/22, as well as that preliminary inquiries were carried out by the Local Police of the locality, without any corrective measure or written document aimed at correcting the detected irregularities having been submitted to this Agency to date.  
The cameras are oriented towards a public transit area, exceeding the necessary capture angle for the protection of the establishment, affecting the rights of third parties who feel intimidated by them as they consider themselves to be recorded by them, without the presence of an informative sign(s) in a visible area regarding this matter.  
Thus, it is deemed appropriate to impose a sanction amounting to €2000 (Two thousand euros) for the infringement of art. 5.1 c) GDPR and €1000 (One thousand euros) for the infringement of art. 13 GDPR, due to the lack of the required signage, with the total sum of both amounts being €3000 (Three thousand euros), a sanction situated at the lower end of the scale for this type of behavior.  
VI
