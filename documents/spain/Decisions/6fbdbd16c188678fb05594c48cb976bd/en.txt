RESOLUTION OF FILE OF PROCEEDINGS  
From the proceedings carried out by the Spanish Agency for Data Protection and based on the following:  

FACTS  
FIRST: On September 6, 2022, a complaint was filed with the Spanish Agency for Data Protection against the responsible party of the website sexchapero.com (hereinafter, the complained party).  
The reasons for the complaint are as follows:  
Complaint transferred from the Catalan Agency for Consumer Affairs against the responsible party of the website sexchapero.com, in which the complainant's phone number and first name appeared included in an advertisement published on the site. It expressly denounces that advertisements can be published without verifying the published phone number.  
Relevant documentation provided by the complainant:  
- Screenshot showing the complainant's phone number published on https://sexchapero.com.  

SECOND: On October 24, 2022, in accordance with Article 65 of the LOPDGDD, the complaint filed was admitted for processing.  

THIRD: The General Sub-Directorate of Data Inspection proceeded to carry out preliminary investigative actions to clarify the facts in question, by virtue of the functions assigned to control authorities in Article 57.1 and the powers granted in Article 58.1 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter GDPR), and in accordance with the provisions established in Title VII, Chapter I, Section two, of the LOPDGDD, being aware of the following points:  
There is no record in the complaint that the complainant has attempted to exercise their right to erasure using the contact form or via WhatsApp messaging, communication channels offered by the website.  
Requested from the registrar of the domain "sexchapero.com", IONOS CLOUD S.L.U., the ownership data of this domain, on November 14, 2022, a written response to the request was received by this Agency informing that the owner of the referred domain is A.A.A. with NIF ***NIF.1.  
Requested from the owner of the website in question to inform this Agency of the data held in their systems regarding the owner of the advertisement, on November 17, 2022, a written response was received by this Agency informing of the following aspects:  
- On August 10, 2022, they received an email sent by the complainant requesting the removal of the phone number from the advertisement, and it was immediately removed.  
- They inform of the data they have, including the date and time of registration, the session IP, and an email address from the GMAIL service.  
On November 15, 2022, a search by phone number was conducted on the website of the advertisement mentioned in the complaint, with no results found.  
Requested the ownership of the IP at the time of the advertisement registration from VODAFONE SPAIN, S.A.U., operator of the internet address provided by the responsible party of the complained website, on February 20, 2023, a written response was received by this Agency from this entity stating that "The IP addresses requested by the Agency are dynamic, not static, which implies that in order to identify the owner of a dynamic IP, it is necessary to access traffic data. For such purposes, traffic data is excluded from the duty of collaboration, in accordance with the provisions of Article 52.3 of Organic Law 3/2018, of December 5, on Personal Data Protection and the guarantee of digital rights, which includes this exclusion and reserves the transfer of data solely for the fulfillment of the obligations provided for in Law 25/2007, of October 18, on the retention of data related to electronic communications and public communications networks, and with prior judicial authorization requested by one of the authorized agents."  
Due to the above, and since the IP address of the advertisement is the only available data that could lead to the identification of the author of the publication of the complainant's personal data, this inspection has not been able to determine such authorship.  
Regarding the causes that have made the reported facts possible, it is observed in the advertiser registration form that the phone number is not mandatory, which implies that the website's registration system will not perform any verification of the authenticity of the provided phone number. However, a simulation was carried out by entering the non-existent phone number "***TELÉFONO.1", verifying that the registration was completed without any verification of the entered phone number. It has also been verified that it is possible to complete the registration without activating the switch for "I accept the terms of use and privacy policy" at the end of the form.

III  
Legality of Processing  
The physical image of a person, according to Article 4.1 of the GDPR, is personal data and its protection is therefore subject to this Regulation.  
Article 4.2 of the GDPR defines "processing" as: “any operation or set of operations performed on personal data or sets of personal data, whether by automated means or not, such as collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation or alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, communication by transmission, dissemination or any other form of enabling access, comparison or interconnection, restriction, erasure, or destruction.”  
Including images that identify or make identifiable a person in published videos constitutes processing of personal data and, therefore, the person doing so must rely on one of the legitimizing grounds indicated in Article 6 of the GDPR.  
In these cases, as in the case subject to the complaint, the usual legitimizing ground is generally consent. And it is the person who uploads the personal data and the images that can be viewed without any limitation who must demonstrate that they have that consent.  
For this processing to be carried out lawfully, it must comply with the provisions of Article 6.1 of the GDPR, which states:  
“1. Processing shall be lawful only if at least one of the following conditions is met:  
a) the data subject has given consent to the processing of their personal data for one or more specific purposes;  
b) the processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is party or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract;  
c) the processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller is subject;  
d) the processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of another natural person;  
e) the processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in the controller;  
f) the processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party, except where such interests are overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject which require protection of personal data, in particular when the data subject is a child.  
The provision in letter f) of the first paragraph shall not apply to processing carried out by public authorities in the performance of their tasks.”

IV  
Conclusion  
The claim presented was directed against the responsible party of the website sexchapero.com; the express reason was that advertisements could be published without verifying the published phone number.  
Upon reviewing the privacy policy of the respondent, it is confirmed that, in the advertiser registration form for services, the phone number is not mandatory, so the website's registration system will not perform any checks on the authenticity of the provided phone number.  
The advertisement containing personal data has been removed by the responsible party of the website indicated in the complaint.  
Even with the cooperation of the responsible party of the website where the advertisement was published, it has not been possible to determine the authorship of the contested publication (the claimant suspects it was their ex-partner) because the operator has not provided the ownership of the session IP of the publication, citing Law 25/2007, of October 18, on the retention of data related to electronic communications and public communications networks, as this IP is dynamic and three months have passed between the publication of the advertisement and the receipt of the request for identification of the owner.  
In accordance with the above, the Director of the Spanish Agency for Data Protection, IT IS AGREED:  
FIRST: TO PROCEED TO FILE the present proceedings.  
SECOND: TO NOTIFY this resolution to the respondent and to the claimant.  
In accordance with the provisions of Article 50 of the LOPDGDD, this Resolution will be made public once it has been notified to the interested parties.
