**Subject: Decision of the President for the assignment of responsibilities of a single-member body to members of the Authority**  

THE PRESIDENT  
OF THE PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION AUTHORITY  

Having regard to:  
1. Article 17 paragraphs 1 and 2 of Law 4624/2017 "Personal Data Protection Authority, measures for the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of April 27, 2016, on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and the incorporation into national legislation of Directive (EU) 2016/680 of the European Parliament and of the Council of April 27, 2016, and other provisions." (A' 137).  
2. Article 4 paragraph 3 of the Operating Regulation of the Personal Data Protection Authority (decision of the Authority 9/2022 (Government Gazette B' 879) as amended by the decisions of the Authority 13/2022 (Government Gazette B’ 1245), 69/2022 (Government Gazette B’ 635/2023), and 35/2024 (Government Gazette B' 5966)).  
3. The fact that the provisions of this decision do not incur any expense against the state budget.  

**DECIDES**  

1. The assignment of the following responsibilities of the Complaints Departments A and B:  
   a) Archiving cases of complaints that are deemed inadmissible, vague, or manifestly unfounded.  
   b) Examination of individual cases with minor impact on the rights and freedoms of natural persons, for which it is deemed that corrective measures are not required and which are processed by sending recommendations or suggestions to the complainant.  
   c) Instruction for the promotion of cross-border cases in the IMI system.  
   d) Submission of opinions on cross-border cases in which the Authority is involved as a concerned Authority.  
   e) Approval of a draft decision in cross-border cases in which the Authority is the lead authority, when no significant issues arise according to the above case b.  
   To the members of the Authority, as follows:  
   1. Sp. Vlachopoulos, Regular Member of the Authority, on cases under the jurisdiction of Complaints Department A concerning public order and national defense, public administration, education, and research.  
   2. N. Libos, Alternate Member of the Authority, on cases under the jurisdiction of Complaints Department A concerning health, social security, private insurance, and labor relations.  
   3. Chr. Kalloniatis, Regular Member of the Authority, on cases under the jurisdiction of Complaints Department A concerning video surveillance systems.  
   4. M. Psalla, Alternate Member of the Authority, on cases under the jurisdiction of Complaints Department B concerning the financial sector.  
   5. Gr. Tsolias, Regular Member of the Authority, on cases under the jurisdiction of Complaints Department B concerning the private economy.  
   6. Char. Anthopoulos, Regular Member of the Authority, on cases under the jurisdiction of Complaints Department B concerning the media.  
   7. K. Lamprinoudakis, Regular Member of the Authority, on cases under the jurisdiction of Complaints Department B concerning electronic communications, including unsolicited communications.  

2. The assignment of the following responsibilities of the Audits and Security Department:  
   a) Instruction for the announcement of a data breach incident to the data subject (Article 34 paragraph 4 GDPR).  
   b) Sending recommendations to the data controller, after examining a case of notification of a data breach incident, with which the examination of the case is concluded.  
   c) Sending recommendations to the complainant, after examining a case conducted with the assistance of the Authority's audit department or independently by this department, with which the examination of the case is concluded and archived simultaneously. These cases also include the Authority's ex officio audits.  

To D. Bougioukas, Alternate Member of the Authority, and in case of his impediment or absence, to G. Kontis, Alternate Member of the Authority.  

3. The assignment of the following responsibilities of the Advisory - Consulting Project and Compliance Means Department:  
   a) Providing an opinion in accordance with Article 13 paragraph 1 c of Law 4624/2019.  
   b) Providing the Authority's opinion on cases with subject matter that falls within the jurisdiction of the Advisory - Consulting Project and Compliance Means Department which are not explicitly mentioned in Article 4 of the Authority's Operating Regulation.  
   To Aik. Iliadou, Regular Member of the Authority, and in case of her impediment or absence, to Chr. Papadopoulos, Alternate Member of the Authority, and in case of his impediment or absence, to N. Faldamis, Alternate Member of the Authority.  

The above responsibilities may also be exercised by the President of the Authority and in case of his impediment or absence by his Deputy.  

This decision shall be posted on the Authority's online portal.
