ISSUES THE FOLLOWING OPINION

A) General Observations

1. The Authority notes that the content of Article 11 of Law 4727/2020, which regulates the basic principles of the use of the PN, came to its attention with the posting of the provisions for voting in public consultation. At the initiative of the Authority, information and details regarding the key elements of the planned regulation were requested. This was followed by an exchange of clarifying documents during the period of July-September 2020 (see documents with protocol no. G/EXE/5099/20-07-2020, G/EIS/5571/10-08-2020, G/EXE/5571-1/13-08-2020, and G/EIS/6117/09-09-2020). It is noted that the law was submitted and voted on without a request for or issuance of an Opinion from the Authority, while clarifications on questions that had been raised were still pending.
4

2. The design choice to maintain the required data of all citizens in the PA registry results in the centralization of data such as full name, patronymic, matronymic, place of birth, as well as all sectoral identifiers used by citizens for various purposes in different sectors (Tax Identification Number, Identity Card Number, Social Security Registration Number). It is emphasized that if the personal data "name – surname – patronymic – matronymic – date of birth - place of birth" were updated in all individual registries (which is considered a basic prerequisite for the proper functioning of the PA as a method of identity verification), then a different architecture could have been considered, and it would not have been necessary to maintain all individual sectoral identifiers in the central PA registry. This issue had already been pointed out by the Authority in the past, even prior to the GDPR (see the Authority's Annual Report for 2012, p. 61), and it was reiterated during the aforementioned exchange of documents prior to the voting of Law 4727/2020 (see the Authority's document with reference number G/EX/5571-1/13-08-2020).

3. Both from the law and from the draft Presidential Decree, it appears that the PA is introduced as a necessary element of the identity verification service for natural persons to public sector entities. The PA registry allows for the matching of the PA with the identification numbers of the individual registries (sectoral identifiers) of each public entity and the correct identification of the citizen through an easily memorable number, but without this number being maintained in the individual registries. Given the purpose of the processing, the Authority considers that the processing concerning the PA registry complies with the processing principles of Article 5(1) of the GDPR. It is emphasized that, for the proper implementation of this design, it must be ensured that this purpose will remain in the future as the sole purpose of processing by all public (as well as other non-public) entities (based on the principle of purpose limitation). Any future processing of the PA for a different purpose will require legislative regulation and an assessment of the legality of the new processing.

5

4. It is particularly noted that up to the stage of issuing the opinion and after inquiries from the Authority, it was stated that there is no provision for a public document in which the PA will be recorded (such as an identity card or other). A corresponding provision is not found in the draft Presidential Decree nor in the Law. The Authority points out that it is issuing an opinion on the Presidential Decree and assesses the level of protection of the use of the PA as personal data in the context of the aforementioned processing (adhering to the principle of minimization), with the assumption that it is not recorded in any public document.

5. It is also noted that, with the establishment of the PA (and after the proper functioning of this registry and the related interoperability processes), the need for public entities to maintain multiple sectoral identifiers for citizens ceases (i.e., a public entity maintaining more than one sectoral identifier for each citizen), as this would contradict the principle of minimization. Therefore, it is proposed that the long-term design should foresee: a) the adaptation of public registries to the logic of maintaining only one sectoral identifier per entity (and thus, where multiple identifiers are currently maintained, the applications should be redesigned to eliminate other unnecessary sectoral identifiers) and b) to exclude, at a time to be appropriately determined by the design of the ΥΨΔ, the possibility of transmitting by the ΥΨΔ to public entities sectoral identifiers different from the identifier corresponding to the sector of each entity, an activity which (as interoperability) falls under the jurisdiction of the ΥΨΔ. A corresponding regulation could be foreseen similar to paragraph 2 of Article 8 of the draft Presidential Decree.

6. From the provisions of the draft Presidential Decree presented to the Authority, there is no design regarding the potential role of the PA as an identification data as provided by the eIDAS Regulation, or regarding its relationship with other identifiers.

B) On the provisions of the draft Presidential Decree

7. In Article 2, paragraph 5, it is stated that the PA is "issued and granted," while in the previous paragraph 4, it is only stated that it is "granted." If there is no specific justification for the differentiation, the same wording should be used in both paragraphs. Otherwise, the difference between issuance and granting should be explained in these two paragraphs.
8. In Article 3, paragraph 1, it is stated that the PA registry is maintained for the purpose of providing identity verification services for natural persons to public sector entities and the matching of PAs with the identification numbers of the registries (specific sectoral numbers) of public sector entities to achieve interoperability of the information systems of these entities through the Interoperability Center of the G.G.P.S.D., according to Article 84 of Law 4727/2020. The reference to achieving interoperability of the information systems of the entities, as part of the purpose of processing, does not seem to be correct. The purpose of the PA is specific and particular, concerning the "provision of identity verification services for natural persons to public sector entities and the matching of PAs with the identification numbers of the registries (specific sectoral numbers) of public sector entities." This is implemented through the Interoperability Center of the G.G.P.S.D., according to Article 84 of Law 4727/2020, but achieving interoperability of the information systems of the public sector does not require the use of the PA, nor is it a purpose of the law and the Presidential Decree. Moreover, for achieving interoperability, the correct matching of the sectoral identifiers of the entities is sufficient, which can be implemented without the use of the PA. Therefore, it is proposed to delete the reference to the above purpose or to accurately rephrase the provision.
9. The above issue regarding the stated purpose of processing is also found in Article 7, paragraph 1 of the draft Presidential Decree. Additionally, the references in Article 7, paragraph 1 should be rephrased as security objectives.
10. In Article 3, paragraph 6, it is stated that "6. Access to the PA Registry is permitted exclusively to authorized employees of the G.G.P.S.D." This specific provision concerns security measures and it is proposed to include it in Article 7, paragraph 4 of the draft Presidential Decree, which addresses issues of personal data protection, and specifically security measures.
11. In Article 7, paragraph 2, a specific legal basis for processing is provided based on Article 6, paragraph 1 of the GDPR, and it mentions the fulfillment of a duty performed in the public interest and in the exercise of public authority, according to point (e) of paragraph 1 of Article 6 of the GDPR. It is noted that the legal basis is grounded in Law 4727/2020, with Article 11 establishing the PA, and thus, it is inferred that the most appropriate legal basis is point (c) of paragraph 1 of Article 6 of the GDPR. In any case, it is proposed to remove the reference to the legal basis for processing, as it does not need to be mentioned in the Presidential Decree and may lead to confusion. This, of course, does not relieve the G.G.P.S.D., as the data controller, from the obligation to provide data subjects with information regarding the legal basis for processing, according to the provisions of Article 13, paragraph 1, point (c) of the GDPR.
12. The reference in paragraph 3 of Article 7 of the draft Presidential Decree to the role of the data controller is also considered unnecessary, as it is already defined in Law 4727/2020, Article 11, paragraph 4, and Article 107, paragraph 6.
13. In Article 7, paragraph 4, the mentioned risks do not include all those described in Recital 83 of the GDPR: "...accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure or access to personal data...". Although the reference to risks is indicative, it is proposed to suitably complete it or to remove it, as in each
8

In the event, the data controller is obliged to ensure the protection of data from risks, in accordance with the explicitly defined provisions of the GDPR.  
14. In Article 7, paragraph 4, subparagraph d), it is proposed to replace the designation "reliable" for the network with "trusted," and to supplement the provision with a reference that "any data exchange through electronic means is carried out, either via the aforementioned trusted network, or by encrypting the data to be sent. Acceptable international standards are used for encryption, with a secure key size."  
15. Article 7, paragraph 4, subparagraph g) refers to the application of data classification techniques and pseudonymization. It is proposed that the content of the reference to classification techniques be explained and specified, as well as which data from the PA registry can undergo pseudonymization for the stated purpose of processing. Otherwise, it is proposed to remove these references.  
16. Article 7, paragraph 5 refers to the access of authorized employees of the K.E.P. to the special application myInfo. It is proposed to examine whether this paragraph should be omitted from the Presidential Decree and included in the Joint Ministerial Decision regarding the myInfo application.  
17. Article 7, paragraph 9 states that public sector entities assign identifying numbers from their registries to natural persons, in accordance with the legislative framework governing each entity and the assignment of special identifying numbers, ensuring the interoperability of their registries with the Interoperability Center (K.E.D.) of the G.G.P.S.D.. This specific reference does not seem to have a place in the Presidential Decree, as it describes a processing activity that is already in effect, does not concern the data controller, and does not introduce any change relevant to the PA.  
18. Article 7, paragraph 10 includes the provision for the retention period of the data of the PA Registry with reference to paragraph 6 of Article 3 regarding its deactivation. However, the reference to the regulation and the terms of deactivation is described in Article 2, paragraph 6. It is proposed to correct the reference. The same erroneous reference to paragraph 6 of Article 3 is also found in paragraph 5 of Article 3.

Article 1
Purpose - Subject
This Presidential Decree establishes:
α) the procedure for the issuance and granting of the Personal Number (hereinafter P.A.), the required supporting documents and the control services thereof, the timeline for its issuance, and the reasons for its suspension,
β) the specific technical and organizational issues for the maintenance of the Personal Number Registry,
γ) the terms and the procedure for providing identity verification services of natural persons to public sector entities and for the matching of P.A. with the identification numbers of the registries (specific sectoral numbers) of public sector entities, as defined in paragraph 57 of Article 2 of Law 4727/2020 (A’ 184).
δ) the measures, guarantees, and security mechanisms for the prevention and addressing of risks for the protection of personal data of natural persons from the processing of the P.A., as well as the technical and organizational measures taken by the General Secretariat for Information Systems and Digital Governance (G.G.P.S.Ψ.D.) and the public sector entities for this purpose.
**Article 2**  
**Personal Number (P.N.) - Beneficiaries**  
1. The P.N. is a mandatory element for the verification of the identity of individuals in their transactions with public sector entities.  
2. The P.N. consists of twelve (12) alphanumeric characters, of which at least nine (9) are numeric. To form the P.N., three (3) alphanumeric characters are generated using the procedure provided in Article 4 of this document, followed by the nine (9) numeric characters of the Tax Identification Number (hereinafter T.I.N.) of each individual at the time of issuance of the P.N.  
3. Alphanumeric characters that may be used to form the P.N. are understood to be only the digits zero (0) to nine (9), as well as the uppercase letters of the Greek alphabet that have corresponding letters in the Latin alphabet.  
4. The P.N. is granted once to each individual, is unique, and does not change during their lifetime.  
5. The P.N. is issued and granted mandatorily to every individual entitled to a T.I.N. or Social Security Registration Number (S.S.R.N.), in accordance with the respective tax and insurance legislation.  
6. The P.N. becomes inactive in the following cases:  
   a) upon the death or declaration of disappearance of the individual,  
   b) if the legal conditions for the issuance of a P.N. cease to apply according to paragraphs 4 and 5 of this article.  

**Article 3**  
**Personal Number Registry (P.N. Registry)**  
1. The General Secretariat for Information Systems and Digital Governance (G.S.I.S.D.G.) of the Ministry of Digital Governance develops and maintains the Personal Number Registry (P.N. Registry), with the purpose of providing identity verification services for individuals to public sector entities and matching P.N.s with the identification numbers of the registries (specific sectoral numbers) of public sector entities to achieve interoperability of the information systems of these entities through the Interoperability Center of G.S.I.S.D.G., in accordance with Article 84 of Law 4727/2020.  
2. The P.N. Registry includes only the data that are absolutely necessary for the verification of the identity of the individual and the achievement of the purposes of this document, specifically:  
   a) Personal number (P.N.)  
   b) First name  
   c) Last name  
   d) Father's name  
   e) Mother's name  
   f) Date of Birth  
   g) Place of Birth  
   h) Tax Identification Number (T.I.N.)  
   i) Identity Card Number, if the individual possesses one  
   j) Social Security Registration Number (S.S.R.N.), if the individual possesses one.  
3. The above data is obtained through interoperability with the following public sector registries:  
   a) Citizens Registry of the Ministry of Interior  
   b) Tax Registry of the Ministry of Finance maintained by the Independent Authority for Public Revenue (I.A.P.R.)  
   c) S.S.R.N. Registry maintained by the Electronic Governance of Social Security S.A. (E.G.S.S.)  
   d) Identity Card Registry of Greek Citizens maintained by the Hellenic Police (H.P.) of the Ministry of Citizen Protection  
   e) Third Country Citizens Registry maintained by the Ministry of Migration  
   f) Asylum Registry maintained by the Ministry of Migration.  
4. The above data is collected and maintained securely, under the conditions specified in Article 6 of this document.  
5. In cases where the P.N. becomes inactive, according to paragraph 6 of Article 3 of this document, a relevant indication is recorded in the P.N. Registry.  
6. Access to the P.N. Registry is permitted exclusively to authorized employees of G.S.I.S.D.G.

Registry.

Article 7
Issues of personal data protection
1. The purpose of processing personal data within the framework of this document is to verify the identity of individuals and to achieve interoperability of the information systems of the competent authorities through the Interoperability Center. This purpose is achieved by (a) facilitating and enhancing security when individuals access digital services provided by public sector entities, (b) developing interoperable systems with valid, accurate, and up-to-date information about individuals interacting with the public sector, and (c) avoiding repetitive entries from different points with increased administrative costs and risk of errors for the individual and the public (application of the "once only" principle).

2. The processing of personal data of individuals is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest and in the exercise of public authority, in accordance with point e) of paragraph 1 of Article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of April 27, 2016, on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter G.D.P.R.), in conjunction with Article 11 of Law 4727/2020 (A’ 184) and this Decree.

3. For the purposes of applying this Decree, the G.G.P.S.Y.D. of the Ministry of Digital Governance is the Data Controller of the personal data as defined in paragraph 7 of Article 4 of the G.D.P.R. maintained in the P.A. Registry. In this context, the Ministry of Digital Governance takes appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure and demonstrate that the processing is carried out lawfully, particularly in accordance with the G.D.P.R., Law 4624/2019 (A’137), and the applicable Information Systems Security Framework of the G.G.P.S.Y.D., as in force.

4. The G.G.P.S.Y.D. is obliged to take and continuously maintain appropriate and necessary technical and organizational security measures for the P.A. Registry of Article 3 of this document, ensuring in particular the protection of data from unauthorized or unlawful processing and accidental loss, destruction, or damage. The above measures are specified in the impact assessment of the processing on the protection of personal data and include at least the following:
   a. Distinct user roles in the system and corresponding access rights, at least for aa) viewing, bb) data extraction, cc) data deletion, dd) granting authorization for data extraction, ee) assigning roles to each user, ff) technical maintenance of the system, and gg) monitoring user actions.
   b. Creation of user accounts and authentication methods based on the Information Systems Security Policy of the G.G.P.S.Y.D.
   c. Access to the data of the Registry only by authorized and appropriately trained users, according to the role assigned to them.
   d. User access only from a registered number of terminals and using a reliable network.

6. The provision of online services is conducted through the Interoperability Center of the General Secretariat for Digital Governance, in accordance with its Information Systems Security Policy, the Policy for Proper Use of online services, as well as the provisions for the protection of personal data.  
7. Recipients of the data from the Public Registry are public sector entities for the provision of services to individuals and the exercise of the powers that have been legally assigned to them.  
8. Public sector entities process the Public Registry as independent data controllers, through the Interoperability Center of the General Secretariat for Digital Governance, taking the necessary technical and organizational measures, with the sole purpose of verifying the identity of individuals for the provision of services to natural and legal persons, the general handling of individuals' cases, and the exercise of their powers, without collecting and storing them in their information systems or filing systems.  
9. Public sector entities assign identification numbers from their registries to individuals, in accordance with the legislative framework governing each entity and the assignment of special identification numbers, ensuring the interoperability of their registries with the Interoperability Center (K.E.D.) of the General Secretariat for Digital Governance.  
10. The data of the Public Registry are retained for the entire period that the Public Registry is active and for a period of twenty (20) years from its deactivation, in accordance with paragraph 6 of Article 3 of this document, in the context of fulfilling a duty performed in the public interest and in the exercise of the special public authority for maintaining the Public Registry assigned to the General Secretariat for Digital Governance based on this document, and under the conditions provided in the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Law 4624/2019 (A’ 137), as applicable. After the expiration of the aforementioned period, the data is destroyed. The General Secretariat for Digital Governance ensures that the data is destroyed in a manner that guarantees it cannot be recovered.  

Article 8  
Timeline - Transitional Provisions  
1. Individuals who will issue a Public Registry through the process provided in Articles 4 and 5 of this decree within three (3) months from the publication of this document have the option to choose two (2) of the three (3) alphanumeric digits of their Public Registry and those of their minor dependents, instead of the digits being randomly generated by the General Secretariat for Digital Governance. The selection is made through the special electronic application myInfo either via the Single Digital Portal of Public Administration (gov.gr-EΨΠ) or through the Citizen Service Centers (K.E.P). By decision of the Ministers of Finance, Labor and Social Affairs, and Digital Governance, this deadline may be extended for a period not exceeding a total of six (6) months.  
2. Until the adaptation of the procedures and information systems of public sector entities to achieve their interoperability with the data of the Public Registry and in any case for a period not exceeding twelve (12) months from the publication of this document, existing procedures for verifying the identity of individuals may continue to be applied by public sector entities, specifically through identification using Tax Identification Number (ΑΦΜ), Social Security Number (ΑΜΚΑ), and Greek Citizen Identity Card. By decision of the Ministers of Finance, Labor and Social Affairs, and Digital Governance, the deadline may be extended for a period not exceeding a total of twelve (12) months.  

Article 9  
Commencement of Effect  
The validity of this presidential decree begins from its publication in the Government Gazette.  
We assign the Minister of Digital Governance with the publication and execution of this decree.
