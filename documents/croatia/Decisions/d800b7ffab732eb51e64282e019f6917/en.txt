The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data will urgently initiate supervisory proceedings against the Croatian Health Insurance Fund based on reports from the media regarding a possible cyber attack. 

We hereby warn citizens to immediately delete e-mails they receive from the “Croatian Health Insurance Fund” from the e-mail address podataka.zastita@hzzo.hr and not to respond to them, open attachments, or click on links provided within the e-mail. 

Advice on how to recognize internet fraud and phishing e-mails can be found at the following link: https://azop.hr/phishing-napadi-kako-ih-prepoznati-i-zastititi-se/
