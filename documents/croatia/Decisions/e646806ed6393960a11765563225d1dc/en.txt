Are you familiar with the obligations set forth by the General Data Protection Regulation? Are you unsure who the data controller is and who the data processor is? What is the role of the data protection officer, and do you need a privacy policy? Do you know what measures you must take to ensure an appropriate level of security for the processing of personal data? If you are unsure of the answers to these and many other GDPR questions, then the workshop is just for you. 

The Agency for Personal Data Protection invites primarily data protection officers from the public and private sectors, as well as anyone else interested in how to align their business with the GDPR, to a free online workshop titled “GDPR – Basic Module,” which will take place on January 30, 2023, starting at 10:00 AM.

The workshop is primarily intended for data protection officers, but also for anyone else who wants to learn the basics of the General Data Protection Regulation, inform themselves about their obligations, as well as their rights. Additionally, the workshop is an opportunity to ask specific questions to the Agency's experts and resolve your uncertainties.
