**Celebrate with us the 20th anniversary of personal data protection in the Republic of Croatia and the establishment of the Agency for Personal Data Protection!**

On the occasion of the 20th anniversary of personal data protection in the Republic of Croatia and the establishment of the Agency for Personal Data Protection, we are pleased to announce a conference titled "Risks and Compliance with GDPR in the Age of Artificial Intelligence," organized by the Agency for Personal Data Protection.  
Conference website: https://20anniversary.azop.hr/  
We invite all data controllers/processors, data protection officers, data protection experts, citizens, and all interested parties to participate in the conference on Friday, May 24, 2024, at the Esplanade Hotel (Emerald Hall), Zagreb from 10:00 AM to 2:30 PM.  
All places for the conference are filled. Registrations are no longer possible.  

In the first part of the conference titled "20 Years of Personal Data Protection in the Republic of Croatia," we expect presentations from government officials, representatives of the European Commission, the European Data Protection Board (EDPB), the European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS), and the OECD.  
In the second part of the conference titled "Results of the EU Project ARC II: Supporting Croatian and Italian Entrepreneurs in Compliance with the Legal Framework for Data Protection," the focus will be on micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises. Despite nearly six years having passed since the implementation of the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area, many small and medium-sized enterprises still face challenges regarding compliance, primarily due to limited human and financial resources. During this part of the conference, we will present the activities and results of the ARC II project, with a special emphasis on the innovative digital tool "Olivia," designed to facilitate the compliance process with the General Data Protection Regulation for entrepreneurs.  
We will conclude the conference with a panel discussion titled "The Interaction Between the Artificial Intelligence Act and GDPR: How to Develop Human-Centric Artificial Intelligence." During this panel discussion, conference participants will have the opportunity to deepen their understanding of the legal and ethical aspects related to the development and use of artificial intelligence and the protection of fundamental rights and freedoms.  
Join us at the conference and celebrate with us the 20th anniversary of personal data protection in the Republic of Croatia and the establishment of the Agency for Personal Data Protection.  
We look forward to your attendance!
