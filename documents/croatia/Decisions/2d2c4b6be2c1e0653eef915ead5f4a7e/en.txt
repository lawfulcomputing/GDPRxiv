The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data submitted a request to the supervisory authority for the protection of personal data of the Republic of Serbia – the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and the Protection of Personal Data, to initiate an urgent supervisory procedure due to the unlawful public disclosure of personal data of Croatian citizens in a Serbian media outlet.  
During a live appearance on the Serbian television channel Informer, convicted war criminal Vojislav Šešelj publicly disclosed the personal data of Croatian citizens, and their identity cards were displayed on the screen. Due to the scandalous actions of the Serbian media and the unlawful processing and disclosure of personal data of Croatian citizens, the Agency requested urgent supervisory action from the Serbian supervisory authority.
