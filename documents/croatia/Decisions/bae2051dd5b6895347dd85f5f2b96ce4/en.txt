The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data is organizing a free three-day training for data protection officers in the public health sector, the program of which has been developed within the framework of the SPE (Support pool of experts) program of the European Data Protection Board. The training will take place from October 23 to October 25, 2024, at the premises of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, Selska cesta 136, Zagreb.

Join our training where Agency officials will cover the national legal framework of the health system of the Republic of Croatia regarding data protection over two days of education, teach you how to conduct a legitimate interest assessment, determine appropriate legal bases, maintain records of processing activities, etc. This is an ideal opportunity to acquire the necessary knowledge and skills to perform your duties and receive a certificate for it, as the third day is dedicated to knowledge assessment. After successfully passing the test, you will receive a certificate confirming that you have successfully completed the training course for data protection officers in the public health sector.

Join us to enhance the standards of personal data protection in public health together! The training will be conducted exclusively in person, and the number of places is limited to 30!

Applications are accepted at the following link: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/DPO-trening-zdravstvo-listopad2024
