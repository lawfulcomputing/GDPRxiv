Director of the Agency for Personal Data Protection Zdravko Vukić and the Commissioner of the Agency for Information and Privacy of the Republic of Kosovo Krenare Sogojeva Dermaku signed a Memorandum of Cooperation last week at the premises of the AZOP, aimed at promoting European standards in the field of personal data protection.  
The Croatian and Kosovo agencies will carry out joint activities such as awareness-raising campaigns, study visits, and will mutually exchange knowledge and experiences, as well as experts who will specialize in specific sectors.  
Director Zdravko Vukić expressed satisfaction that the Agency will provide concrete knowledge and experiences from its area of work. “I am glad that with our previous experience and good practices we can contribute to your work and thus assist in your European journey.”  
“I am pleased that today we are signing this memorandum, which was one of my priorities when I was appointed Commissioner. This is a continuation of the good cooperation we have previously established and an opportunity to learn and progress,” emphasized Commissioner Krenare Sogojeva Dermaku.
