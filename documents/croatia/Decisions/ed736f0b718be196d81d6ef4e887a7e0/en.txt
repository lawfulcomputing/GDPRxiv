At the request of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, and in relation to the publication of personal data of Croatian citizens suspected of committing criminal offenses or misdemeanors on a Greek portal, the supervisory authority for the protection of personal data in Greece has initiated supervisory proceedings against the data controller, i.e., the portal in question.  
The data controller has removed the article in which the personal data of Croatian citizens were published (https://www.protothema.gr/greece/article/1401189/nea-filadelfeia-oi-katatheseis-astunomikon-gia-dolofonia-tou-mihali-mas-epitethikan-150-kroates/), and the Greek supervisory authority will continue the supervisory proceedings and will inform AZOP of all relevant information.
