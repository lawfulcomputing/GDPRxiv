Croatian Employers' Association and the Agency for Personal Data Protection invite you to a free consultation titled “How to Comply with GDPR and Prepare for AZOP Oversight?” on May 28, 2024, at Hotel Mondo, Kopilica 5, Split, starting at 10:00 AM.  
Participation in the consultation is free, but registration is mandatory via the online form: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/HUP-Split-savjetovanje  
Each participant will receive a certificate of participation.  
This consultation is an activity within the EU project ARC2, which the Agency implements with the aim of providing support to micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises in aligning their business with legal regulations on data protection.  
At the same time, this consultation marks the 6th anniversary of the full application of GDPR. The Agency emphasizes that even after 6 years, entrepreneurs still have many uncertainties regarding compliance with GDPR and wishes to provide them with concrete support to raise the level of compliance in their organizations and avoid high monetary fines prescribed for violations of GDPR.  
As part of the ARC2 project, we have created an online tool, Olivia https://olivia-gdpr-arc.eu/hr, which can help you master the basics of GDPR and create documentation with which you will prove compliance during AZOP oversight. Certificates of completed courses can serve entrepreneurs and data protection officers as proof of acquiring knowledge in the field of personal data protection.  
The consultation consists of lectures on 3 topics, and during each lecture, it is possible to ask the lecturer questions.  
Lectures will be held by one of the best experts from the Agency for Personal Data Protection, Antonio Katavić, who is also the president of the AZOP Commission for Imposing Administrative Monetary Fines.  
The aim of the General Data Protection Regulation is to protect personal data of individuals, provide citizens with control over their personal data, and create a high and uniform level of personal data protection in the European Union.  
The General Data Protection Regulation determines the rights of individuals and, accordingly, the obligations of entities processing personal data. All business entities that collect, store, and in any way process personal data of Croatian citizens and citizens from the European Economic Area are obliged to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation.  
It is high time for companies, public authorities, and all data controllers/processors to understand that the deadline for adjustment has long passed and that after 6 years since the full application of GDPR began, ignorance and lack of knowledge can no longer serve as justification and excuse for violating the fundamental rights of Croatian and European citizens—the right to personal data protection.  
The aim of organizing this consultation is to provide entrepreneurs with the opportunity to familiarize themselves with examples of good practices in the field of personal data protection, and it is also a unique opportunity to ask questions to the AZOP lecturer and resolve their uncertainties.  
This consultation will help participants acquire the knowledge they need to build a reputation for a trustworthy organization, respect the rights of their clients/service users/employees guaranteed by GDPR, and at the same time avoid high monetary fines prescribed for violators of data protection regulations.  
Do not miss this unique opportunity and register as soon as possible as the number of places is limited!
