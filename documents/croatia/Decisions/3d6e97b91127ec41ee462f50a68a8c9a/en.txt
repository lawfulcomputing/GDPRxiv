**A public competition is underway for the recruitment into the civil service for an indefinite period for 2 positions, job number 31, advisor in the Sector for Supervision, Investigations, and Protection of Respondents' Rights, Service for the Protection and Promotion of Respondents' Rights. Applications are accepted via the Centralized Employment System https://selekcija.gov.hr/natjecaji/objavljeni-natjecaji until June 21, 2025.**

**Professional Requirements:**

- HKO Level: Level 7.1.st HKO or 7.1.sv HKO (University Degree)

- Field of Social Sciences, Law

- At least 3 years of work experience in relevant positions

**Additional Requirements:**

- Passed the state exam of the II level

---

**In addition to the specified professional requirements, candidates must also meet the general requirements for recruitment into the civil service, which are:**

- Health capacity to perform the tasks of the position for which they are being recruited
- Croatian citizenship, unless otherwise stipulated (citizen of a member state of the European Economic Area or the Swiss Confederation, and a citizen of a third country under the conditions prescribed by Article 52 of the Civil Servants Act)

---

**Job Description:**
- Implementation of first-instance administrative procedures, monitoring administrative and judicial practice, preparing reports on the resolution of administrative matters, representing the organization in administrative disputes within the jurisdiction of the organizational unit, handling non-administrative matters.

**Coefficient for Salary Calculation for the Position:**  
1.95

A person cannot be admitted to the civil service if there are obstacles to their recruitment as per Article 54 of the Civil Servants Act. Both genders may apply for the public competition. Persons recruited into the civil service for an indefinite period are subject to a probationary period of three months.

---

**Mandatory Documentation:**

1. Electronic record or confirmation of data recorded in the database of the Croatian Pension Insurance Institute, which must contain information about the employer, basis of insurance, start and end of insurance, working hours, actual and necessary professional qualifications, and duration of insurance period.
2. Employment contract / Contract for work / Decision on assignment from which the required work experience for the position applied for is evident, or a confirmation from the employer about work experience in relevant positions, which must include the type of work performed, the professional qualifications held, and the period during which the candidate performed the specified work.
3. Proof of the appropriate level of education.

---

**The application deadline is 15 days from the publication of the public competition.**  
A candidate who indicated the need for reasonable accommodation in their user profile when applying for the competition will be provided with appropriate adjustments during the testing or interview process. A candidate who invokes the right to priority in recruitment into the civil service according to a special law is required to attach all prescribed documentation according to the special law in their user profile in the Centralized Employment System and has priority over other candidates only under equal conditions. Testing of computer-verifiable competencies will be conducted electronically, followed by the verification of competencies that are not electronically verifiable (if any) and an interview with the Commission. Testing of competencies that are not electronically verifiable and the interview will be conducted by a Commission appointed by the head of the employing body and will take place in that body. Notification of the time and place of the necessary testing and interviews will be published at least five days before the date set for testing, in the Centralized Employment System. Candidates will be informed of the results of the process through a public announcement of the decision regarding the selected candidate in the Centralized Employment System. Delivery of the decision to all candidates is considered completed upon the expiration of the eighth day from the date of publication in the Centralized Employment System. Before the decision is made, the proposed candidate is required to present the originals of the documentation, a certificate of health capacity, and a certificate from the competent court that no criminal proceedings are being conducted against the person.
