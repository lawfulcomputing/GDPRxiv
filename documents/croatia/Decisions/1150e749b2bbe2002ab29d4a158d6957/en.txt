The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data invites all citizens to free webinars on personal data protection and internet security. Although awareness of the importance of personal data protection has significantly increased in recent years, citizens still often handle their personal data carelessly, are unaware of all the rights guaranteed to them by the General Data Protection Regulation, and unfortunately often fall victim to internet fraud. These webinars are an ideal opportunity to listen to expert advice on internet security, learn to recognize potential threats, and find out how to protect your personal data and exercise your rights.

The Agency will hold webinars for citizens on the following topics until the end of 2024:

1. **Personal Data Protection: What Are My Rights?** Did you know that you have the right to access your personal data collected about you by data controllers (various companies)? Get informed about the rights guaranteed by the General Data Protection Regulation and learn how to exercise them!

2. **How to Protect Your Personal Data?** Listen to advice on how to properly protect your personal data to minimize the possibility of misuse, both online and in the real world!

3. **How to Recognize and Avoid Internet Fraud?** Given the increasing frequency of fraud and data theft, it is important to know how to protect yourself and recognize the threats that lurk online every day. Become more aware of internet threats and learn how to avoid the most common forms of online fraud.

4. **Who and When Can Process My Personal Data?** The most common misconception among citizens is that a data controller can only process their data if the citizen has given consent. However, this is not true! Learn when the processing of your personal data is lawful.

Personal data protection is crucial for preserving privacy and security in the digital environment. Find out how you can protect yourself from unauthorized access and misuse of your personal data. Let’s learn to protect our personal data!
