Ministry of Justice, Administration and Digital Transformation has submitted to the European Commission, via the Permanent Representation of the Republic of Croatia to the EU in Brussels, a list of competent authorities in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 (Act on Artificial Intelligence).  
The Regulation, which has entered into force, establishes harmonized rules on artificial intelligence, and its application, with prescribed exceptions, will begin on 2 August 2026.  
Article 77, paragraph 2 of the Act stipulates that member states must designate and publish a list of public authorities or public bodies responsible for monitoring and enforcing obligations related to the protection of fundamental rights in the use of high-risk artificial intelligence systems by 2 November 2024.  
In accordance with this obligation, the Republic of Croatia has designated the following competent authorities: the Agency for Personal Data Protection, the Ombudsman, the Ombudsman for Children, the Ombudsman for Gender Equality, the Ombudsman for Persons with Disabilities, the State Electoral Commission, and the Agency for Electronic Media.  
This step ensures the compliance of the Republic of Croatia with European rules and contributes to the protection of citizens' fundamental rights in the context of the development and application of artificial intelligence.
