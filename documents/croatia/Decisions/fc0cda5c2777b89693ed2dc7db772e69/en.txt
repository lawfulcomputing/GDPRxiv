The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data will, on January 26, 2024, for the 18th consecutive time, appropriately commemorate the European Data Protection Day. Namely, in order to raise public awareness about a fundamental human right – the right to the protection of personal data and privacy, the Council of Europe, with the support of the European Commission, declared January 28 as European Data Protection Day in 2006, which is also known worldwide as Privacy Day.  
On this occasion, the Agency is organizing a conference titled "Entrepreneurs and New Technologies," primarily aimed at small and medium-sized enterprises, which will take place on January 26, 2024, in the Dubrovnik hall, Ericsson Nikola Tesla, Krapinska 45, Zagreb.  
We are using this year's commemoration of the European Data Protection Day to present the web tool "Olivia," a virtual teacher and assistant for GDPR compliance, thanks to which entrepreneurs will have the opportunity to learn about their basic obligations, test their knowledge, and create basic documents that can demonstrate compliance with personal data protection regulations.  
Small and medium-sized enterprises often do not have sufficient financial and human resources necessary for compliance with data protection regulations, and for this reason, the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data is implementing the European project ARC II (Awareness Raising Campaign for SMEs), which aims to increase the knowledge of small and medium-sized enterprises and better understand their obligations in accordance with the GDPR and the legal framework for personal data protection. As part of this project, the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, together with partners, is developing a digital tool called "Olivia," which is free, available to everyone, and tailored to the specific needs of small and medium-sized enterprises.  
In this first phase of the project, Olivia contains 7 modules that are continuously being improved, and we invite you to visit the page https://olivia-gdpr-arc.eu/hr and try out the functionalities that Olivia currently offers.  
In the first part of the event organized on the occasion of the European Data Protection Day, participants will have the opportunity, under the guidance of mentors, data protection experts, to test the new functionalities of the web tool Olivia and ask questions related to the following modules/topics: principles of personal data processing, data protection impact assessment, records of processing activities, organizational measures, video surveillance, the relationship between the controller and the processor, and the rights of data subjects.  
In the second part of the event, a panel discussion will be held on the exciting new possibilities brought by artificial intelligence. Participants will have the opportunity to hear how technology innovations based on artificial intelligence can enhance business operations, reduce costs, and generally increase business efficiency. However, at the same time, these innovations carry numerous risks and can threaten our right to the protection of personal data, privacy, and other human rights.  
Participation is free. The event is intended exclusively for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises and craftsmen. All places for the event are filled. Registrations are no longer possible.
