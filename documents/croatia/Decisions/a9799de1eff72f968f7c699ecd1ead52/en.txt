**9.5.2025.**  
A public competition has been announced for the recruitment into civil service for a fixed term for the position number 30 Senior Advisor in the Sector for Supervision, Investigations, and Protection of Respondents' Rights, Service for the Protection and Promotion of Respondents' Rights; replacement for a long-term absent civil servant until their return.  
Applications are accepted via the Centralized Employment System https://selekcija.gov.hr/natjecaji/objavljeni-natjecaji until 24.5.2025.

**Professional Requirements:**

Level of HKO: Level 7.1 of the HKO (Higher Education)

Field of Social Sciences, Law

At least 4 years of work experience in relevant positions  
**Additional Requirements:**

Passed the state exam of the II level

In addition to the specified professional requirements, candidates must also meet the general conditions for recruitment into civil service which are:

- Health capacity to perform the tasks of the position for which they are being recruited
- Croatian citizenship, unless otherwise stipulated (citizen of a member state of the European Economic Area or the Swiss Confederation, as well as a citizen of a third country under the conditions prescribed by Article 52 of the Civil Servants Act)

**Competencies:**

| Name | Level of Outcome Development | Weight |
|------|-----------------------------|--------|
| Logic | High level of complexity | 40 |
| Digital Literacy | Medium level of complexity | 20 |
| Written Communication in English | Medium level of complexity | 20 |
| Responsibility in Work and Task Orientation | High level of complexity | 20 |

**Job Description of the Position:**  
Implementation of first-instance administrative procedures, monitoring administrative and judicial practice, preparation of reports on the resolution of administrative matters, conducting inspection supervision, taking and determining measures and actions that need to be taken after the conducted inspection procedure, representing the organization in administrative disputes within the scope of the organizational unit, handling non-administrative matters.  
Coefficient for salary calculation for the position:  
2.1  
A person cannot be admitted to civil service if there are obstacles to their recruitment as per Article 54 of the Civil Servants Act.  
Both genders may apply for the public competition.  
A person is admitted to civil service with a mandatory probationary period of two months if the expected duration of the fixed-term service is at least six months.
