July 16, 2024  
CALL FOR TESTING  
to candidates whose applications are timely and complete and  
who meet the formal requirements from the public competition  
for employment in the civil service for an indefinite period in  
the Agency for Personal Data Protection in the Sector for  
supervision, investigations, and protection of the rights of data subjects, Service for  
investigation supervision, technology, and security, job position  
(24) – Advisor in the Agency for Personal Data  
Protection – 2 executors, published in "Narodne  
novine" number 77/24 on June 28, 2024.  
List of candidates:  

Testing will be conducted in two phases.  
1. The first phase of testing, which consists of verifying  
knowledge, abilities, and skills essential for performing  
the tasks of the job position, will include all  
candidates who meet the formal requirements of the Public  
Competition and have submitted a timely and complete  
application, and will take place on  
July 22, 2024 (Monday) at 9:00 AM, at the  
premises of the Agency for Personal Data Protection, at  
Selska cesta 136, Zagreb.  
2. In the second phase of testing, which consists  
of verifying knowledge of computer literacy and  
the English language, only those candidates  
who achieved the best results in the first phase  
of testing will be invited. The results of the first phase of testing will be  
known to candidates on the same day after the testing is completed (during the testing, candidates will be  
informed when and where they will be notified about  
the results), and after the announcement of the results of the first phase  
of testing, the second phase of testing will take place  
at the same location.  

For a candidate who does not attend the testing, it is considered  
that they have withdrawn their application for the public competition.  
Upon arrival for the knowledge verification, candidates will be  
required to present appropriate identification  
documents to establish their identity. Candidates who  
cannot prove their identity will not be allowed to  
participate in the testing.  
Candidates are obliged to adhere to the established time  
for testing.  
During the written verification of knowledge and abilities, it is not  
permitted to:  
- use any literature or notes  
- leave the room where the verification is taking place;  
- use mobile phones or other communication devices;  
- talk to other candidates or otherwise  
disrupt the concentration of candidates.  

If a candidate violates any of the above  
rules, they will be removed from the knowledge verification, and  
their result will not be recognized or evaluated by the Commission for the  
conduct of the public competition.  
Candidates will be timely informed about the exact date of the interview.
