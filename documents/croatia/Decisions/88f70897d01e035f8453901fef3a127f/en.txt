Agency for the Protection of Personal Data based on media reports and a citizen's complaint has come to the information that personal data (name and surname, year of birth, document number, names of father and mother) of Croatian citizens suspected of committing criminal offenses or misdemeanors in Greece have been published on a Greek portal. 

Considering that the business establishment of the mentioned portal is in Greece, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation, the Agency has submitted a request to the Greek supervisory authority (as the lead supervisory authority) for the implementation of further actions regarding the data controller (the Greek portal) to determine whether the data controller has acted in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation.
