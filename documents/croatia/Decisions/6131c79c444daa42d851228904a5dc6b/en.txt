On the occasion of Safer Internet Day, the association Collaborators in Learning, CARNET, and the National CERT, along with partners, are organizing the online conference "Quest for a Better Internet" which will take place on February 7, 2023, from 10 AM to 6 PM. The aim is to send a strong message about the importance of preventing electronic violence, protecting children's personal data, creating a safe virtual environment, and ensuring the availability of quality internet content for children and youth.

Link for participation in the conference and program
The conference "Quest for a Better Internet" is organized by the association Collaborators in Learning, CARNET, and the National CERT with partners: the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, the Agency for Electronic Media, Brave Phone, the Ombudsman for Children, the Croatian Regulatory Authority for Network Activities, the Media Literacy portal, the Agency for Mobility and EU Programs, the Agency for Education and Training, the CroAI association, partner schools of the project Be Internet Awesome, Oracle, Microsoft, and Google.

Numerous excellent ideas and materials for workshops that can be conducted in schools on the occasion of Safer Internet Day, as well as throughout the entire school year, are provided by Google's program Be Internet Awesome, which is implemented in Croatia by the association Collaborators in Learning. 

This year, on the occasion of Safer Internet Day, the association Collaborators in Learning has also prepared a theater play "Internet Geniuses" which will premiere on Thursday, February 9, 2023, at the Trešnja Theater (Moščenićka 1, Zagreb) starting at 6 PM. The play is designed for children aged 8 to 12 years, but it will also be interesting for younger and older audiences.

The association Collaborators in Learning has been conducting various programs to raise awareness about appropriate, responsible, and safe internet use since 2008, and the program Be Internet Awesome consists of various activities that build on the curriculum Be Internet Awesome, the game Interland, and a series of educational content prepared by the association's mentors for Croatia. “Every school year, workshops for teachers are conducted, either in person at schools or online in the form of webinars and courses that show teachers how they can implement these educational contents and activities in their schools,” emphasizes Lidija Kralj, development manager at the association Collaborators in Learning.

In addition to teacher education, the association Collaborators in Learning also organizes workshops for children and parents where they have the opportunity to learn together how to be wise digital citizens, use the internet safely, and help each other by solving tasks while having fun and spending quality time together. 

A workshop for collaborative activities for parents and students from grades 1 to 8 of elementary school, which can be conducted as part of a parent meeting, an open day, or other events at school, has been designed by members of the association Collaborators in Learning, and materials with instructions for workshop leaders, along with worksheets, can be downloaded at the link: https://ucitelji.hr/big-materijali-za-roditeljski/.
