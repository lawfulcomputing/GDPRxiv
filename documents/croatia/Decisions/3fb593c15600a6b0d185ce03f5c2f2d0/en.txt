**Regulatory Sandboxes**  

Regulatory isolated environments (regulatory sandboxes) are becoming an increasingly relevant topic in Croatia, but there is still a lack of concrete experience and a clear vision for their implementation in practice.  
Thanks to the employee exchange program of supervisory authorities for data protection from the European Economic Area, established by the European Data Protection Board and the European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPB Secondment Programme), the Agency for Personal Data Protection has the opportunity to collaborate with Anne Eidsaa Hamre from the Norwegian data protection supervisory authority. The Norwegian supervisory authority has been implementing projects within regulatory isolated environments for three years and possesses the richest practical experience in this area.  
The Agency invites all interested parties to a free webinar that will be held by Anne Eidsaa Hamre from the Norwegian Data Protection Agency, who will share her extensive knowledge and experience related to regulatory isolated environments in Norway.

The goal of the Norwegian regulatory test environment is to encourage innovations that enhance privacy protection and to support responsible digitalization, particularly in the field of artificial intelligence. Over the past three years, activities have been focused specifically on projects related to the development and application of AI systems.  
The Norwegian agency provides interested organizations with free, professionally tailored guidelines in exchange for full transparency, to assist them in developing responsible, ethical, and lawful solutions compliant with data protection regulations.  
The knowledge and experience gathered through this process are further utilized to create practical guidelines for other stakeholders and to strengthen the capacities of supervisory authorities in addressing the challenges of a rapidly evolving technological environment.
