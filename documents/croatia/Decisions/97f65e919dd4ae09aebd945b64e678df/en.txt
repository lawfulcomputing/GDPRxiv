Croatian Medical Chamber and the Faculty of Law  
University of Split, in co-organization with the chambers in  
healthcare, organized the 4th Congress of the Coordination  
of Chambers in Healthcare (KoKoZ) and the 6th Croatian Congress  
of Medical Law, which was held from April 21 to 23,  
2023, in Novi Vinodolski, attended by Deputy Director Igor Vulje and  
officials of the Agency.  
During the congress, numerous  
lectures and panels were held on current topics in healthcare and  
medical law, one of which was the  
information system eKarton. On the second day of the congress, the  
eKarton system was discussed on a panel by Deputy  
Director Igor Vulje and officials of the Agency,  
representatives of the Ministry of Health, Ericsson Nikola  
Tesla, the Faculty of Law in Split, and the Croatian  
Medical Chamber.  
eKarton was also the topic of lectures delivered by  
officials of the Agency. It was emphasized that when  
processing health data, as a special category, it is necessary to have a justified purpose and to process such  
data only if it is necessary for purposes such as patient  
treatment or scientific research with the application  
of anonymization, while it is not justified to access e-  
Karton for private purposes of physicians, as this would  
entail appropriate responsibility from the perspective of the General Regulation on Data Protection. Likewise,  
participants had the opportunity to hear about organizational and  
technical measures for the protection of personal data in  
healthcare systems. The emphasis was placed  
on raising awareness of the importance of implementing  
personal data protection measures among all employees in an  
institution, from the highest to the lowest level of the organizational  
structure.
