Published on September 13, 2024.  
In accordance with Article 160 of the Civil Servants Act (“Official Gazette” No. 155/23 and 85/24), the Decision on the Prohibition of New Employment of Civil Servants and Employees in State Administration Bodies and Offices and Other Professional Services of the Government of the Republic of Croatia (“Official Gazette”, No. 35/22 and 37/23), Article 2 of the Regulation on Announcing and Conducting Public Competitions and Internal Advertisements in the Civil Service (“Official Gazette” No. 78/17 and 89/19), and with the prior opinion of the Ministry of Justice, Administration, and Digital Transformation (CLASS: 112-01/24-01/750 REGISTRATION NUMBER: 514-08-03/03-24-02 dated June 11, 2024), the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data announces

PUBLIC COMPETITION  
for employment in the civil service for an indefinite period  
INDEPENDENT SERVICE FOR HUMAN RESOURCES, GENERAL, FINANCIAL-PLANNING, AND ACCOUNTING AFFAIRS  

Clerk – 1 executor, job position (16)  

Professional requirements: Level 4.2 of the Croatian Qualifications Framework (HKO), at least 1 year of work experience in relevant jobs, passed state exam of the appropriate level, knowledge of the English language, knowledge of computer skills.  
In addition to the above requirements, candidates must also meet the general conditions for employment in the civil service as prescribed by the provisions of Article 52 of the Civil Servants Act.  
A person cannot be employed in the civil service if there are obstacles to their employment as stated in Article 54 of the Civil Servants Act.  
Both genders may apply for the public competition.  
A person is employed in the civil service with a probation period of 3 months.  
Candidates who do not have a passed state exam of the appropriate level may also apply for the public competition, with the obligation to take the state exam in accordance with the provisions of Article 99 of the Civil Servants Act.  
In the application for the public competition, it is necessary to provide personal data of the applicant (full name, address of residence, phone number or mobile number, preferably email address) as well as the number and title of the job position and organizational unit to which the person is applying.  
Candidates may use the application form available on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data Application Form_RM16.  
The application for the public competition must be signed by hand.  
Along with the application, candidates are required to submit:

- a resume  
- proof of Croatian citizenship;  
- proof of work experience in relevant jobs, or jobs in the appropriate professional qualification and field (electronic record or confirmation from the Croatian Pension Insurance Institute regarding the data recorded in the main records from which the employer, duration of insurance, actual and required professional qualification can be seen, or other proof from which the necessary data is visible);  
- proof of completed level of education (copy of the diploma)  
- a copy of the certificate of passing the state exam of the appropriate level (for candidates who have passed the state exam).
If a person has changed their surname and submits documentation with their old surname, it is necessary to provide proof of the surname change.  
Documents are attached in an unverified copy, and the original will be presented before the selection of the candidate.  
Applications with evidence of meeting the requirements must be submitted within 8 days from the date of publication in the "Narodne novine," either in person or by mail to the address: Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (application for public competition), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb.  
A complete application is considered one that contains all the data and attachments specified in the public competition.  
A person who has not submitted a timely and orderly application or does not meet the formal requirements from the public competition is not considered a candidate registered for the public competition.  
Persons who, according to special regulations, have the right to priority must refer to this right in their application, and attach all prescribed documentation according to the special law.  
Candidates who invoke the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 101 of the Act on Croatian Veterans of the Homeland War and Members of Their Families ("Narodne novine," No. 121/17, 98/19, 84/21, 156/23), and Article 47 of the Act on Civilian Victims of the Homeland War ("Narodne novine," No. 84/21), must, along with their application for the public competition, in addition to evidence of meeting the required conditions, also attach evidence of meeting the priority conditions according to the list available on the website of the Ministry of Croatian Veterans via the following link: https://branitelji.gov.hr/zaposljavanje-u-drzavnoj-sluzbi/843.  
A candidate who invokes the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 9 of the Act on Professional Rehabilitation and Employment of Persons with Disabilities ("Narodne novine," No. 157/13, 152/14, 39/18, and 32/20), must, along with their application for the public competition, in addition to evidence of meeting the required conditions, also attach proof of established status as a person with a disability.  
A candidate who invokes the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 48.f of the Act on the Protection of Military and Civilian War Invalids ("Narodne novine," No. 33/92, 57/92, 77/92, 27/93, 58/93, 2/94, 76/94, 108/95, 108/96, 82/01, 103/03, 148/13, and 98/19), must, along with their application for the public competition, in addition to evidence of meeting the required conditions, also attach a decision or certificate indicating the mentioned right.  
A candidate who is a member of a national minority has the right to invoke priority in employment based on Article 22 of the Constitutional Act on the Rights of National Minorities ("Narodne novine," No. 155/02, 47/10, 80/10, and 93/11 – Decision of the Constitutional Court of the Republic of Croatia) and is not required to prove their status as a member of a national minority.  
The Commission for the Implementation of the Public Competition (hereinafter: the Commission) is appointed by the Director of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data. The Commission establishes a list of candidates who have applied for the public competition and meet the formal requirements of the public competition, whose applications are timely and complete, and refers candidates from that list for testing and interviews.  
Testing consists of verifying the knowledge, abilities, and skills of candidates (written part of the testing) and a conversation between the Commission and the candidates (interview).  
A candidate who has not attended the testing is no longer considered a candidate in the process.  
The time and place of the testing will be announced at least five days before the date set for testing on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/).  
A candidate who has not attended the testing is no longer considered a candidate in the process.  
The job description and salary information for the position, the content and method of testing, and the legal sources for preparing candidates for testing will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/) simultaneously with the publication of the public competition.  
The selected candidate will be invited to submit, within a reasonable time and before the decision on admission to the civil service is made, a certificate from the competent court that no criminal proceedings are being conducted against them, a certificate of health capability for performing the duties of the position, and originals of other evidence of meeting the formal requirements from the public competition. Failure to submit these will be considered a withdrawal from admission to the civil service.  
If no persons who meet the prescribed conditions apply for the public competition, or if the registered candidates do not pass the testing, the head of the body will suspend the procedure for this competition.  
Candidates will be informed of the results of the public competition through a public announcement of the decision on the admission of the selected candidate to the civil service on the website of the Ministry of Justice, Administration, and Digital Transformation (https://mpudt.gov.hr/) and the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/).  
The delivery of the decision to all candidates is considered published upon the expiration of the eighth day from the date of publication on the website of the Ministry of Justice, Administration, and Digital Transformation (https://mpudt.gov.hr/).
