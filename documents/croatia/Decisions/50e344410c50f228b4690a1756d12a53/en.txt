**Day of Statehood**

The Day of Statehood is an occasion for us to proudly remember the historical decision that initiated the path towards the creation of an independent, modern, democratic, and sovereign Republic of Croatia.  
On this day, with the constitution of the first democratically elected multiparty Parliament, Croatian democracy was born – founded on the values of freedom, community, responsibility, and pride, which remain the foundation of our society today.  
Let the Day of Statehood inspire us to, motivated by these values, continue to build a safer, fairer, and more prosperous future for all of us.  
Dear citizens of the Republic of Croatia, happy Day of Statehood!
