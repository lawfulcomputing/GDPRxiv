The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, within the framework of the ARC2 project, organized a free workshop with exercises: “Organizational and Technical Measures in GDPR – Access Management and Data Protection, Internal Acts, Contractual Relationships, Fulfillment of Data Subject Rights – Through Examples and Exercises,” on Monday, November 6, 2023, at the Zagreb School of Economics and Management (ZŠEM).

In the introductory part of the workshop, Director Zdravko Vukić pointed out that the Agency has imposed high administrative fines precisely due to the failure to implement appropriate organizational and technical measures for data protection: “Absolute protection does not exist, but it is necessary to maximize security measures, especially preventive ones, in order to minimize the risk of security incidents. Our experience has shown that in the vast majority of incidents, the human factor is one of the weakest links, leading to the conclusion that there is still insufficient education of employees about the potential risks to personal data they work with and the ways in which they can best protect them. Therefore, I believe that this workshop will contribute to a better understanding and fulfillment of obligations related to data protection,” said Director Vukić.

During the one-day workshop with a case study exercise, Dr. Natalija Parlov Una, the lead tutor and senior certification auditor for information security management systems, addressed appropriate organizational and technical measures and compliance with GDPR in the context of managing the collection, storage, access, and deletion of data, forming internal acts, obligations, and opportunities to reduce the risk of penalties when entering into contractual relationships with data processors and ways to fulfill data subject rights.

Due to high interest, the workshop intended for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises and craftsmen (managers and data processors), officials or data protection coordinators, and anyone who needs to be aware of the risks and obligations related to data protection or mechanisms for fulfilling data subject rights in their business will be held on another date – December 11.
