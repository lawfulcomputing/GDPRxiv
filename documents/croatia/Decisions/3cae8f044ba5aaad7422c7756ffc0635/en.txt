Due to high interest, the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, as part of the ARC2 project, organized another free workshop with exercises: “Organizational and Technical Measures in GDPR – Access Management and Data Protection, Internal Acts, Contractual Relationships, Fulfillment of the Rights of Data Subjects – Through Examples and Exercises,” on Monday, December 11, 2023, at the Zagreb School of Economics and Management (ZŠEM).

Director Zdravko Vukić expressed satisfaction with the interest in holding such workshops: “Our experience has shown that in the majority of incidents, the human factor is one of the weakest links, leading to the conclusion that there is still insufficient education among employees regarding the potential threats to personal data they work with and the ways in which they can best protect it. The high interest in these workshops is proof that participants are aware of this, but also that they want to enhance their knowledge and business practices.”

Lecturer Dr. Natalija Parlov Una, leading tutor and senior certification auditor of information security management systems, addressed appropriate organizational and technical measures and compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation in the context of managing the collection, storage, access, and deletion of data, the formation of internal acts, obligations, and possibilities for reducing the risk of penalties when entering into contractual relationships with data processors, as well as ways to fulfill the rights of data subjects during the workshop through a case study.
