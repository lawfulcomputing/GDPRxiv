Director of the Agency Zdravko Vukić, together with his associates, presented the quiz for children "Let's Learn to Protect Our Own and Others' Personal Data" to the 4th-grade students at Rapska Elementary School. This quiz was created by the Agency for Personal Data Protection for younger elementary school students (primarily for students in grades 3 to 5).

Duck Đuro, whose name was chosen by the students of Rapska Elementary School, teaches children about personal data protection and online dangers through the knowledge quiz. This way, children can learn what personal data is, how to manage it, what online scams exist, and how to act when they suspect one, as well as about online violence, which is increasingly prevalent among young people today. The goal is to raise children's awareness that they must manage their personal data responsibly in order to primarily protect themselves.

"Awareness of the importance of personal data protection needs to be built from a young age, so it is important to continuously educate children and talk to them about this very important topic, especially in today's time when young people spend more and more time online. The right to personal data protection is a fundamental human right, and personal data is our invaluable asset, so we must treat it accordingly, and we must teach our children to do the same," said Director Vukić, expressing gratitude to the principal of Rapska Elementary School for the ongoing cooperation.

Principal of Rapska Elementary School Diana Dujmović emphasized the need to teach children responsible behavior. "Considering that children today are getting mobile phones at an increasingly younger age, thus opening them up to the world and a range of dangers that the Internet hides, it is very important that we educate children, that is, teach them safe and responsible behavior online. Therefore, I commend this initiative by the Agency to provide children with certain knowledge and advice through the quiz."

The idea for the quiz arose during the educational sessions for younger elementary school students when it was noticed that children are often unaware of all the dangers on the internet and that they easily share their own and others' personal data. The quiz created by the Agency can be completed by children at school with their teachers, at home with their parents or guardians, and they can discuss this very important topic. Therefore, we invite all parents, guardians, and teachers to try the quiz.
