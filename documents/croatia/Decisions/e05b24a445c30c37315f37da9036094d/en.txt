List of candidates:

Testing will be conducted in two phases.

The first phase of testing, which consists of verifying knowledge, abilities, and skills essential for performing the tasks of the job position, will be attended by all candidates who meet the formal requirements of the Public Competition and have submitted a timely and complete application. This phase will take place on November 15, 2024 (Friday) at 9:00 AM, at the premises of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, located at Selska cesta 136, Zagreb.

Only those candidates who achieve the best results in the first phase of testing will be invited to the second phase of testing, which consists of verifying knowledge of computer literacy and the English language. The results of the first phase of testing will be known to the candidates on the same day after the testing is completed (during the testing, candidates will be informed when and where they will be notified of the results), and after the announcement of the results of the first phase of testing, the second phase will take place at the same location.

A candidate who does not attend the testing will be considered to have withdrawn their application for the public competition. Upon arrival for the knowledge verification, candidates will be required to present an appropriate identification document to establish their identity. Candidates who cannot prove their identity will not be allowed to participate in the testing. Candidates are required to adhere to the established testing schedule. 

During the written verification of knowledge and abilities, the following is not permitted:

- using any literature or notes;
- leaving the room where the verification is taking place;
- using mobile phones or other communication devices;
- talking to other candidates or otherwise disrupting the concentration of candidates.

If a candidate violates any of the above rules, they will be removed from the knowledge verification, and their result will not be recognized or evaluated by the Commission for the Implementation of the Public Competition. Candidates will be timely informed about the exact date of the interview.
