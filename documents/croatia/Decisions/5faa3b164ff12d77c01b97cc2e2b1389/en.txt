The Agency has identified numerous issues in understanding the role of data protection officers in the public sector. Public authorities need to provide their officers with sufficient time to perform their duties and the opportunity to educate themselves and learn what their tasks are and how to carry them out! Namely, employees of public authorities often do not know who the data protection officer is within their body and that they should address all questions regarding the processing of personal data to the data protection officer, who must be appointed by each public authority. It has also been observed that appointed data protection officers lack adequate knowledge in the field of personal data protection, and for this reason, they cannot advise employees.  
We remind you that the tasks of the data protection officer include, among others:

1. Keeping a record of processing activities (which will list all types of personal data processed within the public authority, categories of data subjects, purposes of processing, and will contain other information that the record of processing activities should include in accordance with Article 30 of the GDPR; the Agency recommends adding legal bases).
2. Developing a privacy policy/data processing statement or a similarly titled document that will be easily accessible to data subjects (e.g., on the website of the public authority), through which ALL data subjects are informed about the processing of their personal data, in accordance with Articles 12-14 of the General Data Protection Regulation.
3. Advising the data controller on the technical and organizational measures that the authority should take.
4. Conducting training on personal data protection for employees of the public authority in which the data protection officer works.
5. Participating in the implementation of data protection impact assessments as needed.
6. Participating in the implementation of proportionality and public interest tests as needed. https://www.pristupinfo.hr/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/Smjernice-Test-razmjernosti-i-javnog-interesa-1.pdf
