March 27, 2023  
The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data is conducting supervisory proceedings against the data controller EOS Matrix d.o.o. due to a possible breach of personal data and will inform the public of the findings upon completion of the proceedings. We advise citizens to request access to their personal data from the relevant data controller (EOS Matrix d.o.o.), in accordance with the rights guaranteed by the General Data Protection Regulation, and to obtain information on whether their personal data has been affected by the breach and to what extent. Contact for the Data Protection Officer: dpo@eos-matrix.hr.  
Regarding inquiries from citizens for compensation in the event of a breach of their personal data; any person who has suffered material or non-material damage due to the processing of personal data contrary to the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation has the right to compensation for material and non-material damage from the data controller or processor for the damage suffered. However, we emphasize that the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data is NOT the competent authority for compensation. Anyone who has suffered non-material or material damage has the right to initiate proceedings to assert their right to compensation before the competent court (in the Republic of Croatia, this is the municipal court).
