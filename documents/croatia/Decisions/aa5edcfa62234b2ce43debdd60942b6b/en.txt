July 5, 2024  
Regarding the previous information about the incident of "leakage" of personal data of more than one million individuals who are vehicle owners, we state that the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data has conducted supervisory actions at the Croatian Vehicle Center, the Ministry of the Interior of the Republic of Croatia, as well as other legal entities associated with the mentioned violation. The supervisory activities have determined that the aforementioned business entities do not possess all the data contained within the disputed file delivered indirectly by the media to the Agency (on a USB stick), nor do they have the capability to manage such a structure of personal data.  
The supervisory procedure has established that the structure of the data provided to the Agency corresponds with the records held by the Croatian Insurance Bureau as the data controller. The Agency, along with other competent authorities, is conducting further supervisory actions to ascertain all relevant circumstances surrounding the disclosure of personal data from the database of the Croatian Insurance Bureau and will provide additional information to the public upon the completion of these actions.  
We also emphasize that, according to currently available information, personal data is not accessible to the general public, but has been delivered to the competent authorities that are conducting actions within their jurisdiction.
