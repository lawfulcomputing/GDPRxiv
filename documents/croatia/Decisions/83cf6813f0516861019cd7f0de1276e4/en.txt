**July 16, 2024**  
**CALL FOR TESTING**  
to candidates whose applications are timely and complete and who meet the formal requirements of the public competition for permanent employment in the Agency for Personal Data Protection in the Sector for Supervision, Investigations, and Protection of the Rights of Respondents, Service for Supervision of Investigations, Technology, and Security, job position (24) – Advisor in the Agency for Personal Data Protection – 2 positions, published in "Narodne novine" number 77/24 on June 28, 2024.  
**List of Candidates:**

Testing will be conducted in two phases.  
1. The first phase of testing, which consists of verifying knowledge, abilities, and skills essential for performing the tasks of the job position, will be attended by all candidates who meet the formal requirements of the Public Competition and have submitted a timely and complete application. This phase will take place on **July 22, 2024** (Monday) at **9:00 AM**, at the premises of the Agency for Personal Data Protection, located at Selska cesta 136, Zagreb.  
2. The second phase of testing, which consists of verifying knowledge of computer literacy and the English language, will be attended only by those candidates who achieved the best results in the first phase of testing. The results of the first phase of testing will be known to the candidates on the same day after the testing is completed (during the testing, candidates will be informed when and where they will be notified of the results), and after the announcement of the results of the first phase of testing, the second phase of testing will take place at the same location.

For a candidate who does not attend the testing, it is considered that they have withdrawn their application for the public competition.  
Upon arrival for the knowledge verification, candidates will be required to present an appropriate identification document to establish their identity. Candidates who cannot prove their identity will not be allowed to participate in the testing.  
Candidates are required to adhere to the established testing times.  
During the written knowledge and ability verification, the following is not permitted:

- to use any literature or notes  
- to leave the room where the verification is taking place  
- to use mobile phones or other communication devices  
- to converse with other candidates or otherwise disrupt the concentration of candidates.

If a candidate violates any of the above rules, they will be removed from the knowledge verification, and their result will not be recognized or evaluated by the Commission for the Implementation of the Public Competition.  
Candidates will be timely informed of the exact date of the interview.
