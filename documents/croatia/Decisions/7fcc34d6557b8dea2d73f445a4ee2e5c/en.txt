July 5, 2024

Regarding the previous information about the incident of the "leak" of personal data of more than one million individuals who are vehicle owners, we state that the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data has conducted supervisory proceedings at the Vehicle Center of Croatia, the Ministry of the Interior of the Republic of Croatia, as well as other legal entities associated with the mentioned violation. The supervisory activities have determined that the aforementioned business entities do not possess all the data contained within the disputed file delivered indirectly by the media to the Agency (on a USB stick), nor do they have the capability to manage such a structure of personal data.

The supervisory proceedings have established that the structure of the data provided to the Agency corresponds with the records held by the Croatian Insurance Bureau as the data controller.

The Agency, as well as other competent authorities, is conducting further supervisory proceedings in order to ascertain all relevant circumstances regarding the disclosure of personal data from the database of the Croatian Insurance Bureau and will provide additional information to the public upon the conclusion of these proceedings.

We also emphasize that, according to currently available information, personal data is not accessible to the general public; rather, it has been provided to the competent authorities that are conducting proceedings within their jurisdiction.
