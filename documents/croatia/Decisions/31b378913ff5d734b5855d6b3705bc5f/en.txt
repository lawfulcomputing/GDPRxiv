In the organization of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data and the Center for Missing and Abused Children, on Monday, December 18, 2023, a conference titled “Explain GDPR to Me” was held at the Center in Višnjevac for students, school staff, and kindergarten employees.  
The conference dedicated to the most vulnerable group in society – children, was held in two parts to ensure that knowledge was acquired by the students themselves, as well as by those who care for them while they are in kindergartens and school classrooms. In the first part of the conference, two interactive workshops were organized for students, where they had the opportunity to learn how to recognize online abuse, how to protect themselves, and what actions they can take if they encounter such situations.  
For school and kindergarten staff, in the second part of the conference, representatives of the Agency held a workshop titled “How to Protect Children’s Personal Data in Schools and Preschools?”, where they explained through 10 key steps how to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation, thereby ensuring the protection of children's personal data.  
Using practical examples and addressing the most common questions and dilemmas faced by staff in educational institutions, participants were provided with concrete guidelines and presented with solutions to the most frequent problems. For instance, it was shown that educational institutions often do not know how to determine the legal basis and seek consent for every processing of personal data, believing that it is necessary.  
This is just the first in a series of joint activities of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data and the Center for Missing and Abused Children, following the signing of the Cooperation Agreement between the Agency's Director Zdravko Vukić and the Center's Head Tomislav Ramljak on December 7, 2023, which will jointly raise awareness about the protection of children's data in the virtual environment.  
Additionally, the conference was an opportunity to announce a three-day educational program for data protection officers in the educational sector. This program, the first of its kind in the Republic of Croatia, will be available starting in February 2024 and will include both online and in-person lectures. Participants who successfully complete the program will receive a certificate of completion.
