In the premises of the Infobip campus Alpha Centauri, on Monday, November 27, 2023, the online tool "OLIVIA" was presented, developed within the framework of the European project ARC2 (Awareness Raising Campaign for SMEs). 

"OLIVIA" is a virtual teacher and assistant for GDPR compliance, which will provide entrepreneurs with the opportunity to learn about their basic obligations, test their knowledge, and create essential documents to demonstrate compliance with personal data protection regulations. 

During the introductory address, Director Zdravko Vukić expressed satisfaction that the Agency, together with its partners, has the opportunity to develop a tool for entrepreneurs that will be free and accessible to all. "We are aware that micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises, as well as craftsmen, often do not have sufficient financial resources and human resources necessary for compliance with data protection regulations. However, within the framework of the European project ARC2, we are conducting numerous training sessions to help them resolve the uncertainties they still face, but the online tool 'OLIVIA' is certainly a significant step forward. The goal is to provide entrepreneurs with a concrete tool that will be available to them at all times and to provide all necessary information in one place," said Director Vukić.

Namely, "OLIVIA," which will consist of 15 modules in its final phase, simplifies access to the framework of knowledge, rules, and procedures. 

The project manager of ARC2, Anamarija Mladinić, presented the project and the online tool "OLIVIA" and emphasized that most micro and small entrepreneurs and craftsmen in Croatia have not taken even the basic steps to comply with GDPR, but they will have a tool available to assist them in this regard. This was confirmed by entrepreneurs who tested "OLIVIA" yesterday. 

You can test "OLIVIA" at the link https://olivia-gdpr-arc.eu/hr.
