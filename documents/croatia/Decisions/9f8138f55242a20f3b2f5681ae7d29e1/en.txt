The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data invites all craftsmen, micro, small, and medium-sized entrepreneurs to free online training sessions conducted by the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data as part of the EU ARC2 project aimed at providing support to entrepreneurs in complying with the General Data Protection Regulation. The Agency has developed an online tool called “Olivia” within the project, which aims to assist data controllers/processors in understanding their obligations and in creating basic internal documents to demonstrate compliance with legal regulations on personal data protection. The web tool “Olivia” is still under development, and we plan to add new modules, and it is available for free use to all interested data controllers/processors at the following link: https://olivia-gdpr-arc.eu/hr.

Schedule of workshops in March and April:
4.3.2024. online ARC2 workshop “Contract between Data Controller and Processor,” registration at: Registration Form
Entrepreneurs (data controllers) often make mistakes when engaging processors who process personal data on their behalf and according to their instructions, while failing to conclude a contract that regulates mutual rights and obligations between the data controller and the processor. This is a legal obligation under Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation, and in cases where such a contract is not concluded, it constitutes a violation of the General Data Protection Regulation, which can have serious financial consequences for both the data controller and the processor. 
In this workshop, entrepreneurs/craftsmen will have the opportunity to learn who is the data controller, the processor, the joint data controller, what happens when the processor wants to engage a sub-processor, and how to properly regulate mutual rights and obligations.

26.3.2024. online ARC2 workshop “How to Align Your Website with GDPR?”, registration at: Registration Form
This workshop is primarily intended for craftsmen, micro, small, and medium-sized entrepreneurs, but also for all others who have not yet aligned the processing of personal data through websites with the legal framework for personal data protection. 
In this workshop, participants will have the opportunity to learn about the basic elements of a website that must comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which cookies can be considered necessary, and for which consent is required, in accordance with the Electronic Communications Act and the General Data Protection Regulation. 
Entrepreneurs will receive concrete guidelines on how consent for the processing of personal data through cookies (cookie banner) should look, how to write privacy policies, what technical and organizational measures are necessary for the functioning of online services, and which free tools are available to entrepreneurs for self-checking their websites.

9.4.2024. online ARC2 workshop “How to Become a Successful Data Protection Officer?”, registration at: Registration Form
This workshop is intended for all data protection officers, as well as data controllers/processors who wish to gain a better understanding and insight into the tasks and duties of a data protection officer. Organizations are obliged to appoint data protection officers in cases:
(a) processing is carried out by a public authority or body, except for courts acting in their judicial capacity,
(b) the core activities of the data controller or processor consist of processing operations which, by their nature, scope, and/or purposes, require regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects on a large scale, or
(c) the core activities of the data controller or processor consist of large-scale processing of special categories of data under Article 9 and personal data relating to criminal convictions and offenses under Article 10.
If you are obliged to appoint a data protection officer or wish to appoint one voluntarily, the appointed officer must have the necessary knowledge in the field of personal data protection, must not be in a position that could lead to a conflict of interest, and must understand their tasks in order to successfully perform their job.

16.4.2024. online ARC2 workshop “Personal Data Protection and Compliance with GDPR in Tourism,” registration at: Registration Form
This workshop is exclusively intended for entrepreneurs and craftsmen from the tourism sector, with a program tailored to their specific needs. 
Tourism is specific due to the high flow of people, the fast pace of work, and the processing of a large amount of personal data of Croatian and foreign citizens. The General Data Protection Regulation has been in force since May 25, 2018, and all who process personal data of citizens of the European Union are obliged to comply with it. 
Non-compliance with the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation and failure to take appropriate measures to protect personal data can result in personal data breaches, a lack of trust from clients in tourism service providers, and consequently a decline in reputation, financial losses, and high monetary fines. 
Participants in the workshop will learn what key steps they must take to align their business with the General Data Protection Regulation and will receive answers to their questions in the field of personal data protection.
