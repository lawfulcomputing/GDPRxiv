October 9, 2024  
Based on Article 165, Paragraph 2 of the Civil Servants Act ("Official Gazette", No. 155/23, 85/24) and Point IV, Item 1 of the Decision on the Prohibition of New Employment of Civil Servants and Employees in State Administration Bodies and Offices and Other Professional Services of the Government of the Republic of Croatia ("Official Gazette", No. 35/22, 37/23), the Agency for Personal Data Protection announces  
ADVERTISEMENT  
for recruitment into civil service for a fixed term  

INDEPENDENT SERVICE FOR HUMAN RESOURCES, GENERAL, FINANCIAL-PLANNING AND ACCOUNTING AFFAIRS  
Senior Advisor – 1 position, to replace a long-term absent civil servant – until the return of the absent civil servant, job position No. 11.  

Professional requirements: Level 7.1. or 7.1. of the Croatian Qualifications Framework in the field of economics, at least 4 years of work experience in relevant jobs, passed state exam of the second level.  
In addition to the above requirements, candidates must meet the conditions prescribed in Article 52 of the Civil Servants Act.  
A person cannot be admitted to civil service if there are obstacles to their admission as per Article 54 of the Civil Servants Act.  
Both genders may apply for the advertisement.  

A person is admitted to civil service with a mandatory probation period of two months.  
Persons who do not have a passed state exam of the appropriate level may also apply, with the obligation to take the state exam in accordance with the provisions of Article 99 of the Civil Servants Act.  
In the application for the advertisement, it is necessary to provide personal data of the applicant (full name, residential address, phone number or mobile number, preferably email address) as well as the number and title of the job position and organizational unit to which the person is applying.  
Candidates may use the application form available on the website of the Agency for Personal Data Protection Form-Application_RM11.  
The application for the advertisement must be signed by hand.  
Along with the application, candidates are required to submit:  

- a resume;  

- proof of Croatian citizenship;  

- proof of total work experience and work experience in relevant jobs, i.e., jobs in the relevant professional qualification and field (electronic record or certificate of data recorded in the database of the Croatian Pension Insurance Institute, employer's certificate of work experience in relevant jobs, or other proof indicating the jobs the candidate has worked in);  

- proof of completed level of education (copy of diploma);  

- a copy of the certificate of passing the state exam of the appropriate level, or the state professional exam (for candidates who have passed the state professional exam).
If a person has changed their surname and submits documentation with their old surname, it is necessary to provide proof of the name change.  
Documents are submitted in an unverified copy, and the original will be presented before the selection of the candidate.  
Applications with evidence of meeting the requirements must be submitted within 8 days from the date of publication of the advertisement on the website of the Ministry of Justice, Administration and Digital Transformation of the Republic of Croatia – https://mpudt.gov.hr/, either in person or by mail to the address: Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (application for the advertisement), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb.  

This advertisement will be published on the websites of: the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data – https://azop.hr/, the Ministry of Justice, Administration and Digital Transformation of the Republic of Croatia – https://mpudt.gov.hr/, and the Croatian Employment Service – https://burzarada.hzz.hr/.  
A complete application is considered one that contains all the data and attachments specified in the advertisement.  
A person who has not submitted a timely and proper application or does not meet the formal requirements from the advertisement is not considered a candidate for the advertisement.  

A candidate who invokes the right of preference for employment in the civil service according to a special law must attach all prescribed documentation according to the special law with their application and has preference over other candidates under equal conditions.  
A candidate who invokes the right of preference for employment in accordance with Article 101 of the Act on Croatian Veterans of the Homeland War and Members of Their Families (“Narodne novine”, No. 121/17, 98/19, 84/21, 156/23), and Article 47 of the Act on Civilian Victims of the Homeland War (Narodne novine, No. 84/21), must, along with the application for the advertisement, in addition to proof of meeting the required conditions, also attach evidence of meeting the preference conditions according to the list available on the website of the Ministry of Croatian Veterans via the following link: https://branitelji.gov.hr/zaposljavanje-u-drzavnoj-sluzbi/843.  
A candidate who invokes the right of preference for employment in accordance with Article 9 of the Act on Professional Rehabilitation and Employment of Persons with Disabilities (“Narodne novine”, No. 157/13, 152/14, 39/18, and 32/20), must, along with the application for the advertisement, in addition to proof of meeting the required conditions, also attach proof of established status as a person with a disability.  
A candidate who invokes the right of preference for employment in accordance with Article 48.f of the Act on the Protection of Military and Civil War Invalids (“Narodne novine”, No. 33/92, 57/92, 77/92, 27/93, 58/93, 2/94, 76/94, 108/95, 108/96, 82/01, 94/01, 103/03, 148/13, and 98/19), must, along with the application for the advertisement, in addition to proof of meeting the required conditions, also attach a decision or confirmation from which this right is evident.  
A candidate who is a member of a national minority has the right to invoke preference for employment based on Article 22 of the Constitutional Act on the Rights of National Minorities (“Narodne novine”, No. 155/02, 47/10, 80/10, and 93/11 – Decision of the Constitutional Court of the Republic of Croatia) and is not required to prove their status as a member of a national minority.  

The Commission for the Implementation of the Advertisement (hereinafter referred to as: the Commission) is appointed by the Director of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data. The Commission determines the list of candidates who have applied for the advertisement and meet the formal requirements and invites them for an interview.  
The time and place of the interview will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/) at least five days before the interview is held.  
A candidate who does not attend the interview is considered to have withdrawn their application for the advertisement and is no longer regarded as a candidate.  
The job description and salary information will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/) simultaneously with the advertisement publication.  
The selected candidate will be invited to submit, within a reasonable time and before the decision on their appointment to the civil service is made, a certificate from the competent court that no criminal proceedings are being conducted against them, a certificate of health capability for performing the job duties, and originals of other evidence of meeting the formal requirements from the advertisement. Failure to provide these will be considered a withdrawal from the appointment to the civil service.  
Candidates will be informed of the results of the advertisement by the delivery of a decision on the appointment to the civil service for a fixed term of the selected candidate through a public announcement on the website of the Ministry of Justice, Administration and Digital Transformation of the Republic of Croatia – https://mpudt.gov.hr/.  
The delivery of the decision to all candidates is considered published upon the expiration of the eighth day from the date of publication on the website of the Ministry of Justice, Administration and Digital Transformation of the Republic of Croatia – https://mpudt.gov.hr/.  
If no persons who meet the prescribed conditions apply for the advertisement, or if the candidates who applied do not satisfy in the interview, the Director of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data will suspend the advertisement process.
