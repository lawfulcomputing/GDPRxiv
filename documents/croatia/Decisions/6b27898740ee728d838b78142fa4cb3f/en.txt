The European Data Protection Board has developed a tool for analyzing the compliance of websites with the General Data Protection Regulation. The tool was developed within the framework of the European Data Protection Board's Support pool of experts project, and it can be used by data protection authorities in their investigative activities, as well as by data controllers and processors who wish to analyze their websites. The tool is free open-source software under the EUPL 1.2 license and is available for download at code.europa.eu. The source code is available here. The tool is also compatible with other tools, such as the website analysis tool of the European Data Protection Supervisor.

Although there are already several tools for auditing websites, the use of such tools requires specialized technical knowledge. Therefore, the European Data Protection Board decided to develop a solution that would be easy to use in order to facilitate the implementation and compliance checks conducted by data controllers for national data protection authorities. The software was developed by an expert engaged under the "Support pool of experts" project, in collaboration with and under the supervision of the Secretariat of the European Data Protection Board. The software was presented to data protection authorities in June 2023, and after receiving positive feedback from participants, it was decided to consolidate the software and release it as free open-source software.

The Support pool of experts is a project of the European Data Protection Board that is part of the European Data Protection Board's strategy for the period 2021-2023, aimed at providing support to data protection supervisory authorities.
