Agency for the Protection of Personal Data is organizing a series of educational activities in November aimed at raising awareness about the importance of personal data protection. The training sessions are tailored to various target groups – citizens, data controllers/processors, data protection officers in the private and public sectors – and provide concrete knowledge and guidelines for understanding and applying the legislative framework on personal data protection.

We invite all interested data controllers/processors and data protection officers to join our free webinars and gain additional knowledge about technical measures for personal data protection and personal data breaches. We also invite all citizens to inform themselves about their rights through the webinars and learn how to recognize and avoid online frauds that threaten us daily.

Additionally, to enhance your knowledge in the field of personal data protection and to demonstrate your compliance and reliability, we invite you to try the web tool Olivia, which the Agency developed as part of the EU ARC II project. The use of the web tool Olivia is completely free for all organizations. Olivia contains a range of learning modules, knowledge tests, and templates for internal acts that will help you demonstrate your compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation. An additional value of the web tool Olivia is that it can also serve as a tool for raising awareness about data protection within the organization. After successfully passing the knowledge test, Olivia generates certificates for the completed module, which can serve as proof that the organization takes care of personal data protection, or as one of the organizational measures for data protection.

Register and try it out at the link: https://olivia-gdpr-arc.eu/hr
