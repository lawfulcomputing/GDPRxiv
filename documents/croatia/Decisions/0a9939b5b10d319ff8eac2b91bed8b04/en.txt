International Working Group on Data Protection in Technology (Berlin Group), of which the representative of the Personal Data Protection Agency is a member, has prepared a working document on smart cities, which includes recommendations for service providers and regulators. 

The working document presents a series of data protection and privacy principles that relate to each phase of data use in the context of a city, namely: 
1. Data collection, e.g., images obtained through video surveillance and the reuse of data by public authorities; 
2. Data analysis, e.g., traffic flow management using artificial intelligence (AI) and profiling to predict the location or movement of a person through the city; and 
3. Decision-making, e.g., managing urban resources such as public transport. 

In summary, the working document calls on all involved stakeholders to be aware of data protection principles and to implement them in accordance with the recommendations from this document in order to avoid violations of fundamental rights to personal data protection and privacy.
