The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data received a Report on Personal Data Breach – incident (according to Article 33 of the General Data Protection Regulation) from the data controller – the National Centre for External Evaluation of Education, after which the Agency initiated the relevant supervisory proceedings. 

Let us recall that Article 33 of the General Data Protection Regulation stipulates the obligation to report a personal data breach to the supervisory authority no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of the breach. Regarding the notification of data subjects about the personal data breach, Article 34 stipulates that the data controller must notify the data subjects without undue delay in the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals. Notification of data subjects is not mandatory if any of the conditions from Article 34, paragraph 3, are met:
a) the data controller has implemented appropriate technical and organizational measures of protection, and those measures have been applied to the personal data affected by the personal data breach, particularly those that make the personal data unintelligible to any person not authorized to access it, such as encryption;
b) the data controller has taken subsequent measures that ensure that it is no longer likely that a high risk to the rights and freedoms of the data subjects referred to in paragraph 1 will occur;
c) it would require a disproportionate effort. In such a case, there must be a public communication or a similar measure that informs the data subjects in an equally effective manner.

We will inform the public about all relevant information regarding the supervisory proceedings.
