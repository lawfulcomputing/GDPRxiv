The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, in collaboration with the Croatian Chamber of Commerce, organized a Consultation on Personal Data Protection for Financial Institutions on Tuesday, December 10, 2024, attended by numerous members of associations representing financial institutions within the Chamber.

The Director of the Agency and Vice President of the European Data Protection Board, Zdravko Vukić, emphasized that the recently adopted Act on Artificial Intelligence poses new challenges for the financial sector. “It is undeniable that the use of artificial intelligence in the financial sector has enormous potential. The application of artificial intelligence enables improved user experience, greater efficiency, and more accurate decision-making. It is important to emphasize that any processing of personal data, including that in artificial intelligence systems, must comply with the General Data Protection Regulation at all stages of development and use of artificial intelligence systems,” said Director Vukić, reminding all present: “The General Data Protection Regulation is not here to burden you, but to empower you. By effectively applying the principles of personal data processing, you can build a system that not only protects your clients but also enhances your business. GDPR is an opportunity to show how innovation and data protection can go hand in hand.”

The web tool Olivia, developed by the Agency as part of the ARC II project together with partners, was presented by Anamarija Mladinić, Head of the Sector for EU, International Cooperation, and Legal Affairs. Olivia's mission is to assist all data controllers in mastering the basics of GDPR through short educational materials, videos, quizzes, webinars, and generating basic documentation for compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation. The use of the web tool Olivia is completely free for all organizations and can be tried out via the link https://olivia-gdpr-arc.eu/hr.

Marija Vujeva, Senior Advisor, spoke about the activities of the Agency and the European Data Protection Board in the context of personal data processing by the financial sector, while Antonio Katavić, Senior Advisor – Specialist at the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, presented cases from practice related to violations of the General Data Protection Regulation by the financial sector.

Vanja Dominović, Head of the Department for Financial Institutions and Macroeconomic Analysis at the Croatian Chamber of Commerce, thanked the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data and expressed satisfaction that representatives of financial institutions had the opportunity to resolve any potential uncertainties regarding personal data protection regulations.
