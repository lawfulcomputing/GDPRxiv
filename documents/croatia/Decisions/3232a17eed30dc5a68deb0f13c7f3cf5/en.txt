The European Data Protection Board has published its Annual Report, which contains an overview of the Board's activities during the year 2023. The Annual Report shows the progress of the Personal Data Protection Agency, particularly regarding administrative fines. Specifically, the Agency imposed 28 administrative fines in the previous year, totaling 8,266,350.00 euros, ranking it 9th in the European Union for the total amount of administrative fines imposed in 2023. 

We remind you that last year, two of the highest fines were imposed on debt collection agencies in the amounts of 5.47 and 2.26 million euros, which significantly exceeded previous amounts.

The Annual Report is available at the following link: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/annual-report/edpb-annual-report-2023_en
