**March 24, 2025.**  
The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data has imposed seven administrative fines for violations of the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation, totaling 169,000 euros.  
Below are brief descriptions of the individual fines:  

1. **Illegal processing of personal data for parking fee collection – 80,000 euros**  
The Agency discovered that a commercial company processes personal data of respondents for the purpose of collecting parking fees (issuing daily parking tickets) for retail chains and a general hospital, collecting them from the Register of Registered Vehicles of the Ministry of the Interior or through the web service of the Ministry of the Interior of the Republic of Croatia without a legal basis.  
During supervisory proceedings, it was established that the commercial company abused its powers granted by the Ministry of the Interior (for another purpose and another city) to obtain personal data of a larger number of respondents for contrary purposes and without a legal basis as per Article 5(1)(b) and 6(1) of the General Data Protection Regulation. Specifically, the company received a concession from the local government unit to provide parking supervision services under Article 5(11) of the Road Traffic Safety Act and, for this purpose, obtained access to personal data contained within the Register of Registered Vehicles of the Ministry of the Interior based on an Agreement with the Ministry. However, the company used access to personal data for the organization and collection of parking fees for retail chains and the General Hospital, which is beyond the granted authorization.  
Additionally, it was determined that the company did not have a contract regulating the relationship between the data controller/processor for the purpose of collecting and organizing parking for the General Hospital, which is contrary to the provision of Article 28(3) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
Furthermore, it was established that appropriate technical and organizational measures for the protection of personal data were not taken during the processing of personal data of respondents, resulting in a violation of Article 32(2) and (4) of the General Data Protection Regulation. For these violations, the company was fined 80,000 euros.  

2. **Illegal public disclosure of personal data on the internet – 40,000 euros**  
The Agency received multiple complaints from citizens, primarily individuals who own crafts, regarding the public disclosure of their personal data on the Internet by a commercial company. Specifically, the primary business activity of the company in question is offering basic and financial data about business entities (craftsmen) available on the company's websites, which they collected from publicly available sources such as public registers, e-publications from the court, as well as additional data related to the financial operations of business entities, which they obtain based on a contract signed with Fina.  
The Agency conducted supervisory activities and determined that the data controller processes personal data contrary to the provisions of Article 6(1)(f), in connection with Article 5(1)(a) and (e) of the General Data Protection Regulation, meaning the data controller did not prove the legal basis for processing (disclosure) personal data. Additionally, the company did not prove the legal basis for permanently retaining data about craftsmen whose data was deleted from the official public register.  
Furthermore, the Agency found that the company did not provide transparent information to respondents regarding the processing of their personal data, thus failing to ensure compliance with the requirements of Articles 12 and 14 of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
It was also established that the company maintains a record of processing activities for which it is responsible, which is not in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of the General Data Protection Regulation, and that the company appointed a data protection officer, who is also the director, without ensuring that the performance of his tasks and duties does not lead to a conflict of interest, which is contrary to the provisions of Article 38(3) and (6) of the General Data Protection Regulation. For these violations of the General Data Protection Regulation, the Agency imposed an administrative fine of 40,000 euros.  

3. **Failure to implement technical measures for the protection of personal data – 20,000 euros**  
Following media reports of a security incident within the Clinical Hospital Center Zagreb related to a cyber attack, which indirectly endangered the personal data of respondents, the Agency initiated urgent supervisory proceedings.  
It was established that the failure to implement appropriate technical security measures for the processing of personal data by the data controller KBC Zagreb, specifically acting contrary to Article 32(1)(b) and (d) and (2) of the General Data Protection Regulation, contributed to unauthorized access to personal data of respondents. By this action, KBC Zagreb facilitated an unknown individual/attacker to gain access to personal data in the information system, which, within a period of 7 days, copied/extracted data amounting to at least 3 GB outside the data controller's system.  
The attacker most likely obtained access credentials through social engineering and connected to the information system of the data controller via a VPN connection, after which he exploited a system vulnerability (outdated operating system) and secured the rights of a domain user account that had the highest privileges in the system (Domain admin), which is used as a service account for the operation of a system provided by an external company. Additionally, the attacker locked a large number of servers, deleted backups, and executed executable files (.exe) for unauthorized activities.  
KBC Zagreb made multiple failures in the design of the processing system, including limiting access, monitoring, reporting, timely response, and incorporating appropriate corrective actions into the system, for which an administrative fine of 20,000 euros was imposed.  

**Administrative fines imposed on General Hospitals – 3,000 and 4,000 euros**  
As part of the supervisory proceedings due to the illegal processing of personal data for the purpose of collecting parking fees, the Agency established that the General Hospital Zadar also uses an application solution from a commercial company that automatically retrieves data about the owners...
vehicles through the web service of the Ministry of the Interior without a legal basis, in cases where the conditions for issuing a daily parking ticket were met (if the vehicle user did not pay for parking at the hospital parking lot that is subject to charges). Furthermore, the hospital did not transparently and properly inform the respondents (parking lot users) about the processing/collection of their personal data related to parking charges, which is contrary to Article 13 and 14, paragraph 2, point (f) of the General Data Protection Regulation. Additionally, it was determined that the hospital did not take appropriate organizational measures to protect the personal data of the respondents/parking lot users when collecting the personal data of vehicle owners related to parking charges in the application, which is contrary to the provision of Article 25, paragraph 1 of the General Data Protection Regulation, nor did it conclude a contract for the processing of personal data of parking lot users with a commercial company as a processor of personal data, which is contrary to the provision of Article 28, paragraph 3 of the General Data Protection Regulation. For the aforementioned violations, a fine of 4,000 euros was imposed.

Moreover, the Agency also imposed an administrative monetary fine on the General County Hospital Vinkovci in the amount of 3,000 euros for violations of obligations under Articles 13, 32, 33, and 34, paragraph 1 of the General Data Protection Regulation. Despite the high-risk and extensive processing of health data as a core activity, the hospital, among other things, did not adequately implement and test organizational measures for protection aimed at ensuring the security of health data processing. The protection of patients' personal data and the confidentiality of health information is important not only for respecting patient privacy but also for maintaining trust in the medical profession and the healthcare services provided by doctors and other medical staff.

When imposing administrative monetary fines on the aforementioned healthcare institutions, the Agency is obliged to apply the provisions of Article 44, paragraph 2 of the Law on the Implementation of the General Data Protection Regulation, which stipulates that if an administrative monetary fine is imposed against a legal entity with public authority or against a legal entity performing a public service, the imposed administrative monetary fine must not jeopardize the performance of such public authority or public service.

Appointment and position of the Data Protection Officer – 10,000 and 12,000 euros

Two administrative monetary fines were also imposed for non-compliance with the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation regarding the obligation to appoint and position a Data Protection Officer. A fine of 12,000 euros was imposed on a casino, while 10,000 euros was imposed on a company engaged in the production of oils and fats.
