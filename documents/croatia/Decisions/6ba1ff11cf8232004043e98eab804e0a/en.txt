Dear Sir/Madam,

The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data invites you to participate in a survey regarding the scope, type of work, and education of data protection officers in Croatia. The survey is conducted via a simple online questionnaire available at the following link: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/anketa-sluzbenici-za-zastitu-podataka, and the expected time for completion is approximately 10 minutes.

Participation in this survey is anonymous, meaning that no personal data will be collected through the questionnaire that could identify the respondent. 

The purpose of the survey is to gain insight into the challenges faced by data protection officers, and your responses are crucial for our better understanding of your needs and issues, as well as for improving cooperation between the supervisory authority and data protection officers.

The results of the survey will assist the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data in designing activities that will facilitate the performance of tasks by data protection officers. 

We kindly ask you to be as honest and objective as possible when filling out the questionnaire, so that the results of the survey can be credible.

We thank you in advance for taking the time to participate in this survey and for contributing to strengthening the capacity of data protection officers and improving the field of personal data protection in Croatia.

Sincerely,

The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data
