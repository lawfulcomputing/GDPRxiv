**September 7, 2023.**

As part of the European project ARC 2, the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, in collaboration with the HUP Regional Office Osijek, organized workshops on the topic: "How to Prepare for the Supervision of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data?" in Slavonski Brod, Vinkovci, and Antunovac on September 5, 6, and 7.

At the workshop in Slavonski Brod, Director Zdravko Vukić emphasized that the activities carried out by the Agency show that the level of compliance with the legislative framework for data protection in the Republic of Croatia is not at a satisfactory level. "The deadline for adaptation has long passed, and after more than five years since the full application of the General Data Protection Regulation, ignorance and lack of knowledge can no longer serve as justification and excuse for violating the fundamental rights of Croatian citizens. Therefore, by continuously holding such workshops, our goal is to provide data controllers and processors, as well as data protection officers, with the opportunity to familiarize themselves with examples of good practices in the field of personal data protection, share with the experts from the Agency the problems they encounter in applying the General Data Protection Regulation, and, with their support, find solutions that will elevate the level of compliance in their organizations to a higher level," said Director Vukić, wishing all attendees success in their future work.

Participants in the workshop were presented with practical examples of how they can align their business operations with the General Data Protection Regulation, specifically the 10 key steps that need to be taken and where entrepreneurs most commonly make mistakes when aligning business processes with the Regulation. Additionally, Agency officials informed participants about supervisory and investigative activities and how to prepare for the supervision of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data.

The presentation "How to Prepare for the Supervision of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data" can be viewed at the following link: https://arc-rec-project.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Slavonija-rujan-2023.pdf
