In accordance with Article 45 of the Civil Servants Act (“Official Gazette”, No. 92/05, 140/05, 142/06, 77/07, 107/07, 27/08, 34/11, 49/11, 150/11, 34/12, 37/13, 38/13, 1/2015, 138/15, 61/17, 70/19, 98/19, 141/22) and Article 2 of the Regulation on the Announcement and Implementation of Public Competitions and Internal Advertisements in the Civil Service (“Official Gazette”, No. 78/2017 and 89/19), with prior approval from the Ministry of Justice and Administration (CLASS: 112-01/23-01/42 REG. NO: 514-08-01-02/03-23-02 dated January 24, 2023; CLASS: 112-01/23-01/554 REG. NO: 514-08-01-02/02-23-02 dated April 5, 2023), the Agency for Personal Data Protection announces

PUBLIC COMPETITION
for employment in the civil service for an indefinite period

SECTOR FOR OVERSIGHT, INVESTIGATIONS, AND PROTECTION OF THE RIGHTS OF DATA SUBJECTS,
Service for Oversight of Investigations, Technology, and Security
Professional Advisor at the Agency for Personal Data Protection – 2 positions, job position (24)
Professional requirements: completed undergraduate and graduate university studies or integrated undergraduate and graduate university studies or specialist graduate professional studies in law, criminology, or other social sciences, at least 3 years of work experience in relevant positions, passed state exam of the appropriate level, knowledge of the English language, computer skills, driving license category B.

Service for the Protection and Promotion of the Rights of Data Subjects
Professional Associate at the Agency for Personal Data Protection – 1 position, job position (32)

Professional requirements: completed undergraduate and graduate university studies or integrated undergraduate and graduate university studies or specialist graduate professional studies in law, at least 1 year of work experience in relevant positions, passed state exam of the appropriate level, knowledge of the English language, computer skills.

SECTOR FOR EU, INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION, AND LEGAL AFFAIRS
Service for Legal Advice, Cooperation, and Projects
Professional Advisor at the Agency for Personal Data Protection – 1 position, job position (38)
Professional requirements: completed undergraduate and graduate university studies or integrated undergraduate and graduate university studies or specialist graduate professional studies in law, at least 3 years of work experience in relevant positions, passed state exam of the appropriate level, knowledge of the English language, computer skills.

In addition to the specified requirements, candidates must also meet the general conditions for employment in the civil service as prescribed by the provisions of Article 48 of the Civil Servants Act. A person cannot be admitted to the civil service if there are obstacles to their admission as stated in Article 49 of the Civil Servants Act. Both male and female candidates may apply for the public competition. A person is admitted to the civil service with a probationary period of 3 months. Candidates who do not have a passed state exam of the appropriate level may also apply for the public competition, with the obligation to take the state exam in accordance with the provisions of Article 56 of the Civil Servants Act. Candidates who have passed the judicial exam are not required to take the state exam.

In the Application Form for the public competition, which can be found on the website of the Agency for Personal Data Protection (https://azop.hr/), it is necessary to provide the personal data of the applicant (first name, date and place of birth, address of residence, phone number, and email address) and the name of the job position being applied for. The application form must be signed by hand.

Along with the application, candidates are required to submit:
- a resume
- proof of Croatian citizenship;
- proof of work experience in relevant positions, i.e., positions in the appropriate professional qualification and field (electronic record or confirmation of data recorded in the database of the Croatian Pension Insurance Institute, employer's confirmation of work experience in relevant positions, or other proof indicating the positions the candidate has worked in);
- proof of completed level of education (copy of diploma)
- a copy of the certificate of passing the state exam of the appropriate level, or the judicial exam (for candidates who have passed the state/judicial exam).

If a person has changed their surname and submits documentation with the old surname, it is necessary to provide proof of the name change. Documents are submitted in unverified copies, and before the selection of candidates, the original will be presented. The deadline for submitting applications for the public competition is eight days from the date of publication in the “Official Gazette”.
**Complete Application**

A complete application is considered one that contains all the data and attachments specified in the public competition. A person who has not submitted a timely and proper application or does not meet the formal requirements of the public competition is not considered a candidate registered for the public competition. Applications with evidence of meeting the requirements must be submitted within 8 days from the date of publication in the "Narodne novine," either in person or by mail to the address: Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (application for public competition), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb.

**Priority Rights**

Persons who, according to special regulations, have the right to priority must refer to that right in their application, i.e., attach all prescribed documentation according to the special law. A candidate who invokes the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 101 of the Act on Croatian Veterans of the Homeland War and Members of Their Families ("Narodne novine," No. 121/17, 98/19, 84/21), and Article 47 of the Act on Civilian Victims of the Homeland War (Narodne novine, No. 84/21), must, along with the application for the advertisement, in addition to evidence of meeting the required conditions, also submit evidence of meeting the priority conditions according to the list available on the website of the Ministry of Croatian Veterans via the following link: https://branitelji.gov.hr/zaposljavanje-u-drzavnoj-sluzbi/843. 

A candidate who invokes the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 9 of the Act on Professional Rehabilitation and Employment of Persons with Disabilities ("Narodne novine," No. 157/13, 152/14, 39/18, and 32/20), must, along with the application for the public competition, in addition to evidence of meeting the required conditions, also submit proof of established status as a person with a disability. 

A candidate who invokes the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 48.f of the Act on the Protection of Military and Civil War Invalids ("Narodne novine," No. 33/92, 57/92, 77/92, 27/93, 58/93, 2/94, 76/94, 108/95, 108/96, 82/01, 94/01, 103/03, 148/13, and 98/19), must, along with the application for the advertisement, in addition to evidence of meeting the required conditions, also submit a decision or confirmation from which the mentioned right is evident. 

A candidate who is a member of a national minority has the right to invoke priority in employment based on Article 22 of the Constitutional Act on the Rights of National Minorities ("Narodne novine," No. 155/02, 47/10, 80/10, and 93/11 – Decision of the Constitutional Court of the Republic of Croatia), and is not required to prove their status as a member of a national minority.

**Commission for the Public Competition**

The Commission for the Implementation of the Public Competition (hereinafter: the Commission) is appointed by the Director of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data. The Commission determines the list of candidates who have applied for the public competition and meet the formal requirements of the public competition, whose applications are timely and complete, and refers candidates from that list for testing and interviews.

**Testing**

Testing consists of verifying the knowledge, abilities, and skills of candidates (written part of the testing) and an interview with the candidates by the Commission. A candidate who has not participated in the testing is no longer considered a candidate in the process. The time and place of the testing will be announced at least five days before the date set for testing on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/). It is considered that a candidate who has not participated in the testing is no longer considered a candidate in the process.

**Job Description and Testing Information**

The job description and salary information for the position, the content and method of testing, and the legal sources for preparing candidates for testing will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/). Legal sources for preparing candidates for testing will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/).

**Selected Candidate**

The selected candidate will be invited to submit, within a reasonable time and before the decision on admission to the civil service is made, a certificate from the competent court that no criminal proceedings are being conducted against them, a certificate of health capacity for performing the duties of the position, and original documents proving the fulfillment of formal requirements from the advertisement. Failure to submit these documents will be considered a withdrawal from the admission to the civil service.

If no persons who meet the prescribed conditions apply for the public competition, or if the registered candidates do not meet the requirements in the testing, the head of the body will suspend the procedure for this competition. 

Candidates will be informed of the results of the public competition by a public announcement of the decision on the admission to the civil service of the selected candidate on the website of the Ministry of Justice and Administration (https://mpu.gov.hr/) and the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/). The delivery of the decision to all candidates is considered published upon the expiration of the eighth day from the date of publication on the website of the Ministry of Justice and Administration (https://mpu.gov.hr/).
