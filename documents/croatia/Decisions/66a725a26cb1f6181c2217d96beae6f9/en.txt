As part of the Privacy Symposium 2024 in Venice, on Wednesday, June 12, a panel titled "Innovative Tools for Supervisory Authorities to Assist with Compliance" was held, during which the Director of the Agency, Zdravko Vukić, presented the web tool "Olivia," developed for Croatian and Italian small and medium-sized enterprises within the framework of the European project ARC2.

During the panel, Director Zdravko Vukić introduced the structure of "Olivia" and three highlighted modules: the basics of GDPR, privacy policies, and video surveillance. "Olivia" contains 15 theoretical and practical modules and 20 webinars on various data protection topics. In the theoretical part, entrepreneurs can go through short educational lessons to learn more about their obligations under GDPR, watch educational videos, and subsequently access a test to check their knowledge. After successfully completing the questionnaire (with at least 80% correct answers), the user will receive a certificate as proof of completion of the individual theoretical module. In the practical part, users have the opportunity to create internal documents that can help them demonstrate their compliance or assess the level of compliance within their organization.

"I am pleased to have the opportunity to participate in this thematic panel and proudly present the web tool 'Olivia' that we developed with our project partners – the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Faculty of Organization and Informatics, Vrije University Brussels, and the University of Florence. Given that our tool is open-source, user-friendly, and free, I invite other data protection authorities to use 'Olivia' and adapt it to the needs of their small and medium-sized enterprises, so that entrepreneurs across Europe can benefit from the ARC2 project," said Director Vukić, adding that entrepreneurs in the Republic of Croatia expressed satisfaction with having access to a tool that enables them to achieve compliance with data protection regulations.

The panel also featured Louis Dutheillet de Lamothe (French Data Protection Authority), Jérôme Commodi (Luxembourg Data Protection Authority), and Luigi Montuori (Italian Data Protection Authority), who presented three additional modules of the web tool "Olivia."

The Privacy Symposium, held from June 10 to June 14, 2024, in Venice, aims to promote international dialogue, cooperation, and knowledge exchange on personal data protection and compliance by bringing together data protection experts, competent data protection supervisory authorities, international organizations, and the research community. The Symposium was hosted by Ca' Foscari University and organized in collaboration with several partners, including the Data Protection Department of the Council of Europe, the European Centre for Certification and Privacy (ECCP), the European Law Students' Association (ELSA), the European Cyber Security Organization (ECSO), the European Federation of Data Protection Officers (EFDPO), the European Centre for Privacy and Cybersecurity (ECPC), the Italian Institute for Privacy (IIP), the Italian Cyber Security Association (CLUSIT), the IoT Forum, Mandat International, IoT Lab, and European research projects such as Gatekeeper.
