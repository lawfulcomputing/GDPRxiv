**9.5.2025.**  
A public competition has been announced for admission to the civil service for an indefinite period for the position number 3 Senior Advisor in the Director's Cabinet.  
Applications are accepted via the Centralized Employment System https://selekcija.gov.hr/natjecaji/objavljeni-natjecaji until 24.5.2025.

**Professional Requirements:**

Level of HKO: Level 7.1.st HKO or 7.1.sv HKO (Higher Education)

Field of Social Sciences, Economics

At least 4 years of work experience in relevant positions  
**Additional Requirements:**

Passed the state exam of the II level

In addition to the specified professional requirements, candidates must also meet the general conditions for admission to the civil service, which are:

- Health capacity to perform the duties of the position for which they are being hired
- Croatian citizenship, unless otherwise stipulated (citizen of a member state of the European Economic Area or the Swiss Confederation, and a citizen of a third country under the conditions prescribed by Article 52 of the Civil Servants Act)

**Competencies:**

| Name | Level of Outcome Specification | Weight |
|------|-------------------------------|--------|
| Logic | High complexity level | 40 |
| Digital Literacy | Medium complexity level | 20 |
| Written Communication in English | Medium complexity level | 20 |
| Responsibility in Work and Task Orientation | High complexity level | 20 |

**Job Description:**
Providing opinions on drafts of legislative proposals prepared by other proposers, as needed (depending on the quality of input data obtained through monitoring and evaluation activities in the area of 1.A), preparing additional analytical bases on possible directions for improving personal data protection, administrative and professional tasks related to the establishment and coordination of financial management and controls, preparing expert opinions, analytical bases, and reports of various complexity levels on issues in the field of work, monitoring the workload of internal organizational units and the success in achieving set goals.  
Salary calculation coefficient for the position:  
2.1  
A person cannot be admitted to the civil service if there are obstacles to their admission as per Article 54 of the Civil Servants Act.  
Both genders may apply for the public competition.  
Persons admitted to the civil service for an indefinite period are subject to a probationary period of three months.

**Mandatory Documentation:**

- Electronic record or confirmation of data recorded in the database of the Croatian Pension Insurance Institute, which must contain information about the employer, the basis of insurance, the start and termination of insurance, working hours, actual and necessary educational qualifications, and the duration of insurance period.
- Employment contract / Contract for work / Decision on assignment from which the required work experience for the position applied for is evident, or a confirmation from the employer about work experience in relevant jobs, which must include the type of work performed, the educational qualifications held, and the period during which the candidate performed the specified work.
- Proof of the appropriate level of education.

The deadline for applications is 15 days from the announcement of the public competition.  
Candidates who indicated the need for reasonable accommodation in their user profile when applying for the competition will be provided with appropriate adjustments during the testing or interview process.  
Candidates who invoke the right of preference in the admission to the civil service according to a special law during the procedure are required to attach all prescribed documentation according to the special law to their user profile in the Centralized Employment System and have preference over other candidates only under equal conditions.  
Testing of computer-verifiable competencies is conducted electronically, followed by the verification of competencies that are not electronically verifiable (if any) and an interview with the Commission. Testing of competencies that are not electronically verifiable and the interview are conducted by a Commission appointed by the head of the hiring body and take place in that body. Notification of the time and place of the necessary tests and interviews will be published at least five days before the date set for testing in the Centralized Employment System. Candidates will be informed of the results of the procedure by a public announcement of the decision regarding the selected candidate in the Centralized Employment System.  
Delivery of the decision to all candidates is considered completed upon the expiration of the eighth day from the date of publication in the Centralized Employment System. Before the decision is made, the proposed candidate must present the original documentation, a certificate of health capacity, and a certificate from the competent court that no criminal proceedings are being conducted against the person to the hiring body.
