August 30, 2024.  
In accordance with Article 160 of the Civil Servants Act (“Official Gazette” No. 155/23, 85/24) and Article 2 of the Regulation on the Announcement and Implementation of Public Competitions and Internal Advertisements in the Civil Service (“Official Gazette” Nos. 78/17 and 89/19), with the prior opinion of the Ministry of Justice, Administration and Digital Transformation (CLASS: 112-01/24-01/750 REGISTRATION NUMBER: 514-08-03/03-24-02 dated June 11, 2024), the Agency for Personal Data Protection announces  

PUBLIC COMPETITION  
for recruitment into civil service for an indefinite period  
SECTOR FOR OVERSIGHT, INVESTIGATIONS AND PROTECTION OF THE RIGHTS OF THE SUBJECTS,  
Department for Oversight, Investigations, Technologies and Security  

Senior Advisor – 1 position, job position (21)  

Professional requirements: Level 7.1. of the Croatian Qualifications Framework (HKO) in legal, technical, or IT fields, at least 4 years of work experience in relevant jobs, passed state exam of the appropriate level, knowledge of the English language, computer skills, valid category B driving license.  

SECTOR FOR EU, INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION AND LEGAL AFFAIRS  
Department for International Data Transfers and Compliance Mechanisms  

Senior Advisor – 1 position, job position (42)  

Professional requirements: Level 7.1. of the Croatian Qualifications Framework (HKO) in legal or other social fields, at least 4 years of work experience in relevant jobs, passed state exam of the appropriate level, knowledge of the English language, computer skills.  

In addition to the specified requirements, candidates must also meet the general conditions for recruitment into civil service as prescribed by Article 52 of the Civil Servants Act.  
A person cannot be admitted to civil service if there are obstacles to their recruitment as per Article 54 of the Civil Servants Act.  
Both genders may apply for the public competition.  
A person is admitted to civil service with a probation period of 3 months.  
Candidates who have not passed the state exam of the appropriate level may also apply for the public competition, with the obligation to pass the state exam in accordance with the provisions of Article 99 of the Civil Servants Act. Candidates who have passed the judicial exam are not required to take the state exam.  

In the Application Form for the public competition, which can be found on the website of the Agency for Personal Data Protection (https://azop.hr/), it is necessary to provide personal data of the applicant (full name, date and place of birth, residential address, phone number, and email address) and the name of the job position for which they are applying.  

The application form must be signed by hand. Application form_RM21,42  
Along with the application, candidates are required to submit:  

- a resume  
- proof of Croatian citizenship;  
- proof of work experience in relevant jobs, i.e., jobs in the appropriate professional qualification and field (electronic record or confirmation from the Croatian Pension Insurance Institute regarding data recorded in the main registry showing the employer, duration of insurance, actual and required professional qualification, or other proof showing the necessary data);  
- proof of completed level of education (copy of diploma)  
- a copy of the certificate of passing the state exam of the appropriate level, or the judicial exam (for candidates who have passed the state/judicial exam).  
- proof of possession of a valid category B driving license for vehicles (for the job position under No. 1 for which the professional requirement is a category B driving license)
If a person has changed their surname and submits documentation with their old surname, it is necessary to provide proof of the surname change. Documents are attached in an unverified copy, and the original will be presented before the selection of the candidate. The deadline for submitting applications for the public competition is eight days from the date of publication in the "Narodne novine."

A complete application is considered one that contains all the data and attachments specified in the public competition. A person who has not submitted a timely and proper application or does not meet the formal requirements of the public competition is not considered a candidate for the public competition.

Applications with evidence of meeting the requirements must be submitted within 8 days from the date of publication in the "Narodne novine," either in person or by mail to the address: Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (application for the public competition), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb. Persons who, according to special regulations, have the right to priority must refer to that right in their application, and attach all prescribed documentation according to the special law.

Candidates who invoke the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 101 of the Law on Croatian Veterans of the Homeland War and Members of Their Families ("Narodne novine," No. 121/17, 98/19, 84/21, 156/23), and Article 47 of the Law on Civilian Victims of the Homeland War ("Narodne novine," No. 84/21), must, along with their application for the advertisement, in addition to proof of meeting the required conditions, also attach evidence of meeting the priority conditions according to the list available on the website of the Ministry of Croatian Veterans via the following link: https://branitelji.gov.hr/zaposljavanje-u-drzavnoj-sluzbi/843. 

Candidates who invoke the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 9 of the Law on Professional Rehabilitation and Employment of Persons with Disabilities ("Narodne novine," No. 157/13, 152/14, 39/18, and 32/20), must, along with their application for the public competition, in addition to proof of meeting the required conditions, also attach proof of established status as a person with a disability. 

Candidates who invoke the right to priority in employment in accordance with Article 48.f of the Law on the Protection of Military and Civilian War Invalids ("Narodne novine," No. 33/92, 57/92, 77/92, 27/93, 58/93, 2/94, 76/94, 108/95, 108/96, 82/01, 94/01, 103/03, 148/13, and 98/19), must, along with their application for the advertisement, in addition to proof of meeting the required conditions, also attach a decision or confirmation from which this right is evident. 

A candidate who is a member of a national minority has the right to invoke priority in employment based on Article 22 of the Constitutional Law on the Rights of National Minorities ("Narodne novine," No. 155/02, 47/10, 80/10, and 93/11 – Decision of the Constitutional Court of the Republic of Croatia) and is not required to prove their status as a member of a national minority. 

The Commission for the Implementation of the Public Competition (hereinafter referred to as: the Commission) is appointed by the Director of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data. The Commission establishes a list of candidates who have applied for the public competition and meet the formal requirements of the public competition, whose applications are timely and complete, and refers candidates from that list for testing and interviews.

Testing consists of verifying the knowledge, abilities, and skills of candidates (written part of the testing) and an interview with the candidates by the Commission.

A candidate who has not attended the testing is no longer considered a candidate in the process. The time and place of the testing will be announced at least five days before the date set for testing on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/). It is considered that a candidate who has not attended the testing is no longer considered a candidate in the process. 

The job description and salary data for the position, the content and method of testing, and the legal sources for preparing candidates for testing will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/). Legal sources for preparing candidates for testing will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/).

The selected candidate will be invited to submit, within a reasonable time before the decision on admission to the civil service is made, a certificate from the competent court that no criminal proceedings are being conducted against them, a certificate of health capacity to perform the duties of the position, and original documents proving the fulfillment of formal conditions from the advertisement. Failure to provide these will be considered a withdrawal from admission to the civil service.

If no persons who meet the prescribed conditions apply for the public competition, or if the applicants do not pass the testing, the head of the body will suspend the procedure for this competition. Candidates will be informed of the results of the public competition by public announcement of the decision on the admission of the selected candidate to the civil service on the website of the Ministry of Justice, Administration, and Digital Transformation (https://mpudt.gov.hr/) and the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/). 

The delivery of the decision to all candidates is considered published upon the expiration of the eighth day from the date of publication on the website of the Ministry of Justice, Administration, and Digital Transformation (https://mpudt.gov.hr/).
