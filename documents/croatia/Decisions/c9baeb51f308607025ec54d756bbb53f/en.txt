At the 19th session of the Croatian Parliament held on Friday, December 15, 2023, the Annual Report on the Work of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data for the period from January 1 to December 31, 2022, was adopted. 

The reporting period, from January 1, 2022, to December 31, 2022, was marked by international, project, and educational-consultative activities of the Agency, as well as a significant increase in the number of corrective measures, among which administrative fines stand out. In 2022, the Agency imposed 257 corrective measures, representing an increase of 117.80% compared to 2021, when 118 corrective measures were imposed, and it issued 14 administrative fines totaling 528,369.00 euros, marking an increase of 250% compared to 2021.

During the mentioned reporting period, the Agency had a total of 3,761 cases, of which 2,204 cases were received in 2022, while 1,557 cases were carried over from the previous period due to their complexity. In 2022, a total of 2,914 cases were resolved. The received cases pertained to complaints/requests from citizens for the determination of violations of rights, submissions from other bodies forwarding requests for the determination of violations of rights for further processing, inquiries and doubts from citizens regarding unlawful processing of personal data, and inquiries from citizens seeking opinions on the application of the General Data Protection Regulation in specific sectors. Additionally, the Agency recorded an increase in the number of received requests for the determination of violations of rights, which, in accordance with Article 34 of the Law on the Implementation of the General Data Protection Regulation, the Agency decides by resolution, with the number of resolved requests for the determination of violations of rights amounting to 317.

In accordance with its investigative powers, the Agency conducted a total of 1,140 inspections in 2022, which included inspections at the request of respondents, at the suggestion of third parties, and ex officio. Out of the total number of inspections/monitoring activities, 299 were conducted directly, while 841 inspections/monitoring activities were conducted indirectly. The number of citizens who contact the Agency by phone daily is significantly higher than the number of inquiries received in writing, with over 2,800 calls recorded.

In 2022, the Agency had the honor of hosting the 30th anniversary conference of European supervisory authorities, which took place under the auspices of the Government of the Republic of Croatia on May 19 and 20, 2022, in Cavtat and Dubrovnik.

Noteworthy is the Twinning project "Providing Support in the Implementation of the Modernized Legislative Framework for the Protection of Personal Data of the Republic of North Macedonia" and the project co-financed by the European Union program for Rights, Equality, and Citizenship titled "Data Protection Awareness Campaign for Small and Medium Enterprises" (ARC), during which the Agency educated over 2,500 small and medium enterprises and produced a range of educational materials.
