Delegation of the Agency for Personal Data Protection, led by the Director of the Agency and Vice-President of the EDPB Zdravko Vukić, visited the Republic of Kosovo from November 25 to 27, 2024, at the invitation of the Commissioner of the Agency for Information and Privacy of the Republic of Kosovo Krenare Sogojeva Dermaku. 

During this visit, Director Zdravko Vukić, Deputy Director Igor Vulje, and Head of the Sector for Supervision, Investigations, and Protection of the Rights of Data Subjects Mario Milner, accompanied by the Ambassador of the Republic of Croatia to the Republic of Kosovo Danijela Barišić and Diplomatic Advisor Nedjeljko Špilek, as well as the delegation of the Kosovo Agency, held working meetings in the Parliament of the Republic of Kosovo, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Diaspora, and the Parliamentary Committee for Security and Defense Issues. 

This working visit is a continuation of the good cooperation between the Croatian and Kosovo supervisory authorities, following the granting of observer status to the Kosovo supervisory authority in the activities of the European Data Protection Board in October, thanks to the support of the Director of the AZOP and Vice-President of the EDPB Zdravko Vukić, which was particularly emphasized during the held working meetings. 

Thus, the President of the Parliament Glauk Konjufca emphasized that the observer status of the Kosovo agency is particularly important for Kosovo, as the EDPB represents the highest European authority for data protection standards and provides a platform for exchange, expertise, and updating of ongoing legal development in the field of personal data protection. Additionally, President Konjufca thanked Director Vukić and the Agency for their previous cooperation, highlighting how the experience of AZOP contributes to the Republic of Kosovo in aligning with European data protection standards. 

The importance of good cooperation was also highlighted by the Deputy Prime Minister of the Republic of Kosovo and Minister of Foreign Affairs and Diaspora Donika Gërvalla-Schwarz, as well as the First Deputy of the Parliamentary Committee for Security and Defense Issues Enver Dugolli. 

As part of the working visit, the delegation of the Agency also visited Lipljan, where they were received by Mayor Imri Ahmeti. During this occasion, they also visited Janjevo, a part of the city of Lipljan, accompanied by the parish priest Don Matej Palić. This is a place that was settled by Croats during the 14th century, who managed to preserve their Catholic faith and Croatian identity through the centuries. 

At the end of their stay in the Republic of Kosovo, the delegation of the Agency paid tribute to the women who suffered in the war for the independence of the Republic of Kosovo at the "Heroina" monument in Pristina.
