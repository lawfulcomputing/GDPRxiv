The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data held a free training for data protection officers in the public health sector, the program of which was developed within the SPE (Support pool of experts) program of the European Data Protection Board. 

Namely, according to the Agency's research and insights, in a large number of cases, data protection officers in the public health sector do not have adequate knowledge about personal data protection and face challenges and numerous uncertainties in their attempts to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation. Through this three-day comprehensive training, the Agency provided support in raising the level of knowledge for about twenty data protection officers from the public health sector.

The Director of the Agency for the Protection of Data and Vice-President of the European Data Protection Board, Zdravko Vukić, emphasized that the Agency continuously provides support to all data protection officers. "I believe that this training will help you understand your obligations and that you will apply the knowledge gained in your daily operations, thereby contributing to raising the level of compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation in the health sector in the Republic of Croatia."

On the first day of training, the officers of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data covered the basic concepts of the General Data Protection Regulation in healthcare institutions, the legal basis for data processing, the rights of data subjects, privacy policy, and the role of the data protection officer, i.e., the tasks of the officer.

During the second day, participants had the opportunity to learn about personal data protection measures, the roles and obligations of the data controller and processor, how to maintain a record of processing activities, how to conduct a data protection impact assessment, and what to do in the event of a personal data breach, as well as how to prepare for the Agency's oversight.

The last day was reserved for knowledge verification, and after successfully completing the test, participants received a certificate of completion for the data protection officer course in the public health sector.

Let us remind you that this year the Agency also held two trainings for data protection officers from the education sector.
