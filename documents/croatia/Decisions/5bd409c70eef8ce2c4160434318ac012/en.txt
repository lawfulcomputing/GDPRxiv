**September 27, 2024.**  
**Notice of Testing for Position 42 – Senior Advisor**  
to candidates whose applications are timely and complete and who meet the formal requirements of the public competition for permanent employment in the Agency for Personal Data Protection in the Sector for EU, International Cooperation, and Legal Affairs, Service for International Data Transfers and Compliance Mechanisms, position (42) – Senior Advisor – 1 executor, published in the "Narodne novine" number 102/24 on August 30, 2024.  
**List of Candidates:**  

Testing will be conducted in two phases.

The first phase of testing, which consists of assessing knowledge, skills, and abilities essential for performing the duties of the position, will be attended by all candidates who meet the formal requirements of the Public Competition and have submitted a timely and complete application. This phase will take place on October 4, 2024 (Friday) at 9:00 AM, at the premises of the Agency for Personal Data Protection, located at Selska cesta 136, Zagreb.

Only those candidates who achieve the best results in the first phase of testing will be invited to the second phase of testing, which consists of assessing knowledge of computer literacy and the English language. The results of the first phase of testing will be known to candidates on the same day after the completion of the testing (during the testing, candidates will be informed when and where they will be notified of the results), and after the announcement of the results of the first phase of testing, the second phase will take place at the same location.

A candidate who does not attend the testing will be considered to have withdrawn their application for the public competition. Upon arrival for the knowledge assessment, candidates will be required to present appropriate identification to verify their identity. Candidates who cannot prove their identity will not be allowed to participate in the testing. Candidates are required to adhere to the established testing schedule. 

During the written assessment of knowledge and abilities, the following is not permitted:

1. Using any literature or notes;  
2. Leaving the room where the assessment is taking place;  
3. Using mobile phones or other communication devices;  
4. Talking to other candidates or otherwise disrupting the concentration of candidates.

If a candidate violates any of the above rules, they will be removed from the knowledge assessment, and their result will not be recognized or evaluated by the Commission for the Implementation of the Public Competition. Candidates will be timely informed of the exact date of the interview.
