The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data has prepared a short Christmas story for the youngest about personal data.  
Boy Jakov was a curious boy who greatly wanted to see Santa Claus's list. He was interested in who among his friends had been good throughout the year, so he decided to write a letter to Santa Claus and request the list in hopes of receiving it. Did Santa Claus fulfill Jakov's wish? Read in the story "Jakov in Search of Santa Claus's List."
