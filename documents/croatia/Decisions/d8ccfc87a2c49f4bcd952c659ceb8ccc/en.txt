Director Zdravko Vukić and officials of the Agency held a guest lecture on the topic "Legal Framework for the Protection of Personal Data in the Republic of Croatia" on Thursday, March 23, 2023, as part of the Administrative Informatics with Statistics course in the first year of the Professional Administrative Studies at the Faculty of Law in Split, at the invitation of Associate Professor Dr. Marija Boban, Head of the Department of Economic, Financial and Information Sciences and Statistics, and Chair of the Department of Information Sciences and Statistics. 

Since the protection of personal data is an integral part of the course program, students had the opportunity to hear more about the General Data Protection Regulation and the scope of its application, as well as about the independent supervisory authority, namely the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, and the activities that the Agency carries out directly from the head of the authority. 

"The right to the protection of personal data is a democratic imperative, but it is also an economic necessity. The misuse of personal data deeply undermines trust, security, and privacy as essential segments of every individual's personality. Without individuals' trust in how their personal data is handled, sustainable growth of the European data-driven economy is impossible," said Director Zdravko Vukić, wishing the students success in their further studies and that they successfully protect their own and others' personal data. 

Additionally, the topic of the lecture included the Act on the Implementation of the General Data Protection Regulation, which ensures the implementation of the Regulation, but also regulates specific issues that are not defined by the Regulation, such as defining the concept of public authority, age limits for offering information society services directly to children, processing of biometric and genetic data, processing of personal data through video surveillance, etc. 

Before the lecture, Director Zdravko Vukić and his associates were received by the Dean of the Faculty of Law in Split, Associate Professor Dr. Ratko Brnabić, and future cooperation between the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data and the Faculty of Law in Split was agreed upon.
