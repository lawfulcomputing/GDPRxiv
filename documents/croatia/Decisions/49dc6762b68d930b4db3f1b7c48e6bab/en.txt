The Agency for Personal Data Protection is organizing a series of free educational activities throughout October to mark the European Cybersecurity Month. The aim of the training is to raise awareness about personal data protection and cybersecurity, along with practical advice and solutions that you can apply in your daily business and life. All webinars will start at 10 AM and last for one hour. All our activities are free and intended for all organizations (data controllers and processors from the private and public sectors).

Additionally, to enhance your knowledge in the field of personal data protection and to demonstrate your compliance and reliability, the Agency for Personal Data Protection invites you to try the web tool Olivia, which the Agency developed as part of the EU ARC II project. The use of the web tool Olivia is completely free for all organizations. Olivia contains a range of learning modules, knowledge tests, and templates for internal acts with which you will demonstrate your compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation. An additional value of the web tool Olivia is that it can also serve as a tool for raising awareness about data protection within the organization. After successfully passing the knowledge test, Olivia generates certificates for the completed module, which can serve as proof that the organization takes care of personal data protection, or as one of the organizational measures for data protection. Register and try it out at the link: https://olivia-gdpr-arc.eu/hr
