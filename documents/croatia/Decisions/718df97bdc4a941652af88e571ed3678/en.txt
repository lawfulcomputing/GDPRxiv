The Agency for the Protection of Personal Data organized the annual meeting of the "20i7 Initiative" in Zadar on May 27 and 28, 2024, which brought together supervisory authorities for data protection from Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Kosovo, Slovenia, Serbia, and North Macedonia.  
Director Zdravko Vukić expressed satisfaction that the Agency is hosting this year's meeting, where supervisory authorities have the opportunity to discuss current issues in the field of personal data protection and exchange experiences and best practices from their respective countries.  
The 20i7 Initiative will continue to meet annually, and members will assist each other whenever they need legal or other help in solving problems by seeking advice from other members and whenever common goals can be achieved, for example, through collaboration on projects to raise awareness of the importance of personal data protection.  
The 20i7 Initiative was established in 2017 at the initiative of the Slovenian supervisory authority and brings together high representatives of supervisory authorities for personal data protection from Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Croatia, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Slovenia, and Serbia.
