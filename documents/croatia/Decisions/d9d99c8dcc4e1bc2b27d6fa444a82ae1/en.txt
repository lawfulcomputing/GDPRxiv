Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, from April 22 to April 24, 2024, held a free training for about fifty data protection officers from the education sector at the premises of the Sopot kindergarten. The three-day comprehensive training, aimed at emphasizing the key role of data protection officers, fostering a deeper understanding and knowledge of obligations under the General Data Protection Regulation, was developed by the Agency in collaboration with the European Data Protection Board as part of the Support Pool of Experts initiative.

According to the Agency's research and insights, data protection officers in the education sector face challenges and numerous uncertainties in their attempts to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation. Therefore, during the training, the Agency's officials assisted in understanding all obligations arising from the legislative framework for the protection of personal data and explained, among other things, which data is collected and processed within the educational system, who is responsible for it, what the key common information systems in education are and how they are regulated, what the obligations of data controllers and processors are in accordance with sectoral regulations in the field of education from the perspective of data protection. Additionally, discussions were held regarding issues of understanding and applying basic concepts of data protection and difficulties in conducting data protection impact assessments and proportionality tests.

The last day of the training was reserved for a final exam, and officials who successfully passed it will receive a certificate confirming that they possess the adequate knowledge in the field of personal data protection necessary for performing this job. The officials who participated in the training expressed satisfaction that they would be able to apply the knowledge gained from this comprehensive training in their daily work, thereby contributing to raising the level of compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation in the education sector in the Republic of Croatia.

After two training sessions for data protection officers from the education sector, a free training session for officials from the public health sector will also be held in June.
