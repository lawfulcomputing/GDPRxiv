If you receive an email from addresses that appear to be sent from the official email addresses of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (addresses that have azop.hr after the @ symbol – e.g., zop@azop.hr, admin@azop.hr) with the subject "Third parties have access to your account," please delete it immediately, do not respond to it, and do not forward it!  
For more information about phishing, read at the link: https://azop.hr/phishing-napadi-kako-ih-prepoznati-i-zastititi-se/
