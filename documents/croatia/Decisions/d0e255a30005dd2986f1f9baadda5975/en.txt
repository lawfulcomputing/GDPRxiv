November 1, 2024.  
Representatives of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data participated in a conference organized by the EU-LAC Digital Alliance on the topic of high-level political dialogue between the EU and LAC regarding data governance, held in Montevideo, Uruguay, on September 30 and October 1, 2024. The conference brought together government officials, representatives from the private sector, civil society, and the academic community from Latin America, the Caribbean, and the European Union.  
The dialogue focused on topics such as the regulation of personal data governance, the role of data protection authorities, and interactions with other data protection authorities in the context of GDPR application, international data transfer, and the relationship between artificial intelligence and personal data. Countries in the LAC region and the EU shared their experiences, highlighting challenges and expectations, and will collaborate to reach common conclusions and formulate specific and feasible opportunities for cooperation between the EU and LAC.  
The EU-LAC Digital Alliance, in accordance with the Joint Declaration on the Digital Alliance from 2023, adopted by 20 heads of government and state from Latin America and the Caribbean, the European Union, and its 27 member states, is a voluntary partnership based on shared values and a common vision of human-centered digital transformation aimed at reducing the challenges posed by digitization.  
Marko Trošelj, Head of the Legal Advisory, Cooperation, and Projects Department at the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, also participated in a panel on the topic: Independent Data Protection Authorities, the Importance of Regional and International Initiatives for Creating a Cooperation Area.
