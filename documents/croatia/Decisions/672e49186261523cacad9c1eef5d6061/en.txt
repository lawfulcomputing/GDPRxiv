Agency for Personal Data Protection and the Croatian Chamber of Economy are organizing a consultation on May 5, 2023, starting at 10 AM, titled “5 Years of GDPR Implementation: Issues, Solutions, Penalties, and Examples of Good Practice,” in Zagreb, Roosevelt Square 2 (Croatian Chamber of Economy).

This consultation is organized by the CCE and the Agency on the occasion of the 5th anniversary of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), a legal regulation that has been fully applicable in the European Union since May 25, 2018, marking a milestone in the field of personal data protection and significantly changing the awareness of the importance of personal data protection among businesses and individuals.

The aim of the General Data Protection Regulation is to protect the personal data of individuals, provide citizens with control over their personal data, and create a high and uniform level of personal data protection in the European Union.

The General Data Protection Regulation defines the rights of individuals and, accordingly, the obligations of entities that process personal data. All business entities that collect, store, and in any way process the personal data of Croatian citizens and citizens from the European Economic Area are required to apply the General Data Protection Regulation.

Furthermore, we would like to particularly emphasize on the 5th anniversary of the GDPR: all organizations that process personal data must be aware of the rights of individuals regarding the processing of their personal data and enable them to exercise their rights.

Numerous supervisory and educational activities conducted by the Agency have clearly shown that the level of compliance with the legislative framework for data protection in the Republic of Croatia is not at a satisfactory level. The Agency continuously provides support primarily to data protection officers and micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises through EU projects ARC and ARC2.

It is high time that companies, public authorities, and all data controllers/processors realize that the deadline for adjustment has long passed and that after 5 years of full GDPR implementation, ignorance and lack of knowledge can no longer serve as justification and excuse for violating the fundamental rights of Croatian and European citizens.

Moreover, on this occasion, the Agency wishes to highlight the key role that data protection officers have in the personal data protection system, as they are often the weakest link. In addition to having appropriate knowledge in the field of personal data protection, officers must have a good understanding of the business processes of the organization in which they work, continuously educate themselves, and also educate employees in the organization where they operate as officers.

The goal of organizing this consultation is to provide data controllers and processors and data protection officers with the opportunity to familiarize themselves with the latest trends and examples of good practice in the field of personal data protection, share with AZOP experts the problems they encounter in the implementation of the GDPR, and, with their support, find solutions that will elevate the level of compliance in their organizations to a higher level.

The Agency emphasizes that investing in personal data protection and information security is no longer just a necessary and desirable investment, but has become an essential and indispensable investment for successful business operations.

The GDPR has come to stay, and data protection will become increasingly important in the era of big data and artificial intelligence, as evidenced by the example of the ban on Chat GPT in Italy, where its use was prohibited by the Italian data protection authority due to violations of the GDPR.
