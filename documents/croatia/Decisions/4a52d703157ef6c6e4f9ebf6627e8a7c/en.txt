As part of the project "22HR02 Digital as Default – Optimizing the Efficiency and Quality of Judicial Services and Transparency of Court Decisions," implemented by the Council of Europe, together with the Ministry of Justice and Administration, with the support of the Directorate-General for Structural Reform Support (DG REFORM) of the European Commission, a workshop titled "Strengthening the Capacity of e-Communication Users and the Ministry of Justice and Administration" was held on January 23 and 24, 2024, attended by representatives of the Agency.  
The project aims to expand and promote the use of the e-Communication system in the Croatian judiciary. This workshop is part of a series of events aimed at supporting the implementation of the existing action plan for change management in the Ministry of Justice and Administration and target user groups, with an emphasis on e-Communication. The primary goal of this workshop is to shorten the duration of proceedings in Croatian courts through increased application of the e-Communication system in all types of proceedings.
