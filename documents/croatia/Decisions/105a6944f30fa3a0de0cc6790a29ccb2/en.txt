15.05.2023.  
Dear Data Protection Officers,  
The Agency, in collaboration with the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) and the supervisory authorities for data protection across the European Economic Area, and with the cooperation of the European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS), is conducting a coordinated enforcement action for 2023 (Coordinated Enforcement Framework – CEF 2023) on the topic of the appointment and role of Data Protection Officers. For further competent action, the Agency requires you to provide data or respond to the questions in the Questionnaire that you received via email at https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/koordinirana-provedbena-akcija-azop regarding the appointment and role of Data Protection Officers within your organization.  

The Questionnaire is mandatory for all – the private sector and all public authorities. The deadline for completing the questionnaire has been extended to June 1st.  

If you encounter technical problems when loading the questionnaire (if questions and answers do not appear correctly) – please try opening the questionnaire in a different web browser.  
If you receive an error (Error) – please try submitting the questionnaire later.  
If none of this works, or if you cannot access the questionnaire via the link you received in the email, please send us the completed questionnaire at azop@azop.hr. You can download the questionnaire here: DPO-questionnaire.  
If you have already submitted the questionnaire and wish to change your answers afterwards, this is not possible. However, you can fill out the questionnaire again and note what you have changed.
