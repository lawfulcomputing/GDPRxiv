Director of the Agency and Vice-President of the European Data Protection Board Zdravko Vukić was on a working visit to Pristina, where he participated in the conference "Managing Digital Transformation in the Financial Sector – Opportunities and Challenges in the Age of Artificial Intelligence."  
In a conversation with Kaltrina Haxha Ferizi from ProCredit Bank Kosovo, Director Vukić spoke about the activities of the EDPB and the Personal Data Protection Agency. He emphasized that the primary goal of the Agency is the protection of Croatian citizens and that the imposition of monetary fines in certain cases is necessary. He reminded that in 2023, the Agency imposed the highest fines to date, amounting to 5.47 million and 2.26 million euros on debt collection agencies. He also revealed that last year saw the highest number of monetary fines imposed in a single year, totaling 38.  
The discussion also covered the educational activities conducted by the Agency over the past four years through European projects, which allowed more than 10,000 Croatian entrepreneurs to attend lectures on various data protection topics such as cybersecurity, technical and organizational measures, legal basis, data protection impact assessments, etc.  
"In today's age when everything is digitalized, with life entirely in the virtual space, the protection of our personal data is of crucial importance. The implementation of technical and organizational measures is essential, and there can be no compromise on this. Investing in information security and data protection is no longer an option – it is an obligation. Cyberattacks occur every second, and with AI empowering attackers, the risks are greater than ever. We take our advisory role very seriously, just as we do our investigative and corrective powers," said Director of the Agency and Vice-President of the EDPB Zdravko Vukić.  
Director Vukić took the opportunity to commend the work of the Agency for Information and Privacy of the Republic of Kosovo, led by Commissioner Krenare Sogojeva Dermaku, and highlighted the excellent cooperation between the Croatian and Kosovo supervisory authorities. It is worth noting that in October of last year, with the efforts of EDPB Vice-President Zdravko Vukić, the Kosovo supervisory authority was granted observer status in the European Data Protection Board.  
The conference "Managing Digital Transformation in the Financial Sector – Opportunities and Challenges in the Age of Artificial Intelligence," organized by ProCredit Bank Kosovo, brought together representatives of regulatory bodies, industry leaders, and technology experts who discussed the impact of artificial intelligence on the financial system.  
During the working visit to Pristina, Director Vukić and Commissioner Krenare Sogojeva-Dermaku also visited the Faculty of Law at the University of Pristina, where they were received by the dean and colleagues. During the meeting, the importance of strengthening academic cooperation between Kosovo and Croatia was emphasized, with the aim of raising students' awareness about personal data protection.
