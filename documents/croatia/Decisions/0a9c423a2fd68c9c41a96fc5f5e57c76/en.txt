Agency for the Protection of Personal Data, within the EU project Awareness Raising Campaign for SME II, which it implements in collaboration with the Italian Data Protection Authority Garante Privacy, the Faculty of Organization and Informatics from Varaždin, Vrije University in Brussels, and the University of Florence, is conducting a survey on the attitudes of women and men regarding compliance with GDPR, in the context of respecting gender equality and equality between women and men.

We kindly ask you to complete the survey available at the link: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/ARC2-ravnopravnost-istrazivanje. Your responses are crucial for gaining deeper insight into awareness of data protection and gender equality among managers and data processors in Croatia.

The aim of this research is to identify needs for the purpose of developing an action plan aimed at improving gender equality and combating discrimination against vulnerable groups in society within organizations implementing activities under the ARC II project and within small and medium-sized enterprises covered by this project.

Considering that the main activities of the project are carried out in the field of personal data protection, part of this research will be dedicated to examining awareness of personal data protection within organizations and enterprises. The results of the research will be used exclusively for the purpose of analyzing needs and proposing measures to improve gender equality and combat discrimination against vulnerable groups in society.

The survey is primarily intended for micro, small, and medium-sized entrepreneurs and craftsmen, but we also invite all other organizations to participate in the implementation of this research.

We thank you in advance for your valuable contribution.
