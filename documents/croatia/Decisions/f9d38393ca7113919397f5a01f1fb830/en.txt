At the 4th session of the Croatian Parliament, held on November 15, 2024, with 107 votes "in favor" and 5 "abstentions," the Annual Report on the Work of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data for the period from January 1 to December 31, 2023, was adopted. 

The year 2023 was marked by the highest number of administrative monetary fines imposed for violations of the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation and the Law on the Implementation of the General Data Protection Regulation, as well as administrative monetary fines with the highest amounts. A total of 28 administrative monetary fines were imposed amounting to 8.27 million euros, sending a clear message that any gross violation of the right to personal data protection will be strictly punished. The public's attention was particularly drawn to two fines imposed on debt collection agencies, amounting to 2.26 and 5.47 million euros, which were imposed, among other reasons, for failing to take appropriate technical and organizational measures, resulting in unauthorized access to the personal data of a significant number of citizens.

In accordance with its investigative powers, the Agency conducted 544 inspections and supervisory activities, which include oversight at the request of the data subjects, at the suggestion of a third party, and ex officio, of which 72 were direct inspections and 472 were conducted indirectly. Notably, the Agency participated in a coordinated initiative of the European Data Protection Board on the topic of the appointment and role of data protection officers, within which 3038 supervisory checks were carried out. Regarding education, numerous educational activities were conducted for all stakeholders involved in the overall concept of personal data protection. The implementation of the EU project ARC II, aimed at micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises, also continued, during which an innovative web tool "Olivia" was developed in collaboration with the Italian supervisory authority and the academic community, aimed at supporting entrepreneurs in aligning their business processes with legal regulations on data protection.

During this reporting period, an 18% increase in the number of received cases was recorded, and the Agency had a total of 3448 cases in progress, of which 2601 were received in 2023, and 847 cases were carried over from the previous period. A total of 2412 cases were resolved. Citizens most frequently sought opinions on the processing of their personal data on the internet, the processing of personal data for marketing purposes, and the processing of personal data by financial institutions. The Agency also recorded an increase in the number of requests for the determination of violations of rights, which the Agency decides on by a decision in accordance with Article 34 of the Law on the Implementation of the General Data Protection Regulation. These requests were mostly related to the processing of personal data through video surveillance, the processing of personal data by financial institutions, and the publication of personal data in the media and on social networks. A higher number of telephone inquiries was also recorded compared to the number of written inquiries received. In 2023, more than 3000 phone calls were recorded from citizens, data protection officers, and data controllers.
