Director of the Agency for Personal Data Protection  
Zdravko Vukić and the Commissioner of the Agency for Information  
and Privacy of the Republic of Kosovo Krenare Sogojeva  
Dermaku, last week in the premises of the AZOP,  
signed a Memorandum of Cooperation, with the aim  
of promoting European standards in the field of personal data protection.  
The Croatian and Kosovo agencies will carry out joint  
activities such as awareness-raising campaigns,  
study visits, mutually exchange knowledge and  
experiences, as well as experts who will be trained in  
specific sectors.  
Director Zdravko Vukić expressed satisfaction that  
the Agency will provide concrete knowledge and experience from  
its area of work. “I am glad that with our  
previous experience and good practices we can  
contribute to your work and thus assist in  
your European path.”  
“I am pleased that we are signing this memorandum today,  
which was one of my priorities when I was  
appointed Commissioner. This is a continuation of the good  
cooperation we have previously achieved and an opportunity to  
learn and progress,” emphasized Commissioner  
Krenare Sogojeva Dermaku.
