The Agency has received numerous inquiries in recent months regarding the application of the General Data Protection Regulation in the context of the entry into force of the Regulation on the content and manner of keeping records of employees employed by the employer (NN 55/24) and has also publicly published an opinion that is available at the link on the website: https://azop.hr/prikupljanje-preslike-osobne-iskaznice-radnika-od-strane-poslodavca-pravilnik-o-sadrzaju-i-nacinu-vodenja-evidencija-o-radnicima/

In order to avoid legal uncertainties and achieve the correct application of the provisions of the Regulation, the Agency forwarded the most frequently received inquiries to the Ministry of Labor, Pension System, Family and Social Policy as the competent authority for the interpretation of labor regulations, so that this body could provide its opinion regarding the interpretation of the provisions of the said Regulation within its powers.

Hereby, we publish in full the opinion of the Ministry of Labor, Pension System, Family and Social Policy regarding the most frequent inquiries related to the application of the Regulation: https://azop.hr/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Ministarstvo-rada-misljenje.pdf

We highlight that the competent Ministry emphasized in its opinion that when keeping records of employees, it is necessary to take into account the prescribed obligation that the processing of such data is carried out in accordance with the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation, specifically with respect to the principle of data minimization from Article 5, paragraph 1, point c) of the General Data Protection Regulation.

In this sense, processing can be carried out by collecting and recording data based on insight into documents related to the identity and citizenship of the employee, without the employer being obliged to store and keep them in a copy or original. Based on the conducted insight into the personal identification card or another document that contains any of the data from Article 3, paragraph 1, points 1 to 6 of the Regulation, a note can be made about these employee data in a written review or another document that is part of the records and which is stored and kept in accordance with Article 9, paragraph 1, point 1 or point 11 of the Regulation.
