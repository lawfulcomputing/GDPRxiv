At the 19th session of the Croatian Parliament held on Friday, December 15, 2023, the Annual Report on the Work of the Agency for Personal Data Protection for the period from January 1 to December 31, 2022, was adopted.  
The reporting period, from January 1, 2022, to December 31, 2022, was marked by international, project, and educational-consultative activities of the Agency, as well as a significant increase in the number of corrective measures, among which administrative monetary fines stand out.  
In 2022, the Agency imposed 257 corrective measures, which is an increase of 117.80% compared to 2021 when 118 corrective measures were imposed, and it imposed 14 administrative monetary fines totaling 528,369.00 euros, marking an increase of 250% compared to 2021.  
During the mentioned reporting period, the Agency handled a total of 3,761 cases, of which 2,204 cases were received in 2022, while 1,557 cases were transferred from the previous period due to their complexity. In 2022, a total of 2,914 cases were resolved.  
The received cases related to complaints/requests from citizens for the determination of rights violations, submissions from other bodies forwarding requests for the determination of rights violations for further processing, inquiries and doubts from citizens regarding unlawful processing of personal data, and inquiries from citizens seeking opinions on the application of the General Data Protection Regulation in specific sectors. Additionally, the Agency recorded an increase in the number of received requests for the determination of rights violations, which, in accordance with Article 34 of the Act on the Implementation of the General Data Protection Regulation, the Agency decides by resolution, with the number of resolved requests for the determination of rights violations amounting to 317.  
In accordance with its investigative powers, the Agency conducted a total of 1,140 inspections in 2022, which included inspections at the request of the data subjects, at the suggestion of a third party, and ex officio. Out of the total number of inspections/inspection activities, 299 were conducted directly, while 841 inspections/inspection activities were conducted indirectly. The number of citizens who contacted the Agency by phone daily is significantly higher than the number of inquiries received in writing, with over 2,800 calls recorded.  
In 2022, the Agency had the honor of hosting the 30th anniversary conference of European supervisory authorities, which took place under the auspices of the Government of the Republic of Croatia on May 19 and 20, 2022, in Cavtat and Dubrovnik.  
It is also worth highlighting the Twinning project "Providing Support in Implementing the Modernized Legislative Framework for Personal Data Protection in the Republic of North Macedonia" and the project co-financed by the European Union program for Rights, Equality, and Citizenship titled "Data Protection Awareness Campaign for Small and Medium Enterprises" (ARC), during which the Agency educated over 2,500 small and medium enterprises and produced a whole range of educational materials.
