Based on Article 165, paragraph 2 of the Law on State Officials (,,Official Gazette“, number: 155/23) and point IV, item 1 of the Decision on the Prohibition of New Employment of State Officials and Employees in State Administration Bodies and Offices and Other Professional Services of the Government of the Republic of Croatia (,,Official Gazette“, number 35/22, 37/23), the Agency for Personal Data Protection announces,

ADVERTISEMENT
for recruitment into state service for a fixed term
INDEPENDENT SERVICE FOR HUMAN RESOURCES, GENERAL, FINANCIAL-PLANNING AND ACCOUNTING AFFAIRS

Senior Professional Advisor at the Agency for Personal Data Protection – 1 position, for the replacement of a long-term absent official – until the return of the absent official, job position no. 11.
Professional requirements: completed undergraduate and graduate university studies or integrated undergraduate and graduate university studies or specialist professional studies in the field of economics, at least 4 years of work experience in relevant jobs, passed state exam of the appropriate level, knowledge of the English language, knowledge of computer work.
In addition to the above requirements, candidates must meet the conditions prescribed in Article 52 of the Law on State Officials.
A person cannot be admitted to state service if there are obstacles to their admission as per Article 54 of the Law on State Officials.
Both genders may apply for the advertisement.
The person is admitted to state service with a mandatory probation period of two months.
Persons who do not have a passed state exam of the appropriate level may also apply for the advertisement, with the obligation to take the state exam in accordance with the provisions of Article 99 of the Law on State Officials.
In the application for the advertisement, it is necessary to provide personal data of the applicant (full name, address of residence, phone number or mobile number, preferably email address) and the number and name of the job position and organizational unit to which the person is applying.
Candidates may use the application form available on the website of the Agency for Personal Data Protection Form-Application_RM11.
The application for the advertisement must be signed by hand.

Along with the application, candidates are required to submit:
- Curriculum vitae;
- Proof of Croatian citizenship;
- Proof of total work experience and work experience in relevant jobs, or jobs in the relevant professional qualification and field (electronic record or certificate of data recorded in the database of the Croatian Pension Insurance Institute, employer's certificate of work experience in relevant jobs, or other proof indicating the jobs the candidate has worked on);
- Proof of completed level of education (copy of diploma);
- Copy of the certificate of passed state exam of the appropriate level, or state professional exam (for candidates who have passed the state professional exam).

If a person has changed their surname and is submitting documentation with the old surname, it is necessary to provide proof of the surname change.
Documents are submitted in an unverified copy, and before the selection of the candidate, the original will be presented.
Applications with proof of meeting the conditions must be submitted within 8 days from the date of publication of the advertisement on the website of the Ministry of Justice and Administration of the Republic of Croatia – https://mpu.gov.hr/, in person or by mail to the address: Agency for Personal Data Protection (application for the advertisement), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb.

This advertisement will be published on the websites:
Agency for Personal Data Protection – https://azop.hr/, Ministry of Justice and Administration of the Republic of Croatia – https://mpu.gov.hr/ and Croatian Employment Service – https://burzarada.hzz.hr/.
A complete application is considered one that contains all the data and attachments specified in the advertisement.
A person who has not submitted a timely and proper application or does not meet the formal requirements from the advertisement is not considered a candidate applying for the advertisement.
A candidate who refers to the right of preference in recruitment into state service according to a special law in their application for the advertisement is obliged to attach all prescribed documentation according to the special law and has preference over other candidates under equal conditions.
A candidate who refers to the right of preference in employment in accordance with Article 101 of the Law on Croatian Veterans of the Homeland War and their family members (“Official Gazette”, number 121/17, 98/19, 84/21, 156/23), and Article 47 of the Law on Civilian Victims of the Homeland War (Official Gazette, number 84/21), must, in addition to proof of meeting the required conditions, also submit evidence of meeting the preference conditions according to the list available on the website of the Ministry of Croatian Veterans via the following link: https://branitelji.gov.hr/zaposljavanje-u-drzavnoj-sluzbi/843.
A candidate who refers to the right of preference in employment in accordance with Article 9 of the Law on Professional Rehabilitation and Employment of Persons with Disabilities (“Official Gazette”, number 157/13, 152/14, 39/18 and 32/20), must, in addition to proof of meeting the required conditions, also submit proof of established status as a person with a disability.
A candidate who refers to the right of preference in employment in accordance with Article 48.f of the Law on the Protection of Military and Civilian War Invalids (“Official Gazette”, number 33/92, 57/92, 77/92, 27/93, 58/93, 2/94, 76/94,
108/95, 108/96, 82/01, 94/01, 103/03, 148/13 i 98/19), when applying for the advertisement, in addition to evidence of meeting the required conditions, must also submit a decision or confirmation from which the stated right is evident. 

The candidate belonging to a national minority has the right to invoke preference in employment based on Article 22 of the Constitutional Law on the Rights of National Minorities (“Narodne novine”, No. 155/02, 47/10, 80/10, and 93/11 – Decision of the Constitutional Court of the Republic of Croatia), and is not required to prove their status as a member of a national minority. 

The Commission for the Implementation of the Advertisement (hereinafter referred to as: the Commission) is appointed by the Director of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data. The Commission establishes a list of candidates who have applied for the advertisement and meet the formal requirements and invites them for an interview. 

The time and place of the interview will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/) at least five days before the interview takes place. It is considered that a candidate who does not attend the interview has withdrawn their application for the advertisement and is no longer considered a candidate. 

The job description and salary information will be published on the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/) simultaneously with the advertisement publication. 

The selected candidate will be invited to submit, within a reasonable period and before the decision on their admission to the civil service is made, a certificate from the competent court that no criminal proceedings are being conducted against them, a certificate of health capacity to perform the job duties, and original documents proving the fulfillment of formal requirements from the advertisement. Failure to provide these documents will be considered a withdrawal from the admission to the civil service. 

Candidates will be informed of the results of the advertisement by the delivery of a decision on the admission to the civil service for a fixed term of the selected candidate through a public announcement on the website of the Ministry of Justice and Administration (https://mpu.gov.hr/) and the website of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data (https://azop.hr/). 

The delivery of the decision to all candidates is considered published upon the expiration of the eighth day from the date of publication on the website of the Ministry of Justice and Administration (https://mpu.gov.hr/). 

If no persons who meet the prescribed conditions apply for the Advertisement, or if the applicants do not satisfy in the interview, the Director of the Agency for the Protection of Personal Data will suspend the procedure of the Advertisement.
