DECISION

Complaint regarding the non-fulfillment of the right of access to a recorded call

Based on the duties and powers conferred upon me by Article 57(1)(f) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data (hereinafter referred to as the "Regulation"), I have examined a complaint submitted to my Office by Mr. XXX (hereinafter referred to as the "Complainant") against the Health Insurance Organization (hereinafter referred to as the "Respondent"), regarding the non-fulfillment of the right of access to a recorded call.

Based on the investigation I conducted, I found a violation of the Regulation by the Respondent and, therefore, I issue this Decision.

A. Case Facts

Positions of the Complainant

2. In an electronic message dated April 24, 2023, which the Complainant sent to my Office, as well as to the Respondent (at the email address beneficiaries@gesy.org.cy) and to the Commissioner of Administration and Human Rights, it was stated that:
2.1. The Complainant, regarding his registration in the General Health System (GESY), wishes to deregister as a beneficiary under the S1 Document Holder category and to register as an EU Worker, as he is working in Cyprus as a citizen of the European Union,
2.2. This process was indicated to him by an employee of the GESY call center, whom he asked about the reason for the delay in his registration application,
2.3. On April 24, 2023, at 13:15, the Complainant contacted the GESY call center. During this communication, the Complainant requested from the employee he was speaking with to provide him with the recorded call, so that he would have proof, since until that day he had not been given any proof of the submission of his request, which was submitted in the first week of April 2023. The said employee refused to provide him with the recorded call, stating that "the relevant capability is not provided."

3. Since the sending of the said electronic message to my Office occurred before the deadline set by the Regulation regarding the fulfillment of the rights of data subjects, my Office requested, on July 27, 2023, from the Complainant to inform my Office accordingly if he had not been provided with the recorded call.

4. On December 4, 2023, the Complainant informed my Office that, up to that date, he had not been provided with the recorded conversation, submitting a completed complaint form of my Office.

B. Legal Framework

10. According to Article 4 of the Regulation, personal data is interpreted as "any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ('data subject'); an identifiable natural person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier, or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, psychological, economic, cultural, or social identity of that natural person."

11. In the same article of the Regulation, the data controller is defined as "the natural or legal person, public authority, agency, or other body which, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data; where the purposes and means of such processing are determined by Union law or the law of a Member State, the data controller or the specific criteria for its designation may be provided for by Union law or the law of a Member State."

12. Regarding the time frame for fulfilling the rights of data subjects, Article 12 of the Regulation provides that:
5

«3.  
The data controller provides the data subject with information regarding the action taken in response to a request under Articles 15 to 22 without delay and in any case within one month of receiving the request. This deadline may be extended by a further two months if necessary, taking into account the complexity of the request and the number of requests. The data controller informs the data subject of this extension within one month of receiving the request, as well as the reasons for the delay. If the data subject submits the request by electronic means, the information shall be provided, where possible, by electronic means, unless the data subject requests otherwise.»

Γ.  
Reasoning  

18.  
According to Article 15 of the Regulation, the data subject has the right to access their personal data. It is understood that the provision of data to the data subject occurs provided that such data exist and have not already been deleted.  

19.  
In this case, the data controller is the entity against whom the complaint is made, since the operation of the call center is conducted for purposes determined by the entity itself.  

20.  
The communication of the Complainant with the call center took place on April 24, 2023. Considering that on the same day the Complainant sent a message to the entity against whom the complaint is made, requesting the recorded call, and since according to the position of the entity the recorded calls are retained for a period of three months, it follows that the recorded call was available when the Complainant sent their message.  

21.  
In the electronic message of the Complainant, dated April 24, 2023, it was requested to unsubscribe from a specific category of beneficiary in order to register in another category of beneficiary. Furthermore, as stated by the Complainant, they requested the recorded call in order to have proof, since they were not provided with relevant proof of submission of their request to the Citizen Service Center, which was submitted in the first week of April 2023.  

22.  
Regarding the Complainant's request for the possibility of changing categories, this was implemented, as stated by the entity, immediately. Specifically, the Complainant was informed on May 15, 2023, that their application for a change of beneficiary category would be deleted within the day and that they could submit a new registration request. My Office recognizes that this message was within one month from the Complainant's message, that is, it was within the deadline for satisfying the right of access, as provided in Article 12(3) of the Regulation.  

23.  
However, the possibility of changing the beneficiary category does not automatically mean that the Complainant's request for the recorded call is revoked. It cannot also be considered that the Complainant's access request is revoked because, as they stated, they requested the call as proof of their request. Moreover, in any case, the entity could have asked the Complainant whether they wished to receive the recorded call, despite the fact that the possibility of changing the beneficiary category was realized. However, the entity did not take this action. Therefore, the entity should not have considered the Complainant's request as moot, as the entity stated.  

24.  
In their electronic message, the Complainant noted that the employee of the call center with whom they communicated informed them that the possibility of receiving the recorded call is not provided. Even if the change of beneficiary category was made as the Complainant desired, the entity should have immediately investigated the report that the possibility of receiving the call is not provided, by reviewing the recorded call and informing the data protection officer of the entity accordingly. However, the entity, as stated, did not check the recorded call while it was available.  

25.  
Regarding the position of the entity that assurances were received that the service operators always follow the procedure that provides for informing the subject about the correct way to submit the request, considering that the Complainant's call has not been checked, I conclude that it cannot be proven that the Complainant was properly informed.  

26.  
The Guidelines 01/2022 of the European Data Protection Board regarding the rights of data subjects – Right of access, as issued on March 28, 2023, provide that:
10

"54. It should be noted that the data controller is not obliged to act upon a request sent to a random or incorrect electronic (or postal) address that has not been provided directly by the data controller, or through any communication channel that is clearly not intended to receive requests regarding the rights of the data subject if the data controller has provided an appropriate communication channel that can be used by the data subject.

55. Similarly, the data controller is not obliged to act upon a request sent to the electronic address of an employee of the data controller who may not be involved in processing requests concerning the rights of data subjects (e.g., drivers, cleaning staff, etc.). Such requests are not considered effective if the data controller has clearly provided the data subject with an appropriate communication channel. However, if the data subject sends a request to the employee of the data controller designated as the contact person (e.g., account manager at a bank or regular advisor at a mobile network operator), such contact details should not be considered random, and the data controller should make every reasonable effort to process the request so that it can be redirected to the contact point and responded to within the deadlines set by the GDPR."

27. As stated by the Respondent, since the Complainant did not submit their request in the appropriate manner, it was not examined. Nevertheless, the electronic message from the Complainant to the Respondent was sent to the electronic address beneficiaries@gesy.org.cy, that is, to a non-random or incorrect address, which pertains to issues of beneficiaries of the General Health System.

28. Therefore, considering that the Complainant sent their request to a non-random or incorrect address, which pertains to issues of beneficiaries, the Respondent should have redirected the access request to the contact point, namely the data protection officer of the Respondent, and responded within one month of receipt. Consequently, despite the fact that the Complainant did not send their request to the electronic address dpo@hio.org.cy, I find that their request was successfully exercised and, therefore, that it should have been fulfilled by the Respondent.

29. Based on the above, I find that the Respondent did not fulfill the access right exercised by the Complainant.

D. Conclusion

30. Taking into account all the above elements, as they have been presented, and based on the powers conferred upon me by Article 57(1)(f) of the Regulation, I find that there is a violation by the Respondent of Article 15(1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, because it did not fulfill the access right of the Complainant.

11

31. Based on the provisions of Article 83 of the Regulation, regarding the conditions for imposing administrative fines, insofar as they apply to the specific case, in determining the administrative fine, I took into account the following mitigating factors (a) – (e):

(a) the satisfaction of the Complainant's request for the possibility of changing the category of beneficiary of the GHS,

(b) the notification of the Complainant regarding the possibility of changing the category of beneficiary of the GHS, within one month of receiving the Complainant's request,

(c) the incident concerned a single data subject,

(d) there was no indication of intent from the Respondent,

(e) the cooperation of the Respondent with my Office in the context of the investigation of the complaint.

32. After considering and weighing:

(a) the applicable legislative framework regarding the prescribed administrative penalties in the provisions of Article 58(2) and Article 83 of the Regulation,

(b) all the circumstances and factors presented by the Complainant and the Respondent based on all existing correspondence,

(c) the aforementioned mitigating factors,

I find that, under the circumstances, the imposition of an administrative fine is not justified.

33. Nevertheless, taking into account the aforementioned facts, the legal aspect on which this Decision is based, and the analysis as explained above, and exercising the powers conferred upon me by Article 58(2)(b) of the Regulation,

I have decided

in my judgment and in accordance with the above provisions, to issue a reprimand to the Health Insurance Organization

for the violation of Article 15(1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
