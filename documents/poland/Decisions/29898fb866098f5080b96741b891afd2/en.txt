Warsaw, October 6, 2023

Decision

DOKE.561.10.2023
Based on Article 104 § 1 of the Act of June 14, 1960, the Code of Administrative Procedure (Journal of Laws 2023, item 775, consolidated text) in connection with Article 7 paragraphs 1 and 2 and Article 60 of the Act of May 10, 2018, on the protection of personal data (Journal of Laws 2019, item 1781) and based on Article 57 paragraph 1 letters a) and h), Article 58 paragraph 2 letter b) in connection with Article 31 and Article 58 paragraph 1 letters a) and e) of the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council EU 2016/679 of April 27, 2016, on the protection of natural persons in relation to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation) (Official Journal of the EU L 119 of May 4, 2016, p. 1, with amendments published in Official Journal of the EU L 127 of May 23, 2018, p. 2, and in Official Journal of the EU L 74 of March 4, 2021, p. 35), hereinafter referred to as the "Regulation 2016/679", after conducting an ex officio administrative proceeding regarding the imposition of an administrative monetary penalty on Ms. A. Legal Provisions
According to Article 57(1)(a) of Regulation 2016/679, the President of the UODO, as the supervisory authority within the meaning of Article 51 of Regulation 2016/679, monitors and enforces the application of this regulation within its territory. Within the scope of its competencies, the President of the UODO, among other things, examines complaints lodged by the person to whom the data relates, or by an entity, organization, or association in accordance with Article 80, in
**Legal Assessment**

In the appropriate scope, it conducts proceedings regarding these complaints and informs the complainant about the progress and results of these proceedings within a reasonable time (Article 57(1)(f) of Regulation 2016/679). It also conducts proceedings concerning the application of this regulation, including based on information received from another supervisory authority or another public body (Article 57(1)(h) of Regulation 2016/679).

To enable the realization of the tasks defined in this manner, the President of the UODO has a number of powers specified in Article 58(1) of Regulation 2016/679 concerning the conducted proceedings, including the power to order the controller and the processor to provide any information necessary to fulfill his tasks (Article 58(1)(a) of Regulation 2016/679) and the power to obtain access from the controller and the processor to all personal data and all information necessary to fulfill his tasks (Article 58(1)(e) of Regulation 2016/679).

A violation of the provisions of Regulation 2016/679, resulting in a breach of the powers of the authority specified in Article 58(1), is subject to an administrative fine of up to EUR 20,000,000, and in the case of an enterprise, up to 4% of its total annual worldwide turnover from the previous financial year, with the higher amount applying.

Additionally, both the controller and the processor are obliged, at the request of the supervisory authority, to cooperate with it in the performance of its tasks, as provided in Article 31 of Regulation 2016/679. Failure to comply with this obligation is subject to an administrative fine of up to EUR 10,000,000, and in the case of an enterprise, up to 4% of its total annual worldwide turnover from the previous financial year, with the higher amount applying.

The President of the UODO is also entitled to a number of remedial powers specified in Article 58(2) of Regulation 2016/679, including the right to issue a reprimand to the controller or the processor in the event of a violation of the provisions of Regulation 2016/679 by processing operations. According to Recital 148 of Regulation 2016/679, to make the enforcement of the provisions of Regulation 2016/679 more effective, sanctions, including administrative fines, should be imposed for violations, in addition to or instead of appropriate measures imposed under Regulation 2016/679 by the supervisory authority. If the violation is minor, the monetary penalty may be replaced by a reprimand. However, attention should be paid to the nature, gravity, and duration of the violation, whether the violation was not intentional, the actions taken by the controller to minimize damage, the degree of responsibility or any relevant previous violations, the manner in which the supervisory authority became aware of the violation, compliance with measures imposed on the controller or the processor, adherence to codes of conduct, and any other aggravating or mitigating factors.

Pursuant to Article 60 of the Personal Data Protection Act (u.o.d.o.), proceedings regarding violations of personal data protection regulations are conducted by the President of the UODO. Furthermore, Article 7(1) of the u.o.d.o. states that in matters not regulated by this act, the provisions of the Act of June 14, 1960, the Code of Administrative Procedure (Dz. U. 2022, item 2000, as amended, hereinafter referred to as "k.p.a.") apply to administrative proceedings before the President of the UODO (including proceedings regarding the imposition of administrative fines referred to in Chapter 11 of the u.o.d.o.). According to Article 7(2) of the u.o.d.o., these proceedings are single-instance proceedings.

According to the wording of Article 44 § 4 of the k.p.a. in connection with Article 44 § 1 of the k.p.a., the fiction of delivery occurs upon the expiration of the last day of the period, which is 14 days, in the case of delivering a letter to a natural person at their residence or workplace by a postal operator, and the letter is left in the case files.

**Legal Assessment**

Referring the above-mentioned provisions of Regulation 2016/679 to the established factual state in this case, it should first be considered whether the Entrepreneur is the addressee of the obligations referred to in Articles 31 and 58(1)(a) and (e) of Regulation 2016/679, the violation of which is subject to an administrative fine under Articles 83(4) and (5) of Regulation 2016/679. 

Both of the aforementioned provisions of Regulation 2016/679 impose procedural obligations—within the proceedings conducted by the President of the UODO—on controllers and processors. From the content of the complaints of Mr. B. S. and Ms. A. P. (which are consistent in this regard with the explanations of the Entrepreneur), it follows that the Entrepreneur, in connection with the business activity conducted by him, sent SMS messages containing information about the services provided by the Entrepreneur to the phone numbers indicated by the Complainants, i.e., (...). Thus, the personal data of the Complainants were used by the Entrepreneur for the purpose of marketing his own services—which, according to the wording of Article 4(2) of Regulation 2016/679, constitutes their processing by the Entrepreneur. 

Therefore, it can be concluded that the Entrepreneur, in the factual state described by the Complainants, acts as the controller with respect to the personal data of Mr. B. S. and Ms. A. P. Consequently, since the Entrepreneur is the controller in the sense of Article...
4 point 7 of Regulation 2016/679, the President of the UODO was authorized – in accordance with Article 31 and Article 58(1)(a) and (e) of Regulation 2016/679 – to demand from the Entrepreneur the provision of personal data and information necessary for the performance of his tasks, in this case for the substantive examination of cases with reference numbers: DS.523.4902.2022 and DS.523.6918.2022, and the Entrepreneur – on the same legal basis – was obliged to present such information.

It should be emphasized that the President of the UODO, in order to establish the factual state of the case with reference number DS.523.4902.2022, repeatedly – through letters dated November 10, 2022, February 16, 2023, and April 21, 2023 – addressed the Entrepreneur with a request for explanations by providing answers to detailed questions concerning the case. However, the Entrepreneur did not receive any of the aforementioned letters, as a result of which he did not respond within the time frame set by the supervisory authority to the allegations raised by the Complainant – thereby preventing their comprehensive examination by the supervisory authority. This situation was not changed by the fact that an employee of the Department of Organization, Penalties, and Enforcement of the UODO established direct telephone contact with the Entrepreneur on March 28, 2023, informing him about the progress of the case, primarily about the necessity to respond to the letters sent to him by the President of the UODO regarding the matter.

Having gained knowledge of the ongoing administrative proceedings, the Entrepreneur did not provide the information necessary to establish the factual state of the case – even in the situation where an additional, extended deadline was set for their submission. Although, in letters dated April 5, 2023, and June 28, 2023, the Entrepreneur submitted explanations in which he referred to the circumstances of obtaining and subsequently using the personal data of the Complainant, it should be noted that these explanations were insufficient and internally contradictory (see point 8 of the justification of this decision). Thus, due to the inconsistency of the statements made by the Entrepreneur, there were no grounds to consider them a credible source of evidence at that stage of the proceedings – particularly since the Entrepreneur did not provide any other evidence to support them. Given the Entrepreneur's attitude, which did not contribute to establishing the course of events in the case DS.523.4902.2022, but merely necessitated further prolongation of the proceedings to rectify the aforementioned inaccuracies, the President of the UODO took the position that such actions by the Entrepreneur – although seemingly indicative of his active participation in the proceedings – could not be positively assessed from the perspective of due cooperation with the supervisory authority. The lack of cooperation with the personal data protection authority, indisputable in light of the evidence gathered in this case, resulted in the necessity to initiate this administrative proceeding concerning the imposition of an administrative monetary penalty on the Entrepreneur.

Similar circumstances were found in the administrative proceeding with reference number DS.523.6918.2022, in which the President of the UODO twice – through letters dated January 4, 2023, and March 27, 2023 – requested the Entrepreneur to send information necessary for the examination of the case initiated by the complaint of Ms. A. P. The Entrepreneur, also in this case, did not respond to the correspondence directed to him, as a result of which he did not address the request of the personal data protection authority within the deadline. Only the telephone contact with the Entrepreneur established on May 16, 2023, by an employee of the Department of Organization, Penalties, and Enforcement of the UODO resulted in the Entrepreneur submitting a brief explanation in this matter through a letter dated June 21, 2023 (see point 14 of the justification of this decision); however, these were far from complete and did not contain answers to the detailed questions posed by the supervisory authority in the correspondence sent to the Entrepreneur. Thus, due to the general nature of the aforementioned explanations, the degree of usefulness of the information provided by the Entrepreneur should be assessed as minimal, and the cooperation with the supervisory authority as insufficient. The above – while simultaneously taking into account the attitude of the Entrepreneur presented in the proceedings with reference number DS.523.4902.2022 – became the basis for initiating this proceeding concerning the imposition of an administrative monetary penalty in connection with the violation of Article 31 and Article 58(1)(a) and (e) of Regulation 2016/679, as a result of which the Entrepreneur only then submitted extensive and substantive explanations allowing for the examination of the validity of both complaints lodged against his actions.

Moreover, in the opinion of the President of the UODO, the justification presented by the Entrepreneur in the letter dated July 12, 2023, regarding the lack of response to his requests (see point 19 of the justification of this decision), although credible, does not constitute a basis for excluding the Entrepreneur's liability for the established violation. The fact that the Entrepreneur is facing health problems, due to which he is unable to independently perform all obligations related to his business activities, does not relieve the Entrepreneur of the obligation to provide the President of the UODO, at his request, with all information necessary for the supervisory authority to perform its tasks. The Entrepreneur, realizing the difficulties in personally performing tasks related to handling incoming correspondence, including receiving letters sent to the address indicated in the CEIDG as the permanent place of conducting business or the address for deliveries to the Entrepreneur, should have appointed a proxy for this purpose – at least for the duration of his incapacity. Similarly, in the case of difficulties in timely preparing responses to the letters of the President of the UODO, the Entrepreneur should have considered the justification for appointing a proxy.
**Power of Attorney**

The entrepreneur, deciding to manage their affairs independently, should accept the risks arising from this decision and the possible consequences – in this case, the supervisory authority's recognition that the lack of receipt of correspondence directed to the Entrepreneur will constitute a violation of the obligation to cooperate with the President of the UODO. Moreover, a manifestation of this obligation's violation in the proceedings under case number DS.523.4902.2022 was also the submission by the Entrepreneur of explanations that were significantly incomplete and, in certain aspects, mutually exclusive. The actions described of the Entrepreneur (providing terse, evasive responses to specific questions from the supervisory authority that do not require specialized knowledge in the field of personal data protection), which hindered the establishment of the factual state of the case initiated by Mr. B. S.'s complaint, cannot remain without any reaction from the supervisory authority. 

At the same time, however, taking into account the explanations provided by the Entrepreneur in these proceedings, the President of the UODO took the position that the identified violation, consisting of a lack of cooperation with the President of the UODO in the performance of his duties and the failure to provide the President of the UODO access to personal data and information necessary for the execution of his tasks, was not intentional but resulted from the personal situation concerning the Entrepreneur. At the same time, this violation was rectified by the Entrepreneur immediately after becoming aware of the ongoing proceedings regarding the imposition of an administrative monetary penalty, i.e., upon the submission of additional explanations to the UODO on July 12, 2023 (subsequently supplemented – in the context of the proceedings under case number DS.523.4902.2022 – by letters dated August 24, 2023, and September 25, 2023). It should be noted that these explanations contributed to the issuance of a resolution regarding Mr. B. S.'s complaint, concluded with the administrative decision of the President of the UODO dated September 29, 2023.

Considering the active attitude of the Entrepreneur, the President of the UODO deemed it justified to issue a reprimand regarding the identified violation of Article 31 and Article 58(1)(a) and (e) of Regulation 2016/679, while also accepting that, in light of the criteria specified in Article 83(2) of Regulation 2016/679, it would be effective and sufficient. However, it should be noted that in the event of a similar occurrence in the future, any reprimand issued by the President of the UODO against the Entrepreneur will be taken into account when assessing the conditions justifying the imposition of an administrative penalty, in accordance with the principles specified in Article 83(2) of Regulation 2016/679 and will be considered to the Entrepreneur's detriment.

In this factual and legal state, the President of the UODO decided as stated in the operative part of this decision.
