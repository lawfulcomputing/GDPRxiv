Recommendation TU No. 01/2018 of January 5, 2018

Subject: Further processing (TU) of unencoded personal data for scientific purposes in the context of the study titled "Study of the Origin of Violence Against Animals in Slaughterhouses," conducted by Anne Laure Mathy, researcher at ULB. (CO-LV-2017-015)

The Commission for the Protection of Privacy (hereinafter referred to as the Commission);

Considering the law of December 8, 1992, on the protection of privacy with respect to the processing of personal data (hereinafter referred to as the LVP), particularly Article 4, § 1, 2°, 2nd paragraph;

Considering the royal decree of February 13, 2001, implementing the law of December 8, 1992, on the protection of privacy with respect to the processing of personal data (hereinafter referred to as the AR), particularly Articles 20, 2° and 21;

Considering the declaration of further processing of unencoded personal data for scientific purposes in the context of the study titled "Study of the Origin of Violence Against Animals in Slaughterhouses," conducted by Anne Laure Mathy, researcher at ULB, received by the Commission on December 5, 2017;

Considering that compliance with the obligation to inform the data subjects proves impossible or involves disproportionate efforts;

Recommendation TU 01/2018 - 2/2

Issues the following recommendation on January 5, 2018:

The Commission believes that to achieve an optimal result, the data controller, in this case, UCL, must have the possibility to use unencoded personal data, provided that the following conditions are met:

1. The communication of the data to third parties or the publication of the final results of this investigation is not permitted in a form that allows the identification of the data subjects because such identification is not essential to achieve the intended purpose;

2. The data controller must take the technical and organizational measures defined in Article 16 of the LVP to ensure the confidentiality and security of the processing. To this end, the Commission refers to the reference measures that can serve as a guideline and can be consulted on its website www.privacycommission.be – Privacy Themes – Information Security – Recommendation, reference measures, and guidelines; Given that so-called "sensitive" personal data are processed within the meaning of Articles 6 to 8 of the LVP, the conditions referred to in Article 25 of the AR must also be respected.

3. The identification data and the survey data must be decoupled as soon as this coupling is no longer necessary for the investigation.

The Administrator, acting on behalf of,
