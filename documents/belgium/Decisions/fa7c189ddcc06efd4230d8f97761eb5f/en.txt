Execution Decision 156/2024 — 2/5  
I. Facts and Procedure  
1.  
The Contentious Chamber refers to its Decision 131/2024 of October 11, 2024, regarding a complaint concerning the cookie banner on the RTL Belgium website, for all that relates to the description of the facts and procedure, the reasoning, and the ruling.  
2.  
This decision only concerns the execution of the compliance orders imposed in the aforementioned decision.  
II. Reasoning  
II.1. Context: Appeal of Decision 131/2024 to the Market Court  
3.  
RTL Belgium appealed the decision to the registry of the Brussels Court of Appeal (Market Court section) on November 8, 2024. The case is registered under reference 2024/AR/1848. The hearing is scheduled for March 26, 2025. The Contentious Chamber informed the parties (including the representative of the complainant) of the existence of this procedure on November 18, 2024.  
4.  
Notwithstanding this appeal, Decision 131/2024 is provisionally enforceable in accordance with Article 108, §1, paragraph 2 of the LCA.  
II.2. The Orders and Their Execution  
5.  
Decision 131/2024 issues two orders to RTL Belgium, which are set out in the ruling of the decision as follows:  

2 The decision is accessible via the following link: https://www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be/publications/decision-quant-au-fond-n0-131-2024.pdf.  

Execution Decision 156/2024 — 3/5  

6.  
The orders are further described in points 106 and 107 of Decision 131/2024 as follows:  
"Order 1: The Contentious Chamber requires the defendant to add a button that clearly allows for the refusal of cookie placement with a single click, at the same level as the button allowing for the acceptance of cookie placement at each level of the cookie banner where the button for accepting cookie placement is present.  
Order 2: The Contentious Chamber requires the defendant to use colors and contrasts that are not obviously misleading. The button clearly allowing for the refusal of cookie placement must thus be displayed at least as prominently as the one allowing for acceptance. The Contentious Chamber specifies that the defendant has the option to retain the colors currently used in its cookie banner provided that it reverses the color used for the button allowing for the refusal of cookie placement with that for accepting it; it refers to point 94 in this regard."  
7.  
On November 25, 2024, RTL Belgium notified the Contentious Chamber by electronic and registered mail of its compliance with the above orders.
