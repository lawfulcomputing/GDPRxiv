**Memorandum of the President of the Guarantor for the Protection of Personal Data on the Bill C. 1637 Government, approved by the Senate, concerning "Conversion into law, with amendments, of the decree-law of January 28, 2019, n. 4, containing urgent provisions regarding citizenship income and pensions"**

**Joint Permanent Committees XI (Public and Private Employment) and XII (Social Affairs) of the Chamber of Deputies**  
(6 March 2019)

The decree-law under examination introduces important measures aimed at economic support and the labor and social inclusion of individuals at risk of marginalization.  
In the memorandum presented on February 8 to the Senate Labor Committee, the compliance of the provisions governing citizenship income with the regulations on the protection of personal data was examined.

1. On that occasion, it was highlighted, in particular, how the proposed mechanism for the recognition, disbursement, and management of Citizenship Income (hereinafter Rdc) involves large-scale processing of personal data related to the applicant and the members of their household (including minors), who are granted the highest level of protection due to their relevance to the most intimate sphere of the person or the susceptibility to expose the individual to discrimination.  
This particularly concerns data related to health status and any subjection to restrictive measures of personal freedom, as well as conditions of hardship, particularly from an economic, family, or social perspective.  
The processing of such data, although functional to the disbursement of benefits for the individual, must occur in compliance with the provisions of Regulation 2016/679 (EU) and the amended Code on the protection of personal data and, in particular, in accordance with the principles of reasonableness and proportionality, which have also been emphasized in this matter by the Constitutional Court, with ruling no. 20 of 2019.  
The management system of the Rdc presupposes the establishment of a complex information asset, through the interconnection of multiple archives containing some of the most relevant personal data, as well as through the monitoring of the consumption of the members of the beneficiary's household.  
Regarding the critical issues that this system, in its original architecture, presented – and for which a description is referred to the memorandum presented in the Senate – the text approved in the first reading appears improved in many aspects, having incorporated, in the amendment phase, the observations of the Guarantor.

2. In particular, in order to avoid duplications of databases, a single Information System for Citizenship Income has been established, within which the "two digital platforms" will operate, aimed at enabling the activation and management of the Work Pacts and Social Inclusion Pacts related to the Rdc.  
These platforms are now defined as tools to make information available to the central administrations and the territorial services involved, in compliance with the principles of minimization, integrity, and confidentiality of personal data (cf. art. 6, c. 1, 3, 4, and 5).  
To this end, it is foreseen that the INPS will provide the Information System dedicated to the Rdc only with the information present in the Institute's archives, strictly necessary for the implementation of the measure, according to methods and terms that will be identified by ministerial decree (cf. art. 6, c. 3).  
With this same decree – which must be issued after the opinion of the Guarantor – a technical plan for the activation and interoperability of the two platforms dedicated to the Rdc will be prepared, and measures to protect the interested parties will be provided, along with selective access methods to the information necessary for pursuing the specific objectives, as well as appropriate data retention times (cf. art. 6, c. 1).  
On the other hand, the methods by which the INPS will verify the possession of the requirements for access to the benefit, based on the information available in its own archives and those of other administrations, such as the Tax Registry, will be detailed in a specific provision of the same Institute.  
With this act, on which the opinion of the Guarantor must be requested, relevant rules for selective access to the databases must be established, identifying, where not already regulated, the types of accessible data, the methods of acquiring information, as well as the measures to protect the interested parties (cf. art. 5, c. 3).  
Consultation with the Authority is also provided regarding the preparation, by the INPS, of the application form to obtain the benefit and the definition of the communications due to the same Institute in the event of changes in the requirements possessed by the beneficiaries (cf. art. 5, c. 1).  
In this regard, in order to ensure the necessary transparency of the processing, it must be clearly specified in the information to the interested parties that the data controller is the INPS, both in the case where the request for disbursement of the benefit is submitted through the Caf, and in the case where it is submitted at the post offices.

3. Thanks to the amendments approved in the first reading, the critical issues identified regarding the centralized and systematic "monitoring" of individual purchases made by beneficiaries through the Rdc card, which, as proposed, was likely to involve the acquisition of particularly sensitive data, have also been overcome.  
To this end, it is now provided that all transactions on the card are monitored by verifying only the total amounts spent and withdrawn, according to methods that will be defined by decree, subject to the opinion of the Guarantor (cf. art. 3, c. 15).  
The same information, devoid in any case of the identifying data of the beneficiaries, may be used by the Ministry of Labor for statistical and scientific research purposes (cf.
r. art. 5, c. 6).  
In line with the concerns raised by the Authority, the provision regarding the assignment of functions for detailed control over individual consumption choices and behaviors of beneficiaries to operators of employment centers and municipal services has also been removed, as well as the evaluation of any anomalies that could reveal the non-existence of the declared requirements, with the consequent reporting to the platforms for the Rdc, in the absence of procedures to protect the interested parties and of legally identified criteria.  
The control activity on Rdc beneficiaries, as well as on accredited training entities, may be subject to a specific agreement between the Ministry of Labor, the Ministry of Economy and Finance, and the Guardia di Finanza (see art. 6, c. 6).  
4. The objections raised by the Authority regarding the amendments made by the decree-law to the regulation on the issuance of Isee certifications (art. 10, d.lgs. n. 147 of 2017) have been accepted, which – even disregarding the functioning of the citizenship income – were likely to expose even unaware subjects, not interested in such benefits, to risks of abusive access.  
The proposed regulation, in fact – as already highlighted in the memorandum presented to the Senate – risked compromising the security of the data contained in the Tax Registry and, above all, in the archive of financial relationships of the Revenue Agency, which are subject to very strict access constraints even within the ordinary activities of tax control, due to the particular relevance of the data itself.  
On this aspect, the Guarantor had already had the opportunity to represent to the Government the need to prepare technical and organizational security measures suitable for protecting such relevant information from the risk of abusive access (including through identity theft or impersonation) or cyberattacks, also due to the involvement in the processing chain of Caf and their related information systems (not always adequately protected).  
In accordance with the Guarantor's observations, the provisions that subordinated the pre-filling of the Single Substitute Declaration (Dsu) to a complex mechanism of consent/inhibition to processing by each adult member of the household have been reformulated, which would lead to an unjustified communication to the declarant of the data of the members of their household – even against their will – present in the archives of Inps and the Revenue Agency (see paragraphs 2-bis and 2-ter introduced by the original text of the decree-law in art. 10 of d.lgs. 147 of 2017).  
In particular, while maintaining the possibility of submitting the Dsu in a non-pre-filled mode, it is delegated to a ministerial decree – to be adopted after the opinion of the Guarantor – the identification of the technical methods necessary to allow citizens access to the pre-filled declaration made available electronically by Inps (see art. 11).  
In this context, technical and organizational measures (including appropriate methods of communication to the declarant of any discrepancies found) may therefore be introduced, suitable for preventing risks of violations of data and system security.  
5. A final observation relates to the provisions introduced in the first reading, to allow the National Labor Inspectorate to effectively carry out monitoring activities on any abuses and fraudulent behaviors, in compliance with data protection regulations. In particular, the Inspectorate will be able to access, both in analytical and aggregated form, the information – specified in the annex to the decree-law – available from Inps, including those of a patrimonial, income, and financial nature relating to individuals requesting the benefit and to the members of their household, as well as data relating to social security benefits for illness, maternity, and family allowances or income support benefits.  
The list of information and accessible databases may be supplemented by ministerial decree, to be adopted after the opinion of the Guarantor. Furthermore, the categories of data and their retention periods, access methods, and measures to protect the interested parties will be established by a provision of the Inspectorate, on which the opinion of the Guarantor will also be acquired (see art. 7, c. 15-ter).  
6. Finally, regarding the architecture of the Government's website dedicated to the Rdc, there are still some critical issues related – in the current state of development – to the technical methods of its implementation (which, to date, involve the transmission to third parties of navigation data – such as IP addresses and connection times – of visitors to the same site, which is not necessary in functional terms and therefore excessive).  
In this regard, the necessity is reiterated that the realization of this tool occurs prior to the adoption of technical measures suitable for effectively implementing data protection principles (in particular, that of minimization), integrating the necessary guarantees into the processing to reduce risks in protection of citizens' rights.  
In the specific case of the use of external typographic fonts ("character styles"), it seems preferable to configure the website – while maintaining its graphic and functional characteristics unchanged – in such a way as to avoid the transfer to external suppliers of data relating to its visitors.
