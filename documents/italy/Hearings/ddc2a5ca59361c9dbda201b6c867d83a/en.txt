**Subject:** Processing of personal data carried out in the context of the issuance, release, and verification of green certificates pursuant to Decree-Law No. 52/2021.  

On June 9, the Authority issued its opinion on the draft decree of the President of the Council of Ministers, which must be adopted, pursuant to Article 9, paragraph 10, of Decree-Law No. 52/2021, in agreement with the Minister of Health, the Minister for Technological Innovation and Digital Transition, and the Minister of Economy and Finance, regarding the processing of personal data, including health-related data, carried out through the National Digital Green Certificate Platform (“National-DGC Platform”) for the issuance, release, and verification of Covid-19 green certificates (EU Digital COVID Certificate, previously Digital Green Certificate, hereinafter referred to as green certificates) (Annex No. 1).  

In the aforementioned opinion, the Authority reiterated what had already been noted by the Guarantor in the warning measure of April 23, 2021, namely that Decree-Law No. 52/2021 does not represent a valid legal basis for the introduction and use of green certificates at the national level as it lacks some of the essential elements required by the Regulation (Articles 6, paragraph 2, and 9) and by the Code regarding the protection of personal data (Articles 2-ter and 2-sexies).  

It was also observed that, as of the date of adoption of the aforementioned measure, the proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on the EU Digital COVID Certificate, approved in its final form on May 21, 2021 (subsequently adopted on June 14, 2021), provides that the EU Digital COVID Certificate may be used by Member States for purposes other than those provided for in the aforementioned Regulation (facilitating movement within the Union) (so-called domestic use of certificates), which must be expressly provided for by a national law, in compliance with Union legislation on data protection and the principles of effectiveness, necessity, and proportionality, which must clearly identify the scope and extent of the processing, the purposes, the categories of subjects who may verify the certificate, as well as the relevant safeguards to prevent discrimination and abuse, taking into account the risks to the rights and freedoms of the data subjects (Recital No. 37 in the proposal for the Regulation, Recital No. 48 in the adopted text).  

In this regard, in the intervention of May 6, 2021, made during the informal hearing before the Joint Committees I, II, and XII of the Chamber of Deputies, as well as in the warning measure adopted on May 25, 2021, against the Campania Region, this Authority deemed it necessary that, during the conversion into law of Decree-Law No. 52/2021, a state law reserve be introduced for the precise identification of the purposes for which certificates attesting to vaccination or recovery from Covid-19, or the negative result of an antigenic or molecular test, may be used.  

The limitation of personal freedoms also carried out through the processing of data on the health of the data subjects, realized by conditioning access to places and services on the possession of a certificate attesting to vaccination or recovery from Covid-19, or the negative result of an antigenic or molecular test, can only be considered admissible if provided for by a state law (Articles 6, paragraph 2, and 9 of the Regulation and Articles 2-ter and 2-sexies of the Code regarding the protection of personal data, Recital No. 37 of the Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on the EU Digital COVID Certificate, adopted on June 14, 2021; see also Constitutional Court, ruling 271/2005 on the state law reserve on data protection; Constitutional Court, ruling 37/2021).  

In this context, in the referenced opinion of June 9, the Authority's Board deemed that certificates attesting to vaccination or recovery from Covid-19, or the negative result of an antigenic or molecular test cannot be considered a necessary condition for allowing access to places or services or for establishing or determining the modalities of legal relationships except to the extent that this is provided for by a primary law, within the framework of the adoption of public health measures necessary for containing the SARS-CoV-2 virus.  

In the same opinion, it was also deemed that the use of certifications that attest to vaccination or recovery from Covid-19, or the negative result of an antigenic/molecular test, different from those indicated in the aforementioned draft decree of the President of the Council of Ministers, or the use of verification tools (such as mobile apps) additional to those indicated therein cannot be considered admissible, as they would not guarantee compliance with the principle of accuracy of the processed data (Article 5, paragraph 1, letter d), of the Regulation). This is because the connection with the National-DGC Platform is essential to verify the current status of the conditions certified, taking into account any changes (e.g., subsequent positivity), with significant risks also regarding the actual effectiveness of the containment measure. Only the National-DGC Platform, implemented in full compliance with the guarantees provided by data protection legislation and in accordance with the opinion of the...
The Authority indeed has the characteristics to achieve, once the critical issues regarding the specification of the purposes of the processing outlined below have been overcome, the significant public interest objective underlying it and can be considered proportionate to the legitimate aim pursued. 

In light of these observations, the Guarantor has therefore issued a favorable opinion on the aforementioned draft decree implementing the regulations concerning green certifications, provided that, during the conversion into law of Decree-Law No. 52/2021:

1. the purposes of the processing are specifically defined, and a state law reserve is introduced for the use of certifications to attest to the completion of vaccination or recovery from Covid-19, or the negative result of an antigenic or molecular test;
2. it is stipulated that, in cases where the aforementioned certifications can be used, they may be issued, released, and verified exclusively through the methods indicated in the draft decree under examination;
3. the provision regarding the temporary nature of the applicable national regulations on green certifications is adequately modified to prevent them from ceasing to be effective at the moment of the entry into force of the aforementioned European Regulation.

On June 17, 2021, the President of the Council of Ministers adopted the aforementioned decree referred to in Article 9, paragraph 10 of Decree-Law No. 52/2021, with a formulation that takes into account the observations made by the Guarantor in the aforementioned opinion of June 9, 2021.

In this regard, the attention of Your Excellencies is drawn to what is indicated therein and therefore to the necessity of refraining from adopting or implementing provisions adopted at the local level that:

- introduce cases different from those currently provided for by national legislation in which the presentation of certificates attesting to the status of completed vaccination, recovery, or negative test result for Covid-19 may be required to access places and venues or establish legal relationships;
- provide for methods of issuance, management, and control of the aforementioned certifications that are inconsistent with those regulated in the aforementioned decree of the President of the Council of Ministers.

Finally, it is stated that, with reference to the aforementioned potential treatments, the Authority reserves the right to evaluate any measures aimed at imposing a temporary or definitive limitation on the processing, including the prohibition of processing (Article 58, paragraph 2, letter f) of the Regulation).
