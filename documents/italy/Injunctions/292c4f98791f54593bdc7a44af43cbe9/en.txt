Provision of June 22, 2023  
Register of Provisions  
No. 332 of June 22, 2023  

THE GUARANTOR FOR THE PROTECTION OF PERSONAL DATA  

IN THE meeting held today, attended by  
Prof. Pasquale Stanzione, President, Dr.  
Agostino Ghiglia, and Attorney Guido Scorza, members, and  
Dr. Claudio Filippi, Deputy Secretary General;  

HAVING REGARD TO Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament  
and of the Council of April 27, 2016 (hereinafter, "Regulation");  

HAVING REGARD TO the Code regarding the protection of personal data, containing provisions for the adaptation  
of the national legal system to Regulation (EU)  
2016/679 (Legislative Decree of June 30, 2003, No. 196, as  
amended by Legislative Decree of August 10, 2018, No. 101, hereinafter "Code");  

HAVING REGARD TO the Ethical Rules regarding the processing  
of personal data in the exercise of journalistic activity as per Annex 1 of the Code (hereinafter "Ethical Rules");  

HAVING REGARD TO the complaint submitted to the Guarantor, pursuant  
to Article 77 of the Regulation, on December 22, 2021, by XX, who complained of a violation  
of the regulations concerning the protection of personal data in relation to the article published by  
“Futuro Molise” titled "XX", available at the link  
https://.... This article referred to  
the circumstance of his mobilization with the  
competent Minister regarding the issue of the installation  
of photovoltaic panels in historic centers, in  
relation to the renovation works of his own  
residence, and particularly disclosed the  
town and neighborhood where it is located, also publishing a photograph that showed  
the name of the street and highlighted the entrance  
of the property;  

CONSIDERING that in requesting the definitive limitation  
of the processing concerning "the further dissemination of images depicting  
the residence (..) attached to the aforementioned  
article," the complainant highlighted:  
- the lack of a public interest in the knowledge  
of the news, given the absence of any connection between the  
content of the article and the role of XX held by him;  
- the derogatory nature of the article towards his  
person and the political party to which he belongs, given his role as spokesperson and  
Group Leader of XX within the XX of XX;  
- the violation of the principle of essentiality  
of information as per Article 6 of the aforementioned Ethical Rules regarding the  
publication of the photo depicting his residence, as well as the previous Article 3 concerning the  
protection of the home and places of private residence;  
- the possibility, also due to the location  
of the property in an area characterized by a  
low population density, of its immediate  
recognizability and the risk of harm to  
the safety and privacy of himself and his  
family members;  
- the high number of online views  
of the article recorded at the time of the complaint;  

HAVING REGARD TO the note of April 12, 2022, in which  
the Authority requested Futuro Molise S.r.l. –  
publisher of the online newspaper “Futuro Molise”,  
responsible for the publication of the article  
subject to the complaint – to provide comments regarding  
the complaint itself;  

HAVING REGARD TO the subsequent note of April 20, 2022, in which  
the Company, through the responsible director  
of the newspaper, requested the rejection of the complaint,  
noting the inconsistency of the reasons behind  
the act, and stated that:  
- there is a public interest in the  
dissemination of the news because it "concerns  
the activity of a subject holding a public office" and also highlights "the attitude of a  
political figure running for the Presidency of the XX and leading the main opposition force  
to the XX";  
- there are no derogatory profiles in the article in question, as it is evident that the  
citizens have an interest in knowing that "Councilor XX, a subject holding a public office,  
has approached the Government (...) to obtain a result that the citizens he represents  
very rarely manage to obtain due to the known bureaucratic problems";  
- there are no profiles of violation in the case of  
publication of external images of residences of  
political figures, as also confirmed by other  
articles published by national circulation newspapers (e.g., the article from Il Sole 24ore of  
March 21, 2014, which even includes the address,  
house number, and floor of XX's residence);  
- the circumstance that the published image depicts  
the exterior of the building visible from the street  
and is "a screenshot of images obtained from Google  
Maps," which are therefore present in the  
Google information system and accessible to anyone;  
- the consideration that the footage in question does not violate  
any rules protecting the home or places of  
private residence, as such protection only applies if  
the places are adequately shielded and removed from  
normal observation from the outside;  
- the instrumental nature of the complaint, as the  
interested party could respond to the criticisms of the article and  
"make the truth known to the citizens";  

HAVING REGARD TO the note of April 28, 2023, in which the  
complainant reiterated his grievances, highlighting:  
- the absence of elements aimed at demonstrating both  
the public interest in the dissemination of the news  
and the essentiality of the publication of the photo  
of the residence as reproduced and accompanied by
identifying elements, considering, in particular,  
the absence of any connection between such publication  
and the political role performed;  
- the circumstance that the article, more than an expression  
of “legitimate political criticism,” aimed  
to discredit his image from a  
moral and political standpoint, given also the title, and  
to make his residence known to all,  
exposing him and his family “to a concrete danger  
to our safety”;  
HAVING SEEN the note from the Office dated October 7, 2022, with which, pursuant to art. 166, paragraph 5, of the Code, it was communicated to Futuro Molise S.r.l., the data controller complained of in the complaint, the initiation of proceedings for the potential adoption of  
the measures referred to in art. 58, paragraph 2, of the  
Regulation, noting the alleged violation of the following provisions:  
- the general principles of processing referred to in art.  
5, paragraph 1 of the Regulation and, in particular, the  
principles of “lawfulness and fairness” (letter a) and of  
“data minimization” (letter c);  
- art. 137, paragraph 3, of the Code, and art. 6 of the  
“Ethical Rules concerning the processing of  
personal data in the exercise of journalistic activity”;  
HAVING SEEN the defensive memorandum dated November 4, 2022, with  
which the Company, reiterating its position, highlighted that:  
- the news, disseminated with restrained and  
proportionate language, concerned a matter of public  
interest, given the position held by the complainant and the local relevance of the  
newspaper;  
- the reference to certain details in the contested article  
must be considered an essential condition to ensure  
clear and transparent information to the citizen regarding the right to  
be informed, especially due to the  
subjective qualification of the interested party as a  

public figure and representative of  
the community within the XX;  
- the conduct of the interested party must be considered  
relevant concerning his role or public life, having in his institutional capacity,  
addressed the competent Minister precisely to draw  
attention to “a problem closely related to the  
private facts subject to news within  
the article”;  
- the article not only meets the requirements of  
restraint and truthfulness of the news but presents  
comments and opinions typical of journalistic activity that belong to the freedom of  
information;  
- the information regarding the complainant's  
residence and the photo depicting the street where the  
property is located and its entrance, as per  
the practice used by national newspapers, was  
limited to the mere location of the building of a  
local public figure, without violating his home and obtaining the image from  
the web, nor was there any recourse to invasive techniques;  
- the information, considering that Mr. XX is very  
attached to the town of XX (with  
only 4,500 inhabitants) of which he is originally from as  
can be inferred from a simple web search, cannot  
be considered absolutely confidential as it can  
be deemed already known to the XX community and,  
due to the public relevance of the interested party, also by a large part of the regional community;  
- the limited relevance of the contested information for  
the safety of the interested party who, as a  
public figure of regional importance, would not  
be a victim of hypothetical attacks on his  
person due to the mere publication of the facade of his building, which is already known to most;  
HAVING SEEN the note from the Office dated December 13, 2022, with  
which the modalities for conducting the hearing requested with the aforementioned  
memorandum were communicated to the data controller;  

HAVING SEEN the subsequent communication from the data controller dated January 18, 2023, which acknowledged the  
death of the responsible director of the newspaper and  
the consequent request for postponement of the hearing, also noting the  
removal of the photo available at the aforementioned link;  
HAVING SEEN the last note dated June 7, 2023, with which  
the new management of the newspaper communicated the withdrawal of the requested hearing;  
CONSIDERING that, unless the act constitutes a more serious crime, anyone who, in a proceeding before  
the Guarantor, falsely declares or attests to news or  
circumstances or produces false acts or documents is liable under art. 168 of the Code “Falsehood  
in declarations to the Guarantor and interruption  
of the execution of the tasks or the exercise of the  
powers of the Guarantor”;  
HAVING SEEN the applicable regulations in this case and  
in particular:  
- art. 5, paragraph 1 letters a) and c) of the Regulation regarding the general principles of lawfulness and  
fairness, as well as data minimization in the  
processing of personal data;  
- art. 137, paragraph 3 of the Code which provides that the  
dissemination of data for journalistic purposes must  
take place in compliance with the limits of the right to  
report and the principle of “essentiality  
of information regarding facts of public interest”;  
- art. 6 of the “Ethical Rules concerning the  
processing of personal data in the exercise  
of journalistic activity” which identifies the  
conditions and limits for the processing of personal  
data in the journalistic field, in relation to  
the aforementioned principle of “essentiality  
of information”;  
CONSIDERING that compliance with the aforementioned Ethical Rules  
constitutes an essential condition for  
the lawfulness and fairness of the processing of personal  
data (art. 2–quater of the Code);
CONSIDERING that the aforementioned photo, showing the name of the street where the claimant's residence is located and the visibility of the entrance, due to the property's location in an area characterized by low population density, allowed for immediate recognition by anyone, in contrast to the principle of the essentiality of information, and resulted in an inadequate processing of personal data concerning the legitimate informational purposes pursued (see also the provision of the Guarantor no. 212 of November 27, 2019, web doc no. 9236666);

CONSIDERING, for the reasons stated above, that in this specific case, the dissemination of the photo should be deemed in violation of Article 5, paragraph 1, letters a) and c), of the Regulation, Article 137, paragraph 3, of the Code, as well as Article 6 of the Ethical Rules, and that the related processing, having been carried out in the aforementioned manner, should be considered unlawful;

THEREFORE, IT IS DEEMED necessary to impose on Futuro Molise S.r.l., pursuant to Article 58, paragraph 2, letter f) of the Regulation, the measure of prohibition of further processing of the photograph subject to this proceeding concerning its further dissemination, including online, except for mere preservation for potential use in judicial proceedings;

NOTING that, in case of non-compliance with the prohibition measure imposed by the Guarantor, the sanction referred to in Article 170 of the Code may apply, in addition to the administrative sanction referred to in Article 83, paragraph 5, letter e) of the Regulation;

FURTHER CONSIDERING that, due to the violations identified above, a specific injunction order should be adopted against Futuro Molise S.r.l. pursuant to Articles 166, paragraph 7, of the Code and 18 of Law no. 689/1981, for the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction provided for by the combined provisions of Articles 2-quater, 166, paragraph 2, of the Code, and 83, paragraph 5 of the Regulation;

NOTING that for the determination of the amount of the pecuniary sanction, it is necessary to take into account the elements indicated in Article 83, paragraph 2, of the Regulation, and that in this specific case, the following aggravating circumstance should be considered:
a) the harmfulness, for the privacy of the interested party, of the conduct that led to the dissemination of the photo of their residence with the indication of the street name and the visibility of the entrance, taking into account the property's location in an area characterized by low population density and the consequent immediate recognizability by anyone (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter a) of the Regulation);

and the following mitigating circumstances:
a) the purposes pursued by the data controller, related to the exercise of the right to report and the freedom of information (see Article 83, paragraph 2, letter a) of the Regulation), also considering the subjective qualification of the interested party and, therefore, the necessity to ensure, in this context, the related balancing with the fundamental rights of the interested parties to the protection of privacy, as established by the Regulation (Article 85) and the Code (Articles 136 et seq.);
b) the lack of elements aimed at proving the negative impact on the private life of the interested party and their family and the detriment to their relative safety and tranquility (see Article 83, paragraph 2, letter a) of the Regulation);
c) the declared conviction of the data controller regarding the legitimacy of the processing, without the intention to harm the legal sphere of the interested party, taking into account the characteristics of the photograph and the recognizability of the elements referring to the residence by the local community (see Article 83, paragraph 2, letter b) of the Regulation);

d) the measures adopted by the data controller, as the company removed the photo in question during the proceeding (see Article 83, paragraph 2, letter c) of the Regulation);
e) the absence of previous similar violations committed by the same data controller (see Article 83, paragraph 2, letter e) of the Regulation);
f) the economic and professional conditions of the offender (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter k), taking into account the local nature of the publishing reality and what emerged from the company's financial data;

CONSIDERING the above parameters and the principles of effectiveness, proportionality, and deterrence indicated in Article 83, paragraph 1, of the Regulation;

IT IS DEEMED that, based on the overall elements indicated above, taking into due account the various mitigating circumstances that emerged, the administrative pecuniary sanction should be applied in the amount of €2,000.00 (two thousand), equal to 0.01% of the maximum penalty, against Futuro Molise S.r.l.;

IT IS ALSO DEEMED that, pursuant to Articles 166, paragraph 7, of the Code, and 16, paragraph 1, of the Guarantor's Regulation no. 1/2019, the publication of this provision on the Guarantor's website should proceed as an accessory sanction;

IT IS DEEMED that the conditions exist to proceed with the annotation in the internal register of the Authority referred to in Article 57, paragraph 1, letter u) of the Regulation, regarding the violations and the measures adopted in this specific case in accordance with Article 58, paragraph 2, of the same Regulation;

HAVING SEEN the documentation in the records;

HAVING SEEN the observations made by the Secretary General pursuant to Article 15 of the Guarantor's Regulation no. 1/2000;

REPORTER Dr. Agostino Ghiglia;

ALL OF THE ABOVE CONSIDERED, THE GUARANTOR
pursuant to Article 57, paragraph 1, letter f) of the Regulation, declares the complaint well-founded for the reasons stated in the preamble and, in particular:
pursuant to Article 58, paragraph 2, letter f) of the Regulation, orders against Futuro Molise S.r.l. the measure of prohibition of further processing of the photograph subject to this proceeding with reference to its further dissemination, including online, as well as in the historical archive, except for mere preservation for potential use in judicial proceedings;

ORDERS
pursuant to Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i) and 83 of the Regulation, Futuro Molise S.r.l., based in Sesto Campano (IS), Contrada Mazzarello s.n.c. - VAT: 00925370942 to pay the sum of €2,000.00 (two thousand) as an administrative pecuniary sanction for the violations indicated in the reasoning, representing that the offender, pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 8, of the Code has the option to settle the dispute by payment, within thirty days, of an amount equal to half of the imposed sanction.

ORDERS
the aforementioned Company, in case of failure to settle the dispute pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 8, of the Code to pay the sum of €2,000.00 (two thousand) according to the methods indicated in the annex, within 30 days from the notification of this provision, under penalty of the adoption of the consequent executive acts in accordance with Article 27 of Law No. 689/1981.

PROVIDES
a) pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code, the publication of this provision on the website of the Authority and
b) pursuant to Article 17 of Regulation No. 1/2019 of the Authority, the annotation in the internal register of the Authority referred to in Article 57, paragraph 1, letter u), of the Regulation, regarding the measures adopted in this case in accordance with Article 58, paragraph 2, of the same Regulation.

Pursuant to Article 78 of the Regulation, as well as Articles 152 of the Code and 10 of Legislative Decree No. 150 of September 1, 2011, an appeal against this provision may be filed with the ordinary judicial authority, with a petition submitted, alternatively, to the court of the place where the data controller resides or is based or to that of the place of residence of the interested party within thirty days from the date of communication of this provision or sixty days if the applicant resides abroad.
