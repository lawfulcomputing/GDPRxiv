HAVING REGARD TO the observations made by the deputy secretary general pursuant to Article 15 of the Guarantor's regulation No. 1/2000;

PREMISED  

1. The verification and the initiation of the procedure.  
On December 17, 2021, Aspell s.r.l. reported  
the installation, by M.S.M. Immobiliare s.r.l.  
at its property located in Grumello del Monte  
(BG), via Roma 55, on the land adjacent to that owned by  
Aspell s.r.l., of a video surveillance system not compliant with the regulations regarding  
the protection of personal data, as the  
cameras were capable of capturing areas beyond those pertaining to the property and were not  
indicated by appropriate informational signs.  
Following a request from the Office, the  
Special Unit for Privacy Protection and Technological Fraud,  
with a note dated May 27, 2022, transmitted to this Authority  
the report of the inspection carried out on May 24, 2022  
at the registered office of M.S.M. Immobiliare s.r.l., from which it was noted the presence of a  
functioning video surveillance system, consisting of 7  
cameras, of which 5 were positioned externally  
to the property and 2 internally, not adequately  
indicated by suitable informational signs, as  
the only sign detected was found to be lacking  
the indication of the data controller.  
During the operations, the Company stated  
that: “the sign is not present on the back  
of the property near the boundary with the  
property of Aspell s.r.l.” as they had been  
removed following an act of vandalism.  
The party also represented that: “the detailed  
documentation regarding the information notice has not been  
prepared as the property is unused”; regarding this, the party committed to draft it  
as soon as possible and committed “to put up a compliant  
sign adjacent to all the cameras as soon as possible.”  
Based on the findings made, as per  
the aforementioned report, the Office proceeded to notify  
M.S.M. Immobiliare s.r.l. of the initiation of the  
sanctioning procedure, pursuant to Article 166,  
paragraph 5, of the Code in relation to the violation  
of Article 5, paragraph 1, letter a) and 13 of the Regulation  
(note dated September 13, 2022, prot. No. 48167).  
With a note dated October 13, 2022, the Company sent to  
the Guarantor defensive writings declaring to have  
“provided to affix near each  
camera as well as at each entrance of its  
building on via Bondraccolo – thus also  
on the entrance located at the back of the building – a  
pair of informational signs, for a total of 4  
(four) pairs indicating the monitored area,  
the purpose as well as the references of the data controller”; providing adequate documentary proof of this.  

2. The legal framework of the processing carried out  
The use of video surveillance systems can  
result, in relation to the positioning of  
the cameras and the quality of the images captured, in  
the processing of personal data. Such processing must  
be carried out in compliance with the general principles  
contained in Article 5 of the Regulation and, in  
particular, with the principle of transparency which  
presupposes that “data subjects must always  
be informed that they are about to enter a  
monitored area.”  
For this purpose, the data controller  
must affix suitable informational signs according to  
the indications contained in point 3.1. of the provision  
on video surveillance - April 8, 2010  
[1712680] (in this sense also the FAQs on  
video surveillance, published on the Authority's website).  
Similarly, the Guidelines No. 3/2019 of the European  
Data Protection Board on the processing  
of personal data through video devices,  
point 7) specify that “Regarding video surveillance, the most important  
information must be indicated [by the controller] on the  
warning sign itself (first level), while additional  
mandatory details can be provided by other means (second level). In the guidelines, it is also provided that “Such information can  
be provided in combination with an icon to give,  
in a clearly visible, intelligible, and clearly  
legible manner, an overview of the intended  
processing (Article 12, paragraph 7, of the GDPR). The  
format of the information must adapt to the various  
locations.” The information should be
positioned in such a way as to allow the data subject to easily recognize the circumstances of the surveillance, before entering the monitored area (approximately at eye level) "to allow the data subject to estimate which area is covered by a camera in order to avoid surveillance or adjust their behavior, if necessary."

3. The outcome of the investigation and the sanctioning procedure.
Based on the findings made by the Guardia di Finanza, it emerged that the video surveillance system, installed at the property owned by the party located in Grumello del Monte (BG), via Roma 55, was active and functioning, and that appropriate signs containing the information referred to in Article 13 of the Regulation had not been posted. It is therefore evidenced in the documents that the party has carried out processing of personal data through a video surveillance system, in the absence of the required information. Such conduct is in contrast with what is established by Article 5, paragraph 1, letter a) and Article 13 of the Regulation, according to which the data controller is required to process data in a transparent manner (Article 5, paragraph 1, letter a) of the Regulation) and to provide the data subject with all information regarding the essential characteristics of the processing (Article 13 of the Regulation).

4. Adoption of the injunction order for the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction and accessory sanctions (Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i), and 83 of the Regulation; Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code).
The Guarantor, pursuant to Article 58, paragraph 2, letter i) of the Regulation and Article 166 of the Code, has the power to impose an administrative pecuniary sanction provided for by Article 83, paragraph 5, of the Regulation, through the adoption of an injunction order (Article 18, Law No. 689 of November 24, 1981), in relation to the processing of personal data carried out by the company through the video surveillance system, in the absence of the information referred to in Article 13 of the Regulation. 

With reference to the elements listed in Article 83, paragraph 2, of the Regulation for the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction and its quantification, considering that the sanction must be “effective, proportionate and dissuasive in each individual case” (Article 83, paragraph 1 of the Regulation), it is represented that, in this case, the following circumstances have been taken into account:
- regarding the nature, gravity, and duration of the violation, the conduct of the data controller has been considered, as well as the responsibility related to the failure to fulfill the obligation to provide information to the data subjects;
- the absence of specific previous violations by the company related to breaches of the data protection regulations;
- the circumstance that the Company, as the data controller, cooperated with the Authority during the proceedings by arranging appropriate informational signs and demonstrating compliance with the legal obligation.

In light of all the elements outlined above, assessed in their entirety, it is deemed appropriate to determine the amount of the pecuniary sanction at the sum of €1,000 (one thousand). In this context, also considering the type of violation established, which concerned the principles of personal data protection, it is noted, pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code and Article 16, paragraph 1, of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2019, that it is necessary to proceed with the publication of this provision on the Guarantor's website. 

Finally, it is believed that the conditions referred to in Article 17 of Regulation No. 1/2019 concerning internal procedures with external relevance, aimed at carrying out the tasks and exercising the powers assigned to the Guarantor, are met.

ALL OF THE ABOVE HAVING BEEN STATED, THE GUARANTOR
declares, pursuant to Articles 57, paragraph 1, letter f) and 83 of the Regulation, the illegality of the processing carried out by M.S.M. Immobiliare s.r.l., based in Grumello del Monte (BG), via Roma 55 (VAT No. 03950430169) through the use of the video surveillance system installed at the property owned by it, as stated in the reasoning, for the violation of Articles 5, paragraph 1, letter a) and 13 of the Regulation;

ORDERS
M.S.M. Immobiliare s.r.l., based in Grumello del Monte (BG), via Roma 55, (VAT No. 03950430169) to pay the sum of €1,000.00 (one thousand) as an administrative pecuniary sanction for the violations indicated in this provision;

COMMANDS
therefore the same M.S.M. Immobiliare s.r.l., to pay the sum of €1,000.00 (one thousand), according to the methods indicated in the attachment, within 30 days from the notification of this provision, under penalty of the adoption of the consequent executive acts pursuant to Article 27 of Law No. 689/1981. It is noted that pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 8 of the Code, the offender retains the option to settle the dispute by paying – always according to the methods indicated in the attachment – an amount equal to half of the imposed sanction within the term referred to in Article 10, paragraph 3, of Legislative Decree No. 150 of September 1, 2011 provided for the submission of the appeal as indicated below.
