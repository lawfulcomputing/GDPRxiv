HAVING REGARD to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament  
and of the Council, of April 27, 2016 (hereinafter “Regulation”);  
HAVING REGARD to Legislative Decree No. 196 of June 30, 2003 (Code regarding  
the protection of personal data, hereinafter “Code”) as amended by Legislative Decree No. 101 of August 10, 2018, containing “Provisions for the adaptation of  
national legislation to the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679”;  
HAVING REGARD to the inspection report prepared by the  
Guardia di Finanza – Special Unit for Privacy Protection and  
Technological Fraud, which identified the presence of a  
video surveillance system at the registered office of Mirva s.r.l. that was not compliant with  
the provisions of Articles 5, paragraph 1, letter a),  
114, and 157 of Legislative Decree 196/2003 (Code regarding the  
protection of personal data);  
HAVING EXAMINED the documentation on file;  
HAVING REGARD to the observations made by the Secretary  
General pursuant to Article 15 of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2000;  
REPORTING Prof. Pasquale Stanzione;  
WHEREAS  
1. The acts of verification and the initiation of  
the procedure.  
By note dated February 15, 2019, the Authority was informed of the unlawful installation of a  
video surveillance system by the company Mirva  
s.r.l. (hereinafter, the Company), located at  

the registered office of the Company, situated in Settimo  
Milanese (MI), Via Keplero 21, as it was operating without informative signs and capable of capturing  
areas not pertaining to the Company.  
The Office initiated a preliminary investigation regarding the reported facts by sending two  
requests for information to the Company (note dated  
May 20, 2019, and note dated September 5, 2019).  
Having received no response to the aforementioned requests  
for information, the collaboration of the Special Unit for Privacy Protection and  
Technological Fraud of the Guardia di Finanza was requested regarding the  
collection of information related to the reported incident, and a  
procedure was simultaneously initiated, pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 5, of  
Legislative Decree 196/2003 (prot. 39735 of January 18, 2019), regarding the failure to respond to the  
investigative requests of the Guarantor.  
During the inspection, it was found that, at the Company’s premises, there were  
“six cameras, two inside the warehouse and four monitoring the external areas” capable of capturing  
the work activities of employees (particularly the two  
installed inside the structure).  
The system was found to be operational, and it was  
established that there was only one informative sign regarding the  
video surveillance system, visible to anyone entering the warehouse (cf. documents from the Unit - minutes  
of operations carried out on December 12, 2019).  
During the inspection operations, it was reported:  
- that “The system is not owned by the Company  
[but has been] loaned by the Security Institute”;  
- that the system, installed to ensure the  
safety of goods and people, consists of 6 cameras, of which two are positioned to monitor  
the entrance with a field of view that captures  

“in addition to the area directly in front of the  
warehouse, a shared area with another company”, as well as “a short stretch of the road in front”; two  
that capture only areas pertaining to the Company;  
two others located inside the structure with a  
view directed towards some limited parts of the work area, but – as specified by the  
recorders – capable of “capturing the work  
activities of employees”;  
- of not possessing a specific  
authorization from the Territorial Labor Inspectorate;  
- that “the images, in addition to being viewed by the legal  
representative, can also be viewed via  
the internet by the Security Institute, but only in case of an alarm.”  
Based on the findings of the aforementioned  
verification, the Office, by note No. 4348 of  
February 3, 2020, initiated a second procedure for  
the adoption of corrective measures regarding  
the violation of the principle of lawfulness of  
processing under Article 5, paragraph 1, letter a),  
of Article 4 of Law No. 300/1970, referred to  
by Article 114 of the Code as well as Article 37 of  
the Regulation.  
The notification of the violations related to this  
procedure was sent by registered mail with  
acknowledgment of receipt addressed to the registered office of  
Settimo Milanese (MI), Via Keplero 21.  
With reference to this communication, it is noted  
that, despite the confirmed correspondence  
of the indicated address with that resulting from the  
Company's chamber registration, the relevant package was  
returned for “unavailability” of the  
recipient, as indicated by the annotation dated  
February 10, 2020, affixed to it.  
Two further attempts to notify were then made on  
March 31, 2020, and April 7, 2020, via PEC, to the digital domicile of the Company  
resulting from the chamber registration.
(mirvasrl@pec.ambs.it), both attempts were unsuccessful, as the relevant message was not delivered due to "invalid address."  
Only on 11.11.2021, the Guardia di Finanza – Compagnia Rho, at the request of the Authority, proceeded to notify the party of the note containing the act initiating the sanctioning procedure pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 5 of Legislative Decree 196/2003 (prot. no. 4348 of 3.2.2020).  
In this regard, it is necessary to take into account the established orientation of the jurisprudence of legitimacy that has extended to the notification in the administrative sanctioning procedure the application of the principle established by the United Sections for acts of civil proceedings, according to which in the case of notification […] not having been successful for reasons not attributable to the notifier, upon learning of the negative outcome, in order to preserve the effects related to the original request, the notifier must reactivate the notification process immediately and carry out the necessary acts for its completion in a timely manner, that is, without exceeding the time limit equal to half of the terms indicated by Article 325 of the Civil Procedure Code, except for exceptional circumstances of which rigorous proof is provided (Cass. civ. s.u. ruling no. 14594/2016; previously, Cass. civ. s.u. no. 17352/2009), specifying how this latter reference should be understood as a call to half of the term assigned by law for each notification activity (Cass. civ. ord. no. 36463/2022; Cass. civ. ord. no. 28136/2022; Cass. civ. ord. no. 28388/2017; Cass. civ. ord. no. 28618/2017).  
In light of this principle, it must be noted that the first attempt to renew the notification, carried out on 31.3.2021, more than a year after the Authority learned of the negative outcome of the notification attempt via registered mail, is late, as it was certainly made beyond the 60-day limit (corresponding to half of the peremptory term of 120 days, provided by Table B), attached to the Regulation of the Guarantor no. 2/2019, for the notification of the alleged violations ascertained), identified by applying the criterion developed by the aforementioned jurisprudence of legitimacy.  
And this conclusion must also be confirmed taking into account the suspension of the terms of 83 days, from 23 February 2020 to 15 May 2020, linked to the COVID-19 epidemiological emergency (pursuant to Article 103, paragraphs 1 and 1-bis, D.L. no. 18/2020 and Article 37, paragraph 1, D.L. no. 23/2020).  
All of this considered, it is therefore deemed necessary to order the archiving of the second procedure, initiated by the act prot. no. 4348 of 3.2.2020.  
2. The outcome of the investigation and the sanctioning procedure.  
Based on the documents, it is noted that the Company, having not provided a response to the Guarantor's request within the established terms, has violated the obligation provided for by Article 157 of the Code regarding the protection of personal data.  
3. Adoption of the injunction order for the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction and accessory sanctions (Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i), and 83 of the Regulation; Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code).  
Pursuant to Article 58, paragraph 2, letter i) of the Regulation and Article 166, paragraphs 3 and 7 of the Code, the Guarantor orders the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction provided for by Article 83, paragraph 5, letter a) of the Regulation, by adopting an injunction order (as per Article 18 of Law 24.11.1981, no. 689), in relation to the processing of personal data carried out by the Company, which has been found to be unlawful, in the terms outlined above, of Article 157 of the Code.  
With reference to the elements listed in Article 83, paragraph 2 of the Regulation for the purpose of applying the administrative pecuniary sanction and its quantification, considering that the sanction must "in any case [be] effective, proportionate and dissuasive" (Article 83, paragraph 1 of the Regulation), it is represented that, in this case, the following circumstances have been considered: in relation to the nature, gravity, and duration of the violation, the nature of the violation concerning the duty of loyal cooperation with the Authority has been considered; with reference to the intentional or negligent nature of the violation and the degree of responsibility of the data controller, it is noted that the violation is of a negligent nature; the absence of specific previous offenses against the Company.  
In light of the above elements and the evaluations made, it is deemed appropriate, in this case, to apply to Mirva s.r.l. the administrative sanction of payment of a sum equal to 5,000 euros (five thousand).  
It is also deemed that the conditions referred to in Article 17 of Regulation no. 1/2019 concerning internal procedures with external relevance, aimed at carrying out the tasks and exercising the powers entrusted to the Guarantor, are met.  
It is noted that, if the conditions are met, the sanction referred to in Article 83, paragraph 5, letter e) of the Regulation may be applied administratively.  
ALL OF THE ABOVE CONSIDERED, THE GUARANTOR  
notes the unlawfulness of the processing carried out by Mirva s.r.l., in the terms stated in the motivation, for the violation of Article 157 of Legislative Decree 196/2003;  
DETERMINES  
to archive, for the reasons specifically stated above, the procedure initiated against Mirva s.r.l., with note prot. no. 4348 of 3.2.2020;
