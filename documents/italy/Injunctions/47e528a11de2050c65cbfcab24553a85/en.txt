PREAMBLE  

1. Introduction.  
With a complaint submitted pursuant to Article 77 of  
the Regulation, an interested party alleged a presumed  
violation of the rules on the protection  
of personal data, concerning processing carried out by  
the Municipality of Tuscania through video devices.  
By note prot. n. XX of XX, the Authority, pursuant  
to Article 157 of the Code, sent a  
request for information to the Municipality regarding the facts  
subject to the complaint.  
Not having received a response from the Municipality within the  
set deadline, the Authority, by note of XX  
(prot. n. XX), notified the Entity of the  
administrative violation contestation referred to  
in Article 166, paragraph 5, of the Code, for having acted in  
violation of Article 157 of the Code.  
Although the Municipality had been informed of  
the possibility of sending defensive writings or  
documents to the Guarantor, as well as requesting to be heard in  
a hearing, within 30 days from the date  
of notification of the violation, made on  
XX, the Municipality neither submitted defensive memos  
nor requested to be heard within that deadline.  
Only with note of XX (prot. n. XX), therefore  
belatedly, the Municipality partially provided  
the requested information elements.  
The Entity also requested to be heard,  
a request that was rejected by note of XX (prot.  
n. XX), as it was submitted beyond the thirty-day  
deadline set by law (see Article 166,  
paragraphs 6 and 7 of the Code, as well as Article 18, paragraph 1 of  
Law No. 689 of November 24, 1981), which expired on XX.  
With note of XX (prot. n. XX), formulated pursuant  
to Article 157 of the Code, the Authority therefore  
made a second request for information to the Municipality,  
regarding both the information elements already previously requested,  
which had not been provided, and further aspects related  
to the facts subject to the complaint.  
However, the Municipality did not provide any response  
within the deadline set, and the Authority, with note  
of XX (prot. n. XX), notified the same  
of an additional administrative violation contestation  
referred to in Article 166, paragraph 5, of  
the Code, for having again acted in violation  
of Article 157 of the Code.  
In response to this contestation, the Municipality, in addition to  
not having submitted defensive memos or requested to  
be heard within the thirty-day deadline from the date  
of notification of the violation, has to date  
not provided any response to the aforementioned request  
for information, despite the long time elapsed.  

2. Outcome of the investigative activity.  
In light of what was illustrated in the previous paragraph 1,  
it has been established that the Municipality has failed to provide  
responses to the aforementioned requests for information  
formulated by the Authority pursuant to Article 157 of  
the Code, having committed two violations of such  
provision and still being in default  
of the obligation to respond to the Authority within the  
proceedings concerning the handling of the complaint.  

3. Conclusions.  
Based on the evaluations mentioned above, it is  
noted, given also the absence of defensive writings  
from the Municipality, the impossibility of overcoming the  
objections notified by the Office with the initiation  
of the proceedings, as there are no elements in the  
files that would allow for the archiving of the present  
proceedings and, moreover, none of the  
cases provided for by Article 11 of the Guarantor's  
Regulation No. 1/2019 apply.  
Therefore, the preliminary evaluations of the Office are confirmed  
and the violation by the Municipality of the rules on the protection of  
personal data is noted.
**1. Background**

Due to the Municipality's failure to provide a full and timely response to the request for information referred to in the note dated XX (prot. n. XX) and its complete omission to respond to the request for information referred to in the note dated XX (prot. n. XX), there has been a dual violation of Article 157 of the Code. 

Considering that the violations of the aforementioned provision occurred as a result of a single conduct (see the “Guidelines 4/2022 on the calculation of administrative monetary penalties pursuant to the GDPR,” adopted by the European Data Protection Board on May 24, 2023, par. 40, which highlights that “one could also hypothesize a unit of action when a single action violates the same legal provision multiple times [...] when the circumstances constitute an iterative and congenial violation of the same legal provision in a close space-time succession), Article 83, par. 3, of the Regulation applies, according to which the total amount of the administrative monetary penalty does not exceed the amount specified for the most serious violation. Considering that, in this case, Article 157 of the Code has been violated multiple times within the proceedings concerning the same complaint, the penalty provided for by Article 83, par. 5, of the Regulation applies, as recalled by Article 166, paragraph 2, of the Code, and therefore, the total amount of the penalty is to be quantified up to €20,000,000.

**2. Corrective Measures (Article 58, par. 2, lett. a), of the Regulation)**

Given the Municipality's ongoing non-compliance, it is deemed necessary to order the same, pursuant to Articles 58, par. 2, lett. a), of the Regulation and 157 of the Code, to respond to the Authority within thirty days from the notification of this provision, both to the request for information referred to in the note dated XX (prot. n. XX), to which the Entity has only partially and tardily provided the necessary elements for the investigation related to the complaint, and to the request for information referred to in the note dated XX (prot. n. XX), which remains entirely unaddressed to this day.

**3. Adoption of the injunction order for the application of the administrative monetary penalty and accessory penalties (Articles 58, par. 2, lett. i and 83 of the Regulation; Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code)**

The Guarantor, pursuant to Articles 58, par. 2, lett. i) and 83 of the Regulation as well as Article 166 of the Code, has the power to “impose an administrative monetary penalty pursuant to Article 83, in addition to the [other] corrective measures [referred to in this paragraph, or in lieu of such measures, depending on the circumstances of each individual case” and, in this context, “the Board [of the Guarantor] adopts the injunction order, which also provides for the application of the accessory administrative penalty of its publication, in whole or in part, on the Guarantor's website pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code” (Article 16, paragraph 1, of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2019).

In this regard, considering Article 83, par. 3, of the Regulation, in this case, the violation of the cited provisions is subject to the application of the administrative monetary penalty provided for by Article 83, par. 5, of the Regulation. The aforementioned administrative monetary penalty imposed, depending on the circumstances of each individual case, must be determined in amount taking into due account the elements provided for by Article 83, par. 2, of the Regulation.

Considering that:
- the violation is characterized by particular gravity, given that the Municipality, in the context of the investigation related to the same complaint, has failed twice to provide a full and timely response to requests for information from the Authority, demonstrating a lack of consideration for the regulations on personal data protection and the role of the Guarantor, as well as causing a prolongation of the proceedings, to the detriment of the complainant's position within the same (Article 83, par. 2, lett. a), of the Regulation);
- the violation has a fraudulent character (Article 83, par. 2, lett. b), of the Regulation);
- it is believed that, in this case, the level of severity of the violation committed by the data controller is high (cf. European Data Protection Board, “Guidelines 4/2022 on the calculation of administrative monetary penalties pursuant to the GDPR” of May 24, 2023, point 60).

That said, considering that the data controller is a small Municipality (approximately 8,000 inhabitants), therefore equipped with limited economic resources, it is believed that, for the purposes of quantifying the penalty, the following circumstances should be taken into account:
- the Municipality has cooperated poorly with the Authority during the proceedings, having provided a partial and tardy response only with respect to one of the aforementioned requests for information (Article 83, par. 2, lett. f), of the Regulation);
- there are no previous relevant violations committed by the Municipality (Article 83, par. 2, lett. e), of the Regulation).

In light of the aforementioned elements, assessed in their entirety, it is deemed appropriate to determine the amount of the monetary penalty at €4,000 (four thousand) for the violation of Article 157 of the Code, as the administrative monetary penalty deemed, pursuant to Article 83, par. 1, of the Regulation, effective, proportionate, and deterrent.

It is also believed that, pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code and Article 16, paragraph 1, of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2019, the publication of this section containing the injunction order on the Guarantor's website should proceed. This is in consideration of the particular...
**devaluation of the conduct of the Municipality, as highlighted above.**  
It is finally noted that the conditions referred to in Article 17 of Regulation No. 1/2019 are met.
