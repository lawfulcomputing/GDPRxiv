4.2 The dissemination of data on individual compensations

In reiterating that the personal data protection system requires a suitable legal basis for any processing operation (art. 4, point 2 of the Regulation), including dissemination (art. 2-ter, paragraph 4, letter b) of the Code), it is noted that the Authority has long provided guidance on the prerequisites (and the occurrence of these, as well as the specific modalities) for the lawful publication, including online, of acts and documents containing personal data related to workers (see paragraph 6.3 of the "Guidelines on the processing of personal data of workers for the purpose of managing the employment relationship in the public sector" of June 14, 2007, doc. web n. 1417809, where it is stated that it is not lawful to disseminate personal information regarding individual compensations without a valid legal basis).

5. Conclusion

In light of the above, it is evident that the publication of the aforementioned resolutions and related attachments on the institutional website of the Azienda Sanitaria constitutes a violation of the applicable data protection regulations, particularly concerning the dissemination of personal data without a valid legal basis. The Authority will therefore proceed with the adoption of the necessary measures to ensure compliance with the provisions of the Regulation and the Code, as well as to protect the rights of the individuals involved. 

The Azienda Sanitaria is hereby informed that it has the right to appeal against this decision in accordance with the provisions of the applicable law.

referring to individual workers through their publication in communications and internal documents or acts and circulars intended for the collective of workers (see also the provision of May 15, 2014, no. 243, web doc no. 3134436 “Guidelines on the processing of personal data, including in acts and administrative documents, carried out for the purposes of publicity and transparency on the web by public entities and other obligated bodies”). 

With regard, therefore, to the reference to the transparency regulations invoked by the Health Authority as a premise that would justify the dissemination of the personal data in question, it is observed, preliminarily, that the publication of the aforementioned resolutions took place in the “Albo Pretorio” section of the Health Authority's institutional website and not, instead, in the “Transparent Administration” section, thus rendering this regulatory reference irrelevant to the facts subject to complaint. Nor does any of the provisions of Legislative Decree of March 14, 2013, no. 33 impose the publication of such sensitive information concerning the individual employment relationship.

In the specific case, the online publication was initiated, in the first place, of data relating to the amounts paid by the Health Authority for additional services performed by individual employees. In this regard, the same transparency regulations, in order to provide public evidence of the levels of rewards in the distribution of bonuses and incentives to staff, require administrations to publish only the aggregated values of the amounts paid and not, as occurred in this case, the detailed breakdown of the additional compensation received individually by employees (see Article 20 of Legislative Decree 33 of 2013; see also provision no. 287 of July 6, 2023, web doc 9920145).

The specific regulations regarding the publicity of administrative acts in the “Telematic Albo Pretorio of the Entity” (see Article 124 of Legislative Decree no. 267 of August 18, 2000 - Consolidated Law on the laws governing local authorities; see also Article 32, paragraph 1, of Law no. 69 of June 18, 2009) - which governs the publication for 15 days on the Albo Pretorio of the Entity's resolutions - merely provides that the obligations of publication of acts and administrative provisions having the effect of legal publicity are considered fulfilled with the publication on their own websites by the administrations and public entities obliged, but does not provide anything regarding the publication of personal data relating to the specific employment relationship.

The Guarantor has clarified on numerous occasions that even the presence of a specific publicity regime cannot entail any automaticity regarding the online dissemination of personal data and information, nor a derogation from the principles regarding the protection of personal data (see, among many, most recently provision of July 4, 2024, no. 404, web doc 10050145 and previous ones cited therein). 

In numerous decisions regarding the obligations arising from Article 124 of Legislative Decree 267/2000, in fact, the Guarantor has reiterated that all limits provided by the principles of personal data protection also apply to publications on the online Albo Pretorio of acts or resolutions, having regard primarily to the existence of suitable lawful bases for the online dissemination of the personal data contained therein, even before considering any possible minimization of the same. This is also confirmed by the personal data protection system contained in the Regulation, under which it is provided that the data controller must implement “appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure that, by default, only personal data necessary for each specific purpose of the processing are processed” and must be “able to demonstrate” – in light of the principle of “accountability” – that they have done so (Articles 5, paragraph 2; 24 and 25, paragraph 2, Regulation). Therefore, where the online publication of documents involves the processing of personal data and thus their dissemination, the needs for publicity pursued must be appropriately balanced with the rights and fundamental freedoms, as well as the dignity of the data subject, with particular reference to privacy, personal identity, and the right to the protection of personal data, prioritizing the existence of a suitable legal basis for the dissemination of the data (see, most recently, provisions no. 366 of November 10, 2022, web doc no. 9834986 and no. 299, of September 15, 2022, web doc no. 9815665).

Nor can the necessity to publish the aforementioned resolutions be invoked on the grounds of the need to provide adequate reasoning for the adopted measures, since the general obligation of motivation that permeates administrative action even in the drafting phase of acts (Article 3, Law 241/1990) cannot be invoked to justify the online publication of the same in full version, given that each administrative act adopted, remaining with the administration that formed it, continues to be accessible, by qualified subjects, in the ways and limits provided by law (on this point see the constant orientation of the Guarantor, precisely with regard to administrative documents formed within proceedings involving also the personnel of the administration, see provision no. 84 of March 23, 2023, web doc 9888113, and provisions cited therein) and remaining, in any case, subject to the other forms of accessibility of administrative acts provided by the legal system (Articles 22 and following, Law of August 7, 1990, no. 241; Presidential Decree of April 12, 2006, no. 184; Articles 6, 9, 10, and 86 Regulation).

**6. Adoption of the injunction order for the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction and accessory sanctions (Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i and 83 of the Regulation; Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code).**  
The Garante, pursuant to Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i) and 83 of the Regulation as well as Article 166 of the Code, has the power to "impose an administrative pecuniary sanction pursuant to Article 83, in addition to the [other] [corrective] measures referred to in this paragraph, or in lieu of such measures, depending on the circumstances of each individual case" and, in this context, "the Board [of the Garante] adopts the injunction order, which also provides for the application of the accessory administrative sanction of its publication, in whole or in part, on the Garante's website pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code" (Article 16, paragraph 1, of the Garante Regulation No. 1/2019).  
In this regard, taking into account Article 83, paragraph 3, of the Regulation, in this case, the violation of the cited provisions is subject to the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction provided for by Article 83, paragraph 5, of the Regulation.  
The aforementioned administrative pecuniary sanction imposed, depending on the circumstances of each individual case, must be determined in amount taking into due account the elements provided for by Article 83, paragraph 2, of the Regulation.  
Considering that:  
- with specific regard to the nature, severity, and duration of the violation, it should be noted that the dissemination of data relating to the complainant and numerous other interested parties occurred on the institutional website of the Health Authority without indexing on search engines (cf. Article 83, paragraph 2, letter a), of the Regulation);  
- with specific regard to the subjective profile of the violation, the Health Authority acted under the mistaken belief of fulfilling a legal obligation despite the numerous indications provided by the Garante to all public entities since 2014 with the aforementioned guidelines (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter b), of the Regulation);  
- regarding the categories of personal data, data belonging to special categories such as health information concerning the complainant and certain colleagues were disseminated, including detailed indications regarding specific reasons for absence and information related to the exercise of the complainant's trade union rights (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter g) of the Regulation).  
In light of this specific circumstance, it is believed that, in this case, the level of severity of the violation committed by the data controller is medium (cf. European Data Protection Board, "Guidelines 4/2022 on the calculation of administrative pecuniary sanctions pursuant to the GDPR" of May 24, 2023, point 60).  
That said, the following aggravating and mitigating circumstances must be considered:  
- there are some previous violations of the same provisions of the Regulation and the Code, even in the same context (cf. Article 83, paragraph 2, letter e), of the Regulation);  
- the Health Authority has offered good cooperation with the Authority, stating that it removed, on XX, resolutions No. XX and XX of XX (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter f), of the Regulation).  
By reason of the aforementioned elements, assessed in their entirety, it is deemed appropriate to determine the amount of the pecuniary sanction at €20,000 (twenty thousand) for the violation of Articles 5, 6, and 9 of the Regulation, as well as 2-ter and 2-septies, paragraph 8 of the Code, as an administrative pecuniary sanction deemed, pursuant to Article 83, paragraph 1, of the Regulation, effective, proportionate, and deterrent.  
In this context, it is also believed that, pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code and Article 16,
**Pursuant to comma 1 of the Regulation of the Guarantor no. 1/2019, it is necessary to proceed with the publication of this chapter containing the injunction order on the website of the Guarantor.**

This is in consideration of the sensitive information that has been disseminated through the website of the Health Authority regarding matters related to the employment relationship of the complainant, which has also led to the dissemination of personal data relating to special categories, as well as the Health Authority's failure to respond to the complainant's request for the deletion of their personal data.

It is noted, finally, that the conditions referred to in Article 17 of Regulation no. 1/2019 are met.
