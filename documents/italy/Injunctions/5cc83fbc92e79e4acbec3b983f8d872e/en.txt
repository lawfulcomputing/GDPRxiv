1. Introduction.  
With a complaint submitted, pursuant to Article 77 of the Regulation, against the Municipality of Guardiagrele (hereinafter, the "Municipality"), Mr. XX has represented to this Authority that he exercised, on XX, the right of access under Article 15 of the Regulation against the Municipality and did not receive an adequate response.  
In particular, the complainant stated:  
- "As a recipient of public housing, following communication from ATER CHIETI on XX, I submitted a request to purchase the housing on XX [...] On XX, I received, as a copy for information, an internal communication from ATER, [...] in which it is practically stated that the sale of the property cannot proceed as it was transferred on XX to the Municipality of Guardiagrele";  
- "Having also learned that on XX the Municipality of Guardiagrele proceeded to sell the apartments in my same condominium except for me, on XX I formally requested justification for this exclusion [...] from the Municipality of Guardiagrele [...] On XX, the response I received from [...] the Municipality of Guardiagrele is that the owner of the apartment is not the Municipality of Guardiagrele but ATER CHIETI."  
In order to understand, therefore, the owner of the property, as well as the data controller, the complainant informally approached the Municipality on several occasions, without obtaining a definitive clarification. Most recently, the complainant exercised, against the Municipality, the right of access without receiving a response.

**3. Outcome of the Instructive Activity.**  
The processing of personal data by public entities must occur in compliance with the provisions of the Regulation and the Code.  
The processing of personal data carried out by public entities is, as a rule, lawful only if necessary “to fulfill a legal obligation to which the data controller is subject” or “for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of public powers vested in the data controller” (art. 6, par. 1, lett. c) and e), of the Regulation). The data controller, even in the presence of a condition of lawfulness, is in any case required to respect the principles regarding the protection of personal data, among which is the principle of “lawfulness, fairness, and transparency” according to which data must be “processed in a lawful, fair, and transparent manner in relation to the data subject” (art. 5, par. 1, lett. a) of the Regulation).  
Pursuant to art. 15 of the Regulation, the data subject has the right to obtain from the data controller or (as in the present case, the presumed data controller), “confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning him or her is being processed and, if so, to obtain access to the personal data and the following information: a) the purposes of the processing; b) the categories of personal data concerned; c) the recipients or the
recipients to whom personal data have been or will be communicated […]”. Article 12, paragraph 3, of the Regulation establishes that the data controller must respond to the request of the data subject without undue delay and, in any case, no later than one month from the receipt of the request. This period may be extended by two months, if necessary, taking into account the complexity and number of requests, provided that the data subject is informed of such extension and the reasons for the delay within one month of receiving the request (recital 59 and Article 12, paragraph 4, of the Regulation). 

During the investigation, it was established that there was no response to the exercise of the rights of the data subjects (specifically, the right of access under Article 15 of the Regulation), in violation of Articles 5 and 12 of the same Regulation. It is worth clarifying that, in the case at hand, the data subject, in order to benefit from the right of first refusal to purchase the housing assigned to him, needed to identify the owner of the property - as well as the data controller of the personal data related to the management of the housing. For these reasons, after numerous and fruitless communications with the Municipality, the complainant exercised the right of access against the Municipality, which, in any case, did not provide a response within the time frame established by the Regulation. 

The Supervisory Authority, within the scope of the tasks and powers conferred by the Regulation, processes complaints by conducting appropriate investigations, also regarding the correct application of data protection legislation (Article 57, paragraph 1, letters a), f) and h) and Article 58 of the Regulation). For this purpose, the Authority has the power to order the data controller and the data processor to provide any information necessary for the execution of its tasks (Article 58, paragraph 1, letter a), of the Regulation). 

Article 157 of the Code also provides that, in relation to the powers referred to in Article 57 of the Regulation and for the performance of its tasks, the Guarantor may request the data controller, the processor, the representative of the controller or the processor, the data subject, or even third parties, to provide information and to exhibit documents, and that failure to respond to such a request within the indicated time frame makes the administrative pecuniary sanction provided for in Article 83, paragraph 5 of the Regulation applicable (see Article 166, paragraph 2, of the Code). 

In this case, it was established that the Municipality failed to respond to the Authority's request for information (note prot. XX of XX), thus confirming the violation of Article 157 of the Code. In this regard, it is noted that, also in light of the general principles of good administration, efficiency, effectiveness, and economy of administrative action (Article 97 of the Constitution, as well as Article 9, paragraph 1 and Article 10, paragraph 3, of the internal Regulation of the Guarantor No. 1/2019 of April 4, 2019, doc. web No. 9107633), the lack of response affected the completeness and timeliness of the investigative activity. Regarding this complaint, it is noted that the Municipality did not submit defensive briefs. 

4. Conclusions. 
In light of the evaluations mentioned above, it is noted that the statements made by the Municipality regarding the processing during the investigation - the veracity of which may be subject to accountability under Article 168 of the Code - although deserving of consideration, do not allow for overcoming the observations notified by the Office with the initiation of the procedure and are insufficient to allow for the closure of the present procedure, as none of the cases provided for in Article 11 of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2019 apply. Therefore, the preliminary evaluations of the Office are confirmed, and the illegality of the processing of personal data carried out by the Municipality is noted, for having provided the data subject with a late and partial response to the access request, in violation of Articles 5, paragraph 1, letter a), 12, paragraphs 3 and 4, and 15 of the Regulation, and for failing to respond to the request for information made by the Guarantor, in violation of Article 157 of the Code. 

5. Adoption of the injunction order for the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction and accessory sanctions (Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i), and 83 of the Regulation; Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code). 
The Guarantor, pursuant to Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i), and 83 of the Regulation as well as Article 166 of the Code, has the power to “impose an administrative pecuniary sanction pursuant to Article 83, in addition to the [other] corrective measures referred to in this paragraph, or in lieu of such measures, depending on the circumstances of each individual case” and, in this context, “the Board [of the Guarantor] adopts the injunction order, which also provides for the application of the accessory administrative sanction of its publication, in whole or in part, on the Guarantor's website pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code” (Article 16, paragraph 1, of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2019). 

In this regard, taking into account Article 83, paragraph 3, of the Regulation, in this case, the violation of the cited provisions is subject to the application of the same administrative pecuniary sanction provided for in Article 83, paragraph 5, of the Regulation. The aforementioned administrative pecuniary sanction imposed, depending on the circumstances of each individual case, must be determined in amount taking into due account the elements provided for in Article 83, paragraph 2, of the Regulation, regarding which the following observations are made. 

The delay and incompleteness of the responses provided to the complainant, as well as the failure to respond to the
REQUEST FOR INFORMATION FROM THE GUARANTOR, have negatively impacted the completeness and promptness of the investigative activity. 

On the other hand, it is noted that the Municipality has taken steps to remedy the violation and mitigate its possible negative effects, and that there are no previous relevant violations or prior measures against the Municipality under Article 58 of the Regulation. It is also noted that the violation is of a negligent nature. In light of the aforementioned elements, assessed as a whole, it is deemed appropriate to determine the amount of the monetary sanction at €5,000 (five thousand) for the violation of Articles 5, 12, and 15 of the Regulation and Article 157 of the Code, as an administrative monetary sanction considered, pursuant to Article 83, paragraph 1, of the Regulation, effective, proportionate, and dissuasive.

It is also considered that the accessory sanction of publication of this provision on the Guarantor's website should be applied, as provided for by Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code and Article 16 of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2019.

Finally, it is believed that the conditions referred to in Article 17 of Regulation No. 1/2019 concerning internal procedures with external relevance, aimed at carrying out the tasks and exercising the powers assigned to the Guarantor, are met.

ALL OF THE ABOVE HAVING BEEN STATED, THE GUARANTOR 
pursuant to Article 57, paragraph 1, letter f), of the Regulation, declares the conduct of the Municipality of Guardiagrele, described in the reasoning, to be unlawful, consisting of the violation of Articles 5, 12, and 15 of the Regulation and Article 157 of the Code.
