4. Conclusion.
In light of the above, it is evident that the Province of Catanzaro has failed to comply with the obligation to communicate the contact details of the Data Protection Officer to the Authority, as required by Article 37, paragraph 7 of the Regulation. This constitutes a violation of the provisions set forth in the Regulation and the Code.

5. Measures.
Therefore, the Authority, in accordance with Article 58, paragraph 2, of the Regulation, decides to impose the following measures on the Province of Catanzaro:
a) to communicate the contact details of the Data Protection Officer to the Authority within 30 days from the notification of this provision;
b) to ensure that the contact details of the Data Protection Officer are published on its institutional website, in compliance with Article 37, paragraph 7 of the Regulation.

6. Notification.
This provision shall be notified to the Province of Catanzaro and published on the Authority's website.
