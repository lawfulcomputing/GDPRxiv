**3. Outcome of the investigative activity**  
Taking note of what was represented by the Company, it is observed that:  
1. For “personal data,” it is understood as “any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (‘data subject’)” and for “data concerning health,” it is understood as “personal data related to the physical or mental health of a natural person, including the provision of health care, which reveals information about their health status” (art. 4, par. 1, n. 1 and 15 of the Regulation). The latter data deserves greater protection since the context of their processing could create significant risks to the rights and fundamental freedoms (Recital no. 51);  
2. The data controller is required to comply with the principles regarding data protection, including the principle of “integrity and confidentiality,” according to which personal data must be “processed in a manner that ensures adequate security (...), including protection against unauthorized or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage” (art. 5, par. 1, lett. f) of the Regulation). The adequacy of such measures must be assessed by the data controller in order to “ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk,” taking into account, among other things, “the nature, scope, context and purposes of the processing, as well as the risk of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons” (art. 32 of the Regulation);  
3. Regarding information, communication, and transparent modalities for the exercise of the rights of the data subject, art. 12, par. 3, of the Regulation establishes that the data controller must respond to the request of the data subject, made pursuant to arts. 15 to 22 of the Regulation, without undue delay and, in any case, no later than one month from receipt of the same. This period may be extended by two months, if necessary, taking into account the complexity and number of requests, provided that the data subject is informed of such extension and the reasons for the delay within one month of receiving the request. If the data controller does not comply with the request of the data subject, they must, in any case, inform the data subject without delay and, at the latest, within one month from receipt of the request, of the reasons for non-compliance and the possibility of lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority and seeking judicial remedy (cons. 59 and art. 12, par. 4, of the Regulation). In the case of a request made pursuant to art. 15 of the Regulation, the data subject has the right to ask the data controller and obtain within the aforementioned terms, if the conditions are met, confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning them is being processed, as well as access to personal data and the information referred to in par. 1 (letters a) to h)) and 2 of the same article 15;  
4. The regulation on personal data protection provides that information on health status may only be communicated to the data subject and may be communicated to third parties only on the basis of an appropriate legal basis or at the request of the data subject itself, upon prior written delegation from the latter (art. 9 of the Regulation).  

**4. Conclusions.**  
In light of the evaluations mentioned above, taking into account the statements made by the data controller...
treatment during the investigation and considering that, unless the act constitutes a more serious crime, anyone who, in a proceeding before the Guarantor, falsely declares or certifies news or circumstances or produces false acts or documents is liable under Article 168 of the Code "Falsehood in declarations to the Guarantor and interruption of the execution of the tasks or the exercise of the powers of the Guarantor." The elements provided by the data controller in the aforementioned defense memorandum, although deserving of consideration, do not allow for overcoming the observations notified by the Office with the referenced initiation act of the proceedings, nor do any of the cases provided for in Article 11 of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2019 apply.

4.1. Failure to comply with the basic principles of processing (Article 5, paragraph 1, letter f) and 9 of the Regulation) and security obligations related to processing (Article 32 of the Regulation)  
First of all, regarding the existence of technical and organizational measures for managing second-level screenings in compliance with personal data regulations, from the elements acquired during the investigative activity, it appears that the Company, at the time of the event that occurred, had not adopted measures, technical and organizational, adequate to prevent the event, in violation of the security obligations referred to in Article 32 of the Regulation. In fact, although the health company, with reference to the document "Operational Guidelines for the Management of Mammograms and First and Second Level Mammographic Screening Exams" dated XX, attached to the response provided to the Authority, stated that "the employee in charge of calling the patient for the booking of a second-level screening was aware of the standardized procedure to be used, implemented for years (and) (...) (made) an error of judgment (...)", only in the new "Operational Guidelines under approval" did it provide that "(...) to schedule second-level appointments (...) only the directly interested party is called (...) (and) no communication is left to other people," also planning training events for staff as organizational measures to prevent similar events from occurring in the future (see response note to the request for information dated XX).  
Furthermore, the Company, having communicated to the mother of the interested party - in the absence of a legitimate legal basis - the necessity for the latter to undergo further diagnostic checks, compared to those already performed, acted in violation of Article 9 of the Regulation. In this regard, it is highlighted that human error is "a symptom of negligence" (see in this sense the document "Guidelines regarding the application and prediction of administrative monetary sanctions for the purposes of Regulation (EU) No. 2016/679" - Adopted on October 3, 2017 - WP 253 - Chapter III "Evaluation Criteria pursuant to Article 83, paragraph 2", paragraph b) "Intentional or negligent nature of the violation") which, although unintentional, can be avoided through adequate and effective measures to comply with diligence obligations.

4.2. Late response to the request for access to data (Article 12 of the Regulation)  
As for the response to the request for the exercise of rights, it appears that the Company did not provide a response to the request for access to the personal data of the interested party, pursuant to Article 15 of the Regulation, within the time limits set by Article 12 of the same Regulation. Specifically, to the request made by the interested party on January 9, followed up on XX, the health company provided a response with a note dated XX (prot. no. XX), following the invitation to comply issued by the Authority, therefore, 162 days after the day it received such request.  
In light of all the above, it is established that the Company, as the data controller, processed the complainant's data in violation of the basic principles of processing referred to in Articles 5, paragraph 1, letter f), and 9 of the Regulation and the security obligations related to processing referred to in Article 32 of the Regulation, and also in violation of Article 15, having failed to provide a response to the request for the exercise of rights submitted by the interested party within the time limits set out in Article 12 of the Regulation (Article 12 of the Regulation in relation to Article 15 of the same Regulation).  
Finally, it is believed that the conditions referred to in Article 17 of the Guarantor's Regulation No. 1/2019 are met.

5. Adoption of the injunction order for the application of the administrative monetary sanction and accessory sanctions (Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i and 83 of the Regulation; Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code).  
The violation of Articles 5, paragraph 1, letter f), 9, 12, in relation to Article 15, and 32 of the Regulation, caused by the conduct carried out by the Company, is subject to the application of the administrative monetary sanction pursuant to Article 83, paragraphs 4 and 5 of the Regulation.  
It should be noted that the Guarantor, pursuant to Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i) and 83 of the Regulation, as well as Article 166 of the Code, has the power to "impose an administrative monetary sanction pursuant to Article 83, in addition to (other) corrective measures referred to in this paragraph, or in lieu of such measures, depending on the circumstances of each individual case," and, in this context, "the Board (of the Guarantor) adopts the injunction order, which also provides for the application of the accessory administrative sanction of its publication, in whole or in part, on the Guarantor's website pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code" (Article 16, paragraph 1, of the Guarantor's Regulation).
Guarantee No. 1/2019).  
In this specific case, the Company has engaged in two distinct behaviors, which must therefore be considered separately for the purpose of quantifying the administrative sanctions to be applied.  
The aforementioned administrative monetary sanctions imposed, depending on the circumstances of each individual case, must be determined in amount while duly taking into account the elements provided for in Article 83, paragraph 2, of the Regulation.  

5.1. In this regard, considering Article 83, paragraph 3, of the Regulation, the violation of the provisions cited in paragraph 4.1 of this decision is subject to the application of the administrative monetary sanction provided for in Article 83, paragraph 5 of the Regulation.  
In light of the above and, in particular, the characteristics of the processing, in relation to the type of operation (communication) and the data subject (patient), and the category of personal data affected by the violation (health data, which, by their nature, are particularly sensitive in terms of fundamental rights and freedoms), it is believed that the level of severity of the violation committed by the Company, based on the elements referred to in Article 83, paragraph 2, letters a) and g) of the Regulation, is high (see European Data Protection Board, “Guidelines 04/2022 on the calculation of administrative fines under the GDPR” of May 23, 2023, point 60).  
That said, having evaluated certain elements as a whole and, in particular, that:  
- the data controller, in order to avoid the recurrence of the event that occurred, has committed to implementing measures aimed at increasing the level of security of the processing carried out and, therefore, to reduce the replicability of the event that occurred (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter f) of the Regulation);  
- the Company has not been the recipient of a previous sanctioning measure in relation to relevant violations (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter e) of the Regulation);  
- the conduct was due to the operator's intention to establish contact with the complainant as soon as possible, considering that her clinical situation presented profiles of particular criticality and severity (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter k) of the Regulation);  
it is deemed appropriate to determine the amount of the monetary sanction provided for by Articles 83, paragraphs 4 and 5 of the Regulation, in the amount of €4,000.00 (four thousand/00) for the violation of Articles 5, paragraph 1, letter f), 9, and 32 of the same Regulation, as an administrative monetary sanction deemed, pursuant to Article 83, paragraph 1, of the Regulation, effective, proportionate, and dissuasive.  

5.2. The distinct conduct that led to a violation of the provision cited in paragraph 4.2 of this decision, namely Article 12 of the Regulation in relation to Article 15 of the same Regulation, entails the application of the administrative monetary sanction provided for in Article 83, paragraph 5, of the Regulation.  
With specific regard to the nature and severity of the violation (Article 83, paragraph 2, letters a) and g) of the Regulation), it is believed that the level of severity of such violation committed by the Company is medium (see the aforementioned “Guidelines 04/2022 on the calculation of administrative fines under the GDPR” of May 23, 2023, point 60). Additionally:  
- the Company has responded to the data subject; even in this circumstance, it has not been the recipient of a previous sanctioning measure in relation to relevant violations (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter e) of the Regulation);  
- the circumstance that the complainant shares a name with an employee of the Company (who is part of the U.O.S.D. that manages cancer screening) has mistakenly led the protocol service operators to transmit the documentation solely to the Personnel Office and not to the Office responsible for managing such requests (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter k) of the Regulation).  
In light of the aforementioned elements, it is deemed appropriate to determine the amount of the monetary sanction provided for in Article 83, paragraph 5, of the Regulation in the amount of €1,000.00 (one thousand/00) for the violation of Article 12 of the Regulation, in relation to Article 15 of the same Regulation, for failing to respond to the request for the exercise of rights submitted by the data subject within the terms of Article 12 of the Regulation, as an administrative monetary sanction deemed, pursuant to Article 83, paragraph 1, of the Regulation, effective, proportionate, and dissuasive.  
In light of the above, the total quantification of the sanction amounts to €5,000.00 (five thousand/00).  

5.3. Considering the type of personal data subject to unlawful processing (health data), the type of operation (communication), and the data subject (patient), it is also believed that pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code and Article 16, paragraph 1, of the Regulation of the Guarantee No. 1/2019, the publication of this section, containing the injunction order, on the website of the Guarantee should proceed.
