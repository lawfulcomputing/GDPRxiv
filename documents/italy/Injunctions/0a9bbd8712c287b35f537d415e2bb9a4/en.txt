**2. Outcome of the investigative activity.**  
Having taken note of what was represented by AUSL Toscana Sud Est in the documentation on record and in the defense memos, it is observed that:  
- **pursuant to the Regulation, "health-related data" are considered personal data relating to the physical or mental health of a natural person, including the provision of health care services, which reveal information regarding their health status (Article 4, paragraph 1, no. 15, of the Regulation). Recital no. 35 of the Regulation further specifies that health-related data "includes information about the natural person collected during their registration to receive health care services";**

- **pursuant to the Regulation, personal data must be "processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently concerning the data subject ('lawfulness, fairness, and transparency')" and must be "adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the purposes for which they are processed ('data minimization')" (Article 5, paragraph 1, letters a) and c) of the Regulation);**

- **data must also be processed "in a manner that ensures adequate security (...), including protection against unauthorized or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage (principle of 'integrity and confidentiality')" (Article 5, paragraph 1, letter f), of the Regulation);**

- **the data controller is required to implement "from the design stage," that is, both at the time of determining the means of processing and at the time of the processing itself, "appropriate technical and organizational measures, [...] aimed at effectively implementing the principles of data protection, such as the**
**Minimization, and to integrate into the processing the necessary guarantees in order to meet the requirements of this regulation and protect the rights of the data subjects** (privacy by design), ensuring that "only the personal data necessary for each specific purpose of processing are processed, by default" (privacy by default) (art. 25, paras. 1 and 2, of the Regulation);

The regulations concerning the protection of personal data provide that information regarding health status cannot be disseminated and can only be communicated to a party other than the data subject based on an appropriate legal basis or at the request of the data subject itself, following a written delegation from the latter (arts. 2 septies, para. 8 and art. 166, para. 2, of the Code and art. 9 of the Regulation);

Since 2014, the Authority, in accordance with art. 22, para. 8 then in force, of the Code, has stated that "the publication of any information from which the state of illness or the existence of pathologies of the data subjects can be inferred is prohibited, including any reference to conditions of disability, physical and/or mental handicap. For this purpose, from the drafting phase of the acts and documents subject to publication, in compliance with the principle of adequate motivation, personal data that is 'excessive', 'irrelevant', 'not indispensable' (and, even less, 'prohibited') should not be included. Otherwise, it is necessary to ensure the relevant obscuring" (cf. Guidelines on the processing of personal data, also contained in acts and administrative documents, carried out for the purposes of publicity and transparency on the web by public entities and other obligated entities, part II, para. 1, of 15.5.2014, doc. web no. 3488002);

From the documentation produced in the records and in light of what is indicated in the order for the dismissal of the criminal proceedings initiated regarding the facts of the appeal adopted by the Judge for preliminary investigations of Arezzo on XX, it is noted that in the aforementioned advertising billboard, the name and surname of the complainant were recognizable as present in the screen of the emergency operator captured in the billboard;

The presence on the aforementioned billboard of the complainant's personal data associated with the healthcare service provided to him in an emergency constitutes a dissemination of data regarding his health since, according to what is stated in the records, the aforementioned billboard was located in the main corridor of patient entry to the Emergency Room, that is, in an area accessible to anyone;

From the documentation in the records, it is therefore established that AUSL Toscana Sud Est has disseminated information regarding the health status of a data subject since in the aforementioned advertising billboard, located in a place accessible to anyone, the name and surname of the complainant were recognizable on an emergency card displayed on the screen of the operator captured in the aforementioned billboard.

**3. Conclusions.**

In light of the evaluations mentioned above, taking into account the statements made by the data controller during the investigation - and considering that, unless the act constitutes a more serious offense, anyone who, in a proceeding before the Guarantor, falsely declares or certifies news or circumstances or produces false acts or documents is liable under art. 168 of the Code "Falsehood in declarations to the Guarantor and interruption of the execution of the tasks or exercise of the powers of the Guarantor" - the elements provided by the data controller in the defensive memoranda do not allow for overcoming the observations notified by the Office with the act of initiation of the procedure, nor do any of the cases provided for in art. 11 of the Guarantor Regulation no. 1/2019 apply.

For these reasons, the illegality of the processing of personal data carried out by AUSL Toscana Sud Est is noted, as detailed in the reasoning, in violation of arts. 5, para. 1, lett. a), c) and f), 9 and 25, paras. 1 and 2, of the Regulation, and art. 2 septies, para. 8 of the Code.

In this context, while AUSL Toscana Sud Est has declared that it has removed the aforementioned advertising billboard, the conditions for the adoption of corrective measures under art. 58, para. 2, of the Regulation do not exist.

**4. Adoption of the injunction order for the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction and accessory sanctions (arts. 58, para. 2, lett. i), and 83 of the Regulation; art. 166, para. 7, of the Code).**

The violation of arts. 5, para. 1, lett. a), c) and f), 9 and 25, paras. 1 and 2, of the Regulation, and art. 2 septies, para. 8 of the Code, caused by the conduct carried out by AUSL Toscana Sud Est, is subject to the application of the administrative pecuniary sanction pursuant to art. 83, paras. 4 and 5, of the Regulation and art. 166, para. 2 of the Code.

It should be noted that the Guarantor, pursuant to arts. 58, para. 2, lett. i), and 83 of the Regulation, as well as art. 166 of the Code, has the power to "impose an administrative pecuniary sanction pursuant to article 83, in addition to [other] corrective measures [provided for in this paragraph], or in lieu of such measures, depending on the circumstances of each individual case" and, in this context, "the Board [of the Guarantor] adopts the injunction order, which also provides for the application of the accessory administrative sanction of its publication, in whole or in part, on the Guarantor's website pursuant to article 166, para. 7, of the Code" (art. 16, para. 1, of the Guarantor Regulation no. 1/2019).

The aforementioned administrative pecuniary sanction imposed, depending on the circumstances of each individual case, must be determined in the amount held.
**Account of the principles of effectiveness, proportionality, and deterrence, as indicated in Article 83, paragraph 1, of the Regulation, in light of the elements provided in Article 85, paragraph 2, of the Regulation, with respect to which it is observed that:**

1. The Authority became aware of the event following the notification of the violation submitted by the AUSL on XX pursuant to Article 33 of the Regulation and the complaint submitted by the interested party on XX (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter h), of the Regulation);
   
2. The dissemination concerns personal data related to the provision of an emergency service of an interested party present on a single billboard placed in a location accessible to the public (Article 83, paragraph 2, letters a) and g), of the Regulation);
   
3. The aforementioned billboard was displayed for a few weeks in a location that, although open to the public (main entrance corridor for patients in the Emergency Room), due to the concurrent pandemic period, was intended for the transit of only Covid-19 negative patients and was closed to their companions (Article 83, paragraph 2, letters a) and g), of the Regulation);
   
4. The facts under examination occurred during the ongoing state of health emergency due to Covid-19 (Article 83, paragraph 2, letters a) and g), of the Regulation);
   
5. The AUSL promptly cooperated to remedy the violation by removing the aforementioned billboard and warning the companies involved in its creation to delete the personal data of the complainant, promptly informing the complainant (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter f) of the Regulation);
   
6. The AUSL has implemented mandatory training courses for staff for the year 2022 and planned a new training cycle for the year 2023, and in December 2022 conducted the Training Course "Data Processing - with particular reference to images - in the Health Authority," which was attended by numerous operators of various profiles (technical, administrative, health), with a new edition planned for 2023 (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter c), of the Regulation);
   
7. Following the facts subject to investigation, the AUSL has prepared a company procedure that regulates the establishment of a centralized oversight at the company level regarding the creation of materials using images on any medium (billboards, brochures, videos, photos, posts, etc.) aimed at publication/dissemination within the institutional initiatives of the Company for information, communication, education/promotion of health (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter c), of the Regulation);
   
8. Although the AUSL has been sanctioned for previous violations of the regulations on the processing of personal data, these do not concern the dissemination of health data (Article 83, paragraph 2, letter e) of the Regulation).

**In light of the aforementioned elements, evaluated as a whole, it is deemed appropriate to determine the amount of the administrative fine provided for in Article 83, paragraph 5, letter a), of the Regulation, in the amount of €20,000 (twenty thousand) for the violation of Articles 5, paragraph 1, letters a), c), and f), 9 and 25, paragraphs 1 and 2, of the Regulation, and Article 2 septies, paragraph 8 of the Code, as an administrative pecuniary sanction deemed, pursuant to Article 83, paragraph 1, of the Regulation, effective, proportionate, and deterrent.**

**It is also considered that the accessory sanction of publication on the website of the Authority of this provision should be applied, as provided for in Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code and Article 16 of the Authority's Regulation No. 1/2019, also in consideration of the type of personal data subject to unlawful processing.**

**Finally, it is noted that the conditions set forth in Article 17 of Regulation No. 1/2019 concerning internal procedures with external relevance, aimed at carrying out the tasks and exercising the powers entrusted to the Authority, are met.**

**ALL OF THE ABOVE, THE AUTHORITY declares the unlawfulness of the processing of personal data carried out by the Local Health Authority of Tuscany South East for the violation of Articles 5, paragraph 1, letters a), c), and f), 9 and 25, paragraphs 1 and 2, of the Regulation, and Article 2 septies, paragraph 8 of the Code as stated in the reasoning.**

**ORDERS**

pursuant to Articles 58, paragraph 2, letter i), and 83 of the Regulation, as well as Article 166 of the Code, the Local Health Authority of Tuscany South East, VAT and Tax Code 02236310518, represented by its legal representative pro tempore, to pay the sum of €20,000 (twenty thousand) as an administrative pecuniary sanction for the violations indicated in this provision; it is noted that the offender, pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 8, of the Code, has the option to settle the dispute by payment, within 30 days, of an amount equal to half of the imposed sanction.

**COMMANDS**

the aforementioned Authority, in case of failure to settle the dispute pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 8, of the Code, to pay the sum of €20,000 (twenty thousand) according to the methods indicated in the annex, within 30 days from the notification of this provision, under penalty of the adoption of the consequent executive acts in accordance with Article 27 of Law No. 689/1981.

**DISPOSES**

pursuant to Article 166, paragraph 7, of the Code, the full publication of this provision on the website of the Authority and the annotation of this provision in the internal register of the Authority, as provided for in Article 57, paragraph 1, letter u), of the Regulation, of the violations and the measures adopted in accordance with Article 58, paragraph 2, of the Regulation.

**Pursuant to Article 78 of the Regulation, Articles 152 of the Code, and Article 10 of Legislative Decree No. 150/2011, against this provision it is possible to appeal to the ordinary judicial authority, under penalty of inadmissibility, within thirty days from the date of communication of the provision itself or within sixty days if the appellant resides abroad.**
