This can be done through a formal request, which will initiate an investigation into the matter. Additionally, individuals may also report such incidents to law enforcement authorities, especially when they involve criminal behavior such as defamation or incitement to violence. It is crucial for victims to be aware of their rights and the available channels for seeking redress in these situations. 

In summary, while the Garante plays a significant role in addressing issues related to personal data and privacy, victims of online abuse should also consider involving law enforcement to tackle more serious offenses.

For the last two cases, a special procedure is provided which, in summary, allows for the blocking of content uploads in the first case and the request for deletion of the same in the second, within 48 hours. These are two particularly important protections, as they focus, more on the protection of the victim and the prevention of further harm that the permanence, online, of harmful content can cause, rather than on the sanctioning of the perpetrator, which is primarily the responsibility of the judicial authority. An innovative form of "soft law" that the web increasingly needs.
