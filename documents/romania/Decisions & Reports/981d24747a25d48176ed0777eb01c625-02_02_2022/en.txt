**February 2, 2022**  
**Conduct of the Conference on January 28, 2022**  

In celebration of the European Data Protection Day, the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing organized an online conference on January 28, 2022, entitled “Key Findings in the Activity of the National Supervisory Authority,” which was attended by important representatives from various ministries, central public institutions, as well as numerous representatives from the most significant professional associations and unions, private companies from various sectors, and the media. The large and diverse participation recorded at this meeting highlights the interest and concern for the high-standard application of the principles and rules for the protection of personal data.  

At the opening of the event, the President of the National Supervisory Authority, Ms. Ancuța Opre, emphasized the significance of this celebration in the context of the Romanian Parliament's ratification, through Law no. 290/9.12.2021, of the Protocol amending Convention 108.  

Relevant aspects identified in the activities of operators during the year 2021 were also highlighted, and they were specifically recommended to intensify the necessary internal organizational measures, including through the periodic training of their employees, in relation to the specific nature of the operator's activity and their correlated responsibilities.  

The first speech was delivered by Mr. Alexandru Rafila, Minister of Health, who underscored the importance given to the protection of personal data, especially in the pandemic context, and the ministry's concerns in identifying appropriate solutions during this period.  

Other distinguished guests who delivered speeches included high-level representatives from the Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of Education, the institution of the Ombudsman, and the General Inspectorate of the Romanian Police, highlighting specific aspects of the activities of these authorities.  

During the event, representatives from the academic environment, the public sector, as well as the private sector presented topics that generated particular interest regarding the effective exercise of the right of access, data processing at the level of websites and mobile applications, considerations regarding privacy in the digital age, international data transfers, as well as new methods used to affect personal data.  

At the conclusion of the conference, the President of the Authority thanked the guests for their involvement and expressed the institution's willingness to collaborate with the public and private sectors to ensure effective compliance with the right to personal data protection.
