June 22, 2021  
EDPB Plenary - June  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on June 18, 2021, the following documents were primarily adopted:  
1. Joint Opinion with EDPS regarding the draft Regulation of the European Commission on establishing harmonized regulations on artificial intelligence;  
2. Recommendations No. 1/2020 on additional transfer measures (final version)
