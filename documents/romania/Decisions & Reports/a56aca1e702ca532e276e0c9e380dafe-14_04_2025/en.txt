**April 14, 2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in March 2025, an investigation into the operator Office Nova Concept SRL and found a violation of the provisions of art. 12 para. (1)-(4), in relation to art. 21 and art. 17 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was sanctioned with a fine of 4,977.4 lei (equivalent to 1,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint submitted by an individual who reported that the operator had installed cameras directed towards the entrance of their residence without their consent.  
The data subject requested, via email, that the operator reorient the cameras so that images from their personal residence would no longer be captured.  
Additionally, the deletion of images concerning individuals who used the entrance of the building was also requested.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator did not provide a timely written response to the data subject's request, thus violating the provisions of art. 12 para. (1)-(4) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, in relation to art. 21 and art. 17 of the same European legislative act.  
Furthermore, the operator was ordered to take the following corrective measures:  
1. To provide an appropriate response to the data subject's request at the contact details indicated by them, in accordance with the provisions of art. 21 and art. 17, correlated with art. 12 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679;  
2. To ensure compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 regarding the processing of personal data operations, by adopting the necessary technical and organizational measures, including proper training of the designated personnel for this purpose, so that the operator is capable of correctly managing and appropriately responding to requests through which data subjects exercise their rights, within the deadlines and according to the conditions provided by art. 12-23 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. Additionally, to ensure complete, transparent, and accessible information for data subjects, according to art. 12-14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as applicable, at all locations where video surveillance cameras are used;  
3. To ensure compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 regarding the processing of personal data operations, so that the video surveillance cameras installed at the address indicated by the data subject do not capture images from their property without their consent.
