**May 14, 2024**  
**Competition for 7 Vacant Positions at ANSPDCP**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing is organizing a competition for the filling of 7 vacant execution positions for the activity of personal data protection.  
The competition schedule, general and specific participation conditions, stages of the competition, as well as other detailed information regarding the competition can be found on the Authority's website in the section "Job Vacancies/Competitions" or by accessing the link https://www.dataprotection.ro/?page=Anunt_concurs_ocupare_posturi_2024.
