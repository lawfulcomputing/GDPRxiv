**03.03.2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in February 2025, an investigation into the operator BEKO ROMÂNIA SA and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b) and d) and Article 32 paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was sanctioned with a fine amounting to 49,766.00 lei (equivalent to 10,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following a notification of a personal data security breach, in accordance with the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
During the investigation, it was found that an unauthorized person, who exploited a programming vulnerability, illegally accessed the operator's website that contained its customer database.  
Thus, the individual in question had access to the personal data of a large number of the operator's customers, namely: first name, last name, phone number, email address, residence, product details.  
As a result, it was established that BEKO ROMÂNIA SA did not implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, neither at the time of establishing the means of processing nor at the time of the processing itself.  
It was also found that the operator did not carry out testing, evaluation, and periodic assessment of the effectiveness of the technical and organizational measures to ensure the security of the processing.  
This situation led to unauthorized access by a third party to personal data, violating the provisions of Article 25 paragraph (1) in conjunction with Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b) and d) and Article 32 paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  

Under Article 58 paragraph (2) letter d) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the operator was ordered to implement, from a technical and organizational perspective, a data volume analysis system within the IT infrastructure of BEKO ROMÂNIA SA, including performing backups of this data.  
It is noted that the operator has paid the imposed fine.
