January 28, 2024 - EUROPEAN DATA PROTECTION DAY  

On January 28, 2024, the European Data Protection Day is celebrated by all member states of the Council of Europe, marking 43 years since the adoption in 1981 of Convention 108 for the protection of individuals with regard to the automated processing of personal data.  
The celebration of this day each year through a series of specific events is aimed at increasing public awareness regarding the necessity of protecting personal data, under the auspices of highlighting the importance of the right to privacy and the protection of personal data.  
To mark the European Data Protection Day, the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing will organize, on January 26, 2024, an online conference titled "The Role and Effectiveness of the Data Protection Officer's Activity."  
This event has primarily invited representative central public authorities and institutions, the most important associations and professional unions, chambers of commerce, companies, and media representatives, with the purpose of analyzing the theoretical and practical aspects regarding the status conferred to the data protection officer within public institutions and companies, as well as their role in promoting compliance with the rules for the use of personal data.  
The marking of the European Data Protection Day will also be supported by the national television station TVR and the Bucharest Transport Company, which will broadcast an informative clip regarding the General Data Protection Regulation (public interest message), produced by ANSPDCP.  

In this context, we mention that our institution has initiated collaborations with the Ministry of Internal Affairs and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, in order to identify appropriate means aimed at increasing the level of training of the personnel of these institutions, including regarding the Schengen acquis.  
On this occasion, we note that synthetic information regarding the activity of the Authority is presented, as every year, in the Brochure dedicated to the European Data Protection Day 2024, available, along with the related leaflet, at the address https://www.dataprotection.ro/?page=Materiale_informative&lang=ro
