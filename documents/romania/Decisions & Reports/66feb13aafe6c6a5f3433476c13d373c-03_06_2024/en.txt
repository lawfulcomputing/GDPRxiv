03.06.2024  
EDPB Plenary – May 2024  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) held online in May 2024, the following documents were primarily adopted:  
1. Opinion No. 11/2024 on the use of facial recognition for streamlining passenger flows at airports (compatibility with Article 5 (1) e) and f), Article 25, and Article 32 of the GDPR);  
2. Declaration No. 2/2024 on access to financial data and the legislative package on payment services.
