08.07.2021  
EDPB Plenary - July  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on July 7, 2021, the following documents were primarily adopted:  
- Guideline No. 2/2021 on Virtual Voice Assistance (final version after public consultation)  
- Guideline No. 7/2020 on the concepts of controller and processor (final version after public consultation)  
- Guideline on Codes of Conduct as a Transfer Tool, open for public consultation  

The purpose of the Guideline on Codes of Conduct as a Transfer Tool is to clarify the application of the provisions of Article 40(3) in conjunction with Article 46(2)(e) of the General Data Protection Regulation, including by presenting specific examples.
