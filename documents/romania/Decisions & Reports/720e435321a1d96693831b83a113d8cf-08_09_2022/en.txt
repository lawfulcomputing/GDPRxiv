**08.09.2022**  
**A New Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation in August of the current year regarding the operator Realmedia Network SA (imobiliare.ro), during which it found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and paragraph (2) of the General Data Protection Regulation. As such, Realmedia Network SA has been fined in the amount of 39,272 lei, equivalent to 8,000 EURO.  

Following information from the online environment, our institution has self-reported regarding a possible breach of security of personal data that occurred at Realmedia Network SA. During the investigation conducted, it was found that the breach of data processing security occurred at the level of a service used by the operator for the operation of the platform imobiliare.ro. This situation led to the unauthorized disclosure or unauthorized access to the following personal data: first name, last name, phone number, email address, postal address, personal identification number, signature, copies of identity cards, including identification codes, position/quality, bank data, information included in land registry extracts/cadastral sketches, property titles, profile images of users, which affected a total of 194,309 data subjects.  

Thus, the operator Realmedia Network SA was sanctioned with a fine for violating the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and paragraph (2) of the General Data Protection Regulation, as it did not implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security corresponding to the risk of processing.
