**15.05.2025**  
**EDPB Plenary**  

During the Plenaries of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB), held online in April and May 2025, the following relevant documents were primarily adopted:  

**Opinion No. 6/2025** regarding the EC proposal on the extension of the validity of decisions made by the United Kingdom concerning the adequate level of protection in the application of the GDPR and Directive 2016/680;  
The Opinion addresses the European Commission's proposal to extend the two decisions of the United Kingdom regarding the adequacy of the level of protection under the GDPR and Directive 2016/680, which are set to expire on June 27, 2025. This Opinion refers only to the proposal for a six-month extension of these decisions regarding the adequacy of the level of protection, until December 27, 2025. This will provide the European Commission with sufficient time to assess the updated legal framework of the United Kingdom following its adoption.  

**Opinion No. 7/2025** regarding the draft decision of the EC under the GDPR concerning the adequate level of protection of the European Patent Organization;  
A decision regarding the adequacy of the level of protection is a key mechanism in EU data protection legislation, which allows the European Commission to determine whether a third country or international organization provides an adequate level of data protection.  

**EDPB Annual Report 2024** – which provides an overview of the Committee's activities in the previous year;  

**Adoption of the EDPB Strategy for the period 2024-2027**, which established the priorities of the European Committee's activities.  

**Guideline 2/2025 on the processing of personal data through blockchain technologies** (currently under public consultation until June 9, 2025)
