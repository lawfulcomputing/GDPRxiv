**July 26, 2021**  
**Press Release**  

The National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing has issued Decision no. 20 dated June 24, 2021, regarding the approval of additional requirements for the accreditation of certification bodies under Article 43 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
This was published in the Official Gazette of Romania, number 689, dated July 12, 2021, Part I, and entered into force on the date of publication.  
Through this decision, additional requirements for the accreditation of certification bodies are established under Article 43 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
The decision published on the Authority's website in the section "Legislation/Decisions ANSPDCP/Published" can also be consulted here.
