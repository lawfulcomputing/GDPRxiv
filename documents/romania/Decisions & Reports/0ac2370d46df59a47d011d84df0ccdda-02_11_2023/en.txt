November 2, 2023  
EDPB Plenary – October 2023  

During the Plenaries of the European Data Protection Board held on October 17 and October 27, 2023, the following documents were primarily adopted:  
Opinion No. 2/2023 of the EDPB and EDPS regarding the proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on the establishment of the digital euro;  
Binding Decision of the EDPB, pursuant to Article 66(2) of the GDPR.
