07/04/2022  
Fine for Violation of GDPR  

The National Supervisory Authority completed an investigation in March 2022 at the Homeowners' Association located at Str. Soporului 17, Cluj-Napoca municipality, to which it imposed a fine due to the fact that this operator did not provide the information requested by our institution, thus violating the provisions of Article 83 paragraph (5) letter e) correlated with the provisions of Article 58 paragraph (1) letters (a) and (e) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
As such, the homeowners' association was sanctioned with an administrative fine in the amount of 2,474.5 lei (equivalent to 500 EURO).  
The investigation was conducted following a complaint made by a data subject, who reported that the operator disclosed images of them from the video surveillance system managed by the association on the Facebook group of the building where they reside.  
Since the operator did not respond to our institution's requests, although it confirmed receipt of them, it was sanctioned with a fine.  
Furthermore, it was ordered to transmit all requested information to the supervisory authority within 5 working days from the communication of the minutes.
