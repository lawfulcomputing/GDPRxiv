30.06.2022  
A new sanction for violation of GDPR  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation into the operator Continental Automotive Romania SRL and found a violation of the provisions of art. 24 and art. 32 para. (1) letter d) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
The operator Continental Automotive Romania SRL has been fined in the amount of 9,886.00 lei (equivalent to 2,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following the operator's notification in 2022, under the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation, of a personal data security breach.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator discovered at its headquarters in Timișoara, outside the official camera system, a number of 135 unapproved surveillance cameras (not configured according to internal policies) connected to unofficial camera systems, most of which were located in the middle of the production area.  
As such, the National Supervisory Authority found that the operator had not established adequate technical and organizational measures and had not periodically evaluated the technical and organizational measures implemented to determine their effectiveness in ensuring adequate security for the processing of video images of employees and to prevent unauthorized processing, in violation of the policies established by the operator/work instructions.
