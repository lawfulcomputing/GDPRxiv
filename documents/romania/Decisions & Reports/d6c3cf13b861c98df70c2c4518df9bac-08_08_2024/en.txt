August 8, 2024  
Competition for 5 Vacant Positions at ANSPDCP  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing is organizing a competition for the filling of 5 vacant execution positions, for activities related to the protection of personal data and activities within the Economic Service.  
The competition calendar, general and specific participation conditions, stages of the competition, as well as other detailed information regarding the competition can be found on the Authority's website in the "Announcements for Vacant Positions/Competitions" section or by accessing the link https://www.dataprotection.ro/?page=Anunt_concurs_august_2024.
