15.06.2022  
Sanction for Violation of GDPR  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation in May 2022 regarding the operator S.C. Wine Point S.R.L. and found a violation of the provisions of art. 32 para. (1) lit. b) of the General Regulation on Data Protection.  
As such, the operator S.C. Wine Point S.R.L. was sanctioned with a fine amounting to 14,826.90 lei (equivalent to 3,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint from a data subject who reported that they received a commercial message from the operator via email, which was also addressed to other individuals, with their email addresses visible to all 810 recipients.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator had not implemented sufficient technical and organizational measures to ensure the confidentiality of the personal data processed by sending a promotional commercial message from the operator's email address to the email addresses of several data subjects.  
Consequently, S.C. Wine Point S.R.L. unlawfully disclosed personal data, which constituted a violation of the provisions of art. 32 para. (1) lit. b) of the General Regulation on Data Protection.  
Additionally, the operator S.C. Wine Point S.R.L. was recommended to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures in the case of remote transmission of personal data.
