**July 21, 2022**  
**EDPB Plenary – July 2022**  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on July 12, 2022, the joint Opinion of the EDPB and EDPS regarding the European Commission's proposal for a Regulation on the European Health Data Space was primarily adopted. This document recommended strengthening the rights of data subjects through clear provisions and drew attention to the subsequent use of data.  
More information is available at: https://edpb.europa.eu/news
