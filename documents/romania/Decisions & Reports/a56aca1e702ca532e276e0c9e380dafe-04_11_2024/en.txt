**04.11.2024**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed an investigation in October 2024 regarding the operator Blackcab Systems SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 12 paragraph (3) and Article 15 paragraphs (1) and (3) in relation to the provisions of Article 83 paragraph (5) letter b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was sanctioned with a fine amounting to 4,975.9 lei (equivalent to 1,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint submitted by an individual who reported a possible violation of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
The data subject, a client of the operator, addressed a request regarding the right of access and requested a copy of their personal data.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator Blackcab Systems SRL did not provide evidence that it responded to the applicant's request, thus violating the provisions of Article 12 paragraph (3) and Article 15 paragraphs (1) and (3) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
Additionally, under Article 58 paragraph (2) letter c) of Regulation (EU) 679/2016, a corrective measure was also ordered, consisting of taking the necessary measures to ensure compliance with the rights of data subjects provided by the Regulation and communicating a response to the request of the data subject, through which they exercised their right of access, including the transmission of a copy of their data, in accordance with Article 15 paragraphs (1) and (3) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
