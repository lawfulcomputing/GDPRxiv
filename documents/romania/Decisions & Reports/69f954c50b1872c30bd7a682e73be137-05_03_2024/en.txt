**March 5, 2024**  
**A New Sanction for Non-Compliance with GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in February of the current year, an investigation into the operator EURO MINI STORAGE ROMANIA SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b) and d), in conjunction with Article 32 paragraph (2) and Article 24 paragraph (1) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).  
As such, the operator was sanctioned with an administrative fine of 24,884.50 lei, equivalent to 5,000 EURO.  
The investigation was initiated following the operator's notification of a personal data security breach under the General Data Protection Regulation.  
The data security breach occurred as a result of a cyber attack faced by the operator, which also generated an incident of violation of the availability and confidentiality of personal data at the level of the server used in its IT infrastructure, with EURO MINI STORAGE ROMANIA SRL's operations being unavailable for a period of several weeks.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator had not implemented appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security commensurate with the risk presented by the processing, particularly generated by the destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or unauthorized access to personal data.  
This fact led to a breach of confidentiality through unauthorized access to the IT infrastructure used by EURO MINI STORAGE ROMANIA SRL, to certain personal data of a significant number of clients, as well as affecting the availability of this data for a period of several weeks.  

At the same time, under Article 58 paragraph (2) letter d) of the GDPR, a corrective measure was ordered against EURO MINI STORAGE ROMANIA SRL to implement monitoring/logging systems for access to the IT infrastructure used for processing personal data, which must include a minimum retention period for access logs of 30 days, including the introduction of a backup process for these logs.
