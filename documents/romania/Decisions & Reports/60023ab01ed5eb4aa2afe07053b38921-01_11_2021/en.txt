**01.11.2021**  
**Fine for GDPR Violation**  

The National Supervisory Authority completed on  
October 14, 2021, an investigation into the operator IKEA  
ROMÂNIA SA, following which it was found that there was a violation  
of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and paragraph (2)  
of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
As such, the operator was sanctioned  
administratively with a fine in the amount of 4948.80 lei  
(the equivalent of 1,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated as a result of the transmission  
by IKEA ROMÂNIA SA to the National Supervisory Authority for  
Personal Data Processing of a notification of a personal data  
security breach.  
Thus, according to the mentions in the notification form,  
IKEA ROMÂNIA SA organized a drawing contest in which the children of  
IKEA Family members participated. Participants uploaded on the  
online platform dedicated to members their own drawings, along with  
the participation forms, which contained their personal data as well as  
that of their parents/legal guardians, including their consent. For the  
voting of the best drawing, the children's drawings were published  
in error on the online platform, along with the personal data  
contained in the participation forms.  
At the time of the investigation, it was found that  
the security incident led to the unauthorized disclosure of  
the personal data of IKEA Family members (the names,  
surnames, and ages of minor individuals, the names,  
surnames, city, country, email, IKEA Family member number,  
and the handwritten signature of the parent/legal guardian), on the  
online platform dedicated to IKEA Family members in Romania,  
accessible only to them, for approximately 40 hours, affecting a  
total of 114 individuals (half of whom were minors).  

As such, it was found that this incident led to  
the compromise of data confidentiality, in  
violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and  
paragraph (2) of the GDPR.  
In this context, we emphasize that, according to  
recital 38 of the GDPR, "Children need specific  
protection of their personal data, as they may be less  
aware of the risks, consequences, guarantees in question, and  
their rights regarding the processing of personal data. This  
specific protection should apply, in particular, to the  
use of children's personal data for marketing purposes or for  
creating personality or user profiles and to the  
collection of personal data concerning children when  
using services directly offered to children."
