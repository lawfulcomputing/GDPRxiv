**October 28, 2024**  
**Sanction for Non-Compliance with GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing completed, in September 2024, an investigation into the operator Vodafone Romania S.A. and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).  
As such, the operator was sanctioned with a fine amounting to 24,870.5 lei, equivalent to 5,000 EURO.  
The investigation was initiated following the receipt of complaints from a data subject, in which they reported the disclosure of several email addresses (including their personal address) during the transmission of communications regarding the change of the account manager assigned to certain legal entity clients in the Vodafone Romania S.A. portfolio. The messages were sent via electronic mail in two different months, to several representatives of the clients and to multiple individuals.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator Vodafone Romania S.A. did not adopt sufficient technical and organizational measures to ensure the confidentiality of the processed personal data.  
Since no measures were taken to conceal the email addresses of the recipients, for example by selecting the “BCC” (blind carbon copy) option, an unauthorized disclosure of the email addresses to other recipients occurred, thus violating the obligations imposed by Article 32 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
Additionally, based on Article 58 paragraph (2) letter d) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, a corrective measure was ordered to reevaluate the implemented security measures, in order to ensure an adequate level of security relative to the risk of processing, particularly regarding the training of individuals processing data under its authority (employees or collaborators), and the regular verification of compliance with the instructions provided to them.
