**January 17, 2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in December 2024, an investigation into the operator DELIVERY SOLUTIONS S.A. and found a violation of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was fined in the amount of 9,954 Lei (equivalent to 2,000 Euro).  
The investigation was initiated following the notification by the operator DELIVERY SOLUTIONS S.A. of a personal data security breach, according to the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
Thus, the operator reported a security incident involving the exposure of personal data online.  
Additionally, within the same reported incident, it was reported that the web interface of an internal application of the operator was accessed by fraudulently obtaining valid credentials (username and password), which allowed for the viewing of data.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator did not take sufficient security measures, which allowed personal data to be accessed and viewed illegally by unauthorized persons through accessing the web interface of the internal application used by the operator.  
In this context, a number of categories of data were accessed illegally and affected, such as: first and last name, postal address, phone number, and email address.  
Thus, since sufficient technical and organizational measures were not taken to ensure an appropriate level of security corresponding to the risk of processing, including the ability to ensure data confidentiality, it was found that there was a violation of the provisions of Article 24, Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, and the operator was sanctioned with a fine.  
At the same time, the following corrective measures were ordered for the operator to avoid similar security incidents:  
- Adoption of an updated password policy.  
- Implementation of an access register, so that unwanted access can be recorded.  
- Periodic security audits to identify and remedy vulnerabilities.
