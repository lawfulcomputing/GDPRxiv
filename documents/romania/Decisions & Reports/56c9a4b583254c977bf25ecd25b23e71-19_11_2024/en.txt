**November 19, 2024**  
**Training Sessions for Border Police**  
**November 4-7, 2024**  

During the period of November 4-7, 2024, representatives of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing conducted four training sessions for the personnel of the Border Police in the field of data protection, in the context of our country's accession to the Schengen air and maritime space.  

In these seminars organized by the General Inspectorate of the Border Police, at the initiative of ANSPDCP, information was presented regarding the status and competencies of ANSPDCP, the main rules for the processing of personal data established by Law no. 363/2018, in relation to the specific activities of border police officers. The importance of informing about the rights of data subjects at border crossing points was highlighted, in accordance with the applicable legal provisions.  

Additionally, relevant aspects regarding the assurance of confidentiality and security in data processing were presented, along with practical examples from the control activities in the field of data protection. On this occasion, we note the significant participation in these useful training sessions for the professional development of IGPF personnel.
