**April 23, 2024**  
**A New Sanction for Non-Compliance with GDPR**  
                   
The National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing has completed an investigation into the operator ALPHA BANK ROMANIA SA and has found a violation of the provisions of Article 29, Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b), paragraph (2), and paragraph (4) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).  
As such, the operator has been sanctioned with an administrative fine in the amount of 9,950.60 lei, equivalent to 2,000 EURO.  
The investigation was initiated following the operator's notification of a personal data security breach under the General Data Protection Regulation.  
The data security breach occurred due to the fact that one of the record-keeping systems of ALPHA BANK ROMANIA SA was improperly managed by an employee of the operator.  
During the investigation, it was found that ALPHA BANK ROMANIA SA had not implemented appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of confidentiality and security commensurate with the risk presented by the processing and had not taken sufficient measures to ensure that any natural person acting under the authority of the operator and having access to personal data processes it only at its request.  
This fact led to a violation of the confidentiality of personal data through unauthorized disclosure and unauthorized access to certain personal data of a specific number of clients.
