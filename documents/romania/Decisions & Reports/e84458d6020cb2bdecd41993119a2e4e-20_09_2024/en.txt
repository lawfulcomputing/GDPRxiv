EDPB Plenary – September 2024  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB), held online on September 17, 2024, it was decided to initiate the update of the Guidelines on the application of administrative fines for the purposes set out by the GDPR (WP 253).  
At this meeting, representatives of the European Commission presented the second Report regarding the application of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), published on July 25, 2024.  
More information is available at:
