**02.04.2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in March 2025, an investigation into the operator BINBOX GLOBAL SERVICES S.R.L. and found a violation of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b), d) and Article 32 paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was sanctioned with: a fine in the amount of 14,930.70 lei, equivalent to 3,000 euros, for violating the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b), d) and Article 32 paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
The investigation was initiated following the submission by the operator BINBOX GLOBAL SERVICES S.R.L. of a notification of a personal data security breach, in accordance with the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
During the investigation, it was found that, following a cyber attack, the operator's IT infrastructure was accessed and encrypted, which led to unauthorized disclosure or unauthorized access to personal data.  
As such, it was found that the operator had not implemented appropriate technical and organizational measures, including the ability to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and continuous resilience of processing systems and services.  
In this context, there was unauthorized disclosure or unauthorized access to personal data of a significant number of data subjects, clients of the operator.  
The operator has paid the established contravention fine.
