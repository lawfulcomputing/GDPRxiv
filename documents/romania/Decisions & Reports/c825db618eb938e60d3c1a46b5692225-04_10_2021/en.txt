04.10.2021  
EDPB Plenary – 24.09.2021  
  
During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on September 24, 2021, Opinion No. 32/2021 was adopted regarding the draft Decision of the European Commission on the recognition, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679, of an adequate level of protection for personal data in South Korea.  
More information is available at:  
https://edpb.europa.eu/news/news/2021/edpb-adopts-opinion-draft-south-korea-adequacy-decision_en
