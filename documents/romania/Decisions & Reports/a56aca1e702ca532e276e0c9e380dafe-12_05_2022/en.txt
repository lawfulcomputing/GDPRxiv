**12.05.2022**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation in April 2022 regarding the operator LORIS FUEL SHOP SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 29 and Article 32 paragraph (4) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).  
The operator LORIS FUEL SHOP SRL was fined in the amount of 4,941.3 lei, equivalent to 1,000 EURO.  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint in which the petitioner reported the publication on Facebook of images in which he was captured, originating from the monitor belonging to a video surveillance system installed at a fuel station in Harghita County.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator LORIS FUEL SHOP SRL, as the data controller, did not adopt sufficient technical and organizational measures to ensure the confidentiality of the personal data processed regarding the images recorded through the television system installed at the used stations, particularly concerning the training of the individuals processing data under its authority (employees). This led to the viewing and recording by unauthorized third parties of the images from the video cameras at the work site in Harghita County, which were subsequently disclosed on a social network, thus violating the provisions of Article 29 and Article 32 paragraph (4) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
Additionally, during the investigation, the operator LORIS FUEL SHOP SRL was also subjected to the corrective measure of ensuring compliance with GDPR regarding the operations of personal data processing, by implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures, particularly concerning the training of individuals processing data under its authority (employees or collaborators), by regularly organizing training sessions with them regarding their obligations concerning the processing of personal data through the video system installed at the stations, the verification of access to the recordings stored on the DVR, as well as the rapid detection, management, and reporting of situations of personal data security breaches.
