**April 18, 2024**  
**Preparation of Data Protection Officers in the MAI Structures**  

On March 14, 2024, representatives of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing held a course in Ploiești on personal data protection, aimed at the designated data protection officers within the subordinate structures of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MAI). This course was structured into two modules: “The Rights of Data Subjects in the Context of Personal Data Processing” and “The Organization and Functioning of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing.”  

The participants, who represented structures within the MAI such as the Romanian Gendarmerie, the General Inspectorate of the Border Police, the General Inspectorate for Immigration, the Inspectorate for Emergency Situations, etc., were presented with general and specific aspects such as the rights of data subjects and the supervisory and control duties of the national supervisory authority, in relation to the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation), as well as the provisions of Law no. 363/2018 on the protection of natural persons regarding the processing of personal data by competent authorities for the purpose of preventing, detecting, investigating, prosecuting criminal offenses, or enforcing penalties, educational and safety measures, as well as regarding the free movement of such data.  

On this occasion, practical aspects regarding the application of personal data protection legislation by competent authorities for the purpose of preventing, detecting, investigating, prosecuting criminal offenses, or enforcing penalties, educational and safety measures, as well as regarding the free movement of such data, were also discussed, along with specific aspects concerning the Schengen acquis and its application.  

The representatives of the Authority brought to the attention of the participants the fact that each operator has the obligation to correctly identify the legal grounds for processing in relation to the activities carried out, to conduct assessments following employee training regarding data processing, to carry out impact assessments when necessary, to provide complete information to data subjects, in accordance with GDPR or Law no. 363/2018, depending on each specific situation arising within the MAI structures, as well as to establish specific confidentiality and security measures for the data processing carried out by each structure individually.
