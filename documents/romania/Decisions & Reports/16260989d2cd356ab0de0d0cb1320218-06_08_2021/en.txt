Annual Report of ANSPDCP - 2020  

The activity report for the year 2020 is publicly available on the website of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing in the section "General Information/Public Interest Information/Annual Reports."  
The annual report contains a summary presentation of the Authority's activity, structured in accordance with its main competencies, in the following chapters: Chapter I – Overview, Chapter II – Regulatory, Advisory, Consultation, and Public Information Activities, Chapter III – Monitoring and Control Activities, Chapter IV – Activities in the Field of International Relations, and Chapter V – Economic Management of the Authority.  
This Report contains statistical benchmarks highlighted within each chapter, including comparative graphs with previous years, such as: the total number of investigations carried out or differentiated (ex officio, respectively based on complaints), the number of complaints and notifications received, the number of corrective measures applied (including fines), the number of opinions issued, the number of ongoing litigations, the number of press releases, and the number of corporate rules/IMIs.  
The annual report for 2020 also includes case files related to investigative activities, as well as the most relevant opinions issued, which reflect the complexity of the activities carried out, in relation to the responsibilities of our institution.  
Thus, during the year 2020, the national supervisory authority received a total of 1151 requests for the issuance of opinions regarding various aspects related to the interpretation and application of Regulation (EU) 679/2016, from operators and their representatives, from the public and private sectors, from other entities, as well as from individuals.  
Additionally, our institution issued opinions on a total of 65 draft normative acts developed by public institutions and authorities that involved complex aspects regarding the processing of personal data, based on Article 57 paragraph (1) letter c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
During this period, 60 press releases were issued and posted on the Authority's website.  
Regarding monitoring and control activities, in 2020, the Authority received a total of 5480 complaints, notifications, and reports regarding security incidents, based on which 694 investigations were opened.  
As a result of the investigations, 29 fines were imposed, totaling 892,115.95 lei. At the same time, 64 warnings were issued, and 65 corrective measures were ordered.  
In the context of cooperation with other supervisory authorities in the European Union to ensure mutual assistance, 56 requests regarding the application and compliance with Regulation (EU) 679/2016 were managed during the year 2020.  
The activity report for the year 2020 was transmitted to the competent institutions, in accordance with the provisions of Article 5 paragraph (2) of Law no. 102/2005 regarding the establishment, organization, and functioning of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing.
