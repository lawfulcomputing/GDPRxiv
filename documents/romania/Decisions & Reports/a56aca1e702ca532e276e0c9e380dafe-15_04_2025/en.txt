**15.04.2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing has completed an investigation into the operator United Business Solutions SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and d), paragraph (2) and paragraph (4) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator has been fined in the amount of 9,954 lei, equivalent to 2,000 Euro.  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint from an individual, who reported that the operator United Business Solutions S.R.L., after providing accommodation services, transmitted, without their consent, a fiscal document containing their personal data to a third party.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator communicated via email the personal data of the complainant to a third party.  
It was also found that the operator had not implemented sufficient technical and organizational measures to ensure the confidentiality of the personal data of its client, in their capacity as a beneficiary of accommodation services, which led to the disclosure to a third party of their personal data, namely: name, surname, ID card series and number, information related to the complainant's accommodation.  
At the same time, following the investigation, it was found that the operator had not taken measures to ensure that any individual acting under its authority and having access to personal data processes them only at the request of the operator.  
Furthermore, based on the provisions of Article 58 paragraph (2) letter b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, a corrective measure was ordered against the operator consisting of the development/review of internal procedures and periodic training of all individuals processing data under its authority (employees or collaborators), in order to avoid the illegal or unauthorized disclosure of the data of the data subjects, clients, to third parties.
