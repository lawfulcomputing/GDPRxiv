12.04.2022  
EDPB Plenary – April 2022  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on April 6, 2022, a Declaration of this body was adopted regarding the announcement of the agreement in principle between the European Commission and the USA concerning the conclusion of the new Transatlantic Data Protection Agreement.  
Additionally, on the occasion of the same meeting, a Letter to the European Commission was adopted, concerning the necessity of ensuring the independence of the national data protection authority in Belgium, in relation to certain anticipated legislative changes at the domestic level.
