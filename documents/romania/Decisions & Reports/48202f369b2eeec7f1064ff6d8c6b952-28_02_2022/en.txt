February 28, 2022  
EDPB Plenary – February 2022  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online in February 2022, the final version of Guideline No. 4/2021 on codes of conduct as a transfer tool (version after public consultation) was adopted.  
This document aims to clarify the application of the provisions of Article 40 paragraph (3) and Article 46 paragraph (2) letter e) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
More information is available at: https://edpb.europa.eu/news
