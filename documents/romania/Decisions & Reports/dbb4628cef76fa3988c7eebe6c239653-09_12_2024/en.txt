09.12.2024  
Annual Report of ANSPDCP for 2023  

The activity report for the year 2023 is publicly available on the website of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing in the section "General Information/Public Interest Information/Annual Reports."  
The annual report contains a summary presentation of the Authority's activities, structured in accordance with its main competencies, in the following chapters: Chapter I – Overview, Chapter II – Regulatory, Advisory, Consultation, and Public Information Activities, Chapter III – Monitoring and Control Activities, Chapter IV – Activities in the Field of International Relations, and Chapter V – Economic Management of the Authority.  
The content of this Report includes statistical benchmarks highlighted within each chapter, including comparative graphs with previous years, such as: the number of investigations carried out in total or differentiated (ex officio, respectively based on complaints), the number of complaints, notifications, and reports regarding security incidents, fines imposed, the amount of fines, warnings, corrective measures, the number of opinions issued, advisories issued, lawsuits, press releases.  
Additionally, the activity report for the year 2023 includes presentations of significant cases from the investigations conducted and relevant opinions issued, reflecting the complexity of the activities carried out in relation to the responsibilities of our institution.  
Regarding monitoring and control activities, in 2023 the Authority received a total of 4,772 complaints, notifications, and reports regarding security incidents, based on which 548 investigations were conducted.  

As a result of the investigations, 73 fines were imposed totaling 2,348,265 lei. Additionally, 186 warnings were issued and 138 corrective measures were ordered.  
Furthermore, we specify that during the year 2023, the National Supervisory Authority received 970 requests for the issuance of opinions regarding various aspects related to the interpretation and application of Regulation (EU) 679/2016, from operators and their representatives, from the public and private sectors, from other entities, as well as from individuals.  
Our institution also issued opinions on a total of 103 draft normative acts developed by public institutions and authorities that involved various aspects regarding the processing of personal data, based on Article 57 paragraph (1) letter c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
Regarding representation activities before the courts, the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing managed 155 cases pending in various procedural stages in 2023.  
In this context, the activity report for the year 2023 includes the presentation of several actions that were definitively concluded in 2023, favorably for our institution.  
The activity report for the year 2023 has been transmitted to the competent institutions, in accordance with the applicable legal provisions.
