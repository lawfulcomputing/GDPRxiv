**January 28, 2025**  
**Brochure and Leaflet Dedicated to the European Data Protection Day**  

On the occasion of the celebration of the European Data Protection Day, the National Supervisory Authority prepared informative materials regarding the main elements of its activity, within the Brochure dedicated to this event. The Brochure and Leaflet are available in both Romanian and English on the website www.dataprotection.ro, in the section General Information/Public Interest Information/Informative Materials.
