**May 12, 2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in April 2025, an investigation into the operator CV PRO CONSULT S.R.L. and found a violation of Article 32 paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was fined in the amount of 9,955 Lei, equivalent to 2,000 Euros.  
The investigation was initiated following the notification of a personal data security breach by the operator CV PRO CONSULT S.R.L., in accordance with the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
During the investigation, it was found that, as a result of a cyber attack, access to the operator's own IT infrastructure was compromised and restricted.  
This situation led to the disclosure and unauthorized access to personal data of a significant number of employees of the clients in the operator's portfolio, namely: first name, last name, personal identification number, address, position, salary, bonuses, and other salary rights.  
As such, it was found that the operator had not implemented appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security corresponding to the risk of processing generated, in particular, accidentally or illegally, by the destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, and unauthorized access to personal data transmitted, stored, and processed in other ways.  
At the same time, based on Article 58 paragraph (2) letter d) of the Regulation, a corrective measure was imposed on the operator for periodic verification of compliance with the implemented work procedures regarding the protection of personal data, as well as periodic training of persons acting under its authority, including regarding the risks associated with the processing of personal data.  
The operator has paid the established contravention fine.
