4 years since the application of GDPR – May 25, 2022  

On May 25, 2022, it will be four years since the direct application, in Romania and in the other states of the European Union, of the provisions of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (GDPR).  

On the occasion of celebrating the application of this European legislative act, the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing organizes a series of actions aimed at raising public awareness regarding the new rules for ensuring the protection of personal data and the specific rights that natural persons benefit from.  

Thus, at the headquarters of our institution, on May 25, 2022, at 11:00 AM, a Debate will take place, in which representative associations and professional unions from specific fields of activity will participate. During this event, aspects related to the developments recorded regarding the effective applicability of the new personal data protection rules will be addressed interactively, mainly concerning the data protection officer, impact assessment, compliance with the rights of data subjects, ensuring data security, and notifying security breaches.  

Additionally, to mark this event, with the support of the Bucharest Transport Company STB SA, during the months of May and June 2022, a public interest message (promotional clip) regarding the main aspects regulated by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 will be broadcasted in public transport means.
