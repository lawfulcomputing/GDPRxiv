**28.01.2022**  
**European Data Protection Day**  

On the occasion of the celebration of European Data Protection Day, our institution launches the "Guide on the processing of personal data carried out by homeowners' associations," which can be consulted in the "Useful Information" section on the website of the National Supervisory Authority. This guide is intended to support homeowners' associations in fulfilling their obligations as personal data operators in accordance with Regulation (EU) 679/2016, with the aim of ensuring compliance with the conditions for processing personal data, including the safeguarding of the rights of data subjects. Additionally, in the "Informative Materials" section, the Brochure dedicated to European Data Protection Day has been posted, both in Romanian and in English.
