March 10, 2022  
Press Release  

Considering the cessation of the applicability of Government Decision no. 171/2022 regarding the extension of the state of alert on the territory of Romania and the establishment of measures that apply during this period for the prevention and combating of the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic, we inform you that starting from March 9, 2022, the audience program held at the headquarters of ANSPDCP will resume, from Monday to Friday, between 11:00 AM and 1:00 PM.
