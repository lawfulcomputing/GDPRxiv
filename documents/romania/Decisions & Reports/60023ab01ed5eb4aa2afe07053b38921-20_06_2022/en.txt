**June 20, 2022**  
**Fine for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation in May 2022 regarding SC Interactions Marketing SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
SC Interactions Marketing SRL, as a processor of an operator, was fined in the amount of 4,942.3 lei (equivalent to 1,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following complaints made by a data subject who reported that an operator had sent a commercial message via email to several individuals, thereby disclosing their email addresses.  
During the investigation, it was found that SC Interactions Marketing SRL, as a processor, conducted a campaign for the complained operator, during which it sent a commercial message to the email addresses of 27 individuals, without concealing them, thus allowing the unauthorized disclosure of the email addresses to other recipients.  
As such, SC Interactions Marketing SRL was sanctioned for violating the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) of the General Data Protection Regulation, although, as a processor, it had the obligation to adopt appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the confidentiality of the processed personal data.
