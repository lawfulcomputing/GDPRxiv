**December 11, 2024**  
**EDPB Plenary – December 2024**  

On December 2-3, 2024, the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) took place, during which the following relevant documents were primarily adopted to ensure the proper application of the European Data Protection Regulation:  
- Guideline No. 2/2024 regarding Article 48 of the GDPR, which is open for public consultation until January 27, 2025;  
- Statement No. 6/2024 regarding the second Report of the European Commission on the application of the GDPR.  

Through this statement, the European Data Protection Board emphasizes that, in order to maintain a high level of protection in the context of accelerating complex challenges, it is necessary for national data protection authorities and the EDPB to have additional financial and human resources, as highlighted in the Report of the European Union Agency for Fundamental Rights (FRA).  

More information is available at: https://edpb.europa.eu/news
