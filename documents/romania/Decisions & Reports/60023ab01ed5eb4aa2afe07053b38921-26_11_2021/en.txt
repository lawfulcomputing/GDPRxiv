**26.11.2021**  
**Fine for GDPR Violation**  

The National Supervisory Authority completed in October 2021 an investigation into the operator Valoris Center S.R.L., following which it was found that there was a violation of the provisions of Article 29, Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and paragraph (4) of the General Data Protection Regulation. As such, the operator was sanctioned with a fine amounting to 9,898.00 lei (equivalent to 2,000 EURO).  

The investigation was initiated as a result of a notification of a personal data security breach that was sent by an operator, based on the provisions of Article 33 of the General Data Protection Regulation. According to the information mentioned in the notification form, the breach of personal data processing security occurred because a call center employee of Valoris Center S.R.L. (a person authorized by the operator) mistakenly attached an Excel file containing the data of the clients of the respective operator who have Internet Banking services to a client of the operator.  

During the investigation, it was found that this breach led to unauthorized disclosure or unauthorized access to certain personal data, such as email address, username, personal identification number (CNP), phone number, client name, client code, and client PIN, affecting a total of 11,169 data subjects.  

Considering these aspects, it was established that Valoris Center S.R.L., as the person authorized by the operator, in relation to the provisions of Articles 29 and 32 of the General Data Protection Regulation, did not take adequate measures to ensure that any natural person acting under its authority and having access to personal data processes them only at its request.
