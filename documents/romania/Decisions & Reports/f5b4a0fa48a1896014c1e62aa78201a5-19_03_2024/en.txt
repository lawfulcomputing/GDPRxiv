March 19, 2024  
EDPB Plenary – March 2024  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board held online on March 14, 2024, several current topics were addressed, and the European Union Agency for Fundamental Rights presented the conclusions of the study conducted in the member states regarding the evaluation of the application of the General Data Protection Regulation after 6 years. This study will be publicly presented in June of this year.  
More information is available at:
