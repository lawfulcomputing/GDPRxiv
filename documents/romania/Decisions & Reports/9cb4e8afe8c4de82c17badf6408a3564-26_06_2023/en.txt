26.06.2023  
EDPB Plenary – 20.06.2023  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board held online on June 20, 2023, the following main documents were adopted:  
1. Complaint Form Model, aimed at standardizing practices and usage at the level of EU member states, on a voluntary basis;  
2. Recommendation No. 1/2022 regarding the request for approval and the elements and principles found in the binding corporate rules for the controller (Article 47 GDPR), final version (after public consultation).
