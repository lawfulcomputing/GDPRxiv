**08.09.2023**  
**Information Campaign in Educational Institutions**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing continued this year its communication activities aimed at informing the general public about the rules for processing personal data, in relation to its competencies established by the General Data Protection Regulation, with the purpose of increasing the level of understanding among the general public regarding the risks, rules, and rights in the field of personal data, with a specific focus on activities that address children.  
In this context, we mention that the General Data Protection Regulation creates an additional level of protection when processing the personal data of minors, determined by the fact that they may be less aware of the risks, consequences, and their rights regarding the processing of personal data, including in the online environment.  
In consideration of the above aspects, the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing has deemed it beneficial to increase the level of awareness among students regarding the main concepts and risks involved in the use of personal data.  
As such, our institution initiated at the beginning of the current year a collaboration with the Ministry of Education to identify the most appropriate actions for informing students in secondary and high school education about the general rules for processing personal data, the associated risks, including in the digital environment.  
Thus, it was agreed to prepare two brochures entitled **Data Protection - Secondary Education** and **Data Protection - High School Education**, intended for distribution, primarily in electronic format, by the Ministry of Education in the coordinated educational institutions. These informative materials were created by the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing with the support of representatives from the Ministry of Education, in clear and concise language, adapted and graphically illustrated appropriately for the age of the recipients.  
These brochures are to be distributed in educational institutions by the Ministry of Education starting with the 2023-2024 school year, through the specific means at its disposal.  
On this occasion, we express our hope that these actions will contribute to raising students' awareness regarding the specific concepts of data protection, including the rights they may benefit from and how to exercise them.
