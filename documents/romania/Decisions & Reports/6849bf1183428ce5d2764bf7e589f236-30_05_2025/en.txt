**30.05.2025**  
**Sanction for Non-Compliance with GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing completed, in April 2025, an investigation into the operator AG-BROKER ASIGURARE S.R.L., and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
For the actions committed, the operator was sanctioned with a fine of 24,887 lei (equivalent to 5,000 euros).  
The investigation was initiated following the notification sent by the operator AG-BROKER ASIGURARE S.R.L. regarding a breach of personal data security, according to the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
Thus, the operator notified that, following a cyberattack, the following categories of personal data were affected, namely: personal identification number (CNP), first name, last name, photographs from identity cards of individuals, birth certificates, driving licenses, vehicle registration certificates, email addresses, and phone numbers of a significant number of clients.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator did not have implemented, at the time of the cyberattack, security measures with specific requirements regarding secure access to the network storage equipment that would mitigate the risk of unauthorized access to the aforementioned personal data.  
Therefore, it was established that the operator did not implement adequate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk presented by the processing, generated in particular, accidentally or illegally, by the destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or unauthorized access to the personal data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed, including the ability to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and continuous resilience of processing systems and services, which led to the unauthorized disclosure of the personal data processed by the operator.
