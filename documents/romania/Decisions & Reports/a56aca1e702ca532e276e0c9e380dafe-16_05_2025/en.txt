**May 16, 2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in April 2025, an investigation into the operator ACCOUNTING & AUDIT CONSULTING SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraphs (1) and (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
The operator was sanctioned with a fine of 24,887.00 lei, equivalent to 5,000 EURO.  
The investigation was initiated following a notification of a personal data security breach, in accordance with the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
During the investigation, it was found that unauthorized persons illegally accessed the personal data of the operator's clients' employees, namely: first name, last name, personal identification number, residence, position, salary, bonuses, and other salary rights.  
As a result, it was established that the operator did not implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security corresponding to the risk of processing, particularly against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, and unauthorized access to personal data transmitted, stored, and processed in other ways.  
Additionally, in accordance with the provisions of Article 58 paragraph (2) letter d) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the operator was also subjected to the corrective measure of periodically verifying compliance with the implemented working procedures regarding personal data protection, as well as the periodic training of individuals acting under its authority, including regarding the risks associated with the processing of personal data.  
It is noted that the operator has paid the imposed fine.
