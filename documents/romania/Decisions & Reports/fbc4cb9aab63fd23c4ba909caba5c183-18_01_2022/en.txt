**January 28, 2022 - EUROPEAN DATA PROTECTION DAY**  

The celebration of European Data Protection Day, on January 28, 2022, by all member states of the Council of Europe marks the 41st anniversary of the adoption, in 1981, in Strasbourg, of Convention 108 for the protection of individuals with regard to the automated processing of personal data.  
Recently, through Law no. 290 of December 9, 2021, Romania ratified the Protocol amending Convention 108, which was opened for signature on October 10, 2018, in Strasbourg and signed by our country on June 26, 2020.  

The annual marking of this day serves as an opportunity to draw public attention to the importance of respecting the principles of personal data processing, the right to privacy, and the right to personal data protection, enshrined as fundamental rights.  
To celebrate European Data Protection Day, on January 28, 2022, our institution will organize an online conference titled “Key Findings in the Activity of the National Supervisory Authority,” during which specific aspects of the application of personal data processing rules will be presented and discussed. Authorities and representative central public institutions, the most important associations and professional unions, chambers of commerce, companies, and media representatives have been primarily invited to this event.  

On this occasion, the Guide on Data Processing by Homeowners' Associations, prepared by our institution and intended to clarify specific aspects of this category of operators, will also be launched.  
The celebration of European Data Protection Day will also be supported by the national television station TVR and the Bucharest Transport Society, which will broadcast an informational clip dedicated to the General Data Protection Regulation (public interest message), produced by our institution.  
Additionally, for those interested, summarized information regarding the Authority's activity from the previous year will be presented, as in every year, in the Brochure dedicated to European Data Protection Day, available, along with the related leaflet, at the address www.dataprotection.ro/informatii generale/informatii de interes public/materiale informative.
