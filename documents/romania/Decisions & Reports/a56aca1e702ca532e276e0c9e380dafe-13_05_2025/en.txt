**May 13, 2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing has completed an investigation into the operator Romoffice Construct Holding Ag SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 5 paragraph (1) letter a) and paragraph (2) in relation to Article 6 paragraph (1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator has been fined in the amount of 9,954 lei, equivalent to 2,000 EURO.  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint from an individual, who reported that the operator Romoffice Construct Holding Ag SRL processed their personal data without their consent.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator Romoffice Construct Holding Ag SRL processed personal data belonging to the complainant without legal grounds, specifically the phone number, which was provided during the online completion of information for registration to access a public funding program.  
Additionally, it was found that the operator did not present evidence to demonstrate that it processed the complainant's data legally and used it for the purpose of contacting and communicating information regarding a promotional campaign it was conducting.  
In this context, the processing of personal data was carried out without legal grounds, in violation of the principles of processing related to legality, fairness, and transparency towards the data subject, as provided in Article 5 paragraph 1 letter a) of the GDPR.
