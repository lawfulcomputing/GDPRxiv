**January 28, 2025 - EUROPEAN DATA PROTECTION DAY**  

On January 28, 2025, all member states of the Council of Europe celebrate European Data Protection Day, marking the 44th anniversary of the adoption, in 1981, in Strasbourg, of Convention 108 for the protection of individuals with regard to the automated processing of personal data by the Council of Europe.  
The events organized to commemorate this date by national data protection authorities represent an important opportunity to raise public awareness about the risks of improper use of personal data and the necessity of respecting individuals' right to privacy, especially in the context of the current heightened technological developments.  
In this context, our institution highlights the significant impact that Regulation (EU) 2016/679 has on the effective activities of operators in the public and private sectors, a tool with direct applicability at the level of the European Union and with indirect effects on the legislation of other European states and those from other continents.  
In 2025, considering the special significance of this year for our institution and European Data Protection Day, the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing is organizing, on January 28, 2025, an online conference titled “Anniversary Review: 20 Years since the Establishment of ANSPDCP.”  
This year, our institution celebrates the 20th anniversary of the adoption, on May 3, 2005, of Law no. 102/2005 regarding the establishment, organization, and functioning of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing, republished.  
This special event will be attended by representatives of national central public authorities and institutions, the executive and legislative forums, representatives from academic circles, non-governmental organizations, major professional unions/associations, as well as relevant operators/authorized persons from the public and private sectors.  
The meeting constitutes a timely moment for a substantial exchange of ideas between our institution and operators, related to the specifics of activities in various fields and the implications of using new technologies.  
In this context, we mention that an informative clip dedicated to the General Data Protection Regulation will be broadcast on the national television channel TVR and in the public transport means of the Bucharest Transport Company.  
In consideration of the organized events, we highlight that synthetic information regarding the activities of the Authority is presented, as every year, in the Brochure dedicated to European Data Protection Day, available, along with the related leaflet, at the address www.dataprotection.ro/informatii generale/informatii de interes public/materiale informative.
