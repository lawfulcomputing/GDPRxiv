February 21, 2024  
EDPB Plenary – February 2024  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) held in February 2024, Opinion No. 4/2024 of the EDPB regarding the notion of the main establishment of the controller in the European Union was adopted, in relation to the provisions of Article 4, paragraph 16, letter a) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  

This document states that the place where the central administration of the controller is located in the European Union may be considered the main establishment, in the sense of Article 4, paragraph 16, letter a) of the General Data Protection Regulation, if decisions regarding the purposes and means of processing personal data are made at this establishment and if this establishment has the authority to implement these decisions.
