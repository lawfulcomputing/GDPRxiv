**October 24, 2023**  
**A New Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation in October of this year into the operator Mensajero SRL, during which it found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b) and d) in conjunction with Article 32 paragraph (2) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
As such, the operator was fined in the amount of 14,925.6 lei, equivalent to 3,000 EURO. The sanction was applied following a complaint regarding a possible violation of the security of personal data on the website of the operator Mensajero SRL.  
During the investigation, it was found that the violation of data processing security occurred through access to a link displaying a list of numerous downloadable files that largely contained invoices and warranty certificates for products purchased by the operator's clients.  
This situation led to the unauthorized disclosure of personal data of the operator's clients (both natural and legal persons), such as: name, surname, address, email address, invoice number and date, purchased products, and their value.  
Thus, the operator Mensajero SRL was fined for violating the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b) and d) in conjunction with Article 32 paragraph (2) of the General Data Protection Regulation, as it did not implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security corresponding to the risk of processing.
