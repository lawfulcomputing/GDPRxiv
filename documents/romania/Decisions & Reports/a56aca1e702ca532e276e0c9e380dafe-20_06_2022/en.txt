**20.06.2022**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed, on  
27.05.2022, an investigation into the operator  
Association of Owners Aviation Park, following  
which it was found that the provisions of the  
General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) were violated,  
the operator being sanctioned with a fine as follows:  
- a fine of 9,885.80 lei, equivalent to  
2,000 EURO for violating the provisions of art. 5 para.  
(1) lit. a) and c) and para. (2) in relation to art.  
6 of the GDPR, as the operator excessively processed  
personal data (name, surname, series and number of the  
identity document, destination, arrival time,  
departure time, observations) of deliverers  
and/or couriers as data subjects,  
without a justified legal basis related to the purpose  
of processing (access control in the residential complex)  
and without providing evidence that it ensures  
correct and complete information to the data subjects,  
as well as that the processed data is adequate,  
relevant, and limited to what is necessary in  
relation to the purpose of processing;  
- a fine of 24,714.50 lei, equivalent to  
5,000 EURO for violating the provisions of art. 5 para.  
(1) lit. e) and para. (2) of the GDPR, because  
the operator did not establish a retention period for  
the processed personal data through  
the video surveillance system (the images) and stored them  
for a longer period than necessary for the fulfillment of  
the purpose for which they are processed, namely access  
control in the condominium, although it had the obligation  
to keep the images in a form that allows the identification  
of the data subjects for a period that does not exceed  
the period necessary for fulfilling the purposes for which  
the data is processed.

At the same time, based on art. 58 para. (2) lit. d) of  
the GDPR, the following corrective measures were imposed  
on the operator:  
- Review and update the technical and  
organizational measures implemented as a result of the  
assessment regarding the risk to the rights and freedoms  
of individuals, including procedures related to  
the protection of personal data and establishing  
deadlines regarding the retention of data in a form  
that allows the identification of data subjects for a  
period that does not exceed the period necessary  
for fulfilling the purposes for which the data is processed.  
- Evaluate the processing carried out considering  
the principle of proportionality and minimizing  
data in relation to the purpose and legal basis of  
processing and implement the necessary measures to  
ensure compliance with the principles related to  
the processing of personal data provided by  
art. 5 of the GDPR.  

The investigation was initiated following a  
notification reporting a possible violation of  
the provisions of the GDPR, as representatives of  
the security company collected and processed personal  
data for the purpose of allowing individuals access to  
the entrance of the residential complex, in which  
they requested a series of data from individuals entering  
the complex and noted them in an internal register.  
The investigation revealed that the processing of data  
for access to the residential complex was carried out  
according to a security service contract  
concluded between the owners' association (the operator)  
and the security company (the processor), whereby  
the association mandated the security company to ensure  
the security and protection of the objective through  
security agents and to complete the access record  
of individuals. In this regard, the operator issued for  
the processor the instruction that the agents  
performing security services complete  
the Access Record of Individuals with the personal  
data mentioned in its columns, namely  
name, surname, series and number of the identity document,  
destination, arrival time, departure time, observations,  
exclusively for delivery and/or courier services.  

At the same time, during the investigation, it was found  
that at the level of the residential complex,  
access control was also carried out through  
the video surveillance system, and the Owners' Association  
could not prove compliance with the principle of  
storage limitation, established by  
art. 5 para. (1) lit. e) of the GDPR, namely  
the establishment of appropriate retention periods for  
the images, noting the existence of images  
stored for approximately one and a half years.  
In this context, we emphasize that according to art. 4  
point 7 of the GDPR, the operator determines the purpose and  
means of processing, and according to art. 28 para.  
(3) lit. a) of the GDPR, the processor processes  
the data only based on documented instructions  
from the operator.  
We also remind that according to art. 5 of  
the GDPR, the operator must respect the principles of  
data processing, including those regarding  
“lawfulness, fairness, and transparency,” “data minimization,”  
and “storage limitation.” At the same time, the operator is  
responsible for compliance with the principles and must  
demonstrate this compliance (“the principle of  
accountability”).
