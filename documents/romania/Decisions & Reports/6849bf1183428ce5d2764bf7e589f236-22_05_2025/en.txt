**May 22, 2025**  
**Sanctions Applied for Non-Compliance with GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in 2025, an investigation into the operator Dumitru Viorel Focșa, during which it found a violation of the provisions of Article 5 paragraph (1) letter a) in relation to Article 6 paragraph (1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
The operator was sanctioned with a fine of 4,977.30 lei, equivalent to the amount of 1,000 euros according to the BNR exchange rate on the date the sanction was applied.  
The investigation conducted on the operator was initiated following the receipt of a complaint from a data subject regarding the violation of data protection legislation.  
Thus, it was found that the operator processed on its Facebook page (Meta), by disclosing in a public post, the personal data (name, surname, phone number) of the complainant to the Authority, without their consent and without fulfilling any other conditions provided in Article 6 paragraph (1) of the GDPR, although the operator had the obligation to process personal data legally, fairly, and transparently concerning the data subject.  
In this context, we mention that the operator had been previously sanctioned by the national supervisory authority for a similar act.
