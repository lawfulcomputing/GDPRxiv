**09.05.2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  
   
The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in April 2025, an investigation into the operator ROUMASPORT SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 5 paragraph (1) letters a) and b) and Article 6 paragraph (1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, in conjunction with Article 5 of Law no. 190/2018.  
As such, the operator was sanctioned with an administrative fine in the amount of 24,887 lei (equivalent to 5,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following complaints that reported possible violations of personal data processing.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator accessed the existing video surveillance system at one of its work locations and illegally processed the images of its employees, including for the purpose of their disciplinary investigation, thus violating the principles of processing and the conditions of legality of processing.  
At the same time, a corrective measure was imposed on the operator to ensure compliance of personal data processing operations in video monitoring activities, especially in the case of conducting disciplinary investigations of employees, with respect to the principles and conditions of data processing.
