**April 24, 2024**  
**EDPB Plenary – April 2024**  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) held on April 16-17, 2024, the following documents were primarily adopted: EDPB Strategy for the period 2024-2027;  
Opinion No. 8/2024 regarding consent or payment models implemented by major online platforms.  
Through this opinion, the EDPB emphasizes that when developing alternatives, major online platforms should consider offering an "equivalent alternative" that does not involve paying a fee and that, regarding the necessity for the respective consent to be free, the following criteria should be taken into account: conditionality, harm, power imbalance, and granularity. In issuing this opinion, the Decision of the Court of Justice of the European Union in the case of Bundeskartellamt vs Meta Platforms (C-252/21) was also considered.  
More information is available at: https://edpb.europa.eu/news
