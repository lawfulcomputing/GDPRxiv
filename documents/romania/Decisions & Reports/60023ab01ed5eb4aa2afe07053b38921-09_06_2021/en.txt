09.06.2021  
Fine for GDPR Violation  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation in May 2021 into the operator S.C. Dreamtime Call S.R.L., which was fined as a result of the fact that this operator did not provide the information requested by our institution, thus violating the provisions of Article 83 paragraph (5) letter e) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
As such, the operator S.C. Dreamtime Call S.R.L. was sanctioned with a fine amounting to 9,852.2 lei (equivalent to 2000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint alleging that S.C. Dreamtime Call S.R.L. unlawfully processed the personal data of an individual (the phone number) by repeatedly contacting this individual by phone without prior consent.  
Since the operator did not respond to our institution's requests, although it confirmed receipt of them, it was sanctioned with a fine.  
Additionally, the operator was required to provide the supervisory authority with all requested information within 5 working days from the communication of the minutes.
