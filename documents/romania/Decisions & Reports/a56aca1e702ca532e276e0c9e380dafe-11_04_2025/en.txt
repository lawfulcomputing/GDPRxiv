**11.04.2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in March 2025, an investigation into the operator NEW GAMBLING SOLUTIONS S.R.L. and found a violation of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b), d) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was sanctioned with: a fine in the amount of 9,951.20 lei, equivalent to 2,000 euros, for violating the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b), d) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
The investigation was initiated following the notification by the operator NEW GAMBLING SOLUTIONS S.R.L. of a personal data security breach, according to the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
During the investigation, it was found that, as a result of a cyber attack, data from the operator's IT infrastructure was accessed by a third party, specifically the data of the operator's employees (name, surname, personal identification number, series and number of ID, home address, data regarding the professional activity of the employees).  
In this context, we specify that this situation led to the unauthorized disclosure or unauthorized access by a third party to the personal data of a significant number of data subjects.  
As such, it was found that the operator did not implement appropriate technical and organizational measures and did not carry out testing, evaluation, and periodic assessment of the effectiveness of the technical and organizational measures to ensure the security of data processing, including the ability to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and continuous resilience of processing systems and services, in accordance with the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b), d) and paragraph (2) of GDPR.  
The operator has paid the imposed contravention fine.
