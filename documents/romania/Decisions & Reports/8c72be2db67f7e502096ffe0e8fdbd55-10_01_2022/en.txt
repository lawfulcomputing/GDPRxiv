Top European Fines  

According to data from a recently published survey,  
Romania ranks third in the European Union regarding fines imposed by national supervisory authorities from 2018 to the present.  
Thus, the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing has imposed, since May 25, 2018, a total of 68 fines amounting to 721,000 euros, placing Romania behind Spain and Italy.  
In the same ranking, Hungary ranks fourth with 45 fines imposed, while Norway ranks fifth with 40 fines.  
According to the survey data, from 2018 to 2021, fines totaling:  
 436,000 euros for the year 2018  
 72 million euros for the year 2019  
 171 million euros for the year 2020  
 1 billion euros for the year 2021.
