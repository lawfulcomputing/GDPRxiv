**10.09.2024**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed an investigation in August 2024 at the Pro Economica Foundation – Pro Economica Alapítvány and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and paragraph (2) of the General Data Protection Regulation. As such, the Pro Economica Foundation – Pro Economica Alapítvány was sanctioned with a fine of 4,976.70 lei (equivalent to 1,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following the operator's notification of a personal data security breach.  
The data security breach occurred as a result of a cyber attack that deleted personal data from the Foundation's own server, thereby affecting the availability of the stored data.  
From the checks carried out, it was found that the operator had not implemented appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security commensurate with the risk of processing, including the ability to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and continuous resilience of the processing systems and services, which led to the deletion of existing data on the own server and their unavailability for a certain period of time.  
Consequently, this breach led to unauthorized access to personal data (such as, name, surname, personal identification number, address, email, phone number, position, salary, allowance, the value of the non-reimbursable funding received, goods purchased from the non-reimbursable funding, and the signature of the legal representative) of certain data subjects.  
At the same time, under Article 58 paragraph (2) letter d) of the General Data Protection Regulation, a corrective measure was ordered against the operator Pro Economica Foundation – Pro Economica Alapítvány to review and update the technical and organizational measures implemented regarding the security of personal data processed through the IT infrastructure used, especially those concerning external network connections to the data servers.
