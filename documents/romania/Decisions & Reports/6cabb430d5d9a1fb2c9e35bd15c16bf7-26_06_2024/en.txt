June 26, 2024  
EDPB Plenary – June 2024  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) held on June 18-19, 2024, Guideline No. 1/2023 regarding the application of Article 37 of Directive (EU) 2016/680 (final version after public consultation) was adopted.  
During this meeting, representatives of the European Commission provided a summary presentation of the provisions of Regulation (EU) No. 900/2024 concerning transparency and targeting in political advertising, applicable from October 10, 2025.
