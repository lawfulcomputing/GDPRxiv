**May 23, 2025**  
**May 25, 2025 – Completion of 7 Years since the Application of GDPR**  

On May 25, 2025, seven years will have passed since the application of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (GDPR), across all member states of the European Union. This European Regulation established uniform rules for the effective protection of the right to personal data protection throughout the community space.  

On this occasion, we bring to the public's attention relevant information from the activity of the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing in the first 4 months of 2025.  

Thus, during the period from January to April of the current year, our institution received 3,048 complaints, notifications, and reports regarding security incidents, based on which 153 investigations were opened.  

As a result of these investigations, 52 fines were imposed during this period, totaling 1,134,267 lei (equivalent to 230,500 euros). At the same time, 68 warnings were issued, and 96 corrective measures were ordered, along with one warning and two decisions to cease certain data processing activities.  

Regarding the complaint resolution activity, the ANSPDCP received 2,799 complaints in the first four months of the current year. For the resolution of the admissible complaints, 67 investigations were initiated.  

Additionally, during the same period, data operators submitted 65 notifications regarding data security breaches and 184 reports concerning possible non-compliance with GDPR provisions. Based on these 249 reports and notifications regarding security incidents, 86 investigations were conducted.  

The complaints, reports, and notifications regarding data security breaches received by the ANSPDCP during this period mainly concerned the following aspects:  
- Disclosure of personal data to third parties in the public space, online;  
- Processing of images through video surveillance systems;  
- Non-compliance with the rights of data subjects;  
- Receipt of unsolicited commercial messages;  
- Violation of data processing principles.  

Moreover, in the first 4 months of this year, the ANSPDCP received a total of 289 requests for opinions from operators and their representatives (from both the public and private sectors), from other entities, and from individuals regarding various aspects related to the application of GDPR and other relevant regulations.  

During the same period, opinions were issued on 29 draft normative acts submitted by public institutions, which involved analyzing various and complex aspects regarding the proper application of data protection rules in multiple fields of activity.  

Regarding the representation activity in court, in the first four months of 2025, 15 new lawsuits were registered, of which 7 requests concerned the contestation of minutes of findings/sanctions issued by the ANSPDCP.  

Additionally, from January to April 2025, communication activities aimed at informing the general public about the rules of personal data processing continued. Thus, on the occasion of celebrating the European Data Protection Day on January 28, 2025, a conference titled "Anniversary Review at 20 Years since the Establishment of ANSPDCP" was organized. This event was attended by representatives of national central public authorities and institutions, the executive and legislative forums, representatives from academic circles, non-governmental organizations, major professional unions/associations, as well as relevant operators/representatives from the public and private sectors.  

In this context, at the proposal of our institution, an informational video dedicated to promoting the General Data Protection Regulation was broadcast on the national television channel TVR and in the public transport means of the Bucharest Transport Company.  

In the first 4 months of 2025, the ANSPDCP continued its public information activity by publishing 40 press releases mainly concerning the sanctions imposed.  

Regarding external cooperation activities, we specify that in the first four months of 2025, our institution analyzed 23 requests for approval of BCRs submitted by multinational companies.  

Additionally, the ANSPDCP acted, at the request of some companies for BCR approval, as a co-reviewer for 8 sets of BCRs, as well as being a member of the drafting team for the opinion of the European Data Protection Committee regarding a set of corporate rules.
