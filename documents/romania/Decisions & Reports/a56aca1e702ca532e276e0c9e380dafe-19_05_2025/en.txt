**May 19, 2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing has completed an investigation into the operator Maravet S.R.L. and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (4) in conjunction with Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b) and d) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. For the committed act, the operator was sanctioned with a fine amounting to 24,885.50 Lei, equivalent to the sum of 5,000 Euro.  

The investigation was initiated following the notification by the operator Maravet S.R.L. of a personal data security breach, according to the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. Thus, the operator notified that a former employee of the company copied and unlawfully disclosed information containing personal data from the operator's database and posted it on a website.  

During the investigation, it was found that this individual posted on the website of their new employer, for a determined period of time, personal data belonging to a significant number of data subjects. This situation led to the unauthorized disclosure of personal data, namely: names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, personal identification numbers, numbers and series of identity cards or passports, employment documents, employee salaries, professional evaluations of employees, photographs of employees and clients.  

It was thus established that the operator did not take measures to ensure that any natural person acting under its authority and having access to personal data only processes them at the request of the operator and did not implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security corresponding to the risk of processing, including the ability to ensure the confidentiality and integrity of processing systems and services.  

The operator has paid the established contravention fine.
