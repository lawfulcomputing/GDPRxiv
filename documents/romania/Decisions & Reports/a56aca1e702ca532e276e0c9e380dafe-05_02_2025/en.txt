**05.02.2025**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing completed, in December 2024, an investigation into the operator FARMEC S.A. and found a violation of Article 25 paragraph (1) in conjunction with Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b), d) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was fined in the amount of 24,854.50 lei (equivalent to 5,000 Euro).  
The investigation was initiated following the transmission by the operator FARMEC S.A. of a notification of a personal data security breach, according to the provisions of Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
During the investigation, it was found that, as a result of a cyber attack, a database containing users and administrators of the operator's website was accessed, which led to the extraction of data from the aforementioned record system.  
It was also found that the operator had not implemented the necessary security measures at the time of the incident to prevent the attack and had not updated its IT systems to the latest version permitted by licensing to address new cyber threats.  
This led to the unauthorized disclosure or unauthorized access to personal data of a significant number of data subjects, such as: first name, last name, email address, encrypted password for user account access, thus violating the provisions of Article 25 paragraph (1) in conjunction with Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b), d) and paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
The operator has paid the established contravention fine.
