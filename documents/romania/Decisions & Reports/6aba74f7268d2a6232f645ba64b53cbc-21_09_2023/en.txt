**September 21, 2023**  
**Annual Report of ANSPDCP for 2022**  

The activity report for the year 2022 is publicly available on the website of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing in the section "General Information/Public Interest Information/Annual Reports."  
The annual report contains a summary presentation of the Authority's activities, structured in accordance with its main competencies, in the following chapters: Chapter I – Overview, Chapter II – Regulatory, Advisory, Consultation, and Public Information Activities, Chapter III – Monitoring and Control Activities, Chapter IV – Activities in the Field of International Relations, Chapter V – Economic Management of the Authority, and Chapter VI – Proposals for Legislative Amendments.  
Within this Report, statistical benchmarks are highlighted in each chapter, including comparative graphs with previous years, such as: the total number of investigations conducted or differentiated (ex officio, respectively based on complaints), the number of complaints, notifications, and reports regarding security incidents, fines imposed, the amount of fines, warnings, corrective measures, the number of opinions issued, advisories issued, lawsuits, and press releases.  
Additionally, the activity report for the year 2022 includes presentations of cases from the investigations conducted, as well as the most relevant opinions issued, which reflect the complexity of the activities carried out, in relation to the responsibilities of our institution.  
Regarding monitoring and control activities, in 2022, the Authority received a total of 4,260 complaints, notifications, and reports regarding security incidents, based on which 629 investigations were opened.  
As a result of the investigations, 69 fines were imposed, totaling 1,058,863 lei. Furthermore, 134 warnings were issued, and 93 corrective measures were ordered.  
At the same time, we specify that during the year 2022, the National Supervisory Authority received a total of 948 requests for opinions regarding various aspects related to the interpretation and application of Regulation (EU) 679/2016, from operators and their representatives, in both the public and private sectors, as well as from other entities and individuals.  
Moreover, our institution issued opinions on a total of 107 draft normative acts developed by public institutions and authorities that involved various aspects regarding the processing of personal data, pursuant to Article 57 paragraph (1) letter c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
Regarding representation before the courts, the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing managed a total of 108 cases pending in various procedural stages in 2022.  
The activity report for the year 2022 was submitted to the authorized institutions, in accordance with the provisions of Article 5 paragraph (2) of Law no. 102/2005 regarding the establishment, organization, and functioning of the National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing.
