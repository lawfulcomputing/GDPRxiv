**March 21, 2022**  
**EDPB Plenary – March 2022**  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online in March 2022, the following documents were primarily adopted:  
1. Guideline No. 2/2022 on the application of Article 60 of the General Data Protection Regulation  
2. Guideline No. 3/2022 on non-recommended patterns in the use of social networks: How to recognize and avoid them  

This document contains practical recommendations aimed at avoiding the use of inappropriate patterns at the level of social networks, in relation to the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
More information is available at: https://edpb.europa.eu/news
