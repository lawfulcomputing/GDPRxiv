**February 26, 2024**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Authority for the Supervision of Personal Data Processing has completed an investigation into the operator VESTAS CEU ROMÂNIA SRL and found a violation of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letter b) and Article 32 paragraphs (2) and (4) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator has been sanctioned with an administrative fine in the amount of 14,928 lei (equivalent to 3,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following the operator's notification of a personal data security breach, in accordance with Article 33 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
The data security breach occurred as a result of the unauthorized disclosure of personal data (name, place of residence, salary, CV (which contained, as applicable: photograph, contact details, address, nationality, date of birth, gender, marital status, military service status, links to social media profiles, professional experience, education, technical skills), as well as copies of passports) for a significant number of employees, with this data being accessed internally, repeatedly, and illegally disclosed to a third party.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator had not implemented appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security commensurate with the risk presented by the processing, particularly generated by the unauthorized disclosure or unauthorized access to the personal data stored.  
Additionally, under Article 58 paragraph (2) letter d) of the GDPR, a corrective measure was imposed on the operator VESTAS CEU ROMÂNIA SRL to implement a monitoring solution for the application of the implemented working procedures, to avoid similar security incidents.
