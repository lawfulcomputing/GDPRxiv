**31.01.2025**  
**EDPB Plenary – January 2025**  

On January 16, 2025, an online plenary session of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) took place, during which the following relevant documents were primarily adopted to ensure the proper application of the European Data Protection Regulation:  
- **Guideline No. 1/2025 on Pseudonymization**, which is open for public consultation for a period of six weeks;  
This guideline provides clarifications in the sense that pseudonymized data, which could be attributed to an individual through the use of additional information, remains information relating to an identifiable natural person and, therefore, continues to be personal data. If the data can be correlated with a natural person by the data controller or by another person, it remains personal data. It is also stated that pseudonymization can reduce risks and can facilitate the use of legitimate interests as a legal basis (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR), as long as all other requirements of the GDPR are met. Furthermore, pseudonymization can contribute to ensuring compatibility with the original purpose (Article 6(4) of the GDPR).  
- **Statement on the Interaction between Competition Law and Data Protection Law**
