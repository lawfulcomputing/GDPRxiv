**June 25, 2024**  
**Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed  
two investigations into the operators of Muntenia Electric Networks SA and Dobrogea Electric Networks SA, during which it found violations of the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b) and d) and Article 32 paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operators were sanctioned with a fine for violating the aforementioned provisions, as follows:  
- Muntenia Electric Networks SA - fine in the amount of 14,928.60 lei (equivalent to 3000 EURO);  
- Dobrogea Electric Networks SA - fine in the amount of 4,976.20 lei (equivalent to 1000 EURO).  
The investigations were initiated following complaints indicating that a user connected to their account on the joint website of the two operators, www.e.distributie.com, could view the personal data of other customers of the operators.  
At the same time, the operators Muntenia Electric Networks SA and Dobrogea Electric Networks SA notified our institution of data security breaches regarding the reported issues.  
During the investigations conducted, it was found that the operators had not implemented appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security commensurate with the risk of processing, including the ability to ensure the confidentiality of processing systems and services, which led to unauthorized access by a third party to the personal data (name, surname, street, city, personal identification number) of some customers of the operators, thus violating the provisions of Article 32 paragraph (1) letters b) and d) and Article 32 paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  

At the same time, based on the provisions of Article 58 paragraph (2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, a corrective measure was ordered for the operators Dobrogea Electric Networks SA and Muntenia Electric Networks SA to implement periodic testing for the online services offered to customers on the new website owned by these operators.
