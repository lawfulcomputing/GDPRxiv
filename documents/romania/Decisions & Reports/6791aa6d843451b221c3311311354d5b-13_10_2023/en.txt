**October 13, 2023**  
**EDPB Plenary – September 2023**  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board held on September 19-20, 2023, the following documents were primarily adopted:  

1. **Opinion No. 1/2023 of the EDPB and EDPS on the proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing additional procedural rules regarding the application of Regulation (EU) 2016/679**  

2. **Guidance No. 1/2023 on Article 37 of Directive 2016/680 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data by competent authorities for the purposes of preventing, detecting, investigating, or prosecuting criminal offenses or the execution of criminal penalties and on the free movement of such data and repealing Framework Decision 2008/977/JHA of the Council**  

More information is available at:
