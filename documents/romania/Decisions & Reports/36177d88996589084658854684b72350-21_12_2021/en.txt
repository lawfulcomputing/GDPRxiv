**December 21, 2021**  
**EDPB Plenary – December 14, 2021**  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on December 14, 2021, the final version of Guideline No. 1/2021 regarding examples related to security breaches was adopted. This document aims to support operators and authorized persons, being complementary to the Guidelines on the notification of security incidents, adopted in 2017. Guideline No. 1/2021 contains numerous illustrative concrete cases, related to the risks of processing generated particularly by the nature of the data, their volume, and the number of affected individuals, including examples regarding the impact of human errors by the operators' employees, as well as recommendations for risk prevention.  

More information is available at:
