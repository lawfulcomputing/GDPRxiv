**May 19, 2021**  
**Fine for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority completed on May 6, 2021, an investigation into a Homeowners' Association in the municipality of Iași, to which it imposed a fine, as a result of the fact that this operator did not provide the information requested by our institution, thus violating the provisions of Article 83 paragraph (5) letter e) correlated with the provisions of Article 58 paragraph (1) letters (a) and (e) of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
As such, the Homeowners' Association was sanctioned with an administrative fine in the amount of 2463.3 lei, equivalent to the sum of 500 EURO, based on Article 58 paragraph (2) letter i) of the General Data Protection Regulation, in conjunction with Article 8 of Government Ordinance no. 2/2001.  
The investigation was conducted following a complaint alleging that the operator displayed payment lists detailing the name and surname of each member of the Homeowners' Association. Additionally, the complainant also reported the display of a defamatory document in which their personal data (name and surname) were mentioned.  
Since the operator did not respond to the requests of our institution, although it confirmed receipt of them, it was sanctioned with a fine.  
Furthermore, the Homeowners' Association was ordered to provide the supervisory authority with all requested information within 5 working days from the communication of the minutes.
