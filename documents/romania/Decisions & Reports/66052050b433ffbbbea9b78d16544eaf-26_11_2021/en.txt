**November 26, 2021**  
**EDPB Plenary of November**  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, which took place on November 19, 2021, Guideline No. 5/2021 regarding the interplay between the application of Article 3 and the provisions relating to international transfers in Chapter V of the General Data Protection Regulation was adopted. This document will be open for public consultation until the end of January 2022, during which proposals from interested parties may be submitted. More information is available at the following address:  
https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/documents/public-consultations/2021/guidelines-052021-interplay-between-application_en
