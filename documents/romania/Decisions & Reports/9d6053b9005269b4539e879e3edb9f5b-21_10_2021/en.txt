**21.10.2021**  
**EDPB Plenary – 13.10.2021**  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on October 13, 2021, the final version of Guidelines No. 10/2020 regarding the restrictions provided for in Article 23 of the General Data Protection Regulation was adopted.  
More information is available at the following address:
