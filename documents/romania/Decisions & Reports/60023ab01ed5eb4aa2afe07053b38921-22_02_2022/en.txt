**February 22, 2022**  
**Fine for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation in February into the operator  
the Professional Civil Society of Lawyers "Sabou, Burz & Cuc" and found that it violated  
the provisions of Article 5 paragraph (1) letters a), b), c), f) and  
paragraph (2) and Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
The operator Professional Civil Society of Lawyers "Sabou, Burz & Cuc" was sanctioned  
administratively with a fine of 4946 lei, equivalent to 1,000 EURO.  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint regarding the disclosure by  
the operator of the personal data of a petitioner (client  
of the operator) without their prior consent and information, by posting an address received by  
them from a public institution on a WhatsApp group used by the lawyers of a bar association.  
During the investigation, it was found that the Professional Civil Society of Lawyers "Sabou, Burz & Cuc" disclosed the personal data of the data subject  
(name, surname, home address, information  
related to a case pending before a court) in a WhatsApp group consisting of 247 members, without  
legal basis, excessively and incompatibly with the initial purpose of their collection, as well as without adopting  
technical and organizational measures to  
maintain the confidentiality of this data, thus violating  
the provisions of Article 5 paragraph (1) letters a), b),  
c), f) and paragraph (2), as well as Article 6 of  
the General Data Protection Regulation.  
Additionally, the operator was also subject to the following  
corrective measures:  
- the corrective measure to ensure compliance with  
the General Data Protection Regulation of the  
operations of collecting and further processing of  
the personal data of the petitioner to ensure  
notification of all members of the WhatsApp group  
used by the lawyers of a bar association for the deletion  
of the address posted on this group;  
- the corrective measure to ensure compliance with  
the General Data Protection Regulation of the  
operations of collecting and further processing of  
personal data within the legal relationships of  
assisting and representing the operator's clients,  
so as to avoid the disclosure of personal data  
obtained from them, outside the situations permitted  
by law, including through regular training of  
the persons processing data under the authority  
of the operator.  
A.N.S.P.D.C.P.
