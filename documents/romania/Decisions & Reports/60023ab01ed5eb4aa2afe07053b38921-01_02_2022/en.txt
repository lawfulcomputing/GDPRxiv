**01.02.2022**  
**Fine for Violation of GDPR**  
        
The National Supervisory Authority completed on  
January 7, 2022, an investigation into the operator SC  
Grupex 2000 SRL, during which it was found  
that there was a violation of the provisions of Art. 6 and Art. 9 in  
relation to the principles set forth in Art. 5 para.  
(1) letters a), b), c), and f) and para. (2) of  
the General Data Protection Regulation.  
As such, the operator was sanctioned  
administratively with a fine of 4,943.60 lei  
(the equivalent of 1,000 EURO).  
The investigation was initiated following the submission  
of complaints from a County Directorate for  
Social Assistance and Child Protection, regarding  
the posting of a video material containing images  
of institutionalized patients, published on the website  
owned by SC Grupex 2000 SRL.  
During the investigation, it was found that SC Grupex  
2000 SRL illegally processed the personal data of certain  
individuals, institutionalized patients, within  
a filmed material available on the operator's website,  
by violating the provisions of Art. 6 and  
Art. 9 in relation to the principles set forth in  
Art. 5 para. (1) letters a), b), c), and f) and para. (2)  
of the GDPR.  
Additionally, during the investigation, the operator was  
also subjected to the corrective measure of ensuring  
compliance with the GDPR for the processing  
operations of personal data, by implementing  
appropriate technical and organizational measures, in  
compliance with Art. 5 and Art. 6 of the GDPR.
