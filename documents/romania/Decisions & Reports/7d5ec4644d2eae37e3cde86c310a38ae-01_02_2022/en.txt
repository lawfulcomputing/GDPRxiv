EDPB Plenary – January 2022  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online in January 2022, Guideline No. 1/2022 regarding the rights of data subjects - the right of access was adopted.  
This document aims to support both individuals and data controllers with clarifications and practical examples of application, in order to enhance the effectiveness of the provisions of Article 15 of the General Data Protection Regulation.  
The Guideline is currently undergoing public consultation until March 11, 2022.  
More information is available at:  
https://edpb.europa.eu/news
