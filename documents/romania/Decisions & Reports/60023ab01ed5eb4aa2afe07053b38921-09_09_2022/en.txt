09.09.2022  
Fine for GDPR Violation  
                        
The National Supervisory Authority completed in  
August 2022 an investigation at SC Raiffeisen Bank  
SA and found a violation of the provisions of Art. 5  
para. (1) letters a), b), and d) and Art. 6 of the  
General Data Protection Regulation.  
SC Raiffeisen Bank SA, as a processor of an  
operator, was sanctioned as follows:  
with a warning for violating the provisions of Art. 5  
para. (1) letters a) and b) and Art. 6 of the  
General Data Protection Regulation;  
with a fine in the amount of 9,763.60 lei (equivalent  
to the sum of 2,000 EURO) for violating the provisions  
of Art. 5 para. (1) letter d) of the General Data  
Protection Regulation.  
The investigation was initiated following a  
complaint filed by a petitioner who reported  
that an operator was sending text messages (SMS) to  
his mobile phone regarding the transfer of certain  
amounts of money to specific individuals, transfers  
that the petitioner did not carry out.  
During the investigation, it was found that at  
SC Raiffeisen Bank SA, as a processor, the  
petitioner’s phone number was incorrectly entered  
into the application provided by the operator for  
initiating transactions at the request of clients.  
It was also noted that the petitioner was not a  
client of SC Raiffeisen Bank SA and had not requested  
the initiation of any transactions through the  
operator's application.  
Furthermore, the supervisory authority found that  
SC Raiffeisen Bank SA, as a processor, had  
processed inaccurate data (the phone number) of  
individuals, occasional clients, who had carried out  
money transactions through the operator's application,  
using the petitioner’s phone number in 44  
transactions, thus violating the principle of  
data accuracy provided in Art. 5 para. (1) letter  
d) of the General Data Protection Regulation.
