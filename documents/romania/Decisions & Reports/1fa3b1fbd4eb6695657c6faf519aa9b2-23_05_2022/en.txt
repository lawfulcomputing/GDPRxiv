**May 23, 2022**  
**EDPB Plenary – May 2022**  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on May 4, 2022, the Joint Opinion with the EDPS regarding the proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council on harmonized rules for fair access to data and their fair use (Data Act) was adopted. This document formulated several recommendations and proposed the introduction of guarantees, including limitations regarding the use of data for direct marketing purposes, employee monitoring, or credit scoring.  

During the Plenary of the European Data Protection Board, held online on May 12, 2022, the following were primarily adopted:  
1. **Guideline No. 4/2022 on the calculation of fines under the GDPR**, which is subject to public consultation until June 27, 2022  
2. **Guideline No. 5/2022 on facial recognition in the field of law enforcement**, which is under public consultation until June 27, 2022
