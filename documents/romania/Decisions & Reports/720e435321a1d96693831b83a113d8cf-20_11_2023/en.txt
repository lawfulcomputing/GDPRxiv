**November 20, 2023**  
**A New Sanction for Violation of GDPR**  

The National Supervisory Authority has completed an investigation in November of this year into the operator Libra Internet Bank SA, during which it found violations of the provisions of Article 58 paragraph (2) and Article 83 paragraph (5) letter e) and paragraph (6) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.  
As such, the operator was fined in the amount of 7,454.25 lei, equivalent to 1,500 EURO.  
The investigation was initiated following a complaint regarding the manner in which Libra Internet Bank SA responded to the applicant's request for access to data following the corrective measure imposed by the National Supervisory Authority.  
During the investigation, it was found that the operator Libra Internet Bank SA did not fully comply with the corrective measure ordered under Article 58 paragraph (2) letter c) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as in the response sent to the applicant, it did not properly communicate, within the established timeframe, all information related to the categories of personal data processed concerning the applicant, the purposes of processing, the recipients, or categories of recipients.  
Furthermore, the National Supervisory Authority noted that the operator did not provide evidence regarding the communication of the response to complete the information, as established by the corrective measure.  
Therefore, it was found that the provisions of Article 58 paragraph (2) and Article 83 paragraph (5) letter e) and paragraph (6) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 were violated.  
At the same time, a corrective measure was ordered for the operator to send the complete response to the applicant's request by mail, to the correspondence details indicated by the applicant.
