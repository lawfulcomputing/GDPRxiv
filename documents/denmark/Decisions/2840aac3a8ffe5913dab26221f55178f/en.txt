**Another Municipality Recommended for Fine for Lack of Encryption**  
**Date: 14-08-2024**  

**Decision**  
**Public Authorities**  
**Police Report**  
**Reported Breach of Personal Data Security**  
**Processing Security**  
**Children**  

The Data Protection Agency has reported Vejen Municipality to the police for insufficient security measures. Three stolen computers containing information about children were not encrypted - and it turned out that the same was true for up to 300 other computers in the municipality. The Data Protection Agency emphasizes that encryption is very often required.  

The Data Protection Agency has reported Vejen Municipality and recommended a fine of 200,000 kroner. This is the conclusion of a case that began when the municipality reported a breach of personal data security because five laptops had been stolen.  

The computers were intended solely for use by teachers and students as part of the teaching process. In practice, however, they were also used by teachers to create status descriptions of students, class handovers, etc., and therefore contained, among other things, several pieces of information about students with special challenges. None of the computers were encrypted.  

In the Data Protection Agency's subsequent investigation of the case, it emerged that there were up to 300 computers in the municipality that risked being used in the same way and which were also not encrypted.  

"I must say that I am surprised that we continue to see these cases in municipalities. We have received notifications about this type of breach for several years, we have gone out and warned several times, and we have also recommended fines in previous cases," says Vibeke Dyssemark Thomsen, chief consultant at the Data Protection Agency, and continues:  
"Encryption is a very basic security measure that is relatively easy and not particularly costly to implement. We therefore urge all municipalities to take a thorough look at their portable devices and get encryption in order now."  

There has been a focus on encryption in municipalities during maturity inspections in both 2021 and 2022, and the Data Protection Agency has previously recommended fines in cases regarding the lack of encryption of portable devices in Favrskov Municipality, Gladsaxe Municipality, Hørsholm Municipality, Odsherred Municipality, and the Civil Affairs Agency. Furthermore, processing security in municipalities – including encryption of portable devices – is also a special focus area for the Data Protection Agency's targeted inspection efforts in 2024.
